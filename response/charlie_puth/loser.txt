The song "Loser" by Charlie Puth is an emotional and reflective look at the feeling of loss and regret. In the song, Charlie reflects on a past relationship and the mistakes he made that led to its demise. The lyrics of the song are broken down into verses, pre-choruses, choruses, post-choruses and a bridge, each of which explores different aspects of the pain and regret of losing a loved one. 

In the first verse, Charlie reflects on his drinking habits and how he tried to avoid thinking about their relationship. He acknowledges that he was too late in realizing that she was "one in a million", and that he should have never let her slip away. 

The pre-chorus and chorus both explore the same idea, that he was a "loser" in the relationship and he'll never recover from the pain of losing someone he loved. The post-chorus echoes the chorus, and further emphasizes the feeling of regret and loss. 

The bridge of the song is a plea to a higher power for understanding. Charlie wonders if God is real, and if the person he lost still thinks about him. He acknowledges that it will take some adjusting to move on from the pain of the loss. 

The song "Loser" by Charlie Puth is a poignant and heartfelt exploration of the pain of losing a loved one. The song is honest and reflective, and it is clear that Charlie has gone through a lot of pain and regret to get to the point where he can express his feelings in this song. It is a beautiful and relatable song that speaks to the pain of loss and the hope of recovery.
