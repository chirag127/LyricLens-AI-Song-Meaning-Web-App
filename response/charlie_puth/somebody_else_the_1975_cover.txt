Charlie Puth's song Somebody Else is a heartfelt ballad about the pain of realizing that the person you love has moved on and found someone else. The song starts with the lyrics, “so I heard you found somebody else and at first I thought it was a lie”, which implies that the narrator is in denial over the fact that their relationship is over. The narrator expresses their feelings of loss in the following lines: “I took all my things that make sounds, the rest I can do without”, and “Our love has gone cold, you’re intertwining your soul with somebody else”. 

The narrator recognizes that they can't have their partner back, and is trying to move on, singing “C'mon baby, this ain't the last time that I'll see your face, c'mon baby, you said you'd find someone to take my place”. However, they are still struggling to accept the fact that their love is gone, and they express this in the lines “every time I start to believe in anything you’re saying I’m reminded that I should be gettin’ over it”.

The chorus repeats the main theme of the song; that the narrator's love has gone cold and they are struggling to accept it. They express their feelings of loss and bitterness in the lines “I don't want your body, but I hate to think about you with somebody else”. The song ends with the chant “get someone you love, get someone you need, that get money, I can't give you my soul, 'cause we're never alone”, which serves as a reminder to the narrator that they have to move on and find someone new.

In summary, Charlie Puth's Somebody Else is a song about the pain of realizing that the person you love has moved on and found someone else. The song expresses the narrator's sadness, bitterness, and struggle to accept the end of the relationship, as well as the reminder that they have to move on and find someone new. Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a sense of sadness and loss, as well as a determination to move on and find someone new. Ultimately, the song is a poignant reminder of the pain of letting go.
