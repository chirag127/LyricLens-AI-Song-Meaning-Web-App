In the song "Suffer" by Charlie Puth, the narrator is talking about a romantic relationship with someone who is cold-hearted and difficult to be with. The song begins with the narrator describing their relationship as explosive and toxic, with the other person constantly leaving them waiting and wanting more. In the chorus, the narrator admits that they are a "sucker for a cold-hearted lover" and that the other person makes them suffer.

In the second verse, the narrator continues to describe the tumultuous nature of their relationship and how the other person has a hold on them. They describe the other person as wearing "black magic" and how they feel powerless against their charm. Despite this, the narrator still wants to be with them and make love to them.

In the bridge, the narrator expresses their frustration with the other person always getting their way and how the pattern of their relationship never seems to change. Despite the suffering they endure, they still love the other person.

The song is a painful, yet relatable, portrayal of a toxic and one-sided relationship. The narrator is unable to break free from the hold the other person has on them, even though they know the relationship is causing them suffering. The song is emotive and captures the raw emotions of being in a difficult relationship.
