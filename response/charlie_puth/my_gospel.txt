"My Gospel" by Charlie Puth is a song about an individual who is willing to do whatever it takes to win the affections of a special someone. The song begins by listing the extreme lengths the protagonist is willing to go to prove his love, from stealing a million dollars to taking a limousine ride together. This imagery sets the tone of the song, which is one of passion and determination - the protagonist is willing to do whatever it takes to make the other person love him. 

The chorus of the song further emphasizes this idea of love and dedication, with the protagonist declaring that he will do anything to make the other person love him, regardless of what the world may think. This is further emphasized in the bridge of the song, where the protagonist offers to change the other person's name - showing how far he is willing to go to make them happy.

The lyrics of this song are full of emotion and demonstrate the intensity of the protagonist's feelings. Through the use of vivid imagery and expressive language, the protagonist conveys his feelings of love and dedication in a powerful and moving way. The song also highlights the importance of being willing to take risks and go to extreme lengths in order to show someone that you love them.

The song also contains a lot of linguistic analysis, which helps to interpret the emotions and feelings that the artist is communicating. For example, the use of language such as "crazy about you" and "I'm all but shameless" conveys the intensity of the protagonist's feelings, and the repetition of the chorus further emphasizes the importance of the message being conveyed.

In summary, "My Gospel" by Charlie Puth is a powerful song about taking risks and going to extreme lengths to show someone that you love them. The vivid imagery and expressive language of the lyrics help to illustrate the protagonist's intense emotions, and the linguistic analysis of the song reveals the powerful emotions the artist is communicating.
