"Light Switch" is a song by Charlie Puth that explores the theme of an intense, yet tumultuous relationship. In the first verse, Puth sings about how his partner often calls him late at night, only to do "dirty" things to him. Despite this, Puth continues to be drawn back to this person. In the pre-chorus and chorus, Puth sings about how he is "hypnotized" and can't deny his attraction to this person, even though he knows the relationship is unhealthy. The refrain emphasizes the idea that this person has a strong hold over him, and that he can't help but want them.

In the second verse, Puth sings about the constant guessing and stressing that comes with being in this relationship. Despite knowing that it's not good for him, he continues to be pulled back in by this person.

Overall, "Light Switch" is a song about the allure of a toxic relationship and the difficulty of breaking free from it. It's a relatable and catchy song that showcases Puth's vocal and songwriting abilities.
