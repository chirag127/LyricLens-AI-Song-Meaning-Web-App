"I Won't Tell a Soul" by Charlie Puth is a song about two people who are in a forbidden relationship. In the first verse, the narrator is expressing their feelings of guilt and longing for the other person, even though they know the relationship won't work out. They feel like they can't let go, and they ask the other person to stay with them, even though they know it's wrong. 

In the chorus, the narrator is asking the other person to stay with them even though they know someone else is waiting for them. The narrator wants the relationship to remain a secret, so no one finds out about it. 

In the second verse, the narrator is encouraging the other person to take a chance and indulge in the forbidden relationship. They assure the other person that they won't be judged and that this could be their secret. 

The song ends with the chorus, expressing the narrator's feelings of guilt and longing for the other person. 

The song is a reflection of the complex emotions that come with being in a forbidden relationship. The narrator is torn between wanting to do the right thing and yet still wanting to be with the other person. The lyrics express their feelings of guilt and longing, and the melody perfectly captures the longing and sadness in the narrator's words. 

From a linguistic analysis, the lyrics of the song make use of repetition, particularly in the chorus, to emphasize the narrator's feelings of guilt and longing. The use of words like "secret", "cheating", and "taken" also help to emphasize the forbidden nature of the relationship. The language is also quite poetic, with the use of metaphors to express the narrator's longing for the other person. 

Overall, "I Won't Tell a Soul" by Charlie Puth is a beautiful and melancholy song that perfectly captures the complex emotions of being in a forbidden relationship. The poetic lyrics and the emotive melody create a powerful and moving song that gets to the heart of the narrator's feelings.
