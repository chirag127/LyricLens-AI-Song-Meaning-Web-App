In Charlie Puth’s song “Some Type of Love”, the singer expresses his love for someone in a way that transcends time and space. In the first verse, the singer reflects on how he won't be alone when he's old and grown, and that every single moment will be faded into the one he loves. This reflects the idea of a love that is unending and that won't fade with time. 

The chorus reflects the idea of an unyielding love, with the singer stating that even when the world is on fire, they won't even move, as there is no reason to be apart. He also expresses his commitment to his lover, stating that when it's said and done, he'll give himself to them.

The bridge of the song brings these ideas full circle, with the singer acknowledging that every story must eventually end, but that they can still pretend that every single moment will be just as they planned. He reiterates his commitment to his lover, saying that he will be with them and give himself to them.

In summary, “Some Type of Love” is a song about a love so strong that it transcends time and space and will never fade away. Lyrically, the song explores the idea of an enduring, unyielding love that is strong enough to withstand anything, an idea that is further reinforced by the singer’s commitment to his lover.

This song is an ode to the power of love, a powerful emotion that can keep two people together despite all odds. The lyrics are heartfelt and sincere, and the singer’s commitment to his lover is clear. The use of imagery in the lyrics helps to highlight the power of their love, with the idea of a love that transcends time and space being particularly poignant. Linguistically, the lyrics are full of emotion, conveying strong feelings of love and devotion. The song is sure to tug at the listener’s heartstrings, making it a memorable and powerful love song.
