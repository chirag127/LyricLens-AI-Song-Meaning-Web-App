In "Change," Charlie Puth and James Taylor explore the idea that love is the only thing that can truly bring people together. In the first verse, Puth sings about how people often look down on others who are different from them, but asks why this is the case when everyone has the same basic needs and desires. The chorus repeats the question, "Why can't we just get along?" and suggests that if loving one another is wrong, then how can people come together and understand each other.

In the second verse, Taylor and Puth sing about the waste of denying someone the chance to be themselves, and again express the belief that the world can change for the better if people can learn to love and accept one another. The bridge offers a glimmer of hope, with Puth singing that he knows that people can break apart, but that they don't have to go that way.

Overall, the song is a call to action for people to come together and work towards understanding and acceptance of one another, regardless of their differences. It encourages people to make a change in the world and strive for a more loving and inclusive society.

As a review or opinion of the song, "Change" is a powerful and poignant message of hope and unity. The catchy chorus and heartfelt lyrics make it an enjoyable and memorable song, and the collaboration between Puth and Taylor adds depth and richness to the message. Overall, "Change" is a powerful and inspiring call to action for people to come together and work towards a better future.
