Charlie Puth's song "Left Right Left" is a powerful anthem of resilience and perseverance in the face of hard times. The chorus of the song features the phrase "left right left right left," which symbolizes the idea of taking one step at a time and never giving up.

Despite the hurt and pain they are experiencing, the protagonist resolves not to give up and to take on the tough times. The bridge of the song then encourages listeners to keep on smiling and to never forget that others have gone through similar experiences. 

The final chorus of the song drives home the idea of perseverance in spite of the hard times. It encourages listeners to keep going and to never give up on their journey, no matter how far they have to go. The repetition of the phrase "left right left right left" emphasizes this point and emphasizes the importance of taking one step at a time.
