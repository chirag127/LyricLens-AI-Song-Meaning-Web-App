"Does It Feel" by Charlie Puth is a song about heartbreak and the realization that the person you once loved isn't the same anymore. The song begins with Puth asking his partner to lie to him, pretending that they are better off without him. However, he can tell from the look on their face that this isn't true. He then sings about how he loved them differently and how he kept them coming back. The chorus of the song is a question he asks his partner - does it feel like they will never find something better? 

The lyrics of the song are full of emotion and evoke a sense of longing and regret. The chorus is especially powerful, as Puth is asking his partner if they feel the same way he does - that they will never find something better. This sentiment is further reinforced by the repetition of the chorus, emphasizing Puth's feelings. 

The song is a great example of how emotions can be expressed through language. Puth is able to capture the idea of heartbreak and regret in a few simple words, and the repetition of the chorus further emphasizes his feelings. Additionally, the song also touches on the idea that sometimes it's better to let go of someone even though you still love them. 

In summary, "Does It Feel" by Charlie Puth is a song about heartbreak and the emotions surrounding it. The lyrics are full of emotion and the repetition of the chorus further emphasizes Puth's feelings. The song is a great example of how emotions can be expressed through language and how sometimes it's better to let go of someone even though you still love them.
