"Cheating on You" is a song by Charlie Puth about a man who regrets breaking up with his partner. In the song, Puth reflects on how he was arrogant and thought he could get his partner back whenever he wanted, but now he realizes how much he misses her and wishes he could take back what he did. The song is structured around the chorus, in which Puth sings about how he feels like he's cheating on his ex-partner whenever he's with someone else.

The first verse of the song sets the scene, with Puth describing how their relationship started with a kiss on his partner's mother's couch. In the pre-chorus, Puth admits that he regrets running away and wishes he could undo the damage he's done.

In the chorus, Puth sings about how he said goodbye to his partner, but now he feels like he's cheating on her whenever he's with someone else. He admits that he thought he'd be better off with someone new, but now he realizes that he's not over his ex-partner and he still loves her. The post-chorus repeats the line "baby, no" as Puth laments the fact that he's cheating on his ex-partner.

In the second verse, Puth continues to reflect on his regret and how he misses his ex-partner. He admits that he was cocky and shouldn't have done her wrong, and now he realizes that he didn't appreciate what he had until it was gone. In the pre-chorus and chorus, Puth repeats the same sentiments about how he wishes he could take back what he did and how he feels like he's cheating on his ex-partner.

In the bridge, Puth admits that there's no moving on and no one who can replace his ex-partner. He begs her to come back and take him back, even though he knows it's too late. The chorus repeats once more, with Puth admitting that he's still in love with his ex-partner and that he feels like he's cheating on her whenever he's with someone else.

Overall, "Cheating on You" is a heartfelt and emotional song about regret and lost love. Puth's raw vocals and emotional lyrics capture the pain and longing of someone who realizes too late that they've made a mistake. It's a song that will resonate with anyone who's ever been in a similar situation, and it showcases Puth's ability to create relatable and impactful music.
