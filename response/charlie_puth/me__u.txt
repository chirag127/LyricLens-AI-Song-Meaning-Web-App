"Me & U" by Charlie Puth is a fun and upbeat song that celebrates the joy of being in a relationship despite what others may say. The song begins with a simple yet catchy chorus that sets the tone for the song. The chorus then repeats, emphasizing the couple’s commitment to each other and their desire to ignore the opinions of those who try to get in the way of their relationship. The bridge of the song then brings the song to a close with the lyrics, “But one thing that I know is that we’re gonna be alright / So don’t listen to what people say ‘bout me and you,” sending the message that no matter what anyone else says, the couple will remain strong and happy together.

In summary, "Me & U" is a fun and upbeat song that celebrates a committed and fulfilling relationship despite the pressures of the outside world. The lyrics demonstrate how the couple is free to make their own choices and enjoy their relationship without feeling the pressure of other people’s opinions. The song is a testament to the power of relationships and the importance of standing strong in the face of opposition.
