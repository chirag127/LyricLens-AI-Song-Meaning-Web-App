The song "Tears On My Shoulder" by Charlie Puth is a heart-wrenching ode to the pain of leaving someone you love. In the song, Puth expresses the difficulty of having to leave someone in order to pursue his career and dreams, while simultaneously wishing that he could stay. The lyrics describe the emotions of guilt and regret that come along with having to make such a difficult decision. 

The song begins with Puth expressing his need to leave and asking his partner to trust him and understand that he is doing this for the both of them. He pleads with her to take his heart, but not to let it break, as he promises to come back again. He expresses his desire to stay, but knows that he must go. 

The chorus of the song is particularly poignant, as Puth sings about the sadness of having to leave, and not wanting to cause his partner any more pain. He expresses his reluctance to leave, but knows that it is necessary in order to achieve his goals. The song ends with Puth repeating the chorus, emphasizing the difficulty of having to leave the one he loves. 

The lyrics of "Tears On My Shoulder" provide a powerful insight into the emotions associated with having to leave someone you love. Puth conveys the conflict between pursuing his dreams and wishing he could stay, as well as the guilt and regret of leaving someone behind. The lyrics contain a great deal of emotion, and through linguistic analysis of the song, one can detect and interpret the emotions, social tendencies, and language style which helps analyze the emotions and feelings that musical artists express in their songs. 

Overall, "Tears On My Shoulder" is a beautiful and heartbreaking song that captures the difficulty of having to make a difficult decision. The lyrics are poignant and honest, and Charlie Puth does a great job of conveying the emotions associated with such a decision. It is sure to tug at the heartstrings of many who have had to make the same difficult choice.
