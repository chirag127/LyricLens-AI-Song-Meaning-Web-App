"Stress Me Out" by Charlie Puth is an upbeat and catchy song about the struggles of a relationship gone wrong. The song starts with the narrator admitting that their partner used to be a source of comfort and relaxation, but now they are feeling overwhelmed and stressed out. 

The chorus repeats the line "you stress me out," emphasizing the narrator's distress. This is further highlighted by the repetition of the line "but now I'm going, going, going out of my mind" as the narrator's anxiety mounts. The lyrics also speak to the difficulty of letting go of a relationship, even when it is toxic and stressful, with the narrator admitting that they can't seem to find the strength to move on. 

The song also makes use of clever wordplay, with the narrator finding solace in the chaos of the relationship, saying "it's a mess, but I like it." The use of this wordplay gives the song an added layer of depth and insight into the narrator's mindset. 

Through linguistic analysis of the lyrics, we can see that the song conveys a sense of frustration, confusion, and helplessness. This is further highlighted by the use of words such as "stressed" and "out of my mind," which are often associated with feelings of distress and anxiety. These words help to bring the narrator's feelings to life and make it easier for the listener to relate to the struggles they are facing.

In summary, "Stress Me Out" is a heartfelt and relatable song about the struggles of a difficult relationship. The clever use of wordplay and the inclusion of words associated with anxiety helps to bring the narrator's feelings to life, allowing the listener to relate to the emotions they are expressing.
