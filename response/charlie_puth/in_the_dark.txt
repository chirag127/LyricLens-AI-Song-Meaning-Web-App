In the song “In the Dark” by Charlie Puth, the narrator expresses his desire to spend time with a girl he has his eye on. He is interested in her, but she is playing hard to get and is constantly distracted by other guys. He urges her to rearrange her plans and spend time with him, as he knows that it could be something special. The chorus reflects this desire, as he sings “we don’t have the time, just put your lips on mine, fine lady”. 

The lyrics of this song explore themes of romance and desire, as the narrator is willing to take a chance on a potential relationship. The song paints a vivid picture of two people with chemistry, but who are hesitant to take the plunge and give themselves to each other. The song is a reflection on the risks and rewards of taking a chance and trying something new, which is something that many people can relate to.

Linguistically, the song is full of allusions to physical intimacy and connection. The narrator speaks of “friction” and “crazy little things” that they could be doing in the dark, which suggests a level of intimacy beyond what is seen in many other songs. This suggests the narrator is not only interested in the physical aspects of the relationship, but also in the emotional connection that can come with it.

Overall, “In the Dark” is a romantic and passionate song that speaks to the desire to take a chance on something new and the potential rewards that come with it. It is a great example of how music can be used to express emotions and thoughts in a powerful and meaningful way.
