"1984" by Charlie Puth is a deeply personal and reflective song about the difficult choices that accompany a tumultuous relationship. The song begins with a plea from the narrator to his lover to leave him and make better choices for herself. The chorus follows, with the narrator offering a dream-like setting of "running with the night like it's 1984" as a way of escaping the present reality. 

The verse lyrics provide a deeper exploration of the narrator’s emotions, as he begs his lover to stay and his voice of reason telling him that it is not enough. He calls out the lies and broken promises, and reveals the pain of knowing that his lover is on the edge and he is the only one who can save her. The chorus returns and the narrator offers the dream of escape once again. 

The bridge of the song provides a moment of hope, as the narrator recognizes his lover as a "queen of a castle" who was destined to catch her fall. This moment of understanding and recognition of her strength and potential allows for the possibility of a better future. 

The song provides a poignant and honest look at the complexities of relationships, and the difficult choices that accompany them. The narrator provides a hopeful and romantic vision of a better future, and the song provides a powerful reminder to look for the light in darkness. 

From a linguistic analysis, the song is a powerful expression of the narrator's pain and confusion. The lyrics are full of emotion, with a range of words that reflect the complexity of the situation. The use of alliteration and repetition further emphasizes the narrator's feelings, and the imagery of running with the night creates an evocative and dream-like atmosphere. 

Overall, "1984" by Charlie Puth is a powerful and evocative song. The lyrics are honest and emotional, and the narrator's pleas for a better future are both heartbreaking and inspiring. The song is a reminder to look for the light in darkness and to never give up hope.
