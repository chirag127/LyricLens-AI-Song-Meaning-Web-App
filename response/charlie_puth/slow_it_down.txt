In the bridge, the man pleads with the girl to slow down and realize the harm she is doing to their relationship. In the outro, the man repeats the chorus, imploring the girl to slow down and not try to force him into a commitment he's not ready for.

Overall, Slow It Down is a song about the struggles of resisting temptation and the challenges of navigating a complicated relationship. The song showcases Charlie Puth's emotive vocals and clever lyrics, making it a standout track on his album.
