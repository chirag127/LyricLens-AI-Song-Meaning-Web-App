Charlie Puth's song "Instagram Models" is a melodic exploration of the pitfalls of chasing after superficial, fleeting relationships. In the opening lines of the song, Puth employs a metaphor of "facing the bottle" to describe his struggles with his emotions as a result of being drawn in by the appeal of Instagram models. This sets the stage for the rest of the song, in which Puth laments the consequences of being drawn in by these models and the difficulty of managing his emotions.

The chorus of the song highlights the core of Puth's message: Instagram models are nothing but trouble. He croons that they are "leading [him] on the round and then closing down," as well as "so good at playing cards but the minute she brings you joy she's showing you the door." This paints a vivid picture of a relationship based purely on convenience and superficiality, with no real emotional connection.

The second verse of the song furthers this theme, with Puth singing that the Instagram models are "situated on her roof now, so tired of waiting her turn." This line perfectly encapsulates the idea that the relationships he is seeking out are ultimately unfulfilling and unsatisfying, as he is always waiting for something more.

The bridge of the song is a somber yet catchy refrain, in which Puth repeats the line "Oh, oh, oh, oh," as if to express his exasperation at his own situation.

To summarize, "Instagram Models" is a song about the consequences of seeking out shallow, superficial relationships. Through his clever use of metaphors and vivid imagery, Puth is able to capture the emotions of the situation and the difficulty of managing one's own emotions in the face of this kind of situation.

Linguistically, the song is mostly composed of simple, repetitive lyrics, with a few more complex lines mixed in. The language is mostly melancholic and reflective, with the recurring line of "oh oh oh oh" providing a sense of resignation and helplessness in the face of his own emotions.

Overall, Charlie Puth's "Instagram Models" is an emotionally charged exploration of the pitfalls of superficial relationships. The lyrics are clever and the melody is catchy, making it an enjoyable song to listen to.
