The song “One Call Away (Remix)” by Charlie Puth featuring Tyga is a powerful, uplifting ballad about the strength of friendship and the courage to reach out for help when needed. In the first verse, Charlie Puth sings about how he is only a phone call away, and he will always be there to save the day. He conveys the idea that, no matter how tough the situation is, he can be counted on to help. The chorus emphasizes the message of the song, as he repeats the phrase “I’m only one call away” to emphasize the availability of help. 

The second verse features Tyga, who raps about a past relationship that he had with a girl. He reflects on the time when he was poor and they would share a meal with two straws and a chocolate shake. He expresses his gratitude for the girl’s support and the strength of their friendship. The chorus returns and emphasizes the message of the song once more.

The lyrics of “One Call Away (Remix)” contain a powerful message of hope and support. The message of the song is that no matter how difficult or challenging a situation may be, help is always available if we are willing to reach out for it. It encourages people to not be afraid to ask for help, and to rely on the strength of friendship and support from those around us. 

Linguistically, the song is full of positive language, with words such as “save”, “love”, and “smile”. This positive language conveys the idea of hope, and the idea that help is always available. Additionally, the lyrics contain repetition to emphasize the message of the song, which further reinforces the idea that help is always available.

In summary, “One Call Away (Remix)” is an uplifting, hopeful song about the strength of friendship and the courage to reach out for help when needed. The lyrics contain positive language and repetition to emphasize the message, which is that help is always available if we are willing to ask for it.
