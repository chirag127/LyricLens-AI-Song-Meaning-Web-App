"How Long" is a song by American singer-songwriter Charlie Puth. The song was released as the second single from Puth's second studio album, Voicenotes, on October 5, 2017.

In the song, Puth sings about a past relationship and how his ex-partner has moved on, but he is still wondering about the status of their relationship. He asks her how long she has been seeing someone else and admits that he was wrong to let her go.

The chorus repeats the line, "How long has this been going on?" as Puth tries to come to terms with the fact that his ex has moved on. The pre-chorus contains the line, "She said, 'Boy, tell me honestly, was it real or just for show?'" which suggests that the ex-partner is questioning the validity of the relationship.

Overall, "How Long" is a heart-wrenching song about dealing with the aftermath of a break-up and trying to move on. The song's catchy melody and Puth's soulful vocals make it an enjoyable listen.
