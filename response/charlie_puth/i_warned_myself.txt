I Warned Myself is a song by Charlie Puth in which he reflects on his own behavior and warns himself not to get involved with someone who is not good for him. In the first verse, he sings about how the person in question lied to him and told him that their partner was out of town when they were actually still together. The chorus repeats the line "I warned myself that I shouldn't play with fire / But I can tell that I'll do it one more time," indicating that even though he knows it's not a good idea, he still can't resist getting involved with this person.

The second verse continues this theme, with the person threatening to harm him if he tells anyone about their relationship. Despite this, the narrator still can't stay away and continues to play with fire.

The chorus repeats the warning to himself and the acknowledgement that he will likely ignore it, indicating the narrator's struggle to break free from this destructive relationship.

Overall, the song is a cautionary tale about the dangers of getting involved with someone who is not good for you, and the ways in which we can sometimes ignore our own warnings in pursuit of love or attraction. It's a relatable and catchy tune that many listeners can likely relate to.
