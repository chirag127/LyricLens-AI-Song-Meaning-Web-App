Charlie Puth’s song “Full of It” is a melancholic, acoustic-driven track about the difficulty of accepting a break-up and the pain that comes with it. The song captures the emotional roller coaster of a relationship, with the lyrics illustrating the internal struggle of wanting to move on but being unable to do so.

The song starts with the narrator asking if the situation with the other person feels different now, if they ever send roses just because, and if they would hold them through the night. These questions reflect the narrator’s longing for the past and their desire to be held and cared for.

The chorus follows with the narrator questioning whether the other person is really going to love them or let them go and if they really mean it when they say they’re beautiful. These questions reflect the narrator’s doubt and insecurity in the relationship, unable to trust the other person’s feelings.

The second verse follows with the narrator reflecting on their past relationship and asking how many times the other person said goodbye and if they still think of them every day. These questions reflect the narrator’s pain and sadness at the end of the relationship and their need to move on.

The chorus repeats and the song ends with the narrator asking if the other person is really going to love them or let them go and if they really mean it when they say they’re beautiful.

In summary, Charlie Puth’s “Full of It” is a melancholic reflection on the pain of a break-up and the difficulty of accepting it. The lyrics reflect the narrator’s longing for the past and their uncertainty and doubt in the future. The song is a powerful illustration of the emotional roller coaster of a relationship and the difficulty of letting go.
