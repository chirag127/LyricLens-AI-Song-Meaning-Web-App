The song “Kiss Me” by Charlie Puth is a heart-wrenching song that encapsulates the emotions of wanting someone to know how you feel but being too scared to tell them. The song starts off with the narrator regretting not telling their crush how they feel about them before it was too late. They express their feelings of wishing they could have worked things out, but now it’s too late. The chorus then begins to pick up with the narrator’s confession that they are done playing games and they can’t believe what they are about to say. They express their fear of saying goodbye with their love left behind, so they plead for their crush to kiss them before they “lose their mind”. 

In the second verse, the narrator expresses their loneliness and desperation by talking about late night drinking and pacing around the bar, hoping their crush will walk in. In the bridge, they express the same feelings they had in the first verse, wishing they had told their feelings before it was too late. The chorus then picks up again with the same sentiments, only this time the narrator expresses their fear of dying rather than just tell their crush goodbye with their love left behind. 

The song is a powerful expression of the fears and emotions associated with unrequited love. The lyrics create a feeling of desperation, as the narrator pleads desperately for the other person to kiss them before they lose their sanity. The language used is emotive, as the narrator expresses their fear of dying rather than letting go of their love. Furthermore, the use of repetition in the chorus creates a sense of urgency, as the narrator is pleading for the other person to kiss them before it’s too late. 

The song is a powerful showcase of Charlie Puth’s lyrical prowess and vocal ability. The lyrics are emotionally charged, and the melody is powerful enough to pull on the heartstrings.
