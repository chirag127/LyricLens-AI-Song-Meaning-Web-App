The song Through It All, by Charlie Puth, is a reflective and empowering anthem that speaks of resilience and strength in the face of life's difficulties. The song follows a young man's journey as he reflects on the trials and tribulations he has faced in life and how he has "done it all [his] way." Through the lyrics, Puth conveys the message that he has been through it all and, while it has been difficult, he is still standing and living his life the way he chooses.

The song begins with Puth reflecting on the wild life he has led thus far, "the kind that made me question my faith." The lyrics then move on to him asking himself where the time has gone, and recognizing it is "just the price you pay." Here, Puth is referring to the cost of living life on one's own terms - no matter the hardships and losses, he is determined to live life on his own terms.

The chorus is a powerful declaration of his strength and resilience, as he proclaims "you won't see me crying if tomorrow never comes, God only knows I've been through it all." This chorus is a powerful reminder that, no matter the difficulties, Puth is still standing strong.

The bridge of the song further reinforces this message of strength and courage, as Puth reflects on growing up in a "wild city" and how he had to fight or run. Here, Puth is reflecting on the struggles he has had to face in life, and how they have shaped him into the person he is today.

When taken as a whole, the song is an anthem of resilience and strength. Puth has experienced a lot of pain and sadness, but he is still standing and determined to live life on his own terms. Linguistically, the song is filled with powerful and emphatic words, such as "recklessness" and "crying," that help to convey the emotions and feelings that Puth wants to express. The song is an ode to those who have been through it all and are still standing, and serves as an inspiration to those who are currently facing their own struggles.
