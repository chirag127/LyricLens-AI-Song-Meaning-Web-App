"Next To You" by Charlie Puth is a romantic pop ballad about the longing and desire to be close to the person that the speaker loves. The song begins with a gentle guitar-driven melody as Puth expresses his desire to be near this person, to be "right next to you". With a longing for connection, the speaker wishes to be able to talk and hold the person's hand, to be together and not be apart. 

The chorus of the song brings in a slightly more upbeat tone as the speaker promises that one day they will be together and that the person they love will see that they belong to them. The speaker also reflects on how they don't know what to do when they are not close to this person and that the only help they can find is when they are right next to them. 

Lyrically, the song is an exploration of the emotions that come with wanting to be close to someone. The language of the song conveys a sense of longing and desire to be close to the person and to make a connection with them. The metaphors and imagery of being able to touch and talk to the person emphasize the speaker's desire to be in close proximity to them and to be able to express their love for them.

In summary, "Next To You" by Charlie Puth is a romantic ballad about longing for connection and wanting to be close to the person that the speaker loves. The imagery and metaphors in the lyrics create a vivid picture of the speaker's desire to be with this person, and the gentle guitar-driven melody helps to express the emotion of the song. This is a beautiful and heartfelt song that is sure to resonate with listeners and make them feel connected to the emotions expressed in the lyrics.
