The song "The News" by Paramore appears to be about the effects of constant exposure to the news and the overwhelming feeling of helplessness that can come from it. The first verse describes a war on the other side of the planet, while the second verse suggests that the speaker has a war raging inside of them. The refrain of the song, "turn on the news/turn off the news," seems to be a commentary on the constant barrage of negative news and the need to disconnect from it in order to find some peace.

The second half of the song delves into the issues with the news itself, calling it exploitative, performative, and rhetorical, among other things. The song suggests that the news is not always informative or objective, and that it can be detrimental to our mental health. The final refrain of the song emphasizes the constant, never-ending nature of the negative news cycle.

Overall, the song appears to be a critique of the effects of the news on individuals and society, and the need to disconnect from it in order to maintain mental health and well-being.
