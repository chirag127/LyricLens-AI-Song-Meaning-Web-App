The song "Girls Love Beyoncé" by Drake is a song about a man's fear of commitment and his attempts to find the courage to pursue a relationship. The song begins with Drake reflecting on the difficulty of meeting women and his fear of letting someone in, and then transitions into the chorus which features James Fauntleroy providing the hook: "Say my name, say my name/When no one is around you/Say baby I love you/If you ain't running games." This chorus serves as the main theme of the song, illustrating how Drake is searching for a meaningful relationship that isn't based on manipulation or deception.

He also mentions how he is not alone, as there are other admirers in his life, though he is unwilling to let them in. The second verse continues with Drake expressing his fear of commitment, as well as his desire to find someone to be with. He expresses his desire to put an end to the games and deceptions in relationships, and to instead just be honest and open with someone.

The third verse further elaborates on Drake's fear of commitment, as well as his desire to find someone to be with. He mentions how time has been moving quickly and how he wants to find a reason to stay away from the partying life. Drake then expresses his desire to find someone to think of besides himself, and someone to go through the front door with. The song ends with the chorus repeating, emphasizing the main theme of the song once again.

In summary, "Girls Love Beyoncé" is a song about Drake's attempts to find the courage to pursue a meaningful relationship, and his fears of commitment and deception.
