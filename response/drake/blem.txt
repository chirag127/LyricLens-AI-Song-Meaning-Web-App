In Drake's song "Blem," he expresses the difficulty of maintaining a relationship with someone, while also wishing for a more peaceful and fulfilling life together. The song opens with the lines "Unruly, unruly maddas who keeps bringing more / I've had too many this Virginia done me off already," painting a picture of a chaotic and difficult situation. This is followed by the chorus, where Drake says "I'm Blem for real / I might just say how I feel," emphasizing the vulnerability of expressing his true feelings. 

The verses of the song expand on this theme, with Drake detailing his desire to move away from the chaos and find a different life with his partner: "Don't switch on me / I got big plans / We need to forward to the islands and get you gold no spray tans." He also expresses his frustration with his partner, as they repeatedly go back to their ex: "I need you to stop runnin' back to your ex / He's a wasteman / I wanna know how come we can never slash and stay friends." 

The song ends with a message of hope, as Drake calls out to Lil Wayne and Lionel Richie to join him in singing "Together Forever." This is a clear indication that Drake still has faith that his relationship can work out and find some peace, despite the difficulties they have gone through. 

In summary, "Blem" is a song about the difficulties of relationships, and Drake's desire to find a more peaceful and fulfilling life with his partner. The song is an honest and vulnerable expression of his feelings about the situation, and the desire for a better future. Through the use of linguistic analysis, this song shows Drake's emotions of vulnerability, frustration, and hope as he expresses his true feelings.
