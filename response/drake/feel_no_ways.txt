In the song "Feel No Ways" by Drake, the artist expresses his feelings of hurt and frustration after a failed relationship. In the first verse, Drake talks about how the other person had something that belonged to him and how their body language said it all despite the things they said to him. He talks about how he was only gone for a few months, but the other person didn't have the time to wait for him. In the chorus, Drake talks about how he tried with the other person and that there's more to life than sleeping in and getting high with them. He had to let go of the relationship to show himself what he could do, and this didn't sit right with the other person. The other person is now trying to make him feel a way and throwing it back in his face. The chorus ends with Drake saying "you don't feel no way you think I deserve it," which expresses his frustration and disappointment. 

The second verse talks about how Drake has stopped listening to the things the other person says because they don't mean it anyway. He expresses his regret that he didn't do things his way instead of the other way. The chorus repeats and reasserts his feelings of hurt and frustration. 

The song is a reflection of Drake's emotional state after a failed relationship. He expresses his feelings of hurt, frustration, and regret that things didn't work out. Linguistic analysis of the song lyrics reveals a sense of emotional anguish and sadness. The song speaks to the common feeling of being wronged and feeling like the other person doesn't deserve the pain they are causing. 

Overall, "Feel No Ways" is an emotional and powerful song that speaks to the pain of a failed relationship. Drake's lyrics reflect his emotional state and capture the feeling of being wronged by someone. The song has a strong emotional impact and speaks to the common experience of failed relationships.
