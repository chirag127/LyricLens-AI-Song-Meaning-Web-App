"Talk Is Cheap" by Drake is a song about the frustrations of a relationship gone stagnant. Drake talks about how he is tired of the lack of real conversation and effort from his significant other, and how she always has excuses for why she is not available. He expresses his feelings of not being wanted or appreciated and his longing for more meaningful interactions. The chorus is a call to action for his significant other, to prove that their relationship is worth saving by pushing his buttons and showing him that she does care. 

The song's lyrics are full of emotion, with Drake repeatedly asking why his significant other is not living up to his expectations. This is shown through his use of repetition in line such as "turn me on, turn me on, turn me on" and "why that's some girl shit". Drake expresses his feelings of hurt, frustration and rejection throughout the song, making it a powerful and relatable piece of art. 

The song is also full of metaphors and similes, with Drake comparing his significant other to a drug and saying that she needs to "smoke a little then record". This is a clever way of expressing his desire for her to open up and share her feelings with him, and for her to show her true self. 

A linguistic analysis of the song reveals that Drake expresses feelings of hurt and rejection in lines such as "you always end up with some other plans" and "you don't know what to do". He also expresses a longing for connection in lines such as "you gotta push my buttons" and "you don't know me". This is a powerful reminder of the importance of communication and understanding in relationships, and of the need to be open and honest with each other. 

In summary, "Talk Is Cheap" by Drake is a song about the frustrations of an unfulfilling relationship. Through clever metaphors and similes, Drake expresses his feelings of hurt, rejection and longing for connection. A linguistic analysis of the song reveals the importance of communication and understanding in relationships, and of the need to be open and honest with each other.
