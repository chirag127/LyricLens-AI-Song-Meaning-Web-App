"Signs" by Drake is a song about a relationship that is just beginning and the uncertainty that comes with it. The lyrics explore the idea of commitment, and the difficulty of trusting someone with your heart. Drake reflects on the idea that, although it is a risk to let someone in so deeply, it is worth it in the end.

The song starts off with Drake singing that he is drinking champagne with breakfast while yawning, signifying that he is embarking on something new and exciting. He then talks about not being able to say no to someone when they say please, showing his vulnerability in the relationship. 

The chorus of the song, featuring Young Thug, emphasizes the idea of commitment and trusting someone with your heart. He talks about never disrespecting the person he is with and asking for her pardon.

The second verse has Drake talking about the "signs of the times," which could mean the signs of a relationship that is about to start. He talks about taking his time and not playing himself, showing his dedication to the relationship. 

The third verse has Drake again talking about not being able to say no when someone says please, emphasizing his vulnerability. He then talks about wanting to drink and dance like a Bajan and a Trini, which could be a reference to the Caribbean islands.

The song ends with Drake singing the chorus again, emphasizing the idea of commitment and trust.

In summary, "Signs" by Drake is a song about the uncertainty of a new relationship, and the risk and trust that comes with it. The lyrics reflect on the idea of taking a chance on someone and the vulnerability that comes with it. The song also explores the idea of signs of the times, and how sometimes it is worth taking a risk.

When it comes to the linguistic analysis of the song, it is clear that Drake is expressing a wide range of emotions. He expresses his vulnerability through the lyrics and talks about being uncertain, yet hopeful at the same time. There is also a sense of optimism in the song, as Drake talks about taking a chance and the possibility of a successful relationship. This is further emphasized by the chorus, which expresses the idea of commitment and trust in the relationship. 

Overall, "Signs" by Drake is a beautiful and heartfelt song about taking a chance and trusting someone with your heart. The lyrics express a wide range of emotions and explore the concept of commitment and trust in a relationship. It is a great song to listen to when trying to make a big decision, as it reminds us that taking a chance can be worth it in the end.
