 The song "After Dark" by Drake featuring Static Major and Ty Dolla $ign is a melodic, dreamy track that speaks of late-night trysts and the intimate moments shared between two people. The song opens with a slight build-up of synths and Drake's signature crooning. The lyrics are filled with seductive imagery and romantic sentiment, as Drake raps about sneaking in late night, not caring if anyone knows, and enjoying each other’s company until morning light. 

The verse by Static Major is a perfect accompaniment to Drake's, as it paints a picture of a passionate late-night affair. He raps about being patient and wanting to make the other person a priority. His words, paired with the melody, create a mood of intimacy and tenderness.

Ty Dolla $ign's verse is more playful and lighthearted, as he speaks about taking a dip in the pool and enjoying a night full of fun and relaxation with the object of his affection. His verse is full of affection and compliments, as he promises to treat her right and make her a priority.

The song is an ode to the beauty of late-night rendezvous and the intimate moments shared between two people. The lyrics are filled with romantic imagery and tender sentiment, making it a perfect song to listen to when one is in the mood for some romance. 

The lyrics of the song provide a great insight into the emotions of the artist and their feelings towards love and romance. The imagery and language used in the song convey a sense of intimate passion and tenderness, as well as a sense of playfulness and fun. The use of repetition and the layering of the verses creates a dreamy atmosphere that speaks of late-night trysts and passionate moments. 

Overall, the song "After Dark" is a beautiful romantic track that speaks of intimate moments and late-night rendezvous. The thoughtful lyrics, lush melody, and playful verses come together to create a dreamy atmosphere that is sure to set the mood for a romantic evening.
