The song "Real Bad Karma" by Drake featuring Bryson Tiller is a hip-hop/R&B song that speaks to the power of karma and its ability to come back and bite you in the end. The song is composed of two verses, each with Drake and Bryson Tiller taking turns rapping. Both verses are filled with tales of their respective lifestyles and the consequences of their actions, emphasizing the idea of karma.

In the first verse, Drake speaks of his luxurious lifestyle, from having a condo in Miami to having the funds to buy front row tickets to games. He also talks of the people he surrounds himself with and how he has to stay alert for any potential issues. He then speaks of the power of karma, warning that it can come back and bite you if you don't watch your words and actions.

In the second verse, Bryson Tiller takes on a more cautionary tone. He speaks of how his life changed when he became a father and how it made him wiser. He then speaks of how he has to separate the real people from the imposters and how karma will come back to bite you if you don't watch out.

Overall, the song speaks to the power of karma and its ability to come back and bite you if you don't watch your words and actions. The lyrics are filled with vivid imagery that speaks to the luxurious lifestyles of Drake and Bryson Tiller, while also warning of the consequences of their actions. Linguistically, the song uses a variety of metaphors and similes to paint a picture of their lives and the consequences of their actions. The chorus emphasizes the idea of karma, with the line "my life is full of drama, I just want the top, don't wanna charm her, talk about the boy and you get karma, bad karma, that's a sad story, yeah, real bad karma". This line serves as a warning to the listener to think twice before speaking or acting, as karma will surely find its way back to you.

In summary, "Real Bad Karma" is a cautionary tale about the consequences of one's words and actions. Through vivid imagery and cleverly worded lyrics, Drake and Bryson Tiller tell the story of their lives and the power of karma. The song serves as a warning to the listener to think twice before speaking or acting, as the consequences of their actions will surely come back to haunt them.
