Drake's song ‘Look What You’ve Done’ is a heartfelt ode to his grandmother, and a celebration of her influence on his life and career. The second verse then shifts to Drake's teenage years, when he was rebelling and getting into trouble. His grandmother was still there for him, however, providing unconditional love and support, and even encouraging him to pursue his dreams. 

The chorus of the song is the most touching moment, as Drake expresses his gratitude for all that his grandmother has done for him. He reflects on the good times they shared, and acknowledges her influence on his life and career. He expresses his hope that he will one day be able to repay her kindness, and show her how much she means to him. 

The lyrics of the song evoke powerful emotions, and Drake's delivery is passionate and heartfelt. He captures the intense bond between him and his grandmother, and the love and appreciation he has for her. The song is a powerful reminder of the importance of family, and the impact that loving and supportive relationships can have on our lives.
