The song "Like I'm Supposed To/Do Things" by Drake is a heartfelt track about the struggles of being in a relationship. In the song, Drake expresses his fear of falling in love and his need to stay in control of his emotions. He is torn between wanting to be with someone and wanting to be alone, and he knows that the other person is scared of becoming too involved. 

The chorus of the song is the most telling part of the track, with Drake singing "you make me want to do things/ love you like I'm supposed to." These lyrics express his desire to express love and affection, but also his fear of being rejected or hurt. He is trying to take things slow and make sure that he is following the "rules" of love, but he is struggling to keep himself in check.

The rest of the song is filled with references to his struggle to stay in control. He acknowledges that he knows the other person "by now," but he still doesn't know how to act around them. He also knows that they are scared of falling in love, and he is trying to show them that he won't ever change. 

Overall, "Like I'm Supposed To/Do Things" is a powerful song about the fear of falling in love and the struggle to stay in control of one's emotions. Drake expresses his feelings in an eloquent way, and his lyrics are both honest and vulnerable. This song is a great reminder of the importance of taking things slow and being mindful of one's emotions.
