More Life, the title track of Drake's 2017 album of the same name, is a statement of resilience and a call to embrace life in the face of challenges. The song is centered around the idea of living life to its fullest, no matter what challenges one faces. The chorus of the song, “More life, more time with family and friends, more life, I’ve still got vibrations to send,” is a call to appreciate the small moments in life and make the most of them. 

The lyrics of the song express the idea that despite the challenges life throws our way, we have to stay resilient and keep pushing forward. The song also acknowledges that life can be fleeting, “At night I pray it’s true, I’ve done so much in my short time and still there’s more to do.” Drake’s words acknowledge the uncertainty of life, but are also a call to live life to the fullest, “But if someone should come collecting, sooner than we’re all expecting, at least the life you lived was one for you.” 

The lyrics of the song express a wide range of emotions, from joy to resilience to sorrow. The song celebrates life and encourages listeners to make the most of the opportunities they get. Through the use of vivid imagery and a range of emotions, Drake conveys the idea that life is to be embraced and enjoyed, no matter what challenges one faces. 

Overall, More Life is an uplifting and inspirational song that encourages listeners to live life to its fullest. The song’s lyrics contain an emotional depth that is rarely seen in popular music, and its message of hope and resilience is one that is sure to stay with the listener long after the song has ended.
