In Drake's song "From Florida With Love," he reflects on coming up in the rap game and the lessons he learned along the way. He talks about linking up with Mexican producers and getting a chain for himself, and later realizing the importance of having a "plug" for protection. He also references his close relationship with Lil Wayne and Kobe Bryant, and his loyalty to the 6ix (his hometown of Toronto).

In the chorus, Drake declares his return to Florida with love, expressing his appreciation for the help he's received from the city. He also promotes his crew YMB (Young Money Boys), and their willingness to "stain anything" - a reference to the group's willingness to protect their turf.

The song is a reflection on the ups and downs of the rap game, and the importance of loyalty and hard work. Drake is unapologetic about his success, and proud of the lessons he has learned along the way. 

From a linguistic analysis, the song has a strong emotional tone, expressing feelings of joy and appreciation for the people that have helped Drake get to where he is today. He is proud of his achievements and expresses his loyalty to the people he has encountered while coming up in the rap game. 

Overall, "From Florida With Love" is a heartfelt ode to Drake's journey in the rap game. It celebrates his success and acknowledges the help he's received along the way, while emphasizing the importance of loyalty and hard work.
