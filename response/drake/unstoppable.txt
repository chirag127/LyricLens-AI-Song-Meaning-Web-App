The song “Unstoppable” by Drake featuring Santigold and Lil Wayne is an upbeat, energetic anthem about achieving success in spite of all of the obstacles that come with it. Drake starts the song off by introducing himself as Drizzy and giving a brief overview of his life; he’s not perfect, but he works hard and deserves what he has. He then talks about his love for women caught up in the glamour and superficiality of the world, and how he always manages to find himself in these situations. 

Lil Wayne then takes the reins, introducing himself as Weezy and explaining his perfection and his dedication to hard work. He talks about how he has a condo in Miami, Louisiana, and Atlanta, and how he can get any girl he wants, no matter what. 

Santigold then comes in with the chorus, repeating the line “I got to be unstoppable”. This is a call to go above and beyond what is expected and to push past any and all obstacles, no matter what they may be. 

The song has a strong message of resilience, determination, and ambition. It encourages listeners to strive for success and not to let anything stop them from achieving their goals. It also provides a commentary on the superficial world of fame and glamour and how it can be tempting and distracting, but it is ultimately not worth it. 

From a linguistic analysis standpoint, the song contains a combination of assertive and aggressive lyrics. Drake’s verse is very assertive, as he is telling his story and showing off his accomplishments. Lil Wayne’s verse is more aggressive, as he is talking about how he can get any girl he wants and how he is unfazed by any obstacles. The song also contains a lot of imagery, with Drake talking about princesses, sand, and public transit, and Lil Wayne talking about doormats and condos.

In summary, “Unstoppable” by Drake is an anthem about pushing through all of the obstacles and achieving success. It contains assertive and aggressive lyrics, as well as a lot of imagery. The song has a strong message of resilience and ambition, and encourages listeners to strive for their goals no matter what.
