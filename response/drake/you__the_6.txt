Drake's song "You & The 6" is an emotionally charged reflection on his upbringing and his relationship with his parents. The song begins with Drake reflecting on his past and how it has shaped his present, discussing how his mother has been worried about him and his father's influence on him. He speaks of his mother's advice to never take advice and the importance of having faith in himself. He then speaks of his father's past mistakes and how he still hopes for his forgiveness. 

The chorus of the song speaks of the role his mother and father have both played in his life, and how they have raised him right. He speaks of the time he spent with his father learning about music, and how his father's influence has helped him along his journey. He goes on to speak of the difficulties he faced growing up due to his race, but how he has been able to overcome those difficulties by standing his ground.

The lyrics of the song are packed full of emotion, displaying a range of feelings such as guilt, regret, love, and appreciation. The language used is reflective of Drake's upbringing, and his personal struggle with his identity. This is further emphasized by his use of alliteration and repetition, displaying his determination to overcome the obstacles he has faced in his life. 

The song is a powerful reflection on the role of family and the importance of having faith in oneself. It is an uplifting tribute to the people who have helped shape Drake's life, and a reminder to always stay true to yourself and your roots. 

Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals the use of poetic techniques such as alliteration and repetition, which helps to emphasize the emotions that Drake is conveying in the song. Themes of family, faith, and identity are all present, and Drake conveys his appreciation through the use of vivid imagery and personal anecdotes. 

Overall, "You & The 6" is a powerful and emotionally charged song that speaks of resilience and determination. Drake's use of poetic techniques helps to emphasize the emotions he is conveying, and the song is a powerful tribute to the people who have helped shape his life.
