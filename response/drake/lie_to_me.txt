Lie To Me by Drake is an exploration of the difficult realities of relationships, and the lies and inconsistencies that can arise within them. Through the use of vivid imagery and storytelling, Drake paints a picture of a relationship that is constantly in flux, and one where the lies and half-truths have become commonplace. 

The song begins with Drake rapping about the hustle, and his desire to stay away from people who only want money. The chorus of the song is a plea to his partner, begging them to stop lying, as they are taking him for granted while he seeks true love and companionship. 

The second verse finds Drake reflecting on his past. He recalls a situation in which he was taken aback by someone’s success, and how it inspired him to work harder. He also talks about how one must be either "in the game" or "out of the game," and how he is now back in the game. 

The bridge of the song finds Drake reflecting on his relationship and his plea to his partner, and how it could be the beginning of something special. He talks about how he wants to take the relationship to the next level, but his partner continues to lie. 

From a linguistic analysis perspective, the song’s lyrics are filled with powerful imagery, and the lyrics are filled with emotion. There is a sense of desperation in the lyrics, as Drake pleads for his partner to be honest and true to him. The song also speaks of the difficulty of navigating a relationship, and how lies and inconsistencies can arise.
