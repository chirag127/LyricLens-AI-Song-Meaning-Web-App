The song "One Dance" by Drake is a mellow, mid-tempo track with a smooth R&B sound. The lyrics tell the story of a relationship, with Drake singing about his desire to have one more night of romance with his lover before they part ways. The chorus, "I need a one dance, got a Hennessy in my hand, one more time 'fore I go, higher powers taking a hold on me," is repeated throughout the song and emphasizes the idea of having a meaningful, intimate moment with his lover before they move on.

The verses of the song provide more insight into the story. In the first verse, Drake sings about his desire for his lover to stay by his side, even though the streets are unsafe. The second verse speaks of his need for strength and guidance, while also expressing his fear of not making it back in one piece. The bridge and breakdown introduce a new element to the song, with Drake asking his lover to tell him where he wants to go and offering to take it slow.

Overall, "One Dance" is a mellow, romantic song that tells the story of two lovers trying to make the most of their time before they part ways. The lyrics are filled with emotion and longing, and the chorus is an anthem for the idea of having one last meaningful moment with someone you care about.
