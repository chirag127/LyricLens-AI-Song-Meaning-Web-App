Fancy by Drake is an uplifting song that celebrates the strength and beauty of independent women. It is an ode to the modern woman, who is confident and self-sufficient, and does not need the approval of a man or anyone else to feel fulfilled. The song begins with a beat produced by Swizz Beatz, followed by Drake's verses, which feature his signature flow and playful lyrics. 

In the first verse, Drake paints a picture of a woman getting ready to go out. She spends time pampering herself in the bathroom with flat irons and nail files, hits the gym to stay fit, and shops for fashionable clothes. He admires her independence and her confidence to do things for herself, rather than trying to please a man. 

In the second verse, Drake raps about a woman who is independent, intelligent, and attractive. He appreciates her for being street smart and book smart, and for having the confidence to overlook his past mistakes. He also praises her for her fashion sense, mentioning her designer clothes and handbags. 

In the third verse, TI compares the independent woman that Drake raps about to other women who are more superficial and only looking for material things. He implies that this woman is different, and she doesn't need a wealthy man to buy her things. 

The chorus repeats the phrase "Oh you fancy huh?", celebrating the woman's independence and beauty. It is followed by a short interlude of Drake giving a shout out to women from different cities, and telling them to show off their strength and independence. 

Overall, Fancy is a song that celebrates the beauty and strength of independent women. It is a tribute to modern women who are confident and self-sufficient, and don't need the approval of a man to feel fulfilled. Linguistically, the song contains a mix of playful and serious lyrics that convey the message of empowerment and independence. It is a reminder that beauty is more than just material things and that real beauty comes from within.
