"Emotionless" by Drake is a powerful song that speaks to the harsh reality of life and the difficulties of navigating the ever-changing landscape of relationships. The song opens with a verse sung by Mariah Carey, highlighting the emotions that are stirred up by Drake's presence. The first verse of Drake's rap paints a picture of an individual feeling out of place and alone in the world, struggling to keep track of who is on their side. Drake reflects on the people he looks up to, noting how their actions seem out of character and how the land of the free often seems more exciting than it really is. He goes on to talk about the struggle of finding someone genuine, and the difficulty of trusting anyone he meets. 

The chorus is sung by Mariah Carey again, emphasizing the emotions Drake is feeling. In the second verse Drake talks about the superficiality of social media and the pressure to post perfect pictures, as well as the difficulty of maintaining relationships when it feels like everyone is just trying to impress each other. The song ends with Drake reflecting on the fact that he will eventually find out the truth, and that he is exhausted from the people taking advantage of him. 

The song is an exploration of the complexity of life and relationships, and an insight into the struggles of navigating the modern world. Through the narrative of the song, Drake speaks to the difficulty of trusting people, the superficiality of social media, and the feeling of being alone in the world. Linguistically, the song is full of emotionally charged words and phrases, with Drake often speaking in the first-person to emphasize his experiences. The song also contains various metaphors and similes, such as the line “breaking speed records on roads that these niggas paved”, which speaks to the idea of walking in someone else’s shoes and learning from them. 

Overall, "Emotionless" by Drake is a powerful song that speaks to the harsh realities of life and the difficulty of navigating the ever-changing landscape of relationships. The song is an exploration of the complexities of life and relationships and the reality of feeling alone in a world full of superficiality. Drake’s emotionally charged lyrics, coupled with the metaphors and similes, make for a powerful song that speaks to the reality of life.
