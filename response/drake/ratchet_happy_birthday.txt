The song “Ratchet Happy Birthday” by Drake and PartyNextDoor is a celebration of life and the joys of a birthday. The song is an upbeat number and has a positive message about enjoying life and making the most of every day. The lyrics focus on celebrating life, appreciating the good times, and ignoring the bad. The song is primarily driven by Drake’s rap and PartyNextDoor’s chorus. 

The verses of the song focus on the importance of living life to the fullest, ignoring petty issues, and appreciating the good times. Drake begins the song by wishing PartyNextDoor a happy birthday and then proceeds to rap about enjoying life, ignoring the bad, and staying positive. He talks about the need to fill the emptiness inside with joy and positivity, and not being swayed by the negativity of others. 

PartyNextDoor’s chorus further emphasizes the message of the song, urging the listener to “party like it's your birthday” and to make the most of the day. He also mentions the importance of believing in yourself and staying strong in the face of adversity.

The song is a perfect celebration of life and of birthdays. The upbeat tempo and positive message of the lyrics make it a great choice for celebrating any occasion. The lyrics provide a great reminder to always stay positive and make the most of every day.

Linguistically, the song is full of positive emotion. The lyrics are uplifting and encouraging, and the use of repetition helps emphasize the message of the song. The use of rhymes and alliteration also helps to drive home the message of the song. 

Overall, “Ratchet Happy Birthday” is an excellent song. The upbeat tempo and positive message make it an ideal choice for any celebration. The lyrics provide a great reminder to stay positive and make the most of life. With its great production and positive message, this song is sure to make any birthday celebration memorable.
