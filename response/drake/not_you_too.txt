"Not You Too" by Drake and Chris Brown is a heartfelt R&B track that speaks to the struggles of a tumultuous relationship. The song paints a vivid picture of the emotional turmoil of a couple coming to terms with their differences. 

The beginning of the song finds Drake rapping about the pain of feeling alone and longing for a partner who can offer understanding and support. In the chorus, Drake begs his partner to "save it" and not waste their time, as he's been saying things he doesn't mean - a common occurrence when two people are not understanding each other. 

The second verse, sung by Chris Brown, furthers the narrative of the relationship. He speaks of his partner's loyalty and how their love for each other helped them through difficult times, but also touches on the underlying issues that have caused them to drift apart. 

The bridge of the song finds Drake reflecting on the relationship and how his partner kept certain things from him. He expresses shock and disbelief at the revelations and wonders why his partner would keep certain things from him. 

In terms of its lyrical analysis, the song speaks to the raw emotions that come with a relationship in turmoil. Drake and Chris Brown's verses portray a feeling of helplessness, while also expressing a sense of hope that the two will eventually come to terms with their differences and reconcile. 

Overall, "Not You Too" is a powerful song that speaks to the struggles and emotions of a relationship in turmoil. Drake and Chris Brown's verses are emotionally honest and realistic, making the song both relatable and captivating. The combination of the two artists is a perfect match, as each brings their own unique style to the track. This track is sure to be a hit with fans of both artists.
