In his song “Energy”, Drake reflects on the struggles of life, particularly the enemies he has acquired and the people who try to drain him of his energy. Drake begins the song by rapping about the power of his music, and the need to “rewind” and “forward” in order to properly present it. He then speaks of the people in his life who are trying to “drain” him of his energy, whether they are enemies or people who are trying to take advantage of him. 

The second verse of the song sees Drake reflecting on his success and how it has caused people to try to take advantage of him. He raps about buying people purses, trucks, houses, and malls, as well as his attempts to keep track of it all. He speaks of his ex-girlfriend and how she is the female version of him, and of the strippers in his life who he sees as “virgins”. 

The third verse of “Energy” sees Drake become more direct in his lyrics, as he raps about “fuck all of you niggas” and how he is “tired of saving all these niggas.” He speaks of the people in his life who he considers “real ones”, those who are living past Kennedy Road and those who are with him wherever he goes. 

The chorus of “Energy” sees Drake reflecting on his enemies and the people who are trying to “drain” him of his energy. He speaks of the need to “pray for your nigga” and the power of his wave. 

In summary, Drake’s “Energy” is a reflection on the struggles of life, particularly the enemies he has acquired and the people who try to drain him of his energy. The song is a powerful reminder of the need to stay true to oneself and remain focused on one’s goals in the face of adversity.

The lyrics of “Energy” contain a number of linguistic features that help to convey Drake’s emotions and feelings. The use of repetition is prominent throughout the song, particularly in the chorus, as Drake repeats the phrase “I gots enemies, got a lot of enemies”. This repetition helps to emphasize Drake’s feelings of being drained of energy and the need to stay focused. The use of imperative verbs such as “forward” and “rewind” also helps to emphasize the importance of Drake’s message, as does the use of words such as “fuck”, “pray” and “real”. 

Overall, “Energy” is a powerful and inspiring song that speaks to the struggles and triumphs of life. Drake’s message is clear; stay focused on your goals and don’t let anyone take your energy away from you.
