"Can't Have Everything" by Drake is an upbeat and passionate song about embracing life and not letting challenges stop you from achieving your goals. The song starts with Drake reflecting on his journey and how he has evolved from performing at college shows to being an internationally acclaimed artist. He talks about how he wants a lot but can't have everything, before addressing those who doubt his success, telling them to watch him succeed and that he will keep the lights on in the building.

In the second verse, Drake takes a more serious tone, reflecting on people who are two-faced and don't see the difference between him and his peers. He then talks about how he has achieved a lot, mentioning his record deal and financial success. He ends the verse by affirming that he will never forget his roots.

The bridge of the song shifts to a more personal tone, with Drake addressing his listener and warning them that having a negative attitude will not lead to success. He encourages them to stay positive and have faith in their abilities, as it will help them reach their desired destination.

Overall, "Can't Have Everything" is an inspiring and motivational song about having faith in yourself and never giving up. Drake's lyrics are clever and relatable, and the upbeat instrumental gives the track a positive and uplifting energy.

Linguistic analysis of the song reveals that Drake expresses a range of emotions and social tendencies in his lyrics. He conveys a sense of ambition and determination, as well as a feeling of pride in his accomplishments. He also speaks about the importance of resilience and staying positive in the face of adversity, showing a sense of confidence in his own abilities. Additionally, Drake speaks about the need for trustworthiness and loyalty in relationships, which speaks to his desire for meaningful connections. Overall, Drake's lyrics show a strong sense of self-belief and a desire to make the most of life.
