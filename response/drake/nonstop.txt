In Drake's song "Nonstop," he reflects on his success and speaks about his hustle, money and fame. The song opens with him rapping about flipping a switch and bodies dropping, referencing his rise to fame and the power he now has. Drake then talks about his success in London and his sneaker deal, and then moves on to his money, hustle, and pride.

The third verse focuses on his money and power. He raps about getting a billion dollars and how he's resilient. He mentions his big checks and the big profits he gets, and how he hardly takes offense since revenge is hardly an expense.

The song then moves back to the chorus, repeating the same lyrics as before.

Overall, "Nonstop" is a song that focuses on Drake's success, influence, and money. He reflects on his ascension to fame, and how he's now living the life that he dreamed of. It's a song about hustle, money, and pride, and how these things have taken over Drake's life.

The song has a very upbeat, catchy beat, and Drake's flow is very smooth and powerful. His lyrics are full of emotion, and he paints a vivid picture of the power and money he now has.

Linguistically, the song is full of strong emotions. Drake speaks with confidence and pride, and his lyrics are full of power and strength. He speaks about his power and influence, and the money and status he now has. He also speaks about his resilience and hustle, and how he has taken the business and ran it.
