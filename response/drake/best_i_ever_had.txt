"Best I Ever Had" by Drake is a love song about his appreciation for his significant other. The song starts by Drake addressing the fact that some people may think the song is about them, but that it’s really for his special someone. He goes on to explain how she is his “everything” and how he wants to do things “real big” and “bigger than ever done it.” He praises her for her loyalty and reliability, and promises to always be there for her. 

In the chorus, Drake reiterates his feelings for her, expressing that she is the “best he ever had.” He acknowledges that she may have other options, but that she chooses him, and he expresses his appreciation for that. In the second verse, Drake expands on his appreciation for her and how she is always there for him, no matter what. He also talks about how she stands by him, even when other people may not. 

The song continues with Drake discussing how she is always patient with him and how she never pays attention to rumors or assumptions. He also mentions his appreciation for her beauty, even when she’s not wearing makeup. In the bridge, Drake reiterates his feelings for her and how he wants to be with her forever. He also promises that they’ll always have fun together. 

In terms of linguistic analysis of the song, the lyrics are mainly Romantic in nature. The main themes explored in the song are love, appreciation, loyalty, and commitment. Additionally, the song expresses a sense of longing and desire for a deeper connection with his partner. 

Overall, "Best I Ever Had" is a love song that expresses Drake's appreciation for his significant other. The lyrics are mainly Romantic in nature, and the song expresses themes of love, appreciation, loyalty, and commitment.
