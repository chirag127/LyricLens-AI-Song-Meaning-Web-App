In Drake's song "Brand New", he expresses his emotional struggle as he attempts to move on from a past relationship. He is questioning his ability to truly move on, feeling like he is always competing with the past. He is asking whether anything that he is doing is truly brand new. 

The song begins with Drake reflecting on how his ex-partner is used to his actions, leading him to hide his true emotions. He then reflects on how they are moving too fast in this new relationship, and how his ex-partner is still up on a pedestal. Drake continues to express his confusion and doubt, as he is unable to paint a perfect picture of what he is feeling. 

The second verse shifts to a more personal tone, as Drake expresses the pain of knowing his ex-partner's mistakes. He questions whether the feeling is reversible, and if what he is doing is brand new. 

The chorus is a plea for Drake's new partner to answer his questions about their relationship. He wonders if his ex-partner's experiences with him are "brand new" or if he is too late.

Through his song, Drake conveys his emotions of doubt and confusion as he struggles to move on from a past relationship. He is questioning whether anything he is doing is truly new, or if he is just running behind himself in a competition with his past. 

A linguistic analysis of the song reveals that the lyrics focus heavily on themes of insecurity and comparison. Drake expresses his fear that he has not moved on from his past, and his uncertainty in the new relationship. The repetition of the phrase "brand new" reflects his desire to start anew, but also his fear that he is unable to truly do so. 

Overall, "Brand New" is a powerful song about the struggles of moving on from a past relationship and starting anew. Drake conveys his emotions of doubt and confusion as he questions whether anything he is doing is truly brand new.
