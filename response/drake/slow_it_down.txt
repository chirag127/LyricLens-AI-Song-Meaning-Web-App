In the song “Slow It Down” by Drake, the narrator is expressing his desire to take things slow and not rush into a relationship. In the first verse, he suggests that she give him her phone number so that he can call her when he comes into town. In the second verse, the narrator is back in Atlanta and is suggesting a second date. He offers various suggestions, such as the movies, mini golf, or ice cream. He states that he is not picky, and that she can let him know what she prefers. 

The chorus is repeated in both verses, and it emphasizes the idea of taking things slow. He also suggests that if he knows what she likes and it feels right, they should not act on it and should instead slow it down. The chorus also expresses his fear that if he doesn’t call her back or prove her right, all of this could last just one night. 

In summary, the song “Slow It Down” by Drake is about taking things slow in a relationship and not rushing into things. The narrator expresses his desire to take things slow and cautions against acting on the feelings or emotions that come with the start of a relationship. 

The song’s lyrics provide a great example of linguistic analysis in terms of emotions and feelings that musical artists express in their songs. Drake’s use of words such as “slow”, “rush”, and “over with” indicate a fear of getting too attached too quickly and suggests that the narrator wants to take things slow instead.
