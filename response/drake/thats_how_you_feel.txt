"That's How You Feel" by Drake is a song about a relationship gone wrong, with Drake reflecting on how his ex-lover has left him feeling betrayed and hurt. The lyrics illustrate a feeling of frustration and disbelief, as Drake questions his ex's behavior and how it has affected him. 

The first verse begins with Drake describing how his ex has left him feeling, "sittin' sideways" and "time gets to tickin' and it takes a toll". The phrase "sittin' sideways" implies a feeling of being out of control or off-balance, while the idea of time taking a toll suggests that the pain and hurt caused by the relationship has been mounting over time.

The second verse of the song further emphasizes Drake's hurt and betrayal, as he talks about how his ex has been "sidetrackin' him" and "playin' him". He accuses her of using him to do her "dirty work" and of not really caring about him and his feelings.

The refrain is sung by Nicki Minaj and DJ Boof, and provides some advice for dealing with the situation. They suggest avoiding being "played" by a clown and instead "fuck his best friends and make 'em yes men". This line is a comment on the power dynamics of relationships and how those who have been betrayed can take control and assert their own power.

The song ends with Drake repeating the chorus, with the phrase "that's how you feel" emphasizing his pain and frustration with the situation.

In summary, "That's How You Feel" is a song about a relationship gone wrong, and Drake's feelings of hurt and betrayal. The lyrics describe how his ex has been taking advantage of him and how he has been left feeling powerless. The refrain provides some advice for how to take control and assert power in such a situation.

Linguistically, the song is full of allusions to feelings of powerlessness and betrayal, with words like "sittin' sideways" and "sidetrackin'" conveying a sense of being off-balance and out of control. The song also has a distinct sense of frustration and disbelief, with Drake's repeated refrain of "that's how you feel" emphasizing his hurt and confusion. 

Overall, "That's How You Feel" is an emotionally charged song that conveys a feeling of betrayal and hurt. It is a powerful statement on the power dynamics of relationships and the sense of powerlessness that comes with being betrayed.
