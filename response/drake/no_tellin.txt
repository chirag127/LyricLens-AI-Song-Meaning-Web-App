No Tellin’ by Drake is a song about the uncertainty of life and the unknowns that come with it. In the first verse, Drake raps about envelopes coming in the mail and hoping they contain a check. He also speaks of a blade he keeps on him when checking a "bitch" and the police coming around looking for help to solve a case. He ends the verse by saying that he stays up late at night wondering if he will get what he wants out of life. 

The second verse features Rick Ross, who raps about switching up the flow and taking the summer off to get things right. He also talks about buying an island and having to pick the casket he’ll put a nail through if need be. 

The third verse features Popcaan and OVO Unruly, and talks about making it in life from the 6 to the 876. 

The chorus to the song is Drake repeating "Ain't no tellin’" as a reminder that there is no way to predict the future.

The song is a reminder that life is uncertain and that no one can predict what will happen. Drake reflects on his own life and experiences, and reflects on the fact that there is no one to blame for not getting what one wants. He also talks about the need to keep watch for “oppers”, as anything is possible.

The song is musically uplifting, with a catchy hook and a beat that will make you want to dance. Lyrically, the song is reflective and thought-provoking, as Drake reflects on his own experiences and the uncertainty of life.

Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a lot about Drake’s mindset and emotions. The repetition of the phrase “ain’t no tellin’” emphasizes the uncertainty of life, and the use of words such as “bitch” and “oppers” reflects a feeling of distrust. The song is also full of bravado, as Drake talks about buying an island and putting a nail in a casket.

Overall, No Tellin’ by Drake is an uplifting and reflective song that speaks to the uncertainty of life. It is musically catchy and lyrically thought-provoking, and provides insight into Drake’s mindset and emotions.
