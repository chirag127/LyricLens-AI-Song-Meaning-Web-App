The song “Wu-Tang Forever (Remix)” by Drake is an ode to the hip-hop and rap group Wu-Tang Clan and their influence on Drake’s own musical career. The song opens with a hook sung by Drake himself, reflecting on his relationship with the group and how it has grown over the years, and how the group and its members belong to no one else but him. The first verse is rapped by A$AP Rocky, where he reflects on his experiences at the Coachella music festival, how his ex never listened to him, and his desire to be the king. 

The second verse is rapped by Drake, who reflects on the power of his rap flow, how he’s been running up in the game, and how even though he’s not in Mason, he still gets respect. He also touches on his relationships with women, how he can do better and knows better, and how he always gets high to maintain his edge. 

The song as a whole is a reflection on Drake’s journey in hip-hop, his relationships with the Wu-Tang Clan and the people around him, and his growth as an artist. It’s a celebration of his success and his appreciation for the people that have been there for him along the way. 

Linguistically, the song is full of strong imagery, with Drake and Rocky both delivering punchy verses that capture their feelings and experiences in a vivid and powerful way. There are also references to the hip-hop culture and its influence on Drake, with the use of phrases like “petite gun raps” and “machine gun raps” to describe the strength of his flow. The song also contains hints of Drake’s own personal struggles, with lines like “half the times niggas don’t even make sense” and “I don’t know what’s getting into me” hinting at deeper issues. 

Overall, “Wu-Tang Forever (Remix)” is an inspiring and uplifting track that pays homage to the Wu-Tang Clan and celebrates the success of Drake as an artist. The song’s powerful imagery and vivid descriptions make it a perfect example of how hip-hop can be used to express emotions, feelings, and experiences in a creative and captivating way.
