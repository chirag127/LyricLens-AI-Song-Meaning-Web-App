"Weston Road Flows" by Drake is a song about the rapper's experiences growing up in Toronto and how he has achieved success. The song is filled with nostalgia for the past, as Drake reflects on the days when he and his friends couldn't afford pizza and how he has now become one of the most successful rappers. He remembers the days when he was just beginning to make it in the rap game and how he used to have secret handshakes with his friends. He also mentions his newfound fame and how it has changed his life.

The lyrics of the song are filled with vivid imagery and a vivid narrative. Drake paints a picture of the times he used to spend with his friends, and the experiences they shared together. He also reflects on the people who have been there for him and how they have helped him to achieve his success. He talks about the challenges he has faced and how he has been able to overcome them.

The song also has some strong messages about fame and success. Drake acknowledges that money cannot buy happiness and emphasizes the importance of having meaningful relationships in life. He also talks about how his fame has changed his life and how it can be both a blessing and a curse.

The lyrics of the song are filled with emotion and the language is simple yet powerful. Drake's delivery is smooth and his flow is effortless, which helps to emphasize the lyrics and bring out the emotion in the song.

The overall message of the song is that success is achievable with hard work and dedication, but it should not be the only thing that defines a person. Drake emphasizes the importance of having meaningful relationships and experiences in life, and how money cannot buy happiness. 

In terms of linguistic analysis of the song, the lyrics are filled with emotive language, with the repetition of words like "love" and "affection" to emphasize the importance of relationships in life. Additionally, the use of vivid imagery and storytelling helps to create a more immersive experience for the listener. The language is also often reflective and introspective, as Drake reflects on his experiences and the people that have been there for him.

Overall, "Weston Road Flows" is an emotionally powerful song that speaks to the struggles and triumphs of life. Drake's delivery is smooth and his lyrics are filled with emotion, making for an enjoyable and captivating listening experience. The song serves to remind us of the importance of meaningful relationships, hard work, and dedication, and that money cannot buy happiness.
