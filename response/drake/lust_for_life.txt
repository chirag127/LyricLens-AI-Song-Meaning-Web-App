Lust for Life is a song by Canadian artist Drake, released in 2018. The song is a celebration of life, and the lyrics reflect on the joys and struggles of life. The song begins with Drake expressing his ambition to make the most of life, and his desire to make the most of his opportunities. This is followed by a description of the lavish lifestyle Drake enjoys, and the luxuries he is able to access. This is followed by a description of trying to ignore temptation and his ex, who still wants him. The chorus celebrates a life of luxury, with Drake urging listeners to throw their money in the air for his girl. 

Lyrically, the song is an exploration of the feelings of ambition and desire for the good things in life. Drake uses the metaphor of throwing money in the air to celebrate the joy of having the things he desires, and the idea of making it last for as long as possible. The song also touches on themes of ambition, desire, and the temptations of life. The use of the metaphor of money in the chorus also ties in with the theme of ambition and the desire for success.

In terms of linguistic analysis, the lyrics of the song contain elements of both positive and negative sentiment. The positive sentiment is seen in the lines “I’m tryna do it all tonight/I got plans/I got a certain lust for life”, which illustrate Drake’s ambition and positive attitude. The negative sentiment is seen in the lines “my ex sendin’ late night texts/’cause she don’t know how to let go”, which portrays a feeling of frustration and regret. 

Overall, Lust for Life is a song that celebrates life and the joy of getting what you desire. The lyrics explore the themes of ambition, desire, and the temptations of life, and the use of metaphors help to illustrate these themes. The song also contains elements of both positive and negative sentiment, which helps to capture the complexity of the human experience.
