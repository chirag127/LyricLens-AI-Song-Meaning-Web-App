"AM 2 PM" by Drake is an ode to the hustle and grind of the rap game. He paints a vivid picture of his life, highlighting the challenges and struggles of life in the music industry, while also acknowledging the joys and successes that come with it.

The song paints a vivid picture of Drake's life, from his early beginnings of waking up and getting ready for work, to the late nights at the club and chasing his dreams. He speaks of the need to make his mother proud, and to live up to the legacy of his father. He acknowledges the dangers of his lifestyle, including the potential for jail time or getting shot, but the upside of fame and fortune.

The hook of the song features Drake emphasizing the need for respect, and how his success will be the result of hard work. He speaks of the need to get money, and how it will lead to more respect.

The second verse of the song takes a more reflective tone, as Drake speaks of his own struggles and successes, and the people who have been there along the way. He speaks of his city and the people in it, and how they strive to make it in the rap game. He speaks of the need to stay up late and write lyrics that people will believe in, and the respect he receives when DJs play his music.

Themes of ambition, determination, and striving for success are all present in the song. Drake speaks of the need to make it in the rap game and the respect he will gain when he does. He speaks of the importance of family, and the dangers of the lifestyle he leads, but also the joys of success.

The linguistic analysis of the song's lyrics reveals a mix of emotions, from determination and ambition to frustration and even sadness at times. Drake's lyrics reflect his life as an up and coming rapper, as he speaks of his dreams, his struggles and his successes. The use of language is effective in conveying his emotions, as he speaks of his ambition and determination, as well as his fear and frustrated moments.

In summary, "AM 2 PM" by Drake is a song about the hustle and grind of the rap game. Through his lyrics, Drake paints a vivid picture of his life - from his early mornings, to his late nights in the club, to the dangers and successes of his lifestyle. Themes of ambition, determination, and striving for success are all present in the song, and Drake conveys his emotions effectively through his use of language.
