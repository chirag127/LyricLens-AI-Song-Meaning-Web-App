"4PM in Calabasas" by Drake is a track off his 2016 mixtape, "More Life". The song is a powerful and personal reflection on Drake’s life, from his humble beginnings to his current status as a global superstar. The track is propelled by a hypnotic, melodic beat and Drake’s signature flow and lyrics. 

The track opens with Drake reflecting on how he has been able to become successful despite the odds being stacked against him. He talks about how he has had to take the road less travelled and how he has made the right choices in order to make it in the music industry. He reflects on the support he has had from his mother and father in terms of advice and guidance.

He talks about how his own success has made it difficult for him to trust people and how he has had to take certain precautions in order to protect himself. He also speaks about being able to keep his head above water despite being surrounded by people who are trying to bring him down. 

The chorus of the track sees Drake reflecting on how he is able to remain focused and determined despite the numerous distractions around him. He speaks about how he has been able to remain humble despite his success and how he is not swayed by material possessions. He also speaks about how he has been able to remain focused despite the numerous obstacles and challenges he has faced. 

The third verse sees Drake speaking about how he is able to take time to himself and unwind, despite his hectic lifestyle. He speaks about how he is able to enjoy the finer things in life such as his car and his clothing, but how he is not materialistic or obsessed with status symbols. He also speaks about how he is able to remain focused despite being surrounded by people who are trying to tempt him. 

The fourth verse sees Drake reflecting on his current lifestyle and how he is able to remain focused despite the numerous distractions around him. He speaks about how he is able to stay ahead of the competition and how he is able to stay true to his values in the face of temptation. He also speaks about how he has been able to remain focused on his music and how he is not swayed by the fame or money. 

The track is a powerful reflection on Drake’s life and his journey to success. The lyrics reflect his determination and focus, as well as his ability to remain humble and true to himself despite the numerous distractions in his life. Through his lyrics, Drake is able to provide an insight into his life and his mentality, while also reflecting on the importance of staying focused and determined in the face of adversity. 

Linguistically, the song is full of emotion and passion, as Drake reflects on his life and his journey to success. Drake’s words are full of confidence, determination and resilience. He speaks about his struggles and his successes, and how he has been able to remain focused on his goals despite the numerous distractions around him. 

In summary, "4PM in Calabasas" is an impressive and powerful reflection on Drake’s life and journey to success.
