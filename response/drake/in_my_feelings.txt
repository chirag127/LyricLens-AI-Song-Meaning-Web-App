In Drake's song “In My Feelings,” he explores the depths of his emotions as he expresses his love and dedication to someone special. In the chorus, he begs the person to stay by his side and never leave him, emphasizing the importance of their relationship. The verses also feature Drake reflecting on his newfound fame and the choices he has to make in order to stay on top. The song features a guest verse from Lil Wayne and a skit from Zazie Beetz.

The song is structured in a way that emphasizes the range of emotions Drake is feeling. The chorus is a passionate plea for his lover to stay by his side, while the verses focus on the implications of fame and his reflection on the choices he has to make. The production of the song is characterized by a bouncy trap beat and Drake’s melodic delivery.

Lyrically, Drake is exploring the highs and lows of his emotions. He expresses his love and dedication to his partner in the chorus and the verses, while also reflecting on the implications of fame in his life. He touches on the idea of feeling pressure to make the right choices, as well as the idea of needing someone to be there for him no matter what.

The song also includes a skit from Zazie Beetz that adds an element of comedy and lightheartedness to the track. The skit serves to break up the intensity of the song, offering a more playful tone to the track.

Overall, “In My Feelings” is a powerful and passionate song that captures the range of emotions Drake is feeling. The production and lyrics are both powerful and emotive, providing the perfect backdrop for Drake to explore the depths of his emotions. Additionally, the skit from Zazie Beetz adds a lighthearted touch to the track, serving to break up the intensity of the song.
