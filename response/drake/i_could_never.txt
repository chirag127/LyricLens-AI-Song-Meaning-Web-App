The song "I Could Never" by Drake and Jorja Smith is a powerful and emotive track, which speaks of the complex relationship between two people. The lyrics explore the idea of being ashamed of one another, and how it can be difficult to accept and love someone who has made mistakes.

The song starts off with Drake rapping about how the other person has let him down, and how he's been left with hesitation. He acknowledges that he should not be ashamed of the other person, despite their mistakes. This is further explored in the chorus sung by Jorja Smith, as she asks how someone could ever be ashamed of her. 

In the second verse, Drake speaks of how he has been told to walk away from the other person, yet still chose to stay. He then talks of how this person was the first to show him true love and care. This is further explored in the bridge, as Jorja Smith sings of how the other person could never be ashamed of her.

The song finishes with Jorja Smith asking why the other person can't ever accept her for who she is. This is a powerful message about the power of acceptance, and how it is important to recognize that everyone has flaws, but that shouldn't stop us from loving them.

This song is a powerful exploration of the complexities of relationships. It speaks to the importance of acceptance, and how it is possible to love someone despite their mistakes. The lyrics are uplifting and emotive, and the production is smooth and pleasant to listen to. Overall, this is an excellent song that speaks to the importance of love and understanding.
