The song "Right Hand" by Drake is an ode to a special woman in his life. In the song, Drake speaks of his admiration for this woman and provides an insight into her personality and her struggles. The song is a reminder that while Drake is there to support her, the streets never will.

The song begins with a hook that serves as the chorus of the song. In the chorus, Drake sings "You my right hand, you my go to / Told me everything about you, that's a bold move." This line speaks to the trust Drake has in this woman, as he has been told all about her life and is still by her side. He also acknowledges that it is a bold move for her to confide in him, showing his appreciation for her vulnerability.

The verses of the song provide more detail about the woman and her life. Drake mentions her desire for material things such as a Benz wheel and a trip to Maui, and speaks of her past relationships. He also alludes to her knack for posting pictures of herself and her tendency to party hard.

The chorus of the song is repeated again in the final verse, but with a different tone. This time, Drake speaks of his love and admiration for this woman, and reassures her that he will always be there for her, even when the streets don't love her like he does.

Overall, "Right Hand" by Drake is a heartfelt ode to a special woman in his life. The lyrics provide a detailed insight into her personality and her struggles, as well as Drake's admiration and love for her. In terms of linguistic analysis, the song is full of emotion and speaks to the importance of friendship and loyalty. Drake's words reflect the power of his love, and the chorus serves as a reminder of his unwavering support.
