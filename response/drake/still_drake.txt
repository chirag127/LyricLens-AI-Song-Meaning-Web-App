"Still Drake" by Drake is a powerful track that speaks to the universal experience of striving for success while navigating the complexities of life. The song explores the idea of an artist's journey to achieving success, while reflecting on the lessons learned along the way. Drake begins by reflecting on his own personal journey and how it has shaped him, from the early days of chopping porch lights to the current days of being able to afford a Porsche. He speaks about his relationships with women and how his fame has impacted them, as well as his relationships with other artists. Drake also touches on his spiritual journey and how, despite his successes and mistakes, he still believes in himself and his mission.

The song is a powerful reflection of the artist's life and struggles, and Drake's lyrics are poetic and deeply personal. He conveys a wide range of emotions and experiences, from pain and struggle to determination and resilience. This is highlighted in the chorus, where Drake sings "I never got to make it rain, but it look like fun". Here, Drake conveys his appreciation for the journey and his determination to keep going even when the odds are seemingly stacked against him.

The song also contains a variety of linguistic devices that add to the emotional impact of the song. These include the use of alliteration, assonance, and internal rhyme. Alliteration is used to emphasize certain words, creating a sense of flow and power. Assonance is used to create a rhythm and add a musical quality to the song, while internal rhyme is used to add emphasis to certain lines and create a sense of continuity.

In summary, "Still Drake" is a powerful and deeply personal track that speaks to the human experience of striving for success while navigating the complexities of life. Through poetic lyrics and effective use of linguistic devices, Drake paints a vivid picture of his journey and conveys a wide range of emotions.
