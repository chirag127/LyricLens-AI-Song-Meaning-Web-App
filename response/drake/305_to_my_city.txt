In his song "305 to My City," Drake explores the theme of achieving success against all odds. He begins the song by declaring that "drop down dropdrop shit is real out here," acknowledging the struggles of the street life, and the drive and hustle it takes to make it out. He goes on to praise his own accomplishments, bragging that he and his crew have "done it" and are "so far from finished." Drake reflects on his hard work, emphasizing that it "don't ever go unnoticed," and that he has earned the success he has achieved. 

Throughout the song, Drake celebrates the rewards that come with his success. He talks about his crew's newfound wealth and luxury, from the "locker room full of money" to the "down payment on the Jaguar." He also celebrates his success by reflecting on how it has provided for his friends and family, from his "best friend's room full of money" to his "parents sayin' this another phase in your life." 

Drake also speaks about how his success has allowed him to provide for his crew, from his offer to "make the calls to get y'all through customs" to his invitation to "get you some lobsters and shrimp." The song ends with Drake reflecting on how far he has come, noting that he and his crew are "not in Kansas anymore."

At its core, "305 to My City" is an ode to the hustle and determination it takes to make it out of the streets. Drake emphasizes that his success has come from hard work and dedication, and he celebrates the rewards it has brought him. The song is full of braggadocious lyrics and boasts about the rewards of a successful life, but it also serves to motivate those who are still hustling, and to remind them that success is attainable. 

When it comes to linguistic analysis, the song is full of positive emotions and uplifting messages. The lyrics are full of pride and joy, as Drake celebrates his own success and that of his crew. He speaks with confidence and determination, conveying an underlying message of ambition and ambition. The song is also full of social tendencies, as Drake shares his story and celebrates the success of those around him.
