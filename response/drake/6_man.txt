The song "6 Man" by Drake is an ode to being a hustler, with the title referring to being able to survive on one's own and make it to the top without help. The verses discuss Drake's ambition and struggle to make it on his own, as well as his determination to succeed despite any obstacles. He uses metaphors such as being in the Matrix and taking the blue pill, as well as references to other famous hustlers such as Lou Will and MacGyver, to emphasize his message.

In the first verse, Drake talks about how he doesn't need anyone to help him, as he is determined to make it on his own. He also brags about his success, as he has already achieved a lot in his young age. In the second verse, Drake talks about how he is not trying to win awards, as he is focused on achieving success on his own terms and not letting others dictate his life. He also mentions how he is not trying to be friends with anyone, as he is only focused on his own goals.

The chorus of the song emphasizes the importance of staying focused and working hard to achieve one's goals. Drake talks about how he is making millions by working the night shift, a reference to the long hours that he has put in to get to where he is today. He also mentions how he can handle any obstacles that may come his way and that he will always have the support of his family and friends.

In summary, the song "6 Man" by Drake is an inspirational song about hustling and determination. It emphasizes the importance of working hard and staying focused in order to achieve one's goals, as well as the importance of having a strong support system of family and friends. The song also provides a lesson to listeners about the importance of being independent and relying on oneself to get to where one wants to be.

In terms of its linguistic analysis, this song contains a great deal of positive sentiment, with many phrases focusing on success and achieving goals. The song also contains a lot of references to hustling and hard work, with Drake conveying the importance of putting in the effort to get what one is aiming for. Additionally, the song also conveys a strong sense of determination, as Drake is not afraid to take risks and put in the work to get what he wants. This is further emphasized by the chorus, which emphasizes the importance of staying focused and working hard, even when it may seem difficult.
