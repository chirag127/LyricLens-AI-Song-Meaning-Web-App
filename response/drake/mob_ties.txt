Mob Ties by Drake is a song about loyalty and betrayal in the rap game, and the struggle to stay true to oneself and one’s friends in the face of temptation. The song opens with Drake rapping about his frustrations with his peers, expressing how he’s tired of the lack of loyalty and is looking to hire some help to “get rid of these niggas.” This sets the tone for the rest of the song, as Drake reflects on the consequences of his decisions and how he has to stay vigilant in order to avoid being taken advantage of. 

The chorus of the song reflects on the importance of loyalty and how it is “too late for all that loveydovey shit,” as Drake is no longer able to be trusting and carefree. The lyrics describe how he has to be guarded and be prepared for betrayal, as “they want me gone but don’t know why.” The verses further explore this theme, as Drake raps about his lavish lifestyle and how he’s not afraid to “knock you off to pay their tithes.”

Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a strong sense of defiance, as Drake emphasizes his own power and success while simultaneously expressing his cynicism towards his peers. This is demonstrated in lines such as “twentynine is comin’ they on edge when I cook cook,” and “I don’t care how they look nah.” These lines show Drake’s self-confidence and his willingness to stand up for himself in the face of adversity. 

Overall, Mob Ties is an intense and powerful track. With its hard-hitting beat and Drake’s determined and intense delivery, the song paints a vivid picture of the struggles of loyalty and betrayal in the rap game. Drake’s lyrics are sharp and incisive, and the song is a perfect example of his ability to tell a story and express complex emotions with his words.
