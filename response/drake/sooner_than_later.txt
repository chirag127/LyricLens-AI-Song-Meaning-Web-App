In Drake's song "Sooner Than Later," the artist reflects on a failed relationship and his regrets for not taking action sooner. The song starts off with Drake admitting that he forgot to call someone on their birthday, which he recognizes as an indication that they were not on his mind. He then talks about how the lights don't seem to glow the same way they used to, hinting at how his life has changed since the relationship ended. The chorus finds Drake asking to be given a chance to make things right, and begging the person not to move on yet. 

The second verse finds Drake discussing the changes the person has made since the breakup, indicating how much he still cares for them. He also reflects on how he has to choose between his own ambitions and the person he loves, and how fame and money cannot buy time. The bridge finds Drake reflecting on his decision and the consequences of his actions, and finally making a last plea for the person to give him a chance. 

The lyrics of the song are full of emotion, as Drake reflects on his regrets and expresses his longing for the person. The use of metaphors and imagery helps to convey the message of the song, and the musical elements such as the chorus and bridge emphasize the emotions of the song.
