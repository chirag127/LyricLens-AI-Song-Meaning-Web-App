The song "Desires" by Drake is a deeply introspective track that looks inward at the rapper's innermost feelings and desires. In the song, Drake reflects on his past relationships and questions how he could have treated them better. He also examines his own flaws and insecurities, and how they have affected the people around him.

He reflects on how he should have moved them away from the hustle and bustle of the city, to somewhere more peaceful. This is a metaphor for his wishes to protect his partner from the outside world and to provide them with a safe haven away from any potential harm.

The chorus of the song then follows, with Drake repeating the line "cause you got desires" a few times. This is a reference to his partner's wishes and needs, which Drake is recognizing and acknowledging. He is recognizing that his partner has her own set of desires and wishes, and that he should respect them.

In the second verse, Drake talks about how his partner has always been self-controlled, but how she has let go of that recently and has let her desires take over. He then reflects on how he has tried to help her financially, but that she still has her own secrets. Drake then goes on to reminisce about how he has taken her to various places around the world, and how he has gotten her nice things, but that she might not appreciate them.

The third verse is where Drake begins to reflect on his own flaws. He talks about how he has moved too fast in the past, and that he might have taken his partner for granted. He then admits that it's his fault for the way he has handled their relationship, and that he should have done more to show her that he cares.

The song ends with a chorus of "cause you got desires" as a reminder that even though Drake has his own flaws, he still recognizes and respects his partner's wishes and needs.

Overall, the song "Desires" by Drake is a reflective and introspective track that looks inward at the rapper's own feelings and insecurities. It serves as a reminder that even though there may be flaws in a relationship, it is still important to recognize and respect the other person's desires and wishes.
