"Get Over It" by Drake is a song about perseverance and resilience, and how sometimes the only way to get through tough times is to just “get over it”. He also references his close relationship with fellow artist Lil Wayne, and how his engineer is “the best in the game”. In the chorus, Drake tells people to “get over it” and accept that things are changing and that he is rising in the music industry.

The second verse of the song finds Drake speaking directly to the people that are hating on him and telling them to “get over it”. He also talks about how he is trying to clean up his act and become a better person. He also mentions how he has been blessed with success and that he is “turning”, a reference to his transformation from rapping to becoming a full-time artist.

The lyrics of “Get Over It” are full of emotion and power, and Drake’s delivery is passionate and sincere. Lyrically, this song is a great example of how Drake is able to express his feelings and experiences through his music.
