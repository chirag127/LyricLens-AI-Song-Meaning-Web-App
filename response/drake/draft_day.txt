"Draft Day" by Drake is a reflective and proud anthem that reflects on the journey taken to get to the top. The song is a celebration of success, while also acknowledging the immense struggle and dedication it takes to make it to the top. 

The first verse of the song sets the tone for the song, as Drake reflects on his journey from being a young, unknown artist to becoming a superstar. He references Johnny Manziel, a former NFL player, to symbolize the hard work that it takes to make it in a competitive field. He also references Andrew Wiggins, another former professional athlete, to emphasize the fact that staying on the winning side is essential for success. 

The chorus of the song follows in the same vein, as Drake emphasizes that he has achieved success through hard work and dedication. He acknowledges that if he had left it to chance, he might not have reached the same level of success. 

The second verse of the song sees Drake reflecting on the current state of his career and the people who have helped him get there. He speaks of his success and the luxurious lifestyle he has been able to attain due to his hard work. He also speaks of the loyalty of his friends, who have stood by him throughout his journey. 

The bridge of the song is a reflection on the state of the world and the need to use one's talents to make a difference. He speaks of his commitment to help others and the importance of using one's influence to make a positive difference. 

The song ends with Drake reflecting on the journey he has taken to get to the top. He acknowledges the struggle and hard work it took to get there, and uses his experiences as motivation to continue to strive for success. 

In summary, "Draft Day" by Drake is an inspiring and reflective anthem that celebrates the hard work and dedication it takes to make it to the top. The song speaks to the importance of loyalty and the need to use one's influence for good. It is a powerful testament to the power of hard work and dedication and an inspiring reminder that anything is possible with the right attitude and commitment.
