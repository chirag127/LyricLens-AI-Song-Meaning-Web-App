Pop Style by Drake is a boastful track that celebrates success and an unapologetic lifestyle. The song's lyrics tell a story of how Drake and his friends have achieved their goals and ambitions, despite the impediments and obstacles they have faced along the way. The lyrics also express Drake's confidence in himself and his ambitions, as well as his disdain for those who have tried to stand in his way.

The first verse of the song describes Drake's journey from dropping out of school and becoming "dumb rich" to having achieved a certain level of success. He then brags about having a lifestyle that many can only dream of, and expresses his appreciation for his mother and his determination to make her proud. The second verse is a more introspective look at Drake and his life, reflecting on his accomplishments and the decisions he has made. He also acknowledges that there are still people who are trying to bring him down, but he will not be stopped.

The third verse of the song is another braggadocious section where Drake speaks of the success he and his friends have achieved, and how they've managed to make it in spite of their opposition. He also brings up his "chain" (a metaphor for his wealth and status) and how it's made him an MVP even before making it to the league. The fourth verse is a reflection on the precarious nature of success, and how easily it can be taken away. Drake also reiterates that he cannot trust anyone, as they are all looking out for themselves.

The chorus of the song is a bold declaration of Drake and his friends' success, as well as their determination to continue living the "pop style" lifestyle. It also serves as a reminder to Drake and his listeners to stay focused on their goals and never give up.

In terms of its emotional content, Pop Style is a very assertive and self-confident track. Drake expresses his pride in himself and his accomplishments, without any hint of self-doubt or hesitation. The lyrics also convey a sense of resilience, as Drake and his friends have faced many obstacles and yet still managed to achieve their goals. On a linguistic level, the song is characterized by its use of metaphors, as Drake uses chains and other objects to represent his wealth and status.

Overall, Pop Style by Drake is an enthusiastic and motivating track that celebrates success and resilience. The song's lyrics are both bold and inspiring, and the production is top-notch, making it a great track to listen to when feeling down.
