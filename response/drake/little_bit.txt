In the song "Little Bit" by Drake and Lykke Li, Drake expresses his feelings for someone, but is hesitant to act on them due to the potential consequences. The chorus is a plea for the object of his affection to reciprocate his feelings. Drake admits he's "a little bit in love with you" and hopes that the other person feels the same. Even though he knows that if it was to become official, it would have to be let go, he is still willing to take the risk. 

The second verse finds Drake worrying about his partner being taken away from him. He's scared of the possibility that she could be stolen away by someone else. He tells her to ignore what people say and that he would do anything to keep her. 

The bridge of the song finds Drake admitting that he is "a little bit in love with you". He knows that he can't confirm the fling, but he still can't deny his feelings. 

Overall, "Little Bit" is a song exploring the conflict between the desire to love and be loved, and the fear of risking it all.
