The song "Think Good Thoughts" by Drake is a powerful exploration of the wrong assumptions people make about others, and the importance of being open-minded before passing judgement. The song features Drake, 9th Wonder, Phonte, and Elzhi, each of whom bring different perspectives to the track.

Finally, Elzhi's verse speaks of how he won't judge a book by its cover, and how he needs to go beyond what he sees on the surface to get to know someone's true character. He also speaks of how he had to look deeper into Drake's story to truly understand him. 

Overall, the song is a powerful exploration of the wrong assumptions people make about others, and the importance of being open-minded before passing judgement. Linguistically, the lyrics are positive and uplifting, conveying themes of hope and understanding. The artists present their perspectives on the topic with eloquence and thoughtfulness, making this song both enjoyable and thought-provoking.
