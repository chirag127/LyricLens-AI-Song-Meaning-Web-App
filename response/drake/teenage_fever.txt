"Teenage Fever" by Drake is a melancholic and reflective song about the longing for a lost love. In the lyrics, Drake reflects on the potential of what could have been had he and his love stuck together. He expresses his regret for not staying and admitting his feelings, and contemplates if his love would have been returned if he had. 

The song begins with Drake reflecting on how difficult it is to carry his heart after dark, blaming himself for not being able to make the relationship work. He then mentions meeting someone new and wanting to go back to spend time with them. 

The chorus features Jennifer Lopez's vocals, with Drake asking questions about what would have happened if he had her love and he gave her all his trust. He wonders if she would be comforted and if she would lie to him if she knew his love was untrue. 

In the second verse, Drake admits to feeling out of body and overwhelmed when he's around his love. He reflects on the night they didn't admit their feelings and contemplates his decision not to stay. He compares the feeling of longing to a teenage fever, and says neither of them are scared of it. 

The song ends with Drake apologizing for rambling and saying that his mind is tired. 

This song is a beautiful and moving exploration of regret, longing and potential. The lyrics showcase Drake's vulnerability, as he contemplates what could have been with his love and the regret of not being able to stay with her. The chorus featuring Jennifer Lopez's vocals provides a beautiful contrast to Drake's rapping, and the two voices combine to create a powerful and emotive song. 

Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a clear sentiment of regret, with Drake expressing his sorrow for not being able to make the relationship work. He also expresses a longing for his love and a desire to have her love, conveying his regret in a poetic and melancholic way. The lyrics also express a sense of hope, as Drake contemplates if his love would have been returned if he had given it.
