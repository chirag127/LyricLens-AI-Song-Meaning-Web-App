In his song “March 14”, Drake takes listeners on a journey through his own personal struggles as he comes to terms with the challenges of fatherhood. The song begins with Drake reflecting on his past, and the consequences of his decisions when it comes to having a family. He speaks about his previous relationships and how they didn’t work out, and how he now finds himself in a situation where he is a co-parent. He expresses his wish that it had been different, as he had always envisioned a traditional family unit. 

In the second verse, Drake speaks about the joy of fatherhood and his excitement for the future. He speaks of his son, and how he feels the need to provide for him and make sure he is taken care of. He also mentions his son’s grandfather, who is yet to meet him, and how he hopes to be able to make it work. 

The third verse is a reflection on the struggles of fatherhood and the hardships that come with it. He speaks of his own feelings of loneliness and of being overwhelmed by the responsibility of raising his son. He expresses his hope that he and his son’s mother can come to an understanding, and that his son will have a better life than he had. 

The song ends with a refrain, in which Drake reflects on the transformation he has gone through and the pain of growing up and taking on the responsibility of being a father. He speaks of feeling alone, and needing shelter from the rain to ease his pain. 

In summary, “March 14” is an emotionally charged and introspective song which allows listeners a glimpse into Drake’s struggles as he comes to terms with being a father and the changes it brings to his life. The song is both personal and relatable, and speaks to the difficulties of being a parent, as well as the joys that come with it.
