"Free Smoke" by Drake is a thought-provoking, introspective track that delves into the artist's innermost feelings and emotions. In the song, Drake reflects on his humble beginnings and the struggles he faced as he climbed to the top. He paints a vivid picture of his journey thus far, from being poor and barely making ends meet, to living a luxurious lifestyle, and all the ups and downs that come along with it. 

The song begins with Drake reflecting on his current situation and how different it is from his past. He acknowledges the strength of his feelings, and how it has helped him to overcome any pain he may have experienced. He then goes on to talk about how he is reaching new heights and is finding a new release.

The main chorus of the song acknowledges the newfound freedom that Drake has experienced, and how it has liberated him from his former struggles. He is no longer tied down to the same problems that he faced before, and is now free to pursue his dreams.

The verses of the song further explore Drake's personal journey, from his early days of sleeping on his friend's couch and struggling to make ends meet, to the present day where he is able to enjoy the fruits of his labour. He reflects on how much his life has changed, from the people he used to know to the lavish lifestyle he now leads. He also acknowledges the people that he has left behind, and the difficulties he faced in letting go of the past.

In the bridge, Drake further reflects on his journey, and how he has gone from being disregarded to being recognized for his efforts. He acknowledges the women who used to ignore him, but now recognize him and his success. 

In conclusion, "Free Smoke" is a poignant track that speaks to Drake's personal journey and the struggles he faced to get to where he is today. With a combination of vivid imagery and heartfelt lyrics, Drake paints a vivid picture of the highs and lows of his life. The song speaks to a range of emotions, from pain and struggle to celebration and freedom. Linguistically, the song conveys a deep sense of longing and reflection, as Drake looks back on his past and looks forward to his future.
