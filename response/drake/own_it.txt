The song "Own It" by Drake is a heartfelt and reflective track about the struggles of modern relationships and the importance of communication. The song starts off with a gentle beat from producer Noah 40 Shebib and a soft hook from PartyNextDoor, emphasizing Drake's introspective lyrics.

The opening verse begins with Drake acknowledging that although he still loves his partner, there is little else that he can find in the world to have feelings for. He also emphasizes his ownership of their relationship, stating that it is ‘guess whose it is’. This sets the tone for a song which is both reflective and defiant, as Drake lays out the terms of their relationship.

The second verse sees Drake further exploring the idea of ownership as he reflects on the need for more meaningful connections and more honest communication. He makes it clear that he wants their interactions to be based on love, trust and respect, and not simply physical pleasure.

The third verse takes a more aggressive turn as Drake takes aim at those who have doubted him and failed to support him. He also points out the hypocrisy of those who ‘talk more than bitches’, implying that they are too quick to judge and too slow to offer support.

The hook, delivered by PartyNextDoor, encourages the listener to ‘own it’. This could be interpreted as a call to take ownership of one's own life, as well as a reminder to Drake's partner to take ownership of their relationship.

In summary, "Own It" is a reflective and defiant track which sees Drake exploring the complexities of modern relationships and the importance of communication. He encourages his partner to take ownership of their relationship, while also taking a shot at those who have doubted him. Linguistic analysis of the song lyrics reveals Drake's inner struggles, as well as his determination to take ownership of his life and his relationships. Ultimately, it is a powerful track that speaks to the struggles of modern relationships and the importance of communication.
