 "Started From the Bottom" by Drake is a powerful and inspirational song that speaks to the struggles and successes of life. The song is about Drake’s story of starting from the bottom and working hard to make it to the top. The verses chronicle Drake’s journey from living with his mother to being a successful rap artist. He outlines the hard work he put in to reach his success and the challenges he faced along the way. 

The chorus of the song serves as the main takeaway, with the words “started from the bottom, now we’re here.” Drake is emphasizing that he and his team started from the bottom and worked their way up to success. The chorus is a reminder to listeners that if you put in the work and believe in yourself, you can make it to the top. 

The song’s lyrics also provide a great deal of insight into Drake’s character. He expresses his desire to get ahead on his own, but also his appreciation for his family and team. He emphasizes the importance of having a strong support system to help one make it through life’s struggles. He also speaks to his own pride in his hard work, showing that he is not afraid to take risks and put in the effort to make it to the top.

Linguistically, the song has a conversational and personal feel to it, as if Drake is talking directly to the listener. The use of repetition also serves to emphasize the main takeaway of the song and to make it more memorable. The language is direct and straightforward, with Drake conveying his message in a clear and concise manner.

Overall, "Started From the Bottom" is an inspirational and uplifting song that speaks to the power of hard work and dedication. The song provides insight into Drake’s character and his journey to success, and serves as a reminder that anything is possible if you put in the effort and believe in yourself.
