Elevate by Drake is a triumphant, uplifting song that celebrates the successes of Drake and his close friends. The song opens with a declaration of Drake's success, as he raps "Elevate, elevate, only obligation is to tell it straight/ So much on my plate I gotta delegate/ Baka passed a drug test we gotta celebrate." This opening verse serves to set the optimistic tone of the song, as Drake reflects on his own success and that of his close friends. He also highlights the importance of perseverance and hard work, as he acknowledges that his success was earned by putting in the effort and making smart decisions.

The chorus further emphasizes the message of success and perseverance, as Drake raps "I'm in bed awake thinkin' how'd I make all this happen for myself and my family/ All this happen for myself and my family/ There's no way that this is real man it can't be." This chorus serves as a reminder that success is achievable through hard work and dedication, and that it is important to be grateful for the things that have been achieved.

In the second verse, Drake raps about his own success and how he managed to achieve it. He raps "French Montana if you need me you can call me/ I stay busy makin' money, you know what is on my mind/ All I think about is hunnids." Here, he emphasizes the importance of taking advantage of opportunities and staying dedicated to one's goals. He also highlights his own dedication to his craft, as he states that he is always working and improving himself.

The third verse serves as a contrast to the previous two, as Drake reflects on his past and how different his life was before his success. He raps "I couldn't picture bein' champagne haan/ When I would buy that shit and save it for the right occasion/ I couldn't picture changes when I was with Ms. Creighton/ That lady hates me couldn't picture wifin' braden." Here, he reflects on the struggles he faced before his success, acknowledging that he was in a very different place before he achieved his current level of success.

Overall, Elevate is an inspiring song that celebrates the success of Drake and his close friends, emphasizing the importance of perseverance and hard work. The song also serves as a reminder that no matter how difficult life may seem, success is achievable with dedication and hard work.

Linguistically, the song is written in a conversational style that is accessible and easy to understand. Drake's lyrics are filled with vivid imagery and personal anecdotes, allowing the listener to connect with the message of the song. Drake also conveys a sense of positivity and optimism, as he reflects on his own success and that of his close friends. This optimism helps to further motivate the listener and encourages them to strive for success.
