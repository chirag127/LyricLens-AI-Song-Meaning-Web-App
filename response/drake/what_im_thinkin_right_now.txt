The song “What I’m Thinkin’ Right Now” by Drake is an exploration of the rapper’s life and his journey to success. He reflects on the importance of his father’s advice and the lessons he has learned along the way. Drake acknowledges the importance of staying grounded, even in the midst of his growing fame and success. He talks about the desire to rebel and spend, but also recognizes the need to remain humble and focused. Lastly, he speaks to the changing culture of the rap world and his desire to stay true to himself.

In the first verse, Drake reflects on the advice given to him by his father and how it has helped shape his life. He references the book “48 Laws”, which he says was instrumental in keeping him focused and grounded. He also talks about his desire to stay true to himself, despite the allure of fame.

The second verse speaks to the changing culture of the rap world and Drake’s desire to stay true to himself. He speaks of his rejection of bright colors and rope chains, and his embrace of classic style. He also speaks of the need to be mindful of one’s spending and actions. 

The third verse speaks to the importance of reading and learning, and Drake’s desire to raise awareness among the younger generation. He speaks of how the new “craze” is to have the latest style, rather than read books, and admits that he would not be true to himself if he said he liked it. 

The song is a reflection on the importance of staying grounded, even in the midst of success. Drake speaks of the importance of learning from people who have come before him and the need to stay true to oneself. The song also speaks to the changing culture of the rap world and the importance of reading and learning. 

Overall, “What I’m Thinkin’ Right Now” by Drake is a powerful ode to staying true to oneself and learning from the lessons of the past. The lyrics are thoughtful and reflective, and the message is one of staying grounded and being conscious of one’s actions. The song is a great reminder of the importance of learning and staying true to one’s roots.
