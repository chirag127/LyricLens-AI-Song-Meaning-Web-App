Say What's Real is a song by Drake that explores themes of loneliness, fame, and relationships. The song begins with Drake reflecting on the feeling of being alone in the studio, surrounded by people who only seem to be there to please him. He acknowledges that he has a need for privacy and to be surrounded by genuine people. Drake then discusses his success in the music industry, noting that he has "transitioning from fitting in to standing out." This statement is further emphasized when he mentions the expensive clothing he wears and the paparazzi that follows him around.

The lyrics then move onto Drake's relationships, as he mentions his ex-girlfriend, his current girlfriend, and a girl he's seeing on the side. He expresses his struggle to maintain relationships with all of these people, as he's constantly on the move. He then acknowledges the influence of his hometown and the gossip that comes with it. He emphasizes that he should not be judged for his success and the money he is making.

The song ends with Drake reflecting on his career, noting that he never expected to become successful and that he has been underestimated. He also acknowledges his mentor, 40, who has pushed him to stay true to himself. The song ends with Drake declaring that he is "making of a legend" and that he is determined to prove all of his doubters wrong.

Overall, Say What's Real is a reflective and introspective song that explores the struggles of fame, relationships, and the need for privacy. Drake expresses his determination to stay true to himself and achieve his goals despite the obstacles that may stand in his way.

In terms of a linguistic analysis of the lyrics, the song is full of words and phrases that express feelings of isolation, loneliness, and self-doubt. Drake's choice of words also reveals a sense of determination and ambition to succeed, as he is determined to prove his doubters wrong and become a legend. The song is written in a confessional manner and the tone is reflective of the struggles that Drake has faced on his journey to success.
