Messages From You by Drake is a melancholic and reflective song about the power of a former flame's words to disrupt the present moment. The narrator is enjoying the company of a new woman, Lorraine, and is considering the possibility of a future with her. However, the moment is interrupted by a text from his past relationship, one that stirs up old emotions and shakes his newfound stability. 

The lyrics are an exploration of the narrator's conflicting emotions and thoughts. Drake's verses express his frustration and confusion over the text - hating the words that his former flame sends, yet still being drawn to them. He reflects on the fact that the feelings have not yet gone away, despite his efforts to move on. The chorus emphasizes the power of these messages to disrupt his day and the fact that, despite his attempts to move on, his emotions remain the same.

The song is a powerful expression of the impact the words of a past relationship can have. Drake's lyrics are full of emotion and provide a relatable experience to anyone who has found themselves in a similar situation. The song also provides an interesting linguistic analysis, as the words used to express the narrator's emotions are often used to create a sense of confusion and frustration. By analyzing the lyrics, it is possible to detect the emotions and feelings that Drake expresses in the song, and to gain insight into his overall state of mind. 

Overall, Messages From You is a powerful and emotional song that provides an insight into the tumultuous nature of relationships and the power of words. It is sure to resonate with many listeners and is an excellent example of Drake's songwriting ability.
