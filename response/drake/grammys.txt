In Drake's song "Grammys", he is expressing his feelings of pride and joy for his success and the recognition he has received for it. He references his success in the music industry, his celebrity status, his financial success, and his ability to purchase expensive luxuries. He also alludes to his past and the struggles he faced to achieve what he has.

The song starts off with Drake reflecting on his success and how he has achieved it without the help of a major label. He brags about his success, noting that while many people with deals can't make a greatest hits album, he has achieved this feat. He also talks about how the hood has accepted him, and that while he never claimed it, it has claimed him.

The chorus of the song references the idea of fame and the idea of being inducted into a hall of fame. He says he's "back inside the matrix" and that he and his squad are "gold mine". He also brags about pulling up in yachts and driving cars with black tints.

The second verse sees Drake talking about how people think he has won a Grammy, and how they envy him for it. He talks about swerving "off the panoramic" and "showin' out" so that people can't stand him. He also talks about his financial success, noting that he can afford to buy expensive things like a percocet and a gallon of Actavis.

The song ends with Drake once again referencing his financial success and his ability to buy luxury items; he says he's about to bring out another whip. He then repeats the chorus.

The song is a celebration of Drake's success, and the pride and joy he feels for it. His lyrics are full of braggadocio and bravado, talking about his financial success, his fame, and how people can't stand him because of it. The lyrics also contain references to his past and the struggles he faced to get to where he is today.

The linguistic analysis of the song's lyrics reveals an overall emotion of pride and joy. The language is full of confidence and references to Drake's success and the envy it has caused in those around him. The song is also full of references to luxury items and money, which can be interpreted as a sign of Drake's pride in his financial success.
