Drake's "We'll Be Fine" is an anthemic ode to resilience in the face of adversity. The song starts off with a verse from Drake where he reflects on his experiences thus far, and the importance of staying alive, even when life feels hopeless. The chorus of the song emphasizes the idea of resilience and strength, as Drake declares that "we'll be fine" despite the struggles. The second verse features a guest appearance from Birdman, who adds a more aggressive energy to the track. Birdman raps about success, money, and the importance of standing together. 

The lyrics of the song contain many emotions, from despair to hope, and Drake and Birdman capture these feelings in their performances. The words are full of references to the struggles of life, with Drake taking a more introspective approach whereas Birdman offers a more direct and braggadocious outlook. Drake's verse is also full of double meanings, as he speaks of his success while also reflecting on his past failures.

The song is further enhanced by the production from tminus and Noah 40 Shebib, with an upbeat and energetic instrumental that keeps the energy high throughout. The beat is interspersed with vocal samples throughout, adding to the overall sound of the track. 

In summary, Drake's "We'll Be Fine" is an uplifting and inspiring track that celebrates resilience and the strength of the human spirit. The lyrics are full of emotion and the production is top-notch, making the track an enjoyable listen. In addition to being an enjoyable listen, the track also serves as an important reminder to never give up and always strive for success.
