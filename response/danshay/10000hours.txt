The song "10,000 Hours" by Danshay is a romantic ballad that expresses the singer's deep desire to learn everything about their partner. The song is structured with two verses and a repeated chorus. In the first verse, the singer asks their partner questions about their likes and interests, showing a desire to understand them better. The second verse focuses on the singer's commitment to their relationship, stating that they are willing to spend "ten thousand hours" learning about their partner and expressing their love for them.

In terms of literary devices, the song uses repetition in the chorus to emphasize the singer's dedication to their partner. The lyrics also use imagery of the rain and dreams to create a romantic and introspective atmosphere.

Overall, the song conveys a strong sense of love and commitment, with the singer willing to put in the time and effort to understand their partner on a deeper level. The song's structure and use of repetition effectively convey this message, making it a heartfelt and romantic ballad.
