"Pointless" is a love song written and performed by Scottish singer-songwriter Lewis Capaldi. The song explores the idea that everything in life becomes meaningless without the presence of a loved one.

In the first verse, Capaldi sings about the small gestures of love that he and his partner exchange on a daily basis. He brings her coffee in the morning and takes her out to fancy restaurants, while she brings him inner peace and takes away his sadness. These simple acts of love and care are what keep their relationship strong.

In the chorus, Capaldi emphasizes that everything he does, from his "airs and graces" to the little things he does, is pointless without her. He also expresses that of all the dreams he's chasing, there's only one he truly cares about - being with her.

The second verse continues to describe the ways in which the couple support each other. Capaldi lights the fire when it's cold out and hopes that she will love him forever, while she lights up the room and takes away his pain. They are each other's source of happiness and strength.

In the final chorus, Capaldi repeats that everything is pointless without her and pledges to wait for her no matter how long it takes. He affirms his love and devotion to her, emphasizing that without her, his life is meaningless.

Overall, "Pointless" is a beautiful and heartfelt love song that captures the deep emotional connection between two people. It conveys the idea that love is the most important thing in life, and without it, everything else becomes meaningless.
