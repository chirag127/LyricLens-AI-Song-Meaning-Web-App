"Somewhere In Brooklyn" is a song by Bruno Mars about a chance encounter he had with a young woman in Brooklyn. The song begins with a description of the woman, who is "covered in leather and gold" and is 21 years old. Mars says he lost her in the cold and wonders if he will ever see her again.

In the first verse, Mars describes how he met the woman at a train stop, where she was wearing red Nike high tops and listening to hip-hop. They start talking and have a brief conversation before a train arrives and she gets on. Mars is left behind, wondering if he will ever see her again.

In the second verse, Mars describes his search for the woman, walking the streets of Brooklyn and asking people if they know her. He visits different neighborhoods, from Greenpoint to Flatbush, but he has no luck finding her.

The chorus repeats the line "She's somewhere, somewhere, somewhere in Brooklyn" and reflects Mars' hope that he will eventually find her. In the final verse, Mars sings about his continued search for the woman and wonders if they will ever meet again.

Overall, "Somewhere In Brooklyn" is a song about a fleeting encounter and the desire to reconnect with a lost love. The lyrics are romantic and nostalgic, and the melody is upbeat and catchy. The song captures the feeling of longing and the hope of finding someone special in the bustling city of Brooklyn.
