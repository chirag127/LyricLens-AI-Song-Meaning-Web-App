"Voices In My Head" by Bruno Mars is a song about a person who is waiting for their partner to come home, and the voices in their head that keep telling them that their partner will return. In the first verse, the narrator is alone in their room, waiting for a phone call from their partner, who they believe will come home soon. However, the voices in their head keep saying that their partner will come back to them. The second verse explores the narrator's mental state, as they talk to themselves and hear other voices in their head. They are unsure of what to do, and the voices continue to tell them that their partner will return.

The chorus repeats the idea that the voices in their head keep saying that their partner will come back to them. In the third verse, the narrator thinks they hear their partner at the door, but it turns out to be just the mailman. The voices in their head continue to tell them that their partner will return, and the narrator repeats the refrain that they will be ok and just need to get through the day.

Overall, the song is a poignant exploration of the power of hope and the way that our minds can play tricks on us when we are lonely and longing for someone we love. The lyrics are relatable and the melody is catchy, making it a strong addition to Bruno Mars' discography.
