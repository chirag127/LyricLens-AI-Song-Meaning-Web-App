"Killa On The Run" by Bruno Mars is a song about seeking revenge after being betrayed by a lover. In the song, Bruno Mars sings from the perspective of someone who has been hurt and taken advantage of by their partner. They warn their partner that their actions will have consequences, but the partner continues to play games and treat them poorly. In response, the narrator decides to take revenge and becomes a "killa on the run," seeking retribution for the pain their partner has caused them.

Throughout the song, the narrator describes their feelings of betrayal and anger, and how they have been transformed into a vengeful and dangerous person. They sing about tying their partner to train tracks and holding them at gunpoint, and revel in the thought of the sweet taste of revenge.

Overall, the message of the song is one of betrayal and the desire for revenge. It portrays the narrator as a victim who has been pushed too far and is now seeking to inflict pain on their former partner in retaliation.
