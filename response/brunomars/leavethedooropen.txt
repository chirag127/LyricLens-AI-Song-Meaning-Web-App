"Leave The Door Open" by Bruno Mars is a song in which the singer invites his partner over to his house for a romantic evening. In the first verse, the singer describes his current state and the preparations he has made for his partner's arrival, such as drinking wine in a robe and cleaning his house. In the chorus, he tells his partner that he is leaving the door open and hoping that she feels the same way he does and wants to spend the night with him.

The overall message of the song is one of invitation and desire. The singer is inviting his partner to come over and spend the night with him, and is expressing his hope that she will accept his invitation and share in the intimacy and romance he has planned for them.

In terms of themes, the song explores the idea of romance and the desire for physical intimacy. The singer is setting the stage for a romantic encounter and is making it clear that he wants to spend the night with his partner.

As for a review of the song, "Leave The Door Open" is a catchy and well-written love song that is likely to appeal to many people.
