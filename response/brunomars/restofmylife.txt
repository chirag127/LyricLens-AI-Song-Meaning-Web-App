"Rest of My Life" by Bruno Mars is a love song in which the narrator describes the happiness he feels in his relationship with his partner. In the first verse, he wakes up next to an angel and reflects on how other people doubted their relationship, but they have proven them wrong by staying together. In the second verse, he remembers the moment they met and how they have grown together over time. In the chorus, he stands before his partner and promises to love her for the rest of his life.

Overall, the song is a celebration of the narrator's love for his partner and the joy they bring to each other's lives. It is a sweet and romantic song that is likely to resonate with listeners who are in love or have experienced the magic of finding the right person to share their lives with.
