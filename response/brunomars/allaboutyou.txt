The song "All About You" by Bruno Mars is a romantic ballad where the singer expresses his feelings for a special someone. In the first verse, he admits that he has been hiding his feelings and that he wants her to know how he feels. The chorus repeats the phrase "it's always been you" and "I'm all about you," indicating that he has been thinking about her for a long time and that she is the center of his attention.

In the second verse, Mars expresses his fear of being rejected, but he decides to take the risk and tell her how he feels. He also mentions changing his ways and wearing his heart on his sleeve, indicating a willingness to be vulnerable for her.

The bridge includes the lyrics "Girl when you smile / Not a place I'd rather be / Just stay for a while / Then you'll see you should be right here with me" where Mars expresses his desire for her to be with him and to see the potential in their relationship. He also sings "I love everything you do / That's why I have to take this time baby to tell ya" where he shows his admiration and appreciation for her.

Overall, the song is a heartfelt and emotional declaration of love and admiration for someone special.
