"Marry You" by Bruno Mars is a fun and upbeat love song about finding that special someone and wanting to get married on the spot. The song starts off with the singer and their love interest out on a beautiful night, looking for something "dumb" to do. The singer then expresses their sudden desire to marry the person they are with, citing the "look in your eyes" or the "dancing juice" as the reason.

The next verse describes the singer's plan to take their love interest to a chapel on the boulevard, where they will exchange vows without anyone else knowing. The singer then encourages their love interest to go along with the plan, reassuring them that it doesn't matter if they are "trashed" or have a pocket full of cash to blow.

The chorus repeats the refrain of wanting to marry the person they are with, and encourages them to say "yes" and go along with the plan. The singer then assures their love interest that if they want to break up in the morning, that's okay, as it was all just for fun.

The song ends with the singer once again urging their love interest to marry them, telling them to "just say 'I do'". Overall, "Marry You" is a playful and lighthearted song about spontaneous love and the desire to get married on the spot.
