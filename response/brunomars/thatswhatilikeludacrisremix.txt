"That's What I Like (Ludacris Remix)" is a song by Bruno Mars featuring Ludacris and PARTYNEXTDOOR. The song is a celebration of luxury and good times, with the lyrics describing various indulgences such as gold jewelry, champagne, and silk sheets. The Ludacris and PARTYNEXTDOOR verses add to this theme, with Ludacris rapping about his wealth and success, and PARTYNEXTDOOR singing about a romantic encounter. Overall, the song is upbeat and catchy, with a smooth, R&B-inflected sound and engaging vocal performances from the artists. It is a fun and enjoyable listen, and fans of Bruno Mars and his style of music are likely to enjoy it.
