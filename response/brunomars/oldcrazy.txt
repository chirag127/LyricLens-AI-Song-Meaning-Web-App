"Old & Crazy" by Bruno Mars is a song about the narrator asking their significant other if they will still love and care for them when they are old and no longer young and vibrant. The lyrics describe the potential physical changes that may come with old age, such as losing teeth, hearing, and physical strength. Despite these changes, the narrator is still hopeful that their partner will still see them as beautiful and call them "baby" as they have in the past.

In the first verse, the narrator asks if their partner will still call them "baby" and treat them the same when they are old and crazy. They also wonder if their partner will still find them attractive when their hair turns grey.

In the second verse, the narrator wonders if their partner will still hold their hand and dance with them, even if they are no longer fashionable and their body is not as strong as it used to be. They also mention that their hearing and ability to find the beat may not be as good as it once was.

In the third verse, the narrator repeats the chorus, asking again if their partner will still call them "baby" and love them when they are old and crazy.

Overall, the song is a heartfelt and emotional expression of love and devotion, even in the face of the inevitable changes that come with old age.
