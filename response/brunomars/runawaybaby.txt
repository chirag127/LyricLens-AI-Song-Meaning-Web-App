"Runaway Baby" is a song by Bruno Mars, in which he sings about his reputation as a womanizer and advises potential partners to avoid getting involved with him. In the first verse, he introduces himself as a "wolf in sheep's clothing," warning a potential partner that he will only be using her for a brief encounter. In the chorus, he tells her to "run away, baby" before he can put his "spell" on her, as he is a "rolling stone" who will never settle down.

In the second verse, Mars compares himself to a rabbit hunter, with many eager "bunnies" trying to win his affections. He admits that he enjoys the attention, but warns that there is only one "carrot" to be shared among all of the women vying for his attention. In the bridge, he sings that he does not want to hurt anyone, but rather just wants to "work" them for his own pleasure.

Overall, "Runaway Baby" is a catchy, upbeat song with a playful tone. The lyrics are clever and engaging, and the chorus is particularly memorable. The song showcases Mars' talent as a performer and songwriter. However, some may find the lyrics to be shallow and superficial, as they portray Mars as a player who is only interested in casual relationships.
