"Straight Up & Down" is a song by Bruno Mars. The song is about a man who is attracted to a woman at a party and compliments her looks. The lyrics describe the man watching the woman dance and how he wants to take her home with him. The song also mentions the two of them being drunk and suggests that they will have a fun time together.

In the first verse, the man tells the woman that her mother must have named her "good lookin'" because she looks good to him. He also compliments her dancing and says that she is the finest thing he has ever seen. The chorus repeats the phrase "straight up and down" and mentions that the man knows what is on the woman's mind.

In the second verse, the man and the woman are both drunk and he suggests that they celebrate her "booty" all night long. The chorus repeats once again.

In the final verse, the man tells the woman to let him know when she is ready to go home with him. He also says that they should not fight their feelings for each other and that they will have a good time together. The chorus repeats once again to end the song.

Overall, "Straight Up & Down" is a typical pop song about attraction and partying. The lyrics are straightforward and repetitive, focusing mainly on the man's attraction to the woman and the potential for a romantic encounter.
