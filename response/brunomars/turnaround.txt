"Turn Around" by Bruno Mars is a song about a couple who have a tumultuous relationship. In the song, the narrator expresses frustration with their partner's behavior, but ultimately decides to stay in the relationship because of their love for them.

In the first verse, the narrator describes how their partner often picks fights and calls them out of their name. Despite this behavior, the narrator admits that they do the same and suggests that this is not normal for lovers.

In the chorus, the narrator says that every time they try to leave their partner, something tells them to turn around. They recognize that their partner holds them down and they know they can work out their issues by talking and sticking it out.

In the second verse, the narrator asks their partner to wait before saying something they will regret. They acknowledge that their partner drives them crazy, but they love them anyway and ask for appreciation in return.

In the final chorus, the narrator repeats that they always turn around for their partner because no one can love them like they do. They acknowledge that their relationship has its challenges, but they are willing to work through them for the sake of their love.

Overall, "Turn Around" is a song about a couple who have a difficult relationship but ultimately decide to stay together because of their love for each other. Despite their disagreements and arguments, they are committed to working through their issues and finding a way to make their relationship work.
