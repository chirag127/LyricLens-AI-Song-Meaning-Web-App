In the song "Again" by Bruno Mars, the singer reflects on a difficult day where everything seemed to go wrong. He initially feels discouraged and thinks about staying in bed, but eventually catches a train where he meets someone who changes his perspective.

In the first verse, Mars describes feeling frustrated and overwhelmed with his day. He puts his hands over his head, indicating a feeling of helplessness, and wonders what else could go wrong. However, he eventually catches a train and meets someone who makes his day better.

The chorus repeats the phrase "for you I'd do it all over again," indicating that meeting this person was worth all the difficulties he experienced earlier in the day. He also sings about how the difficult experiences led him to this person, and he wouldn't change anything about the day.

In the second verse, Mars reflects on how unlikely it seemed that his day could turn out well, but he is now grateful for the unexpected outcome. He repeats that he would do it all over again for this person and that the struggles led him to them.

Overall, the song is a reflective and romantic ballad about the power of chance encounters and how they can change our perspectives.
