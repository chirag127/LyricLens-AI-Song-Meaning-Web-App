In the song "Runaway" by Bruno Mars, the singer expresses his desire to reconnect with his lover after a period of being busy and not spending as much time together. He acknowledges that their love may have been forgotten and offers to take a day to make things right. In the chorus, he asks his partner to take his hand and fall in love with him again, and suggests that they runaway to the place where they first found love. He emphasizes that they don't need anyone else around when they are together and that a day is all it takes to change their current situation.

In the second verse, the singer admits that he has been neglecting his partner and promises to make it up to them. He declares his love and determination to make things work, and asks for one more chance to remind his partner of the love they share. He repeats the chorus, expressing his hope that they can spend a day together to rekindle their relationship.

Overall, the song is a heartfelt plea for a second chance at love and a reminder of the importance of spending quality time together.
