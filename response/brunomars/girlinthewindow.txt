"Girl In The Window" by Bruno Mars is a melancholic ballad about a person who feels neglected and abandoned by their romantic partner. The song's lyrics describe the narrator's feelings of isolation and frustration as they wait for their partner to return and give them the attention and affection they crave.

In the first verse, the narrator watches other couples from their bedroom window and feels envious of their happiness and connection. They express disappointment that their own relationship has become distant and unfulfilling. The chorus repeats the phrase "how do I always end up being the girl at the window," expressing the narrator's frustration at being left alone and ignored by their partner.

The second verse describes the narrator's attempts to understand and support their partner's busy lifestyle, but also expresses their fear that their relationship is drifting apart. They express their love and belief that their partner will eventually come around, but also express doubt and insecurity about their future together.

The final verse pleads with the partner to prioritize the relationship and not take the narrator's love and loyalty for granted. The song ends with the repetition of the chorus, emphasizing the narrator's feelings of isolation and longing for their partner's return.

Overall, "Girl In The Window" is a poignant and emotional song that captures the pain and uncertainty of a relationship in crisis. The lyrics express the narrator's feelings of abandonment and longing for their partner's attention, as well as their hope and faith that their relationship can be salvaged.
