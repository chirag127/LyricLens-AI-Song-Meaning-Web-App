"Watching Her Move" is a song by Bruno Mars that describes a woman who is the center of attention in a club. The singer is captivated by her beauty and the way she moves, and expresses his admiration for her.

In the first verse, the singer describes the woman as she enters the club with retro clothing and bright lips. He notes her porcelain skin and the way her hair falls down her back, with a tattoo accentuated by her movements. The second verse describes her legs as those of a supermodel and her choice of high heel shoes.

The chorus repeats the line "She got every single guy in the club just watching her move" and emphasizes that she doesn't want to dance with anyone else, allowing her to do her own thing. The third verse describes the singer pulling the woman to the side and asking her to leave the club with him, promising to dance with her and show her a good time.

The song concludes with the chorus repeating the line "Just watch her get it" and encourages the listener to watch the woman move. The singer expresses admiration for her beauty and sexiness.

Overall, the song is a celebration of a confident and independent woman who commands attention in a club setting. The singer is captivated by her beauty and the way she moves, and expresses his admiration for her.
