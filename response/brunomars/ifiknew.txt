"If I Knew" is a song by Bruno Mars about regretting the mistakes and wrong choices that one has made in their past. In the song, the narrator reflects on the fact that if they had known that the person they love would come into their life, they would not have done all the things that they have done. The narrator wishes they could go back to a time when they were younger and more innocent, so that they could give that innocence to their partner. The song is a powerful expression of regret and longing for a second chance.
