"Moonshine" is a song by Bruno Mars. The lyrics of the song describe a person's desire to escape the ordinary world and be transported to a special place with their partner. The song uses the metaphor of moonshine, a type of illegal alcohol, to represent the excitement and intensity of the relationship.

In the first verse, the singer describes how their partner looks even better than they did the night before and how their kiss made the singer feel wonderful. The second verse describes the singer's desire to be with their partner again and how being with them makes everything feel alright.

The chorus of the song repeats the line "Moonshine, take us to the stars tonight / Take us to that special place / That place we went the last time, the last time." This line captures the idea of wanting to be transported to a special place with their partner and relive the excitement of previous times together.

In the third verse, the singer encourages their partner not to look down or back and to embrace the idea of living fast and dying young. The final chorus repeats the idea of being transported to a special place with their partner and the idea of being brought to life by their love.

Overall, "Moonshine" is a romantic and exciting song that uses vivid imagery to convey the intensity of the singer's relationship with their partner.
