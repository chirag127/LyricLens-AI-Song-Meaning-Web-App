"Count On Me" is a song by Bruno Mars that is about the bond of friendship and the promise of being there for each other in times of need. The lyrics describe various scenarios where one friend may need help, and the singer assures them that they will be there to support them, just as they know the friend will be there for them in return. The chorus repeats the phrase "you can count on me like 1, 2, 3" and "I can count on you like 4, 3, 2" as a way of emphasizing the reliability and dependability of the friendship. Overall, the song is a celebration of the power of friendship and the importance of being there for each other.
