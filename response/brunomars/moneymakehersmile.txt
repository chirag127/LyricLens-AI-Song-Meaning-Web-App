"Money Make Her Smile" by Bruno Mars is a song about a girl who is new in town and is only interested in money. The lyrics describe her as a "super freak" who only goes to the church where "dollars fall" and is not interested in other things. The chorus repeats the line "money money money make her smile" to emphasize her love of wealth.


The song begins by introducing the girl, who is new in town and goes by the name where "your stacks at," implying that she is interested in people's wealth. The next verse describes her as a "super freak" who only goes to the church where "dollars fall," indicating that she is not interested in religious or spiritual matters, but only in money. The chorus repeats the line "money money money make her smile" to reinforce the idea that she is only interested in wealth.

Overall, the song is a critique of materialism and the shallow pursuit of wealth. The chorus repeats the line "it's not complicated" to suggest that the girl's motivations are simple and unnuanced. The song may be interpreted as a commentary on the corrupting influence of money and the dangers of focusing solely on wealth.
