"That's What I Like" is a song by Bruno Mars that is about indulging in luxury and lavish experiences with a romantic partner. The song begins with Mars offering his partner an invitation to his Manhattan condo, followed by a trip to Miami and a dinner of lobster tail. He then offers to take her shopping in Paris and buy her anything she wants, emphasizing the luxury and extravagance of the experiences he is offering. The chorus repeats the phrase "that's what I like" in reference to the lavish lifestyle that Mars is describing. The song ends with Mars inviting his partner to spend time with him and enjoy all that he has to offer.
