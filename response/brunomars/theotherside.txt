"The Other Side" is a song by Bruno Mars. In the first verse, the singer describes himself as "complicated" and the person he is singing to as "straight as they come." He suggests that it is better if the other person does not understand him because they will not know what it is like until they try. In the chorus, he repeats that he will be waiting on the other side and that all the other person has to do is cross the line. He says that he could wait a lifetime, but it is up to the other person to decide.

The second verse continues the theme of not understanding and suggests that if they did, they would "run and hide." The singer also talks about the price they pay for living on the other side.

In the third and final verse, the singer repeats the line "it's better if you don't understand" multiple times, emphasizing the idea that it is better for the other person not to understand him and his life on the other side.

Overall, the song seems to be about a person who is different from others and is inviting the person they are singing to to join them on the other side, where they can live without consequences and without being judged. However, the singer also seems to be warning the other person that it is not an easy or safe place to be.
