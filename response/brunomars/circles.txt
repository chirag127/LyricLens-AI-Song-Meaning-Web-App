"Circles" by Brunomars is a song about being in a confusing and frustrating relationship. The narrator expresses their frustration with their partner who is not being consistent or clear about their feelings and intentions. The repetition of the chorus "got me going in circles" reflects the confusion and frustration the narrator is feeling.

In the first verse, the narrator describes how their partner has been making them feel and how it is affecting their relationship. They say that their partner has been giving them mixed signals and they are unsure of what to do.

The chorus repeats the line "got me going in circles" and introduces the idea that the narrator's heart is "dizzy" from the confusion.

In the second verse, the narrator describes the specific actions of their partner that are causing them confusion and frustration. They say that their partner says they need them, but then pushes them away and that they don't know if they should stay in the relationship or leave.

The chorus repeats again, emphasizing the theme of confusion and frustration.

Overall, "Circles" is a song about being in a difficult and confusing relationship and the frustration that comes with it. The chorus and repetition of the phrase "got me going in circles" effectively captures the feeling of being stuck in a confusing and frustrating situation.
