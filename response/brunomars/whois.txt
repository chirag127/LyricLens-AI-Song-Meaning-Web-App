In "Who Is" by Bruno Mars, the narrator reflects on a past relationship in which he felt he had to be perfect to satisfy his partner. He describes how he tried to reach her impossible standards but ultimately failed, and wonders who is truly perfect. In the final verse, the narrator realizes that his partner's unrealistic expectations were not worth his future and decides to move on.

The song is a commentary on the impossible standards that people often set in relationships and how they can lead to unhappiness and unfulfilled expectations. The narrator's self-aware realization that he doesn't need to be perfect to be worthy of love is a relatable and empowering message. Overall, "Who Is" is a well-written and thought-provoking song.
