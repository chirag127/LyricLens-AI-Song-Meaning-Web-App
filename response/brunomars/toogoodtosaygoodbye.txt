"Too Good To Say Goodbye" is a song by Bruno Mars in which he reflects on mistakes he has made in a relationship and pleads with his partner to give him another chance. In the first verse, he admits to not treating his partner as well as he should have and regrets letting her go. He asks why they can't try to start over and points out that they were once best friends as well as lovers.

In the chorus, Mars emphasizes the unique bond he has with his partner and how no one else could love him the way she does. He asks what he can do to make things right and begs her not to give up on their relationship because it's "too good to say goodbye."

The second verse is similar to the first, with Mars expressing his continued love for his partner and asking her to give their relationship another chance. He also speaks to the pain of both of them being brokenhearted and hopes it's not too late to fix things.

The song ends with a repetition of the chorus, with Mars declaring that their love is "too good to say goodbye" and asking his partner to take his hand and fight for a better future together. Overall, the song is a heartfelt plea for forgiveness and a second chance in a relationship.
