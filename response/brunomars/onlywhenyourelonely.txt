"Only When You're Lonely" is a song by Bruno Mars that tells the story of a relationship where the other person only seems to want the narrator when they are lonely. The song uses the first-person perspective to express the narrator's frustration and hurt at being used in this way.

In the first verse, the narrator describes the situation they are in, saying that they are once again doing things they said they wouldn't do. It is late at night, and the narrator is rushing out the door to see the other person, who only seems to want to talk to them at this hour. The narrator asks why the other person does this to them all the time.

The chorus repeats the main message of the song, that the other person only wants the narrator when they are lonely. The narrator states that they now see the truth and that they won't be there for the other person anymore. They say that they won't take late night calls or make themselves available in the same way anymore.

In the second verse, the narrator reflects on how they were fooled by the other person from the start, and how they quickly gave their heart to them because they loved them. However, they now realize that they don't need the other person anymore and that they are stronger without them. The narrator tells the other person to get off their knees and to stop using empty words to try and win them back.

The third and final verse of the song continues to explore the theme of the other person only wanting the narrator when they are lonely. The narrator describes how nothing ever seems to change in the relationship, and how the other person never thinks about their needs. They also mention how the other person strings them along when they are with their friends, but that they won't allow this behavior anymore and are moving on.

Overall, "Only When You're Lonely" is a powerful and relatable song that speaks to the pain and frustration of being used in a relationship. The lyrics and melody perfectly capture the emotions of the narrator, and the song is a strong statement of self-empowerment and independence.
