The song "Silk Sonic Intro" is the opening track for the band Silk Sonic's debut album. The lyrics introduce the band's members, Bruno Mars and Anderson .Paak, and invite the audience to get up and dance. In the first verse, Mars and .Paak declare that they are the ones the audience came to see and that they will lock the groove in tight. In the second verse, they give a shoutout to legendary funk musician Bootsy Collins and invite the ladies to come on stage.

Overall, the song is a fun and energetic introduction to Silk Sonic's music and sets the tone for the rest of the album. The lyrics are simple but effective in getting the audience pumped up and ready to dance.
