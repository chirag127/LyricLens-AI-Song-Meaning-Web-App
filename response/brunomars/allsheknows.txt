The song "All She Knows" by Bruno Mars is a emotional ballad about a person who has never experienced true love and is stuck in a painful and unfulfilling relationship. In the first verse, the singer is on the outside looking in and can see how unhappy the person is, but feels helpless to do anything about it.

The chorus repeats the phrase "his love is all she knows" indicating that the person has never known anything different and is unable to imagine a better life. The singer also expresses a desire to help and heal her, but is unsure if it would be selfish to intervene.

In the second verse, the singer reflects on how long the person has been living this way and how they don't know the difference between love and pain. The singer also reveals that they want to be more than just friends, but the person is unable to see it.

Overall, the song is a melancholy and emotional ballad about the pain of unrequited love and the desire to help someone who is stuck in a bad situation.
