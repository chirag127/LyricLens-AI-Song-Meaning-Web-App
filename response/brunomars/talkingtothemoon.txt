"Talking to the Moon" is a song by Bruno Mars that explores the feeling of longing for someone who is no longer with you. The narrator is sitting alone at night, looking up at the stars and talking to the moon, hoping that the person they miss is also on the other side, listening and responding. The chorus repeats the line "Talking to the moon / Tryna get to you / In hopes you're on the other side talking to me, too / Or am I a fool who sits alone talking to the moon?" expressing the narrator's desperation to connect with the person they miss. The song's melancholic yet hopeful tone captures the feeling of longing and the desire to believe that there is still a way to communicate with the person who is gone.
