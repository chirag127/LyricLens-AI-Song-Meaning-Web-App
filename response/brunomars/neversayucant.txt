"Never Say U Can't" by Brunomars is a tribute to the singer's father, who passed away. The song reflects on the influence and guidance the singer's father provided during his life. The lyrics describe how the father taught the singer everything he knows and always provided words of encouragement when the singer doubted himself. The chorus repeats the advice the father gave the singer: "never say you can't." In the bridge, the singer expresses gratitude for his father's influence and hopes to pass on his father's lessons to his own son. Overall, the song is a heartfelt tribute to the singer's father and the impact he had on his life.
