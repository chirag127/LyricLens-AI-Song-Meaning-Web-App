The song "Calling All My Lovelies" by Bruno Mars is a playful and confident anthem about having many options and not being tied down to one person. In the first verse, Mars sings about how he has too many girls waiting for him and that the person he is talking to is acting bold and mean. He warns them not to let the phone ring too long or he will move on.

The chorus repeats the phrase "calling all my lovelies" and references the person not thinking about him, indicating that he is moving on and calling other people for attention and affection.

In the second verse, Mars sings about how he is too fly to be on standby and waiting for the person to call him back. He also accuses them of playing childish games and challenges them to hit him back and show him some respect.

The bridge includes the lyrics "There's a pain in my chest I cannot explain / I told myself I wasn't gon' cry / Then somebody's gotta come dry these eyes" where Mars reveals that he is feeling hurt and emotional, but he is trying to stay strong and not cry. He then leaves a voicemail for Halle Berry, indicating that he is moving on and looking for other options.

Overall, the song is a lighthearted and energetic celebration of independence and having many options in love.
