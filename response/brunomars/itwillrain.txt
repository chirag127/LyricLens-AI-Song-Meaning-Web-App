The song "It Will Rain" by Bruno Mars is a heartbreak ballad about the singer's fear of losing his significant other. In the first verse, he imagines the pain he would feel if they were to leave him and compares it to the need for strong medication. The chorus repeats the line "there'll be no sunlight / If I lose you, baby / There'll be no clear skies / If I lose you, baby" to emphasize the depth of his love and the devastation he would feel without her.

In the second verse, the singer acknowledges that his significant other's parents may not approve of their relationship. He offers to try to change their minds, but ultimately says that he will do anything to keep her by his side. The bridge features the lines "Don't you say goodbye / I'll pick up these broken pieces 'til I'm bleeding / If that'll make it right" which show the lengths the singer is willing to go to in order to hold onto the relationship.

Overall, the song's central theme is the fear of losing someone you love and the lengths one would go to in order to keep them in their life. The lyrics are emotional and heartfelt, and the song's melody is haunting and beautiful. Overall, "It Will Rain" is a powerful and relatable ballad that many people who have experienced heartbreak can relate to.
