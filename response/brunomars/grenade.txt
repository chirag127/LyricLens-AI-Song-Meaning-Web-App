"Grenade" is a song by Bruno Mars that is about a person who is willing to do anything for their partner, even if it means sacrificing their own life. In the song, the narrator describes how they gave their partner everything they had, but their partner took it for granted and never showed them the same level of love and devotion. Despite this, the narrator says they would still do anything for their partner, even if it meant sacrificing themselves. The song is a powerful expression of love and devotion, even in the face of mistreatment.
