The song "Long Distance" by Bruno Mars is a heartfelt ballad about the struggles of being in a long distance relationship. The lyrics express the singer's longing to be with their partner and the difficulties they face while they are apart. The chorus repeats the line "this long distance is killing me" as a way of emphasizing the emotional toll that the separation takes on the singer.

In the first verse, the singer talks about how they are trying to pass the time and keep themselves occupied, but they are running out of things to do to get their partner off their mind. They have a picture of their partner in a frame that they hold close to see their face everyday. The second verse shifts to the singer's perspective of being away from their partner, with the minutes feeling like hours and the hours feeling like days. They express their desire to come home soon to be with their partner again.

The song continues with the singer expressing their longing for their partner and their frustration at being stuck in a long distance relationship. They ask if their partner can hear them crying, and repeat the chorus about the long distance killing them. The song ends with the singer singing the same opening lines about running out of songs to sing to pass the time.

Overall, the song is a powerful and emotional expression of the challenges and heartache of being in a long distance relationship. The lyrics are relatable and the melody is catchy, making it a standout track on the album.
