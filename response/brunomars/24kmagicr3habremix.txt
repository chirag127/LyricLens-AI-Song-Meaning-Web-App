The song "24K Magic (R3hab Remix)" by Bruno Mars is a upbeat, party anthem. In the first verse, Mars boasts about his flashy lifestyle, wearing designer clothes and expensive jewelry. He warns others not to look at him too closely because they might hurt themselves with jealousy. He also references being a "dangerous man with some money in my pocket," indicating that he is successful and confident.

The chorus repeats the phrase "24 karat magic in the air" and encourages listeners to put their pinky rings up to the moon, possibly suggesting a show of wealth or luxury.

In the second verse, Mars addresses "hustlers, gangsters, and bad bitches" and encourages them to have a good time and spend their money freely. He also references being "too fresh" and attributes it to being blessed by Jesus.

Throughout the song, Mars encourages his listeners to keep up with him and not be mad or jealous of his success. He also repeatedly tells them to "fix ya face," possibly suggesting that they shouldn't let their negative emotions show.

Overall, the song is a energetic and confident celebration of success and luxury.
