In the song "Natalie" by Bruno Mars, the narrator expresses his frustration and anger towards a woman named Natalie who has betrayed him and stolen his money. In the first verse, the narrator explains that he has never been in this situation before and never wants to be again. He describes making a mistake and falling for Natalie, but only realizing too late that she was using him for his money.

In the chorus, the narrator refers to Natalie as a "gold-digging bitch" and warns others to watch out for her. He also threatens to seek revenge and make her pay for what she has done to him.

The second verse continues with the narrator's anger towards Natalie, and he makes threats to hurt her and put her in jail. He also reflects on how he should have known better than to trust her, as she never gave him any indication that their relationship would last.

Overall, the song portrays the narrator as a victim of Natalie's deceit and betrayal. The lyrics are full of anger and resentment towards her, and the chorus serves as a warning to others to be cautious of her.
