"Girl I Wait" is a love song by Bruno Mars in which the narrator expresses his deep love for a girl and his willingness to wait for her for as long as it takes. In the first verse, the narrator describes the feeling of the girl's heart beating fast and the desire to make the moment last. He acknowledges that the day of separation will come, but he wishes they could both just run away together. In the chorus, the narrator tells the girl that he will wait for her, no matter how long it takes. He says that she is the only thing worth holding on to, and that he can't live without her.

In the second verse, the narrator describes the difficulties of being separated from the girl, including limited communication and the feeling of being lost without her. He acknowledges that time seems to be moving too fast, but he remains determined to wait for her. The chorus repeats, with the narrator expressing his unwavering love and commitment to waiting for the girl to come back to him.

Overall, "Girl I Wait" is a heartfelt and romantic ballad that captures the depth of the narrator's love for the girl and his willingness to wait for her no matter what. The song's emotional lyrics and catchy melody make it a standout track on Bruno Mars' album.
