"Like Tonight" by Bruno Mars is a song in which the singer expresses his desire for a romantic and intimate encounter with his partner. In the chorus, the singer wonders what Heaven feels like and hopes that it feels like the current moment he is experiencing with his partner. The overall message of the song is one of desire and passion. The singer is expressing his hope that the moment they are experiencing will last forever, and is making it clear that he wants to spend the night with his partner.

In terms of themes, the song explores the idea of physical attraction and the intensity of romantic love. The singer is describing the ways in which his love for his partner is overwhelming and all-consuming, and is expressing his desire to experience this feeling for as long as possible.

As for a review of the song, "Like Tonight" is a catchy and well-written love song that is likely to appeal to many people. Overall, it is a well-crafted and enjoyable song.
