"Finesse" by Bruno Mars is a catchy, upbeat song that celebrates the strong connection and chemistry between two people. The song's lyrics describe how the presence of the singer's romantic partner transforms the atmosphere in a room and captures the attention of others. The singer attributes this effect to their confidence and the partner's physical attractiveness.

In the first verse, the singer describes how they and their partner look good together and command attention from others. They suggest that their confidence and the partner's measurements are the reasons for this effect. The chorus repeats the phrase "we out here drippin' in finesse," which could be interpreted as a boast about their smooth, confident style and effortless charm as a couple.

The second verse slows down the pace and describes a romantic, intimate moment between the singer and their partner on the dance floor. The singer notes the strong, magnetic connection between them and expresses confidence that nothing can stop them from enjoying the moment.

The final verse encourages others to find and appreciate their own perfect partners, with the singer declaring "we got it goin' on" as a couple. The song ends with the repetition of the chorus, emphasizing the couple's "finesse" and their feeling of joy and contentment in their relationship.

Overall, the song celebrates the special connection between two people and the confidence and charm that they exude as a couple. It encourages listeners to find and appreciate their own perfect partners and enjoy the feeling of being in a strong, loving relationship.
