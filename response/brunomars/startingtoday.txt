In "Starting Today" by Bruno Mars, the singer expresses remorse for standing up a significant other and breaking their trust. He apologizes for his actions and promises to change his ways, stating that he doesn't want to be like every other man that the person has had in the past. The song is a declaration of commitment to doing better in the relationship and making amends.

In the first verse, the singer admits that he stood the person up and didn't answer their calls. He apologizes for forgetting about their plans and ruining their night on multiple occasions. In the chorus, he says that he knows he broke their heart and that it's hard for them to trust him, but he promises to change his ways starting today.

In the second verse, the singer says that he's tired of playing the "kiss and make-up" game and apologizes for the person he became. He wishes he could go back and do things differently, helping his best friend and throwing away his selfish ways. He reiterates his commitment to changing his ways and not being like other men the person has had in their life.

Overall, the song is a heartfelt apology and declaration of change from the singer. It seems like the singer is genuinely sorry for their past actions and wants to make things right in the relationship.
