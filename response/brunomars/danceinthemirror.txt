"Dance In The Mirror" is a song by Bruno Mars that describes a man watching his partner dance in front of the mirror. In the first verse, the singer describes how she wakes up in the morning and dances while getting ready, showing off her skills to her reflection. In the second verse, the singer describes how he loves watching her dance and how it makes him want to cancel their plans and just watch her dance instead. The chorus repeats the line "my favorite part of the day is when she dance in the mirror" and describes how cute and tempting she looks as she dances. The song overall is a celebration of the beauty and joy of dancing and the attraction of watching someone dance.
