"When I Was Your Man" by Bruno Mars is a heartfelt ballad in which the narrator reflects on his past relationship and the mistakes he made. In the first verse, the narrator describes how their old bed feels bigger now that they are no longer together, and how their song on the radio no longer sounds the same. The chorus reveals that the narrator regrets not doing more to make their relationship work, such as buying the partner flowers and holding their hand. In the second verse, the narrator admits that their pride, ego, and selfishness caused the relationship to end, and that they will never be able to make things right. In the final chorus, the narrator expresses a hope that their ex's new partner will do all the things that the narrator should have done when they were together.

Overall, the song is a poignant reminder of the importance of appreciating and caring for our loved ones, and the consequences of taking them for granted. The lyrics are relatable and emotional, and Bruno Mars' vocals are soulful and expressive, making for a powerful and moving listening experience.
