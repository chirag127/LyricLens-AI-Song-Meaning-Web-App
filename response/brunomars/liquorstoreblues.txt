"Liquor Store Blues" is a song by Bruno Mars and Damian Marley. In the song, Mars and Marley sing about their struggles with unemployment and their coping mechanisms, which include drinking alcohol and smoking marijuana at the local liquor store. The song has a laid-back, reggae-inspired beat and catchy melodies, and the lyrics provide a glimpse into the lives of the two artists as they try to make ends meet and find solace in the face of hardship.

In the first verse, Mars sings about standing at the liquor store, feeling like he runs the whole block despite his struggles with unemployment. He talks about buying lotto tickets and cheap beer, trying to "scratch his way up to the top" despite the odds being against him. He then repeats the chorus, "this is me and my liquor store blues," expressing his frustration with his situation and his desire to escape it through alcohol and marijuana.

In the second verse, Marley joins in, singing about flying high on marijuana and feeling like he runs the whole block. He talks about the struggles of the "ghetto youth" and the need to escape their circumstances through substance abuse. Like Mars, he repeats the chorus, expressing his own frustration with his situation and his reliance on alcohol and marijuana to cope.

Overall, "Liquor Store Blues" is a poignant and relatable song that captures the struggles of unemployment and the coping mechanisms that people often turn to in order to escape their pain. The lyrics are well-written and the melody is catchy, making for a enjoyable listening experience.
