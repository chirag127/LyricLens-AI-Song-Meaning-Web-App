"Top Of The World" by Bruno Mars is a love song in which the singer expresses his strong feelings for a special someone. In the first verse, the singer describes how he feels "higher than the ceiling" and "floating on this feelin'" when he's with this person. He also mentions that his heart is "for sale" and that the person has bought it. In the chorus, the singer declares that he will give the person "the world" and put it in their hands. He also mentions that he has never felt this way before and that they will stand "on top of the world" together.

In the second verse, the singer compares the person to a flower and says that being with them is a blessing. He also assures the person that he won't take them for granted and that they are a "dream come true." In the final chorus, the singer repeats his promise to give the person the world and to never let them go. He also invites them to take his hand and "float to the sky" together.

Overall, "Top Of The World" is a sweet and romantic love song that captures the feeling of being completely in love with someone. The catchy chorus and upbeat production make it a fun and enjoyable listen.
