"Finesse (Remix)" by Bruno Mars is a remix of the original song "Finesse" that features a guest verse from rapper Cardi B. The song maintains the same upbeat, celebratory tone of the original, but with added elements of braggadocio and flexing from both Mars and Cardi B.

In her verse, Cardi B describes her own wealth and success, mentioning her fancy cars and jewelry. She boasts about her Bronx roots and how she's changed the game with her unique style. She also references Bruno Mars' singing to her and her signature "money dance" move.

The remainder of the song follows the structure and lyrics of the original "Finesse," with Mars and Cardi B singing the chorus together and the second verse being a slowed-down, intimate moment between the singer and their partner. The final verse encourages others to find and appreciate their own perfect partners, with Mars and Cardi B declaring "we got it goin' on" as a couple.

Overall, the remix of "Finesse" adds a layer of bragging and self-promotion to the original song's celebration of a strong, confident relationship. Both Mars and Cardi B use the song to flex their own success and charm, but the core message of appreciating and cherishing a special connection with a partner remains the same.
