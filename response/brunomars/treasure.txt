"Treasure" is a song by Bruno Mars, in which he sings about a girl who is not aware of her own beauty and worth. The song is a declaration of love and admiration for this girl, who the singer sees as a "treasure" and a "golden star."

In the first verse, the singer addresses the girl directly, telling her that she is "a sexy motherfucker" and urging her to give him her attention. He then compliments her appearance and tells her that she is "wonderful" and "flawless." However, he also notes that she doesn't seem to recognize her own worth, and walks around as if she wants to be someone else.

The chorus repeats the refrain, "Treasure, that is what you are / Honey, you're my golden star / You know you can make my wish come true / If you let me treasure you." The singer is expressing his desire to treasure and cherish this girl, and to show her how much she means to him.

In the second verse, the singer repeats the compliments from the first verse, telling the girl that she is "pretty" and that she should be smiling. He also tells her that she is everything he sees in his dreams, and assures her that he wouldn't say these things if they weren't true.

The song concludes with a repetition of the chorus, and the singer repeating the phrase, "You are my treasure" several times. Overall, "Treasure" is a love song that celebrates the beauty and worth of a special girl, and expresses the singer's desire to cherish and treasure her.
