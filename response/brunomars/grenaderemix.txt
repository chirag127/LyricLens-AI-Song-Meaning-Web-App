The "Grenade (Remix)" is a version of the song "Grenade" by Bruno Mars that features additional lyrics by Lil Wayne. In this version, the narrator's love for their partner is described as being so strong that they would be willing to die for them. The narrator also compares their love to a "hell's angel" and says that for their partner, they would "rather sin." Lil Wayne's lyrics add to this theme by describing how he would "jump off Noah's Ark" for his partner, and how he would "die smiling" for them. Overall, the song is a powerful expression of love and devotion, even in the face of mistreatment.
