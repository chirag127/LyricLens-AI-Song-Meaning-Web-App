In "Who Are You" by Bruno Mars, the narrator reflects on the changes in their relationship with their partner. The narrator describes how their partner used to show them love and affection, but now they are distant and no longer the same person they fell in love with. The chorus repeats the line "it's like I don't even know you anymore" as a way of expressing the narrator's confusion and hurt over the changes in their partner's behavior.

The first verse describes the narrator's partner's previous gestures of love, such as buying flowers and surprising the narrator with dinner. The chorus then highlights the changes in the relationship, with the partner behaving differently and not showing the same level of affection.

The second verse continues the theme of the partner's changed behavior, with the narrator mentioning that their partner avoids eye contact and is not as present in the relationship as they used to be. The chorus repeats, emphasizing the narrator's frustration and confusion over the changes in their partner.

In the final verse, the narrator directly addresses their partner and asks what they want from the relationship. The chorus repeats once more, with the line "What happened to the man that you were before?" serving as a poignant reminder of the person the narrator used to know and love.

Overall, "Who Are You" is a powerful and emotional song about the difficulties and challenges that can arise in a relationship. The lyrics are relatable and capture the confusion and hurt that can come from feeling like one's partner has changed in fundamental ways.
