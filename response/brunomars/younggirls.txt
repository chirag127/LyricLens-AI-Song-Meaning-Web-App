In the song "Young Girls" by Bruno Mars, the narrator describes spending his money on a fancy car to attract "bright-eyed honies." He is addicted to this lifestyle, despite the fact that it leads him astray and causes him to get lost under the bright lights and lose himself in the words he says. Despite this, he still dreams of a simple life where boy meets girl and they get married. However, he realizes that love doesn't exist in this lifestyle. In the chorus, he sings about how the young wild girls make a mess of him and will eventually be the death of him, but he will always come back to them.

Overall, the song seems to be about the allure of a wild and reckless lifestyle, despite the consequences it brings. The narrator is drawn to the young wild girls, but ultimately realizes that this lifestyle is not sustainable and does not lead to true happiness.
