"Today My Life Begins" by Bruno Mars is a song about starting over and moving forward from past pain and struggles. The lyrics describe a person who has been working hard and struggling for a long time, feeling like pain has been their only friend. However, they recognize that change is coming and they are determined to break free from their chains and start a new life. The chorus repeats the phrase "Today my life begins," emphasizing the idea that the protagonist is ready to leave the past behind and embrace a new beginning.

In the first verse, the lyrics describe the protagonist's past struggles and the pain they have experienced. The line "my fragile heart's been done so wrong" suggests that they have been hurt or betrayed in the past. Despite this, they are determined to move forward and let go of the past.

The second verse speaks to the idea of leaving the past behind and not dwelling on regrets. The line "life's too short to have regrets" suggests that the protagonist has learned from their experiences and is ready to move forward and make the most of their life.

The chorus repeats the idea of breaking free from the past and starting a new life. The line "leave the past behind me" emphasizes the protagonist's determination to move forward and let go of their struggles. The line "a whole new world is waiting" suggests that the protagonist is excited for what the future holds and is ready to take on whatever challenges may come their way.

Overall, "Today My Life Begins" is a positive and uplifting song about overcoming past struggles and embracing a new beginning. It encourages listeners to let go of the past and move forward with hope and determination.
