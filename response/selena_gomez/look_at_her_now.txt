"Look At Her Now" by Selena Gomez is a song about the power of resilience and growing from heartache. In the first verse, Gomez paints a picture of a couple falling in love too quickly, and the relationship crumbling when one of them finds another lover. She reflects on the difficulty of the situation and the trust levels that plummeted. In the pre-chorus, she acknowledges the sadness and grief of the situation, but also notes that it has strengthened her and made her more of a woman. 

The chorus is a celebration of her resilience and growth. She looks back at the situation with a newfound strength, and celebrates her ability to find her way out of a difficult situation. The post-chorus reinforces the message of empowerment and resilience.

The second verse continues the story, describing the recklessness of the time, and her realization that the situation was neither wrong nor right. The pre-chorus and chorus repeat, further emphasizing the message of resilience and acknowledging the emotions that were felt. The bridge adds an additional layer to the message, with Gomez declaring her faith in her own ability to find love, should she choose to.

The song is an anthem of resilience and strength, as Gomez celebrates her ability to rise above heartache and find satisfaction within herself. In the lyrics, she expresses a sense of empowerment and the belief that she will find love if she chooses to. The music is upbeat, with a strong pop influence, and the lyrics are accessible and easy to relate to. 

The song also has a strong emotional impact, as the lyrics convey a range of emotions, from sadness to joy to empowerment. The language used in the song is emotional, and conveys the feelings of heartache and strength that Gomez is trying to communicate. The linguistics of the song reveal a strong emotional content that helps to connect the listener to the song and its message. 

Overall, "Look At Her Now" is an inspiring song that celebrates the power of resilience and empowerment. Through her lyrics, Gomez conveys a powerful message of hope and strength, offering an anthem of self-love and assurance that you can find joy and satisfaction in yourself.
