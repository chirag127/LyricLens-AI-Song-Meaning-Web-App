The song "Back To You" by Selena Gomez is a heartwarming and reflective track about a love that may have been lost, but still lingers. In the song, Gomez reflects on the past and her decision to move on, and wonders if she made the right choice. The lyrics are filled with a sense of longing, regret, and nostalgia, as Gomez struggles to come to terms with her past and make peace with the decisions she made. 

The song follows a steady and mid-tempo beat, which helps to emphasize the emotion behind the lyrics. The verses are filled with imagery and detail, as Gomez paints a vivid picture of her past relationship, and her current struggle to move on. In the pre-chorus and chorus, Gomez reflects on the pain and regret of her past, and how she wishes things could be different. 

The bridge and post-chorus offer a moment of resolution for Gomez, as she acknowledges that she can't go back to her past relationship, but can still feel the love that she shared. The outro of the song acts as a reminder that she will always be connected to her past, in some way. 

Overall, "Back To You" is an emotionally powerful song that speaks to the pain of letting go of a past relationship. The lyrics are thoughtful, detailed, and reflective, and the steady beat helps to emphasize the emotion of the song. Lyrically, the song is filled with imagery and detail, and the bridge and post-chorus offer a resolution and a moment of peace. It is a powerful song that speaks to the pain of letting go, and the lingering love that will always remain.
