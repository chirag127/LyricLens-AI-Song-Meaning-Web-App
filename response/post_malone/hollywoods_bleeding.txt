The song “Hollywood’s Bleeding” by Post Malone is a reflection on the darker side of fame and stardom. The lyrics suggest that those who seek fame and recognition in Hollywood are doomed to a life of loneliness and despair. The song paints a bleak and desperate portrait of a world where everyone is searching for something they will never find. 

The first verse paints a dismal picture of the “Hollywood” that Post Malone has come to know. He speaks of vampires feeding and darkness turning to dust, with no one left but him. He speaks of trying to chase a feeling that can’t be found, and of being on the last train home, living out a dream that can only be achieved alone. 

The second verse takes a more desperate turn, as the narrator speaks of the sky turning grey, the city up in smoke, and “love for everybody ‘til the drugs fade away”. He talks of a woman checking her pulse and telling herself she’s okay, and of how it seems like dying young is an honor. The narrator also reflects on his own fame, as he speaks of people watching him and “sharpen all their teeth”, and of how he’s “losing hope” and trying to light a candle to protect himself from his enemies.

Overall, “Hollywood’s Bleeding” is a somber reflection on the darker side of fame and stardom. It paints a bleak picture of a world where everyone is searching for something they can never find, and where death is seen as an escape from a life of loneliness and despair.

A linguistic analysis of the song’s lyrics reveals a sense of desperation and hopelessness. The language is dark and haunting, with words like “darkness”, “vampires”, and “dying” used to evoke a sense of fear and despair. The song also contains a few hopeful glimmers, such as the narrator’s desire to “light a candle” and “go on”, but these moments of hope are quickly overshadowed by the despair of the larger picture. 

Overall, “Hollywood’s Bleeding” is a gripping and heartbreaking song that speaks to the darker side of fame and stardom. The lyrics are powerful and evocative, and the music and vocals are haunting and emotional. It’s a song that will stay with you long after you’ve heard it.
