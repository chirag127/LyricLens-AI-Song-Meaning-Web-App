"Hollywood Dreams / Come Down" by Post Malone is a song about having dreams and reaching for them despite any obstacles that may stand in his way. The song starts off with Post Malone describing his current situation; he is in a four-door down rodeo and is getting “fucked up” and losing control. He is with his homie who has a mansion and is offering a place to crash for free. The second verse is about letting go of his inhibitions and the people who are “actin’ sleazy”. 

The chorus of the song is about how “thunder only happens when it’s raining” and “players only love you when they’re playing”. Post Malone is expressing that good and bad things will come and go and that he must be ready to face the storm. The song ends with the same chorus, and Post Malone expressing his hope that he will wake up with the sun.

The overall theme of the song is about following your dreams and not letting anyone hold you back. Post Malone is telling us that we must be ready to face the storm when it comes and to stay true to our dreams no matter what. Through this song, Post Malone is conveying a sense of determination and resilience in the face of adversity. 

When analyzing the lyrics of the song, there are several linguistic elements that reveal the emotions and thoughts of Post Malone. The first verse has a tone of chaos and confusion as Post Malone is describing his current situation. The second verse has a more determined tone as he is expressing his determination to break away from those who are trying to hold him back. The chorus has a tone of acceptance and understanding as Post Malone is conveying his understanding of the ups and downs of life. 

Overall, Post Malone's "Hollywood Dreams/Come Down" is an inspiring and uplifting song about following your dreams and never giving up. The song conveys a sense of determination and resilience in the face of adversity, with Post Malone conveying his understanding of the ups and downs of life. Through the use of linguistic elements, the song conveys a range of emotions and helps to keep the listener motivated and determined in their own pursuits.
