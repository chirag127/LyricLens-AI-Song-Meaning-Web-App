Post Malone's "Allergic" is a heartfelt and moving song that touches on the complicated emotions surrounding a break-up. The song starts off with the narrator describing their current state of mind - feeling "wasted on Sunday, erased on Monday," and feeling "allergic" to giving in. The lyrics in the pre-chorus then reveal that the narrator has resorted to taking pills and drugs to cope with the breakup, as they can no longer feel their former partner.

The chorus further reveals the narrator's inner turmoil, as they are stuck in a difficult situation where their former partner is still in their life, yet they are unable to accept the reasons why the relationship has ended. This is illustrated by the lyrics, "Gave a hundred million reasons, but why can't you believe them? Too bad for you."

The song's bridge further highlights the narrator's frustrations, as they are unable to move on from their former partner. They are stuck in a state of limbo, where their former partner is the only one that can understand them, yet they are unable to move on.

The song's message is one of heartbreak and frustration, as the narrator is in a difficult situation where they are unable to move on from their former partner.
