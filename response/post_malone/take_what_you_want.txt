The song “Take What You Want” by Post Malone featuring Ozzy Osbourne and Travis Scott is an upbeat, alternative-rock song that’s all about taking what you want and moving on. The song starts off with Post Malone singing about how he feels someone crumbling in his arms, and how they’ve bled him dry like the tears they never show. He then asks them to take what they want from him and leave. The chorus is sung by both Post Malone and Travis Scott, and expresses how they don’t need anything from the person they’re singing about, and that their soul will never be owned. 

The song continues with Post Malone singing about how the person he’s addressing has preyed on his every mistake and waited for him to break, and he’s been trying to find his way out. The song then transitions to Travis Scott talking about how he took the stones thrown at him and made chains for the crew and ice, and how he needs more reasons to live out the night and he’ll never be alone. The song then goes back to Post Malone’s chorus, which is repeated for the remainder of the song. 

Overall, “Take What You Want” is a song about taking what you want, and moving on from someone who has done wrong by you. The song has a powerful message of self-empowerment and resilience, and it is expressed through the upbeat, alternative-rock sound of the song. Linguistically, the song is full of powerful imagery, with words such as “crumble” and “bleed” being used to express the feelings of the narrator. The song also uses metaphors, such as the line “your venom was lethal”, to further express the feelings of betrayal and hurt. 

In summary, “Take What You Want” is an empowering song that encourages listeners to take what they want and move on from those who’ve done them wrong. It is full of powerful imagery and metaphors that help to express the narrator’s feelings of betrayal and hurt, and the upbeat, alternative-rock sound of the song helps to emphasize the empowering message of the song.
