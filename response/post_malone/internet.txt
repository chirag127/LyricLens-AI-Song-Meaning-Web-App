The song “Internet” by Post Malone is a reflection on the modern digital age, where everyone seems to be constantly plugged in and connected. In the opening verse, Post Malone paints a picture of a woman he has just seen on Instagram, and how he is captivated by her beauty and style. He goes on to discuss how he is paranoid about his own secrets being leaked online and how he is constantly being pursued by women online. 

The pre-chorus of the song describes how the world has gone to “shit” and how people are freaking out, and how he wants to “fuck the internet”. This is a reflection of the overwhelming amount of information and media that we consume on a daily basis, and how it can be overwhelming and distressing. 

The chorus of the song is a declaration that Post Malone will stay disconnected from the internet and its chaos, by choosing to remain ignorant of what is going on. He wants to remain in his own little world, where he doesn’t have to worry about the constant influx of information and the associated stress. 

Overall, the song is a reflection on the modern digital age and the struggles of staying connected while trying to remain sane. The lyrics paint a vivid picture of the chaos of the digital world, while also providing a sense of hope in the fact that one can still find solace in ignorance and disconnection. 

Linguistic analysis of the song lyrics reveals a strong sense of frustration, sadness, and resignation. The words used evoke a sense of hopelessness and desperation, as the narrator is overwhelmed by the flood of information and feels unable to escape. The repetition of the chorus emphasizes the narrator’s desire to remain ignorant and disconnected from the overwhelming digital world. 

The song “Internet” by Post Malone is a powerful and timely reflection on the modern digital age. With its vivid imagery and emotionally charged lyrics, the song speaks to the struggles of staying connected while trying to remain sane. It offers a sense of solace in the idea that one can still find peace in disconnecting and choosing ignorance.
