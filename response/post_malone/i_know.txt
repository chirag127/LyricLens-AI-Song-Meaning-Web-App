Post Malone's "I Know" is a song about heartbreak and regret in a relationship gone wrong. The chorus is sung in a regretful and melancholic tone, as Post Malone reminisces about the mistakes he and his partner have made and the point of no return they have reached. He reflects on the broken promises, the lies, and the heartache that has come from the relationship. 

The verses of the song focus on the lies and deception Post Malone's partner has exhibited. He states that he found out about the lies and deception when she was in the gutter, and that he learned more than he cared to discover. He also mentions how she acts up and then acts like nothing happened, as well as how she sold her soul and was stone cold. 

The bridge of the song reflects on the relationship's end, as Post Malone admits that he still wants her even though he knows it could never be like it was before. He expresses his desire to be single for life rather than being with her, and states that he isn't playing to lose.

The lyrics of "I Know" demonstrate a range of emotions, from regret to anger. Post Malone conveys his feelings of hurt and betrayal through the use of imagery and powerful metaphors. The language of the song is direct and to the point, making it easy for listeners to relate to it.

The song's main message is one of acceptance and understanding. Despite all the hurt and betrayal, Post Malone still has love for his partner and understands that relationships are not always perfect. He has come to terms with the fact that the relationship is over and is ready to move on.

In summary, Post Malone's "I Know" is a song about heartache and regret in a relationship gone wrong. The lyrics are direct and powerful, conveying a range of emotions from regret to anger. The song's main message is one of acceptance and understanding, as Post Malone comes to terms with the fact that the relationship is over and is ready to move on.
