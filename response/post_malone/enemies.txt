The song Enemies by Post Malone is a reflection on the way people can change when they gain success and money. Throughout the song, Post Malone chronicles the way people have turned against him since he has become successful, from former friends to those seeking a handout. The lyrics also express his feelings of loneliness and betrayal, as he has been left alone after many of his former friends have turned against him.

The song is comprised of four verses, each of which paints a picture of Post Malone’s current situation. In the first verse, he reflects on the way people used to be around him before he gained success, and how now they are all against him. He reflects on how his mother now needs tickets to his stadium shows, and how he is no longer part of the lives of those who were previously close to him.

The second verse further reflects on the situation, with Post Malone noting the way his former friends have let him down and how they have become his enemies. He also discusses the way money has changed people’s behavior, referencing the way people are “packin’ the post” and how they “fold and bite the hand that feeds them.”

The third verse of the song further illustrates Post Malone’s feelings of loneliness and betrayal, as he notes the way his former friends have left him and how he is no longer able to turn the situation around. The fourth verse of the song concludes with Post Malone expressing his sadness over the situation, noting how it is now too late to turn the situation around.

The song Enemies by Post Malone paints a vivid picture of the loneliness and betrayal one can experience when gaining success and money. The lyrics are a reflection on the way people can change when faced with these external pressures, and how one can be left behind in the wake of it all. Musically, the song is an upbeat blend of pop and hip-hop, with an infectious hook that drives the song forward. Linguistically, the lyrics express a deep sense of regret and sadness, as Post Malone reflects on the way his former friends have now become his enemies. 

Overall, Enemies by Post Malone is an emotionally charged song that captures the loneliness and betrayal one can feel when faced with success and money. Post Malone’s vivid lyrics tell a story of betrayal and regret, while the upbeat production provides an infectious backdrop. The song is sure to resonate with many listeners, as it captures the feelings of loneliness and betrayal many of us can relate to.
