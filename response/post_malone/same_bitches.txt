The song "Same Bitches" by Post Malone, featuring YG and G-Eazy, is an ode to the Los Angeles lifestyle, where people are constantly surrounded by the same faces and materialism. In the song, Post Malone expresses his frustration with the city's superficiality and its tendency for people to use each other for their own gain.

The song's lyrics portray a vivid image of the life Post Malone and his fellow artists are living in Los Angeles, with references to designer clothes, cars, and the allure of fame. Post Malone's chorus references the city's large population, pointing out how he sees “the same bitches everywhere [he] goes”.

The following verse is then taken up by G-Eazy, who further emphasizes the “ratchet” side of the city and the people who inhabit it. He also references his own success, noting how he and his friends have made it in the city and how they are able to splurge on their materialistic desires.

YG then follows up with a verse about the women of Los Angeles and how they are willing to do anything for the fame and money. He goes on to mention how his friend has slept with a basketball player and the head of his record label in order to gain success.

In summary, the song is about the materialistic lifestyle that Post Malone and his collaborators live in Los Angeles, and how they are surrounded by the same people everywhere they go. It is a commentary on the superficiality of the city and its inhabitants, and how they are willing to do anything to get ahead.

From a linguistic analysis of the song lyrics, the emotions expressed can be interpreted as frustration and contempt, as well as a hint of admiration for the city and its inhabitants. This is highlighted by the use of words such as “ratchet” and “Hollywood”, which evoke a sense of both admiration and disdain. The tone of the song is also reflective of the culture of Los Angeles, with a heavy emphasis on materialism and fame.

Overall, "Same Bitches" is an interesting and entertaining take on the Los Angeles lifestyle, and the song's clever lyrics and catchy beat make it an enjoyable listen. The song is a great example of how artists can use their music to express their thoughts and emotions about a certain place or situation.
