In his song "Holyfield", Post Malone paints a picture of his newfound success and the luxurious lifestyle it has afforded him. The song begins with Post Malone reflecting on how someone he knew has changed in a way that he can’t quite put his finger on. He then tells the story of how he is living his life in the present, riding in expensive cars and spending lavishly, while simultaneously warning his peers not to underestimate him with the chant “I am the shit”. It is clear that the narrator has achieved a certain level of success, and is reveling in it.

The verses in the song are full of references to luxury items, expensive cars, and lavish spending. Post Malone makes it clear that he is living a life of luxury, and that he is proud of it. He also conveys a sense of pride in his accomplishments, and a refusal to be underestimated. In the chorus, he declares “ain’t got no fucks to give, that’s on moms for real”, a phrase that conveys his determination to succeed and his refusal to let anyone bring him down.

The song’s lyrics are emotionally charged and convey a clear message. Post Malone conveys his success and his pride in it, while simultaneously warning those who might not understand or underestimate him. The language in the song is vivid and paints a picture of the narrator’s world. Post Malone also manages to convey a sense of empowerment and a refusal to be brought down by his peers.

When analyzing the song “Holyfield”, it is easy to see the linguistic analysis that can be done on the lyrics. Post Malone’s use of language conveys a sense of power, pride, and determination. He also conveys a sense of luxurious living and a refusal to be underestimated. Through his language and references, he paints a vivid picture of his world, and conveys his message effectively. 

Overall, “Holyfield” is an anthem for those looking to achieve success and revel in it. Post Malone’s message is clear and his language conveys a sense of luxury, pride, and determination. The song is a great example of how to convey a message through lyrics, and is an enjoyable listen for anyone who wants to feel empowered and inspired.
