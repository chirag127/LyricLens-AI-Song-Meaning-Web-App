In the song "Circles" by Post Malone, the artist uses metaphors, repetition, and lyrical structure to convey the emotions associated with a toxic relationship. The song begins with the repetition of "oh oh oh oh" to create a sense of monotony and despair, emphasizing the feeling of being stuck in a cycle of an unhealthy relationship. In the first verse, the lyrics talk about being "upside down" and not being able to turn around, reflecting the feeling of being unable to break free from the cycle and the helplessness felt when trying to do so. The second verse speaks of the changing seasons, emphasizing how quickly time can pass, and the chorus repeats the phrase "run away, but we’re running in circles" to drive home the idea of being stuck in a loop. 

The third verse talks about letting go, and the fourth verse speaks of understanding and making up one's mind, alluding to the need to make a conscious choice to break away from the cycle. The song ends with the chorus being repeated, further emphasizing the idea of being stuck in a cycle.

The song is a powerful expression of the emotional experience of being in a toxic relationship, and how it can feel like a never-ending cycle. The lyrics are evocative and the music is haunting, making it a powerful and moving track. The use of repetition and metaphors helps to drive home the message of being stuck in an unhealthy relationship and emphasizes the need to break free. 

From a linguistic analysis standpoint, the song contains many emotional words such as "cold", "blame", and "doomed". The use of these words helps to convey the emotions associated with being in a toxic relationship and the desperate need to escape from it. There is also a strong use of repetition in the chorus, which serves to emphasize the idea of being stuck in a never-ending loop. 

Overall, "Circles" by Post Malone is a powerful and emotive song about being stuck in a toxic relationship. The lyrics and music are evocative and the use of repetition and metaphors help to emphasize the feelings of helplessness and despair that come with being in an unhealthy relationship. It is a powerful and moving track that speaks to the experiences of many people in similar circumstances.
