"Over Now" by Post Malone is a song about getting over a former flame and feeling free to move on. The song is written from a first-person perspective, with Malone singing about his own experiences and reflecting on his own emotions.

The song begins with Malone singing about how his former flame left him, and how it didn't faze her. He then sings about how he would have given her the moon, even after all they had been through. This could be seen as an indication of his strong love for the person, and his unwillingness to let go.

In the chorus, Malone sings about how he has moved on and is now living his life without the person. He sings about how he is now driving around in a Bentley, with a lot of money in his pocket, showing that he has achieved a certain level of success and financial stability. He also sings about how he has "turned the tables" and will no longer apologize or care whether or not the person is offended.

The second verse of the song is where Malone expresses his feelings of anger and betrayal. He sings about how the person treated the relationship like a joke, and how he felt like he was begging on his knees for the person to stay. He also expresses his hope that the person will wind up alone in the future.

The bridge of the song is where Malone sings about how he has put the person's "pussy in a motherfuckin' body bag" and how everything has changed. This is a metaphor for how he has completely moved on and is no longer interested in the person.

The song ends with Malone repeating the chorus, emphasizing that he is "over now" and will never go back to the person.

Overall, "Over Now" is a powerful song about getting over a former flame and feeling free to move on. The song is filled with strong lyrical imagery and metaphors that portray Malone's emotions. His lyrics are full of pain and anger, but also show his strength and resilience in the face of adversity. Linguistically, the song contains many examples of assonance and alliteration, which help to emphasize the raw emotion of the song and add to the overall impact.

In summary, "Over Now" is an emotional and powerful song about moving on and not looking back. Post Malone's unique style and lyrical prowess make the song an enjoyable and memorable listen. Through his lyrics, he conveys his pain, anger, and resilience, and ultimately expresses his freedom and triumph in getting over his former flame.
