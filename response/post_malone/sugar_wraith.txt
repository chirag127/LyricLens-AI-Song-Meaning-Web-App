Sugar Wraith by Post Malone is a song about a man's journey from poverty to success. He details his struggles and his determination to pursue his dreams. The lyrics of the song illustrate an individual's growth from an Eighth to a Wraith, a powerful and expensive type of car. The chorus emphasizes the importance of taking chances and living life to the fullest before it's too late. 

The song begins with Post Malone reflecting on his past. He raps that he began with "an eighth," meaning that he started with very little. He then went on to change his life and mentions being able to buy a Wraith, a powerful and expensive type of car. The lyrics also suggest that he had to take risks in order to achieve his dreams, and that he is proud of his accomplishments.

The second verse sees Post Malone talking about his relationship with the woman in his life. He raps that he doesn't want to spend all his money on her and he brags about his success. He also warns her about the dangers of fame and the consequences that come with it.

The third verse finds Post Malone reflecting on his journey from poverty to success. He mentions that he was broke last year and now he can't go back there. He raps about his determination to "light a match" and take what he deserves, emphasizing his ambition and drive.

The chorus of the song emphasizes the importance of living life to the fullest. Post Malone raps that he wants to fly and make it last before he dies. The lyrics suggest that one should take risks and not be afraid to pursue their dreams, as nothing is guaranteed.

In conclusion, Sugar Wraith by Post Malone is a song that is about a man's journey from poverty to success. It emphasizes the importance of taking risks and living life to its fullest before it's too late. The lyrics also illustrate the consequences of fame and the determination to make it in life. Linguistically, the song is filled with metaphors and imagery that are used to illustrate the struggles and determination of the individual. The song provides a powerful message about taking chances and living life to the fullest.
