Post Malone’s song “Staring At The Sun” is a track about the difficulties of relationships and the pain that comes with loving someone. The chorus emphasizes this theme of pain and struggle, singing that if they keep staring at the sun they won’t see what they have become and that they are blinded by the thought of the relationship.

The second verse is sung by SZA, and she speaks of her own struggles in relationships. She knows she has a lot of things going on and that she can’t get too close, as she will be let down. The chorus is once again sung together by both Post Malone and SZA, emphasizing their shared struggles.

The song’s lyrics are filled with emotion and evoke feelings of pain, struggle, and loss. The language is reflective of the difficulty of the situation, with words like “staring” and “blinded” being used to emphasize the difficulty of the relationship. The song also uses repetition of words and phrases to drive home the idea of the situation being a cycle of pain and struggle.

The song is a heartfelt, emotional look at the struggles of relationships and the pain that comes with loving someone. It is a powerful piece that speaks to the emotions of the listener and provides an understanding of the difficulty of relationships.
