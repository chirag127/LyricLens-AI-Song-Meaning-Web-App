Post Malone’s hit single “Patient” is an anthemic ode to the hustle, as he reflects on his come-up and the challenges that come with it. Throughout the song, Post Malone speaks on his newfound success and the financial and social pressures that come with it. He speaks of his ability to remain patient through it all, and of his hard work and dedication that has seen him go from the bottom to the top. 

In the first verse, Post Malone speaks of his newfound success, claiming that his wrist is “dancin’ crazy” and that he’s been “on some fly shit lately.” He speaks of his ability to remain patient, and of his reluctance to let money change him. He also references his parents, speaking of how they raised him, and his unwillingness to “brag” despite his success. The second verse sees Post Malone reflect on his newfound wealth, as he speaks of having “two hundred k and VVS yellow diamonds,” and of people trying to run up on him, though he’s “never gonna find me.” He also speaks of his “100 bands on my body,” and of his refusal to let people get close to him. The third verse sees Post Malone reflect on his success and of the “plotting” he has had to do to get to the top. He speaks of his desire to make money off of his own terms, and of his refusal to let people put him in a box. 

Overall, “Patient” is a powerful ode to the hustle and Post Malone’s ability to remain patient and focused despite the pressures of fame and wealth. The song is a testament to his hard work and dedication, and to his refusal to let money change him. Lyrically, the song is full of vivid imagery and references to his newfound success and the challenges that come with it. The song is a powerful reminder of the importance of remaining patient and to never give up on your dreams.
