"Hit This Hard" by Post Malone is an upbeat, energetic track that speaks to the joys and risks of embracing life to its fullest. The song starts off with the chorus repeating the phrase "I never thought it would hit this hard" and then a booming trap beat. In the verses, Post Malone speaks to his own experiences of taking risks and the consequences of his decisions, both good and bad. 

The first verse finds Post Malone reflecting on his own partying lifestyle and the risks associated with it. He warns against overindulging, noting that "what have you become" if you do and that he himself cannot fix the situation. The prechorus then speaks to embracing the moment, "I ran through/I feel a change in the air tonight/Tonight I saw myself in a different light".

In the second verse, Post Malone speaks to the hedonistic lifestyle of those living in Los Angeles, and the temptations of the city. He speaks to the thrill of taking risks, and the consequences that come with it, noting that "the feeling is different instead when you wake on the ground". 

In terms of its linguistic analysis, "Hit This Hard" speaks to themes of risk-taking, partying, and embracing the moment. It speaks to the joy that comes with taking risks, as well as the potential consequences that can come with it. The song is full of energy and excitement, reflecting Post Malone's own experiences of living life to its fullest. 

Overall, "Hit This Hard" is an upbeat, energetic track that speaks to the joys and risks of embracing life to its fullest. Its energetic, party-friendly production is complemented by Post Malone's lyrical reflections on his own experiences of taking risks and the consequences of his decisions, both good and bad. This is a great track for those who are looking to take a risk and live life to its fullest.
