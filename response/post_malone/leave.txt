The song "Leave" by Post Malone is a heartbreaking story of unrequited love and the harsh realities of life. The song follows the narrator as he recounts his experiences with a woman he loves, but ultimately must leave. The lyrics are filled with vivid imagery and powerful emotions, as Post Malone paints a picture of a relationship strained by expectations and a lack of communication.

The bridge of the song highlights the narrator's inner turmoil and his inability to move on from the situation. He reflects on the pain of the situation and expresses his desire to die as he makes his way home on a plane.
