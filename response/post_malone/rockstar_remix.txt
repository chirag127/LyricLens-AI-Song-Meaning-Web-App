The song “Rockstar (Remix)” by Post Malone featuring Nicky Jam and Ozuna is a hip-hop/rap song about living the lavish lifestyle of a rockstar. The chorus, sung by Post Malone, emphasizes his wealth and power as a rockstar, and he wants everyone to know that he lives like one. The verses, sung by Nicky Jam and Ozuna, discuss the luxuries they can afford with their money and how they take advantage of the privileges they have. 

The lyrics of the song are full of imagery and metaphors to describe the lavish lifestyle of a rockstar. Nicky Jam raps about buying a Panamera, Gucci, Ferragamo, and a Rhino to show off his wealth. Ozuna talks about going to private jets and giving away gold Apple watches. The chorus has a triumphant feeling, with Malone bragging about his money and fame.

The song also contains many references to partying and drugs, with lines such as “I’ve been f*ckin’ hoes and poppin’ pillies.” This indicates that the song is not just about money and luxury items, but also about enjoying the lifestyle of a rockstar.

Overall, “Rockstar (Remix)” is a song about living the lavish lifestyle of a rockstar. The lyrics are full of imagery and metaphors to describe the lifestyle, and there are references to partying and drugs. It has a triumphant feeling, with Malone bragging about his money and fame. The song is an ode to the materialistic and hedonistic lifestyle of a rockstar, and it is sure to be a hit with fans of the genre.
