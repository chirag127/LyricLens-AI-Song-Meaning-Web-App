"Forever" by Justin Bieber, featuring Post Malone and Clever, is a romantic and vulnerable song about love and devotion. The song begins with Justin Bieber singing about wanting to spend forever with someone, and then Post Malone joins in on the chorus, adding his own unique touch of rap. The song then progresses to the verses where Justin and Post trade lines, expressing their feelings of love and commitment to one another. Clever then joins in on the outro, further expressing his own desire to stay with his partner forever.

The lyrics are filled with imagery of a picturesque and romantic scene, with references to the summer and Vegas and the desert. The lyrics also focus on the idea of making mistakes and being forgiven and accepted despite them, emphasizing the importance of unconditional love.

The song is a beautiful expression of the power of love and the willingness to commit to someone forever. It is a reminder that love can be strong and lasting, and that even when we make mistakes, we can still be accepted and loved. Justin Bieber, Post Malone, and Clever combine to create a song that is both emotionally powerful and musically captivating.

Overall, "Forever" is an incredible song, and Justin Bieber, Post Malone, and Clever have done a great job of creating a heartfelt and moving piece of music. The song is filled with emotion and captures the true essence of love and commitment. It is a beautiful reminder of the power of love, and is sure to be enjoyed by all.
