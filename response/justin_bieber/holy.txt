"Holy" is a romantic song by Justin Bieber featuring Chance the Rapper. In the song, Bieber sings about the way his partner makes him feel, calling their connection "holy" and expressing his desire to spend the rest of his life with them. Chance the Rapper adds a verse about faith and commitment, rapping about getting married and honoring the union in the eyes of God. Overall, the song is a romantic and spiritual ode to the special connection between two people.

He raps about the first steps of their relationship, the importance of trusting in God, and the need to clean up and formalize their union in order to honor their connection.

In terms of a review or opinion of the song, "Holy" is a beautifully written and performed love song that seamlessly blends romantic and spiritual themes.
