"What Do You Mean?" is a pop song by Justin Bieber that was released as the lead single from his fourth studio album Purpose. The song's lyrics revolve around confusion and misunderstandings in a relationship. The chorus repeats the phrase "what do you mean?" as Bieber asks his partner to clarify their feelings and actions.

In the first verse, Bieber sings about his partner's indecisiveness and conflicting behavior. He expresses frustration at not being able to understand them and wonders where to begin in trying to fix things. In the pre-chorus, he sings about the back-and-forth arguments and the need for clarity in the relationship.

The second verse continues this theme, with Bieber singing about his partner's overprotective behavior and their inability to compromise. He reflects on the strength of their connection and expresses his desire for the relationship to continue.

The chorus repeats the central question of the song, with Bieber asking his partner to be more clear and make up their mind. The song has a catchy, upbeat melody and Bieber's vocals are strong and emotive.

Overall, "What Do You Mean?" is a catchy pop song that explores the confusion and misunderstandings that can arise in relationships. The repetitive chorus and relatable lyrics make it a memorable and enjoyable listen.
