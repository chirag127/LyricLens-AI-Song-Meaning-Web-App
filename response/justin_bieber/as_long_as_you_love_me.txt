The chorus repeats the title of the song, emphasizing the importance of the partner's love to the singer. He is willing to go through difficult times and make sacrifices for the relationship as long as he is loved in return.

He admits that they have issues, but he would rather work on them together than start over with someone new.

In the final chorus, Bieber repeats the title of the song and reassures his partner that he will always be there for her, no matter what challenges they may face.

Overall, "As Long As You Love Me" is a romantic and emotional song that explores the strength and resilience of love in the face of adversity. It is a powerful and relatable message that resonates with many listeners.
