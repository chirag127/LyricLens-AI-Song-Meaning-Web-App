"Confident" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Chance the Rapper. The song is about a confident woman who catches Bieber's attention. He is captivated by her and can't stop thinking about her. In the chorus, Bieber sings, "Oh, no, no, oh, no, no, she's confident / Oh, no, no, oh, no, no, and I'm down with it / Oh, no, no, oh, no, no, she's confident / You could tell by the way she walks in the room." In the third verse, Chance the Rapper adds his own verse, rapping about the woman and how she stands out from the other "thots" (a slang term for promiscuous women). The song is upbeat and catchy, with a mix of pop and hip hop influences. Overall, "Confident" is a fun and energetic song that showcases Bieber's vocal range and versatility as an artist.
