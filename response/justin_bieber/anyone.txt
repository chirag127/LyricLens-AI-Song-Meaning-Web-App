The song "Anyone" by Justin Bieber is a love song in which the singer expresses his deep love for a significant other and declares that, in his eyes, they are the only one he will ever love. The song begins with the singer inviting the person to dance with him under the diamonds, a metaphor for the sparkle and beauty of their relationship. He then describes how he sees this person as a fundamental part of his life, like breath in the cold, and how he wants to spend his time with them in silence and intimacy.

In the pre-chorus, the singer acknowledges that the future is uncertain and that he can't predict what will happen in their relationship. However, he urges the person to hold on to him and never let go. He also expresses his fear of losing them and the need to make sure they know how much they mean to him.

The chorus repeats the singer's declaration of love, stating that if it's not this person, it's no one. He also looks back on his life and reflects on how this person is the only good thing he's ever done.

In the second verse, the singer expresses his desire to love this person for eternity, but admits that it might not be enough time to fully express his love. He also fears the day when he might not find them in the morning, again highlighting his fear of losing them.

The bridge of the song repeats the chorus, with the singer reaffirming his love and commitment to this person and declaring that if it's not them, it's no one.

In summary, "Anyone" is a heartfelt love song in which Justin Bieber declares his undying love and commitment to his significant other. He acknowledges the uncertainty of the future but remains determined to hold on to this person and make sure they know how much they mean to him.
