"All That Matters" by Justin Bieber is a love song in which Bieber sings about how much he needs and cares for his significant other. The first verse establishes this theme, with Bieber singing about how his partner is as essential to him as the stars in the sky and how he can't function without them.

In the chorus, Bieber reiterates the importance of his partner to him, stating that they are "all that matters" and that without them, he isn't himself. He also uses the metaphor of a king without a queen to emphasize their role in his life.

In the second verse, Bieber continues to sing about how much he needs his partner and how grateful he is for their existence. He also mentions that they are the only person he sees, indicating that they are the only one he has eyes for.

Overall, the song is a heartfelt expression of love and devotion to one's significant other. It conveys the idea that a romantic partner can be an essential part of one's life and happiness.

As a review of the song, it is a well-written and emotionally effective love song. The lyrics are simple and straightforward, but they effectively convey the depth of Bieber's feelings for his partner. The melody is also catchy and memorable, making it a great choice for fans of Bieber's music.
