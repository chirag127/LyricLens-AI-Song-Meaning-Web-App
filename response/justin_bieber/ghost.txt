"Ghost" by Justin Bieber is a song about the pain of losing someone you love and the longing to be close to them again. In the first verse, the singer expresses his sadness and emptiness without the person he loves, saying that he misses their touch and can't follow them to wherever they've gone. In the chorus, he sings that if he can't be physically close to them, he'll settle for being close to their memory or "ghost". He repeats that he misses them more than life itself.

In the second verse, the singer repeats the idea that he wishes he could have more time with the person he loves, but time is not on his side. He expresses a willingness to leave everything behind in order to be with them. The chorus repeats, again expressing the singer's intense longing and desire to be close to the person he loves, even if it's only in memory.

The bridge of the song features the repetition of "Woah-oh-oh-oh" and "Na-na-na" as the singer reflects on the depth of his love and longing for the person he's lost. The chorus repeats once more, reiterating the central theme of the song: the intense longing to be close to the person the singer loves, even if it's only in memory.

Overall, "Ghost" is a haunting and emotional song about the pain of loss and the intense longing to be close to the person you love, even if it's only in memory. The song is well-written and effectively captures the emotions of someone dealing with the loss of a loved one.
