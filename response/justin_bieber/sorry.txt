"Sorry" is a song by Justin Bieber in which he expresses remorse for mistakes he has made in a relationship. In the first verse, he admits to being bad at apologizing and asks for another chance to make things right. He then acknowledges that he has made mistakes many times in the past. In the chorus, he asks if it is too late to apologize and admits to missing more than just the other person's body.

In the second verse, he offers to take all the blame for their problems and suggests that they both move on from the argument. The chorus repeats with the addition of the line "I'm not just trying to get you back on me" indicating that he wants to fix their relationship for more than just physical reasons.

The song is a sincere and emotional expression of regret and a desire for redemption. It showcases Bieber's growth as an artist and his ability to convey complex emotions through his music. Overall, "Sorry" is a powerful and relatable song that many listeners can connect with.
