"I'll Show You" by Justin Bieber is a song about the pressure and expectations placed on Bieber as a celebrity. The song begins with the line, "My life is a movie and everyone's watching," indicating that Bieber feels like he is constantly under the public's scrutiny. In the pre-chorus, Bieber sings about how it can be difficult to do the right thing when he is facing pressure from others to be perfect. He also suggests that people do not truly understand him or his struggles.

In the chorus, Bieber repeats the line, "I'll show you," as a declaration that he will not be defined by other people's expectations or opinions of him. He also reminds listeners that he is not made of steel and is subject to the same struggles and emotions as anyone else.

The second verse of the song continues this theme, with Bieber singing about learning lessons the hard way and feeling the effects of other people's words and actions. The pre-chorus and chorus then repeat, reinforcing the message of the song.

Overall, "I'll Show You" is a song about standing up to pressure and refusing to be defined by others' expectations. It serves as a reminder that even celebrities are human and have their own struggles and challenges.

As for a review of the song, "I'll Show You" is a well-written and emotionally resonant track that showcases Bieber's vocal abilities and introspective lyrics. The production, which was handled by Skrillex, is also top-notch, adding layers of depth and texture to the track. Overall, it is a powerful and impactful song that serves as a reminder of Bieber's talent and artistry.
