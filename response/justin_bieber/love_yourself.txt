"Love Yourself" is a song by Justin Bieber. In the first verse, Bieber sings about the ways in which his former partner mistreated him, but he states that he is not crying over the situation. In the refrain, he expresses that he didn't want to write a song about the experience because he didn't want people to think he still cared. He also says that his mother doesn't like the person and that he is moving on.

In the pre-chorus, Bieber admits that he was wrong in the relationship and that he was too focused on his career to see what was happening. The chorus repeats the line "you should go and love yourself" and suggests that the person still thinks Bieber is holding onto something.

In the second verse, Bieber continues to describe the ways in which the person mistreated him and tried to control him. The bridge discusses the feelings of vulnerability and betrayal that Bieber experienced during the relationship. The chorus repeats at the end of the song.

Overall, the song is about moving on from a toxic relationship and finding self-love. It is a reminder to prioritize oneself and to not let others mistreat or control you. In my opinion, "Love Yourself" is a powerful and relatable song with a strong message. Bieber's vocals are emotive and the lyrics are well-written. It is a standout track on his album.
