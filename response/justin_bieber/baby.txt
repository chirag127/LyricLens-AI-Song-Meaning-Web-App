"Baby" is a song by Canadian singer Justin Bieber, featuring American rapper Ludacris as a guest vocalist. The song was released as the lead single from Bieber's debut album, My World 2.0. In the song, Bieber sings about being in love with a girl and not wanting to lose her. Ludacris' verse discusses his own experiences with young love.

In the first verse, Bieber and Ludacris sing about the strong connection they feel with their significant other. They express their love and commitment to each other, and assure their partner that they will always be there for them. They also address the possibility of other people coming between them, but they brush it off and remain confident in their relationship.

In the second verse, Bieber sings about his heartbreak and desperation to win back his ex-girlfriend. He offers to buy her anything and do anything to fix their relationship. Ludacris continues the theme of young love in his verse, reminiscing about his first love and the feelings of infatuation and adoration he experienced at the time.

The chorus repeats the phrase "Baby, baby, baby oh" as Bieber sings about his disbelief and sadness at the thought of losing his girlfriend. He thought she would always be his, but now he is realizing that may not be the case.

Overall, "Baby" is a love song about the highs and lows of young relationships. The catchy hook and energetic beat make it a popular choice for fans of Justin Bieber and pop music in general.

As a review of the song, "Baby" is a well-crafted pop track that showcases Bieber's vocal abilities and showcases Ludacris' rap skills.
