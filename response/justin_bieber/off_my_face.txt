"Off My Face" is a song by Justin Bieber about being in love and feeling high on emotion. In the first verse, Bieber sings about how one touch from his love interest has him feeling "stoned" and "higher than [he's] ever known." He also speaks about how this person has control over him and he is willing to follow their lead. The pre-chorus describes the impact this person has on him, with their touch blurring his vision and making him unable to think straight.

The chorus repeats the phrase "off my face, in love with you" to emphasize the intensity of Bieber's feelings. He is completely consumed by this person and doesn't know how they have such a strong hold on him. The second verse continues this theme, with Bieber mentioning how he can't sleep because he is "buzzed" on the feeling of being in love.

In the bridge and outro, the repetition of the phrase "ooh-ooh-ooh" further emphasizes the euphoric and dream-like state that Bieber is in.

Overall, the song is a lighthearted and playful depiction of being in love and the effects it can have on a person. It is a catchy and enjoyable tune that captures the excitement and butterflies of new love.
