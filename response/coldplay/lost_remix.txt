"Lost+ (Remix)" by Coldplay is a song that speaks of the feelings of loss and uncertainty when faced with difficult decisions and challenges. In the first verse, Chris Martin speaks of the feeling of being lost and the frustration of being unable to cross every river he tried. He talks about the idea of waiting for the shine to wear off and for the moment of clarity to come. 

In the second verse, Jay-Z takes on the same theme and speaks of the power dynamics that can lead to feeling lost. He speaks of being a big fish in a small pond and how if one is successful, they can expect to be crucified. He also speaks of the power of the media, and how it can meddle in the lives of those it favors. 

The chorus is a call to embrace the idea of having had and lost, as it is better than never having at all. The song speaks of the idea of embracing the journey and the ups and downs that come with it. It emphasizes the idea that one should not be afraid to take risks and to learn from one's mistakes. 

The song's lyrics contain a lot of emotion and offer a complex view of the idea of loss and success. Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a range of emotions, from frustration and uncertainty to hope and resilience. The lyrics also suggest a range of social tendencies, from the power dynamics of the media to the idea of embracing one's journey and learning from mistakes. 

Overall, "Lost+ (Remix)" is an emotive and thought-provoking song that speaks of the difficulties of facing difficult decisions and the uncertainty that comes with it. It offers an encouraging message to embrace the journey of life and to take risks, knowing that mistakes can be learned from.
