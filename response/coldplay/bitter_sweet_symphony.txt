The song "Bitter Sweet Symphony" by Coldplay is an emotionally charged piece of music that speaks to the struggles of life, the power of money, and the need to stay true to oneself. The lyrics, written by Coldplay frontman Chris Martin and sung by Richard Ashcroft, are a story of how life can be a struggle and how, in the end, money can be a slave to all of us.

This is followed by the line "but I'm here in my mold, I'm a million different people from one day to the next" which speaks to the idea that one can be many different people, depending on the situation.

The bridge of the song speaks to the idea of trying to make sense of the world, and how music can help one find peace. The line "now I let the melody shine, let it cleanse my mind, I feel free now" speaks to the power of music and how it can help one find solace.

To summarize, the song "Bitter Sweet Symphony" by Coldplay is a powerful song that speaks to the struggles of life, the power of money, and the need to stay true to oneself.
