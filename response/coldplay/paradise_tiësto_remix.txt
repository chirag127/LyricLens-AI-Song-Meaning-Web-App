The song “Paradise (Tiësto Remix)” by Coldplay is a touching, melancholic ballad about a young girl’s desire to escape her reality and find a place of solace and peace. The chorus repeats the phrase “para para paradise” as the music builds to a crescendo, symbolizing the girl’s desperate longing for her own personal paradise.
