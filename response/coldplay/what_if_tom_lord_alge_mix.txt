The song "What If (Tom Lord Alge Mix)" by Coldplay is a poignant exploration of a relationship in the face of uncertainty. The lyrics express the fear and insecurity of not knowing what the future will bring, as the singer ponders the possibility of their love not lasting. The song begins with the singer asking "what if there was no light, nothing wrong, nothing right?" and this establishes a sense of vulnerability and insecurity. The singer is asking a hypothetical question, but is also reflecting on the fragility of their relationship. 

The second verse is a direct plea to the other person, as the singer begs the question of what will happen if the other person decides that they no longer want the singer in their life. This is a powerful example of the singer's vulnerability and expresses the fear and doubt that can come along with being in a relationship. The chorus is a call for the singer and their partner to take a risk and jump into the unknown, despite the fear of the unknown. 

The last verse is a reminder of hope and resilience, as the singer encourages the other person to take a leap of faith and remember that "darkness always turns into light". This is a beautiful sentiment of love and commitment, and serves as an uplifting reminder that the journey of life and love will always have its ups and downs. 

In summary, "What If (Tom Lord Alge Mix)" by Coldplay is a beautiful and powerful exploration of love, vulnerability, and hope. The song expresses the fear of uncertainty in relationships, but also provides a reminder to take a risk and embrace the unknown. The lyrics provide a beautiful and poetic reflection on the complexities and nuances of relationships, and are sure to provide a deep emotional experience for listeners.
