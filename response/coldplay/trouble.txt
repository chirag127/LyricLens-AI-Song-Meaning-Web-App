The song “Trouble” by Coldplay speaks of a person who has been ensnared in a web of mistakes, and is reflecting on all the wrong they have done. The song begins with the narrator recognizing that they are stuck in this web and regretting the stupid things they have done and said. The chorus repeats the refrain of “I never meant to cause you trouble, and I never meant to do you wrong,” showing that the narrator is aware of the harm their mistakes have caused. 

The second verse speaks to the narrator's feelings of helplessness in their situation, as they are “caught in the middle” and can only “twist and turn” while they remain trapped in their own “little bubble.” The chorus is repeated again, emphasizing the narrator's regret and remorse. 

The third verse speaks to the idea that the narrator has been ensnared in a “spider web,” a metaphor for the web of mistakes they have made. This suggests that the narrator is feeling helpless and trapped in the situation, unable to escape and move past their mistakes. 

The song speaks to the feelings of remorse and regret that often accompany mistakes, and highlights the helplessness of being unable to move past them. It speaks to the idea that mistakes can be so overwhelming that they can feel like you are trapped in a web, unable to escape.

In summary, the song “Trouble” by Coldplay speaks to the feelings of regret and remorse that come with making mistakes. The song uses the metaphor of being ensnared in a “spider web” to show the helplessness of being unable to move past mistakes and the regret of knowing the harm they have caused. The song speaks to the idea that mistakes can be so overwhelming that they can feel like you are trapped in a web, unable to escape.

The lyrics of this song are emotionally charged, conveying a sense of regret and helplessness. The use of metaphors, like the “spider web,” help to add depth to the emotions expressed in the song. The language used in the song also helps to convey the narrator's feelings, as the use of words like “tangle” and “bubble” help to express the sense of being trapped. Overall, the song does an effective job of conveying the narrator's feelings of regret and helplessness.
