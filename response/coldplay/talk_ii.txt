"Talk ii" by Coldplay is a song about the feeling of being lost and trying to find one's way through life. The lyrics explore the idea of feeling disconnected from the world, and the need to find a sense of belonging. The song acknowledges the struggles of being alone and in search of meaning, and speaks to the importance of communication and connection. 

The song begins by describing a sense of disorientation and confusion, with the narrator feeling lost in the "great lone star" and unable to work out where they are. This is followed by a sense of being adrift in the "silence of the sea" and going "nowhere much too fast", symbolizing the feeling of being stuck and unable to find direction. 

The chorus speaks of the need to find a way forward, and a desire to "talk" and "find a home", even though the narrator has no idea where they are going. The narrator is searching for something, and they suggest taking a picture of something they're not sure of to bring back and show. This could be seen as a metaphor for finding something to hold onto, even if it doesn't make any sense. 

The last verse further emphasizes the need to communicate, and speaks of the difficulty of trying to reach someone and being "ignored". The song ends with an emphatic call for communication, as the narrator implores the listener to "talk". 

"Talk ii" by Coldplay speaks to the need for connection and communication, and acknowledges the struggles of feeling lost and alone. The lyrics describe the feeling of not knowing where one is going, and the need to find something to hold onto. The song also speaks to the importance of talking and communicating, as it suggests that talking is the only way to make sense of the world. 

The lyrics of the song contain a range of emotions, including confusion, loneliness, and a sense of hope. The language is poetic and evocative, allowing listeners to draw their own conclusions and experiences from the song. Through its powerful lyrics, "Talk ii" speaks to the importance of communication and connection, and serves as a reminder that we are not alone in our struggles.
