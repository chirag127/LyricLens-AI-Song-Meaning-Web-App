Hymn for the Weekend (Alan Walker Remix) by Coldplay is a song about the euphoria of being in love. The song starts off with Chris Martin singing about the feeling of being so high and the angel that lifts him up when he is down. The chorus is a reminder that life is a drink and love is a drug, and when Chris Martin is feeling dry he knows that his love will bring the rain to flood him with joy. 

Beyoncé then joins in, singing about how her love is like a symphony, lifting him up when he is low. This is further emphasized in the chorus, when both Chris Martin and Beyoncé sing together about how their love is like a shooting star that crosses the sky. 

Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals that the song is a celebration of joy and love. The language is generally positive and uplifting, with words such as “drunk”, “high”, “angel”, and “symphony” being used to evoke a sense of euphoria. The repetition of certain words and phrases also serves to emphasize the positive emotions of the song and create a strong sense of unity.

In summary, Hymn for the Weekend (Alan Walker Remix) by Coldplay is a song about the joys of being in love and how it can provide a sense of euphoria. The lyrics are uplifting and positive, with a strong emphasis on unity and joy. This is a great song to listen to when you’re feeling down and need a pick-me-up.
