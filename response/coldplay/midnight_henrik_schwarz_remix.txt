Midnight (Henrik Schwarz Remix) by Coldplay is a dark and contemplative track, painting a picture of someone struggling with the darkness of their own despair. The song is framed around the idea of leaving a light on in the darkness to provide hope and comfort in moments of difficulty. 

The song opens with the line, “In the darkness before the dawn,” setting the tone of the song. This phrase implies a time of darkness and turmoil before the hope of the dawn arrives. This metaphor is further repeated in the chorus, “Leave a light, a light on.” These phrases are a call to leave a beacon of hope in the darkest of times, a request to not give up when faced with adversity. 

The verses of the song speak to the difficulty that comes with being far away from home, the feeling of being adrift in a sea of troubles. The chorus of “Leave a light, a light on” serves as a reminder to not give up, to keep holding on in the midst of the darkness. 

The song’s lyrics are simple yet effective, conveying a powerful message of hope in the midst of despair. The music is slow and haunting, creating an atmosphere of sadness and longing. The Henrik Schwarz remix adds a darker, more electronic feel to the track, creating a more intense atmosphere. 

Overall, Midnight (Henrik Schwarz Remix) by Coldplay is a powerful song about finding solace in the darkness and never giving up in the face of adversity. The lyrics are simple yet powerful, and the music is haunting and effective. It is a reminder that even in the darkest of times, there is always a light to be found.
