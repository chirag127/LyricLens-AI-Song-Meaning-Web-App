The song Charlie Brown (Jacques Lu Cont Remix) by Coldplay is a hauntingly beautiful track about chasing freedom and love in a chaotic world. The song starts off with a pensive guitar riff and a steady beat that sets the tone for the lyrics. The lyrics tell a story of a lost individual who seeks solace in the lost boys of the city. It is not clear what they offered him, but he takes it and sets himself free. The chorus suggests that the individual found something or somebody to ignite a flame in his heart, to bring color and life to his "scarecrow dreams" and set him free. 

The second verse brings a feeling of hope and joy, as the individual finds a sense of belonging and connection with the lost boys and girls of the city. The chorus repeats and the song ends with a message of resilience against the chaos of the world. 

The song is a reflection of Coldplay's signature style, combining melancholic lyrics with a powerful, uplifting chorus. The lyrics are a poignant reminder of the power of love and freedom, and the importance of standing together and finding strength in one another. 

From a linguistic analysis perspective, the song expresses a wide range of emotions, from sorrow to hope and joy. The lyrics are full of imagery, with references to nature and the night sky, which adds to the melancholic tone of the song. The lyrics also contain a sense of rebellion, with references to "run wild" and "run riot", which suggests a desire to break free from the restraints of society. 

Overall, Charlie Brown (Jacques Lu Cont Remix) by Coldplay is a powerful and emotive track, exploring themes of freedom and love in a chaotic world. The lyrics are full of imagery and emotion, making it a deeply affecting song to listen to.
