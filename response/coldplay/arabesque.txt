"Arabesque" by Coldplay is a beautiful and moving song about the connections and similarities between people. The lyrics, written by Chris Martin and Stromae, are full of poetic imagery and bittersweet sentiment. The song paints a vivid picture of two individuals who, despite their differences, share the same blood. Its powerful message of unity and inclusivity is further emphasized by the sample of Femi Kuti's "Music is the Weapon". 

The song starts with Chris Martin's lyrics, which express the idea that he and another person could be the same, two raindrops in the same sea. He continues to draw comparisons, such as two angels of the same view, emphasizing the idea that even if people seem different, there is a connection between them and they share the same blood. 

The interlude features Stromae's rap, which furthers the message of unity. He states that he will champion those who are different and emphasizes the idea that people can come together, two drops of water that make a great river. Chris Martin's chorus follows, repeating the idea of sharing the same blood.

The song then takes a more upbeat turn with the saxophone solo and the sample of Femi Kuti's "Music is the Weapon". The sample emphasizes the idea that music can bring people together and be used to fight injustice. The chorus returns to conclude the song, repeating the idea that people share the same blood.

In summary, "Arabesque" is a powerful song about the connections between people and the idea that, despite our differences, we all share the same blood. Through its lyrics and sample, the song conveys a powerful message of unity and inclusivity. The song is a great example of Coldplay's ability to create meaningful and thought-provoking music. 

From a linguistic analysis, the song's lyrics depict emotions of hope and unity. The use of poetic imagery and repetition serves to emphasize the idea of connection and togetherness. Additionally, the sample of Femi Kuti's song serves to create an atmosphere of empowerment and strength. 

Overall, "Arabesque" is an emotionally charged and thought-provoking song about unity and inclusivity. Its poetic lyrics, powerful message, and sample of "Music is the Weapon" make it an inspiring and meaningful song.
