Major Minus by Coldplay is an empowering and inspiring song that speaks to the power of facing one's fears and becoming the person they are meant to be. The lyrics present a metaphor for being watched by an ominous force and being aware of the fact that nothing they say is true. The song encourages the listener to take control of their lives and to not let anyone else dictate their actions.

The song opens with the line, "they got one eye watching you one eye on what you do so be careful who it is you're talking to", speaking to the feeling of being watched, and the need to be careful when it comes to trusting others. The chorus follows up with, "got one eye on the road and one on you", emphasizing the need to remain vigilant, and to be prepared for any situation.

The second verse builds on the metaphor, introducing the idea of crocodiles being a representation of the ominous force. The chorus repeats, this time emphasizing the need to keep moving and to not give in to fear. The bridge builds on the chorus, with "got my right side fighting while my left hides under the chairs".

Overall, Major Minus is a powerful song that speaks to the need to stay strong and face one's fears. The lyrics speak to the need to be aware of one's surroundings and to take control of one's life. The song encourages the listener to take charge and to be the person they are meant to be, despite the obstacles that may come their way.

The linguistic analysis of the song reveals that the lyrics of Major Minus express a sense of empowerment, strength, and perseverance. The use of metaphors such as the 'one eye watching you', 'crocodiles ticking around the world', and 'right side fighting while my left hides under the chairs' all help to emphasize the need to remain vigilant and to stay strong. The song also speaks to the need to take control of one's life and to not let anyone else dictate their actions.
