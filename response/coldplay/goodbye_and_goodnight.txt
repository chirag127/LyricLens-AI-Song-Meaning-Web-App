Goodbye and Goodnight by Coldplay is a melancholic and introspective song that speaks of regret and remorse in the face of a relationship ending. The song revolves around the idea of a river and the journey it takes the narrator on, which is symbolic of his life journey. The song begins with the narrator reflecting on how far the river has taken him, and how it has taken his son away from him. This is a metaphor for how he has been left behind, and his son has moved on with his life. The narrator then speaks of a boat that is going around the harbour and he is waiting for it to come back to him. This is a metaphor for how he is waiting for a chance to redeem himself, and to make things right with his son. 

The chorus of the song speaks of the narrator hitting down a button, which is a metaphor for the choices he has made in his life. He expresses regret at not being able to describe the stage he has been put in, showing his sense of helplessness in his current situation. He also expresses remorse at having been known to hurry, and wishes he could take things back and make it right. 

The song ends with the narrator expressing how sorry he is for the choices he has made, and hoping that he can be forgiven for the mistakes he has made. This is symbolic of his desire to make things right, even though he knows it may be too late.

Through the use of metaphors, the song speaks of the narrator's journey through life, and the sadness he feels at his current situation.
