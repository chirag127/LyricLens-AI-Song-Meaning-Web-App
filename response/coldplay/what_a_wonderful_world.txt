 "What a Wonderful World" by Coldplay is a heartfelt song that celebrates the beauty of life and the joy of simply being alive. The song opens with the narrator observing the beauty of the natural world around them, noting the “trees that are green, red roses too” and how they “bloom for me and you”. This image once again conveys the idea that the beauty of the world is something to be appreciated and celebrated.

This conveys the idea that human connection and friendship are some of the most important aspects of life, and should be cherished. In summary, the song is a celebration of life and the beauty of the world around us.
