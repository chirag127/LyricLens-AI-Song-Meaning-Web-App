Coldplay's song "Shiver (Live @ Rockefeller Music Hall)" is an emotional and powerful song that speaks to the heart. The lyrics speak of unrequited love and longing, and the desire to be seen and appreciated by someone who is indifferent. The song starts off in a melancholic tone, with the narrator looking in the direction of the person they love, only to be met with no attention or acknowledgment. This loneliness is made even more palpable by the stanza of "But on and on from the moment I wake 'til the moment I sleep, I'll be there by your side. Just you try and stop me. I'll be waiting in line just to see if you care." 

The chorus is a powerful call for the other person to listen and to understand how much they need them. The singer begs, "Don't you shiver, don't you shiver. Sing it loud and clear, I'll always be waiting for you." The chorus then repeats itself in the bridge, with the singer declaring that they will always be waiting for the other person, even if it takes a lifetime. 

The song is a beautiful expression of longing and desperation. The lyrics are filled with emotion and passionate pleas for the other person to listen, and the music reflects this emotion, with a slow and melancholic melody that builds up to a powerful and intense crescendo. The lyrics also contain a certain amount of hope, as the singer is determined to wait and to never give up on the other person, despite the lack of response.

Lyrically, the song is a powerful expression of longing, loneliness, and hope. The lyrics also contain a certain amount of optimism, as the singer is determined to wait for the other person, no matter how long it takes. The song is both a powerful and emotional expression of love, and a powerful testament to the power of hope in the face of adversity. 

Overall, "Shiver (Live @ Rockefeller Music Hall)" is a powerful and emotional song that speaks to the heart. The song is a powerful expression of love and hope, and a testament to the power of perseverance in the face of adversity.
