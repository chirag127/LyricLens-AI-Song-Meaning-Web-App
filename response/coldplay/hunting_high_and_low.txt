"Hunting High And Low" by Coldplay is a song about the lengths one will go to in order to find love. The lyrics express the narrator's longing for someone, and his determination to never give up on his search. The song starts with the narrator reflecting on his current situation, and how he is unable to reach the person he desires. The second verse of the song further expresses the narrator's longing for this person, as he talks about being able to hear the pounding of her heart next to his. This line speaks to the intensity of his feelings, and how the thought of being with her is the sweetest love he could find. 

In summary, "Hunting High And Low" is a song about the lengths one will go to in order to find and keep love. The lyrics speak to the intensity of the narrator's feelings, and how he is willing to make any sacrifice in order to be with this person. 

The lyrics of this song are very emotive, and evoke a strong sense of longing and desire. The imagery used in the lyrics also helps to express the narrator's innermost feelings, and create a vivid picture of the situation.
