"All Your Friends" by Coldplay is a mysterious and melancholic song that speaks of betrayal and loss. The lyrics capture the feeling of being abandoned and left behind, as the singer watches all of their friends ride into the sunset and away from them. The imagery of the sun setting is used to symbolize the end of a relationship and the feeling of being alone. 

The verses of the song describe the singer's feelings of loss, as they watch their friends ride away and be "thrown into the fire". The chorus of the song speaks of the singer's desire to join their friends, to "ride into the sunset and be taken home".

Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a melancholic tone, as the singer expresses their feelings of betrayal and loneliness. Throughout the song, the singer emphasizes their abandonment and the feeling of being left behind, as they are constantly being left out of the group. 

In summary, "All Your Friends" by Coldplay is a melancholic song that speaks of betrayal and being left behind. The lyrics express the singer's feelings of abandonment and loneliness, as they watch their friends ride away and be taken home. The song is both sad and relatable, as it speaks of the pain of being betrayed and left behind.
