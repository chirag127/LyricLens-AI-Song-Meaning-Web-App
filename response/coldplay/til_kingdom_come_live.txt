"Til Kingdom Come" by Coldplay is an emotional and powerful ballad. The song is written from the perspective of someone who is desperately longing for a loved one. The singer is pouring out their heart, begging for the loved one to come and set them free.

The chorus is a plea to the loved one, begging them to hold the singer's head in their hands, as they need someone who understands. The singer has been waiting for them for years and is willing to wait until their day is done, until kingdom come. They are begging for the other person to come and set them free, asking them to just wait for them. 

The second verse emphasizes the intensity of the singer's feelings, comparing them to the fierceness of a fire and the destructive power of a flood. Despite the intensity of their emotions, they wouldn't change a single thing. 

The chorus is repeated again, emphasizing the desperation in the singer's plea for their loved one to come and set them free. 

In summary, "Til Kingdom Come" by Coldplay is a powerful ballad that expresses the intensity of longing and love. The lyrics are full of emotion, and the singer's plea for the loved one to come and set them free is a desperate one. The song is a perfect mix of hope and despair, and speaks to the power of love.
