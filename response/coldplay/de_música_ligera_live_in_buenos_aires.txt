"De Música Ligera (Live In Buenos Aires)" by Coldplay is a romantic song about a young person's attempt to rekindle a lost love and find the courage to express their feelings. The lyrics tell the story of a young person who was once in love, but was never able to express their feelings. The verses describe the person's regret and longing for the past, and the chorus is a plea for a second chance.

The chorus is a plea for a second chance, with the lyrics "Nada nos libra, nada más queda" (Nothing saves us, nothing else remains) emphasizing the narrator's desperation. The second verse sees the narrator finally sending the letter, but it is too late, and they know they have lost their chance. 

The song is notable for its emotional depth and its combination of Latin music elements with rock and pop. The lyrics are evocative and expressive, and the music builds up the tension as the narrator's story progresses. The chorus is especially powerful, with its mix of Latin rhythms and rock-style power chords. 

Overall, "De Música Ligera (Live In Buenos Aires)" is an emotionally charged song with a powerful message. The combination of Latin music elements and rock and pop create an atmosphere of intense emotion, and the lyrics are full of longing and regret. The song is a great example of Coldplay's ability to create powerful and evocative music, and it is sure to resonate with listeners.
