The song "Sometimes It Snows In April" by Coldplay is a melancholic ballad about the death of a close friend and the pain that it brings. The narrator is left reflecting on the life of his lost friend and the memories they shared, and wishing that life could have been different. The lyrics paint a vivid picture of the narrator's grief, with the title phrase of "sometimes it snows in April" likely referring to the coldness of death and the sorrow that it brings.

The song starts with the narrator describing the death of his friend, Tracy, and how he wished he had been able to save him. He reflects on the fleeting nature of life and how quickly it can be taken away. The narrator then speaks of the sorrow he feels at the loss of his friend, and how he wishes he could see him again.

The chorus of the song paints a vivid picture of the narrator's grief, and how he wishes life could be different. He wishes life never ended, and that all the good things he was promised never lasted. The use of the phrase "springtime only reminds me of Tracy's tears" highlights the narrator's despair, as a time that should be full of life and joy is now filled with sadness.

The second verse of the song speaks of the narrator's dream of heaven, and how he believes that Tracy has found someone else there. He also ponders that maybe one day he will see Tracy again, and that the snow in April may not be a sign of sadness, but of hope.

The song is a powerful reflection on the finality of death and the grief that it brings. It speaks of the narrator's sadness at the loss of his friend, and the acceptance of his grief. The lyrics also explore the idea of finding hope in the midst of sorrow, and of never forgetting those who have passed away.

The linguistic analysis of the song lyrics reveals a strong emotion of sadness and grief. The narrator expresses his despair at the loss of his friend, and his longing to see him again. The use of the phrase "sometimes it snows in April" paints a vivid image of the coldness of death, and the sorrow that it brings. Additionally, the narrator speaks of his dream of heaven and of finding hope in the midst of despair. The lyrics are filled with sorrow, but ultimately are a reflection on the hope of finding solace in the midst of grief.
