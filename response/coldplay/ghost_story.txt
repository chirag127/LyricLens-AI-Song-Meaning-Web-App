The song “Ghost Story” by Coldplay is a haunting and melancholic ballad about the feeling of being invisible and unacknowledged by the person you love. It follows a narrator who is struggling to get through to his lover, but no matter how hard he tries, she doesn’t seem to hear him. He feels like a ghost, unable to make any impact on her or their relationship.

The song begins with the narrator feeling like a ghost, invisible to the person he loves. He describes himself as “disappearing when anybody’s close” and “going through you when you try to hold” him. He feels like he is an empty shell, “emptier than anybody knows”, and even wonders if he is “on the ropes” or “not even here”.

The chorus follows with the narrator trying to make a connection with his lover but being met with walls that seem to grow every time he attempts to break through them. He questions why he should feel love for her if she doesn’t believe he exists, and why he should try to call out for help if no one ever hears him. He is left feeling frustrated and alone, wondering why she won’t let him close.

In the bridge, the narrator compares himself to a ghost, a “whisper in a puff of smoke”, and a “thorn without a rose”, something that people fear. He expresses his helplessness in the situation, feeling like he can never break through the walls she has built around her.

The song ends with the narrator coming to the realization that his efforts are in vain and his lover will never hear him. No matter how hard he tries, she will never let him close.

The song “Ghost Story” is a powerful and emotional expression of the feeling of being invisible and unacknowledged by the person you love. The lyrics explore the frustration of trying to make a connection with someone who doesn’t believe you exist, and the feeling of being left alone in the dark. The song is a haunting and melancholic reminder that sometimes, all our efforts are in vain and that no matter how hard we try, some people will never let us close.

A linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals the narrator’s feelings of helplessness, confusion and despair. The tone of the song is melancholic, using words such as “ghost”, “disappear”, “empty”, “rope”, “wall”, “fear” and “whisper” to express the narrator’s inner turmoil and his inability to make a connection with his beloved. The song also uses the metaphor of a ghost to express the narrator’s feelings of invisibility and unacknowledged love.

Overall, “Ghost Story” is a powerful and emotional song about the feeling of being invisible and unacknowledged by the person you love. The song’s haunting and melancholic lyrics are a poignant reminder of the powerlessness we can sometimes feel in the face of unrequited love.
