"Viva La Vida (Thin White Duke mix)" by Coldplay is a powerful and poignant song that speaks to the impermanence of human power. The song is about the singer's experience of being a powerful ruler, who suddenly finds himself dethroned and powerless. 

The first verse begins with the singer describing his former life as a powerful ruler, whose word could cause the seas to rise. The second verse then shifts to the present, where the singer is now "sleeping alone" and "sweeping the streets [he] used to own". This second verse paints a vivid picture of the singer's sudden fall from power and highlights the loneliness and despair he feels. 

The third verse sees the singer reflecting on his time as a ruler and the fear he instilled in his enemies. This verse further emphasizes the power he once had and allows us to understand the gravity of his fall. The fourth verse is perhaps the most poignant, with the singer describing his "castles [standing] upon pillars of salt and pillars of sand", which serves as a metaphor for his power being fragile and impermanent. 

The chorus of the song sees the singer reflecting on his downfall and calling to "Jerusalem bells aringing" and "Roman cavalry choirs [singing]" to be his "mirror, [his] sword and shield". This chorus serves as a call for strength and guidance in the face of his newfound powerlessness. The bridge of the song then sees the singer asking for a "silver plate" - a sign of his impending death - and realizing that he is now just a puppet on a "lonely string". 

The song ends on a somber note, with the singer reflecting on his time as a ruler and the lack of honesty that surrounded it. He realizes that his power was never permanent and reflects on the fact that "Saint Peter won't call [his] name". 

In summary, "Viva La Vida (Thin White Duke mix)" is a powerful and poignant song that speaks to the impermanence of human power. The song speaks to the loneliness and despair of suddenly losing power and serves as a warning to those in power that their reign may not last forever. 

The lyrics of the song are highly emotive, with the singer's emotions of powerlessness, loneliness, and despair coming through strongly in the song. The language used in the song is also poetic and evocative, using metaphors and imagery to convey the singer's experience.
