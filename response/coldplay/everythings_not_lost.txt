Everything's Not Lost by Coldplay is a song about finding hope in times of difficulty, despite feeling overwhelmed and neglected. The song begins with the narrator counting up their demons, or negative experiences, and realizing that they are outnumbered by the good things in their life. This leads to a feeling of optimism, as the narrator is able to drive away the negative things. In the chorus, the narrator offers hope to anyone who is feeling neglected or thinks all is lost, encouraging them to count up their demons and hope that everything is not lost. The song then transitions to the narrator reflecting on their own mistakes and apologizing for them, but also emphasizing that life is for living and should not be done alone. The chorus is repeated with added emphasis, and the song ends with a call to action to sing out and remember that everything is not lost.

The song speaks to the power of hope and resilience in the face of adversity. It is an uplifting reminder that, although things may seem bleak, there is always something to be hopeful for. The lyrics are filled with emotion, reflecting on both the pain of negative experiences as well as the joy of new beginnings. The use of repetition in the chorus is effective in conveying the message of hope and emphasizing its importance.

A linguistic analysis of the song reveals a range of emotions, from despair to optimism. The lyrics are filled with words of sadness and regret, such as "demons" and "wrong". However, these words of sadness are juxtaposed with words of hope, such as "not lost" and "everything's not lost". This reflects the overall message of the song, that hope can be found in the midst of difficulty. Additionally, the song employs a call-and-response structure with the chorus, encouraging the listener to sing out and join in the message of hope.
