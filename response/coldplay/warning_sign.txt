"Warning Sign" by Coldplay is a song about regret, longing, and hope for reconciliation. The lyrics tell the story of a person who is reflecting on a relationship that has ended, and the warning sign the person missed that could have saved the relationship. The song begins with the narrator saying that they missed the good part, and then "the bubble burst." This could be interpreted as a metaphor for a relationship that started out as a dream or a fantasy, but ended up being too fragile to last. 

The chorus then repeats the line "I started looking for a warning sign," which could be interpreted as the narrator's realization that there were signs that the relationship was not going to last, and they should have paid attention to them. The narrator expresses their regret in the lines "when the truth is, I miss you, yeah, the truth is that I miss you so." This expresses the narrator's longing for the relationship to have worked out, and their regret for not seeing the warning signs. 

The bridge of the song then expresses the narrator's hope for reconciliation, as they "crawl back into your open arms." This could be interpreted as the narrator hoping that the relationship can be repaired, despite the warning signs they missed. 

Overall, "Warning Sign" is a song about regret, longing, and hope for reconciliation. The lyrics contain linguistic analysis of the emotions and feelings that the narrator is experiencing, and the language style conveys the narrator's remorse and longing for the relationship to have worked out. The song is a reflection of the narrator's realization that they should have paid attention to the warning signs that the relationship was not going to last, and their hope that the relationship can still be repaired.
