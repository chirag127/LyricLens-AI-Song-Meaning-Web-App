The song “First Steps” by Coldplay is an emotional and touching song about the struggles of life and the need for help when it all seems too overwhelming. The lyrics focus on the protagonist’s need for someone to help them out and the idea that no one can do it alone. It is a plea for help and understanding without judgment or expectations. 

The first verse of the song sets up the theme of the need for help, with the protagonist asking for help and saying that there isn’t anyone to call for that. This reflects a feeling of loneliness and helplessness that many people experience in life. The second verse speaks to the fear of being alone and the idea that even when the sun sets and the dark begins to climb, we still need a helping hand. 

The chorus of the song speaks to the idea that even when we try our luck and take risks, we need to rely on the grace of God to get us through. This is a powerful reminder that even when life seems overwhelming, there is always something larger at play. 

The song is an emotional and powerful reminder that we all need help at times, and that no one can do it alone. It speaks to the importance of relying on others and having faith that things will work out in the end. Linguistically, the lyrics are emotive and full of longing, reflecting the need for help and understanding. 

In summary, “First Steps” is an emotional and powerful song that speaks to the need for help and understanding in difficult times. Its lyrics are emotive and reflective of the need for a helping hand, and the song serves as a reminder that we all need help at times.
