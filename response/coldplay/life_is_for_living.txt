"Life Is for Living" by Coldplay is a song about regret, reflection, and the importance of living life to the fullest. The lyrics explore the idea of making mistakes and understanding that life is too short to waste away in regret. The song begins with the narrator expressing remorse for any wrongs they may have done, but asking for forgiveness and a second chance. This sets the tone for the rest of the song, which is about learning from mistakes and not letting them stand in the way of a better future.

The chorus of the song encourages the listener to live life to the fullest and enjoy the moments, rather than dwelling on the past and feeling regret. The chorus also has a lighthearted, uplifting feel, with the “ah ah oh” melody providing a sense of joy and freedom. This upbeat chorus serves as a reminder that life is too short to waste away in sorrow and regret, and that we should strive to make the most of the time we have.

The lyrics of "Life Is for Living" are quite simple, but they are delivered in an emotive and meaningful way. The lyrics reflect the idea that mistakes are a part of life, but that we should strive to learn from them and not let them define us. The lyrics also emphasize the importance of living life to the fullest and enjoying every moment.

From a linguistic analysis, the song expresses a wide range of emotions and feelings, from sorrow and regret to joy and freedom. The lyrics use simple language to convey complex emotions, which helps to emphasize the importance of understanding one's mistakes and not letting them stand in the way of a better future.

Overall, "Life Is for Living" is a powerful reminder that life is too short to waste away in regret, and that we should strive to make the most of the time we have. The song encourages listeners to learn from their mistakes and embrace life, however difficult it may be.
