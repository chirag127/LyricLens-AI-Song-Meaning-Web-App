"Clocks/Relojes" by Coldplay is an emotive and reflective song that brings up themes of loneliness, confusion, and a sense of longing for a home. The song is built around the metaphor of time passing, with the ticking of clocks and the tides rising and falling representing the passing of time and the feeling that the protagonist is being left behind. 

The song begins with the protagonist feeling trapped, as the “lights go out” and they have “tried to swim against” the tides. They are left on their knees, begging and pleading for a way out. This desperation is then contrasted with a sense of hope, as the protagonist is willing to “shoot an apple off my head” and take a risk in order to escape their current situation.

The chorus of the song emphasizes the protagonist's feeling of being lost, with “confusion never stops” and “closing walls and ticking clocks” representing the sense of being stuck in an inescapable situation. The chorus is then followed by a resolution, as the protagonist realizes that the only way out is to “come back and take you home”. This realization is then followed by the realization that the protagonist is “part of the cure or am I part of the disease”, showing that the protagonist has come to terms with the choices that they have made and the consequences of their actions.

The song ends with the protagonist finding a sense of comfort in the idea of home, singing “you are home, home where I wanted to go”. This is a powerful conclusion to the song, as the protagonist realizes that they can still find peace and solace in the idea of home, even if they have been lost or confused in the past.

In summary, “Clocks/Relojes” by Coldplay is a reflective and emotive song that explores themes of loneliness, confusion, and a sense of longing for a home. It is a song that is both melancholic and hopeful, as the protagonist struggles to find their way out of their situation and ultimately finds solace in the idea of home.

From a linguistic analysis perspective, the lyrics of the song are full of emotion, with words like “beg”, “plead”, and “trouble” conveying the sense of desperation that the protagonist is feeling. At the same time, words like “cure” and “home” convey a sense of hope and resolution. The song also makes use of repetition in the chorus, emphasizing the idea of being stuck in a cycle of confusion and uncertainty.

Overall, “Clocks/Relojes” by Coldplay is an emotionally powerful song that conveys a sense of loneliness and confusion, as well as hope and resolution. It is a song that is sure to resonate with listeners and can provide comfort in times of uncertainty.
