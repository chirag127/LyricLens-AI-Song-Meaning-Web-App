The third verse is a repetition of the first two verses, emphasizing the importance of sleep and its power to bring satisfaction. 

The song ultimately speaks of the power of sleep to bring peace and satisfaction. It encourages the listener to let go of their worries and insecurities and just rest in the peace of sleep.
