The song “You Only Live Twice” by Coldplay is a song about finding and cherishing love, and taking risks to make your dreams come true. The song starts off with a simple but powerful message, “you only live twice or so it seems, one life for yourself and one for your dreams.” This line speaks to the idea of taking risks to make your dreams come true, as one life is not enough to achieve all your dreams and goals. The idea is further reinforced in the following lines, “you drift through the years and life seems tame/ 'til one dream appears and love is its name.” The lyrics speak to the idea that it is only when one finds love that life can truly feel meaningful and purposeful. 

The song then introduces the idea of taking risks for love, “and love is a stranger who'll beckon you on/ don't think of the danger or the stranger is gone.” This line speaks to the idea that taking risks for love is often viewed as a dangerous thing, but it is a risk worth taking as the reward is great. The chorus of the song reinforces this message, “this dream is for you so pay the price/ make one dream come true you only live twice.” The song then ends with a repetition of the chorus, reinforcing the idea that taking risks and cherishing love is an essential part of life. 

The song’s lyrics are full of emotional depth, speaking to the idea of taking risks and cherishing love. The song speaks to the idea of taking risks and cherishing love, even though it may be a dangerous thing to do. The lyrics also speak to the idea of living life to the fullest, as life is too short not to take risks and cherish love. The song is an uplifting reminder to take risks and cherish love, as life is too short not to.

In terms of a linguistic analysis of the song’s lyrics, the song is written in a fairly straightforward manner, with no real use of figurative language. The language used is simple and direct, but the lyrics still manage to convey a lot of emotion and depth. The use of repetition in the chorus effectively emphasizes the message of the song. The lyrics also contain a lot of references to dreams and love, which speak to the idea of taking risks and cherishing love. 

Overall, “You Only Live Twice” by Coldplay is a song about taking risks and cherishing love.
