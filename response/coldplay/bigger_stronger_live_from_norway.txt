Bigger Stronger (Live from Norway) by Coldplay is an upbeat and inspirational song. The lyrics explore the idea of striving for greatness, and the desire to break free from stagnant routines and embrace new possibilities. The song starts off with the lyrics “I wanna be bigger, stronger, drive a faster car to take me anywhere in seconds to take me anywhere I wanna go and drive around a faster car I will settle for nothing less”. This sets the tone for the rest of the song, as it emphasizes the need to break out of the mundane and reach for something greater. 

The chorus of the song is a repeating chant which further emphasizes these themes. “Bigger and better, bigger and better, bigger and stronger, drive a faster car” is repeated over and over, symbolizing the importance of continuously striving for more. The chorus also serves as a call to action, reminding the listener that they have the power to make the changes they desire. 

The lyrics are full of imagery and emotion, which helps the listener to connect to the themes being explored in the song. The use of the phrase “change my altitude”, for example, suggests the idea of looking at things from a different perspective and suggests that the listener can overcome obstacles and achieve their goals. The phrase “change my oxygen” hints at the idea of rejuvenating, which suggests that the listener can find the strength to carry on, even in difficult times. 

In summary, Bigger Stronger (Live from Norway) is a song about breaking out of a mundane routine and striving for something greater. The lyrics are full of imagery and emotion which helps the listener to connect to the themes being explored in the song. Through the use of repetition and vivid imagery, the song encourages the listener to take action and make the changes they desire. It is an inspirational and uplifting song that serves as a reminder that anything is possible.
