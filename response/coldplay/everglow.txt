"Everglow" by Coldplay is a song that speaks of the power of relationships, even after a loved one has gone. The lyrics describe a particular diamond that was "extra special" and how, even though the person is gone, the narrator still feels their presence. The diamond is used as a metaphor for the connection that the narrator still feels. The lyrics also describe how life moves on and cars don't slow down, even when it feels like the end of the world. 

The chorus of the song emphasizes the idea of everlasting love, as the narrator remembers the light of the departed person that will never fade, and that they will always love them. This is further emphasized by the lyrics "if you love someone, you should let them know". 

The song is a beautiful reminder of the power of love and how it can never be lost, even after a person is gone. The chorus is particularly poignant, emphasizing the idea of everlasting love, and the idea that even if a person is gone, their love can still be felt. 

Linguistically, the song is filled with imagery and metaphors, painting a vivid picture of the narrator's feelings of loss and love. The use of a diamond as a metaphor is particularly powerful, conveying the idea of a strong and lasting connection that cannot be broken. Additionally, the use of the phrase "everglow" is symbolic, indicating that the love of the departed person still shines brightly, even after they have gone. 

In summary, "Everglow" by Coldplay is a beautiful reminder of the power of love and how it can never be lost. The lyrics are filled with powerful imagery and metaphors that convey the idea of a strong and lasting connection, and the use of the phrase "everglow" is symbolic, indicating that the love of the departed person still shines brightly, even after they have gone.
