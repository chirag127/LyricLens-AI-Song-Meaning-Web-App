Daylight by Coldplay is a song that speaks to the power of the sun in our lives. The opening verse describes the narrator's surprise and delight when they witness the sunrise, the first light of the day. They go on to note how insignificant they are in the face of such a powerful force, and how the clouds burst to reveal the daylight. The chorus emphasizes that the sun will always shine on them, no matter what happens, as they realize that they cannot live without it.

The second verse speaks to the feeling of joy and renewal that comes with the sunrise, comparing it to a newborn child being welcomed into the world. Here, the narrator notes how the darkness is defeated by the daylight and encourages listeners to appreciate the beauty of the morning light. The chorus repeats in the bridge, emphasizing the importance of the sun in the narrator's life.

The song serves as a reminder of the importance of the sun in our lives, and how it can bring us joy and hope, even in the darkest of times. The lyrics evoke a feeling of awe and wonder at the power of the sun, as well as a sense of comfort and security that comes with its presence. In terms of linguistic analysis, the lyrics of the song are filled with powerful imagery and metaphors, such as the clouds bursting to reveal the daylight and the sun being like a newborn child. These metaphors illustrate the narrator's appreciation of the sun and its power in their life. 

Overall, Daylight by Coldplay is an uplifting and inspiring song that speaks to the power of the sun and its importance in our lives. Through its powerful lyrics and evocative imagery, the song encourages listeners to find hope and joy in the presence of the sun.
