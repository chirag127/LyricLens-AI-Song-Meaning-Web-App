"Only Superstition" by Coldplay is a song about the fear of the unknown, and the way it can take a toll on our lives. The lyrics are framed around the idea of a "cardboard head," an image that symbolizes the fragility of life and our inability to know what will come next. The song speaks of how this fear can paralyze us and keep us from taking risks and embracing life.

The first verse speaks of the "cardboard head," and how it has found its way to the narrator, making them feel helpless and scared. The chorus speaks of the fear of the unknown, and how it can spoil our day, and encourages us to not be defeated by superstition and fear.

The second verse speaks of how we can keep clean, stand still and wait in line, and how some numbers (like lucky numbers) can bring us solace. The chorus repeats again, this time emphasizing the idea of superstition and fear only being in our imagination.

The final verse speaks of the pain and sadness caused by fear, and how it can make us slip away. The chorus repeats again, this time emphasizing the idea of superstition being nothing more than fear and imagination.

In summary, this song is about the fear of the unknown, and how it can take a toll on us. The lyrics emphasize the idea of not letting superstition and fear control our lives, and instead learning to embrace uncertainty.

When it comes to the linguistic analysis of the song, the lyrics of "Only Superstition" convey feelings of fear and helplessness. The narrator speaks of being paralyzed by fear and losing control, as well as the idea of clinging to superstitions for solace. The lyrics also showcase a strong sense of resilience, with the narrator telling themselves to not be defeated and to keep going. This speaks to the idea of conquering fear and finding strength in the face of uncertainty.
