The song "We Never Change" by Coldplay is a powerful exploration of the human condition and the difficulty of change. The lyrics present a longing for something better, a desire to do the right thing, and an acknowledgement that we often fail to do so. The song begins by expressing a wish to "Live life and never be cruel" and to "Be good to you", followed by a desire to "Fly and never come down". This expresses a longing for something greater, something beyond our normal lives, and a desire to reach for something better. 

The chorus of the song then repeats the main theme: "We never change, do we? No, no, we never learn, do we?". This line reflects the difficulty of achieving change, as it is often easier to stay in our comfort zone and to continue doing what we are used to, even when it may not be the best course of action. 

The second verse then presents a vision of a better life, one "in a wooden house" where "Making more friends would be easy". This suggests the idea that when we are surrounded by positive influences, it is easier to make positive changes and to learn from our mistakes. 

The bridge of the song then acknowledges the difficulty of this journey, with the lines "I don't have a soul to save, yes, and I sin every single day". This reflects the reality that even when we strive to do the right thing, we often fail, and that our human nature is often our greatest obstacle when it comes to achieving lasting change.

In summary, the song "We Never Change" by Coldplay is a powerful exploration of the difficulties of achieving lasting change, and of the human nature that often stands in the way. Through its thoughtful lyrics and heartfelt delivery, the song speaks to the struggle that we all face in our own lives as we strive to become better people.

The song's lyrics also provide an interesting opportunity for linguistic analysis. The lyrics are filled with sentiment and emotion, which can be interpreted to understand the feelings and emotions that Coldplay is expressing. Through the use of words like "cruel", "good", and "sin", the band conveys the difficulty of change, and of the human nature that often stands in the way. The song speaks to the struggle that we all face in our own lives as we strive to become better people, and its message is conveyed through its thoughtful lyrics and heartfelt delivery.
