The song “Crests of Waves” by Coldplay is an uplifting track about not giving up, and having the strength to keep going no matter what. The lyrics are filled with imagery of being lost and alone, and fighting against the odds. In the chorus, the band emphasizes the importance of life and love, as these are the only things that truly matter.

The lyrics open with the idea of being “locked in here on [one’s] own”, suggesting feelings of loneliness and isolation. This is juxtaposed with the imagery of a stone that “suddenly drops” and “never stops”, which can be interpreted as a metaphor for the inevitability of life and the need to keep going no matter what. 

The chorus of the song is a powerful statement about the importance of life and love, as these are the only things that truly matter. The band emphasizes that “nothing matters except life and the love you make”, which can be interpreted as a reminder that life is precious and should be cherished.

The final chorus features the repeated line “screaming out from the crests of waves”, which can be interpreted as a call for help. This is a powerful statement about the need for support and understanding, and serves as a reminder that we are not alone in our struggles.

The song “Crests of Waves” by Coldplay is an uplifting and inspiring track that speaks to the importance of life and love. The lyrics are filled with powerful imagery and metaphors, and the chorus is a powerful reminder of the need for support and understanding. The track is a great reminder to never give up, and to find strength in the knowledge that life and love truly matter.
