Wedding Bells by Coldplay is a song about the pain of a relationship ending, despite the singer’s feelings of love. In the first verse, the singer reflects on the wedding bells ringing on the hill and their unwillingness to accept the pain of the situation. The second verse paints a bleak picture of the aftermath, with days of no sleep, poison in their blood, and the loss of someone they love. The chorus then reflects on the wedding bells ringing up on the hill, and the singer’s desire to remain still in spite of the other person continuing to move forward. The song ends with the singer expressing their love for the other person and a reminder that the love will always remain.

The lyrics of Wedding Bells by Coldplay evoke a range of emotions, from sadness to resilience. The lyrics convey the struggle of accepting the end of a relationship and the pain of letting go. The imagery of the wedding bells ringing on the hill symbolizes the bittersweet nature of the situation: while wedding bells often signify the start of something new and joyful, in this case, they signify the end of something that once was. The song also conveys a sense of resilience, with the singer confidently proclaiming their love in spite of the pain. 

The song’s lyrics also feature a range of linguistic devices that help to convey the emotions of the song.
