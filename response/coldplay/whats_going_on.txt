The song "What's Going On" by Coldplay features Bono as a guest artist, and is a tribute to the Marvin Gaye song of the same name. The lyrics offer an exploration of the struggles and injustices that people face in the world, and calls for love and understanding to overcome them. The song begins with Bono singing "Mother, mother, there's too many of you crying/Brother, brother, there's too many of you dying," which serves to emphasize the importance of addressing the issues that cause suffering and death. This is further emphasized by the chorus, which calls for love and understanding as a way to bring peace. The song then continues with a call to action, asking people to come together and fight for what is right.

The song also offers an analysis of the issues that people face, such as poverty, war, and injustice. The lyrics emphasize the need for change and justice, and criticize those who seek to punish and oppress people instead of helping them. The chorus serves as a rallying cry for peace and understanding, and the song's message of love and unity resonates throughout.

In terms of a linguistic analysis, the song's lyrics use powerful metaphors, personification, and repetition to convey its message. The lyrics also contain many emotional words such as "cry," "dying," and "hate," which emphasize the emotional power of the song. Additionally, the lyrics use words such as "judge," "strong," and "block," which emphasize the need for collective action.

Overall, "What's Going On" is an emotionally powerful song that calls for change and understanding. The lyrics offer an analysis of the world's issues and a call to action for people to come together in order to bring about peace. The song is a powerful reminder of the importance of love and understanding, and its message is still relevant today.
