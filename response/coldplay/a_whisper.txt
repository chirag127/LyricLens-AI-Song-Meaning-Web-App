A Whisper by Coldplay is a song about the struggles of coming to terms with a loved one's passing. The song opens with the hook, a simple and melancholic melody with the lyrics "Just a whisper, a whisper, a whisper, a whisper". This lyrical repetition serves to emphasize the feeling of helplessness and grief, as if begging for a sign of life from the departed.

The first verse talks about the narrator trying to come to terms with their loss. Through the use of imagery and metaphor, they describe the ticking of clocks and the fading memories of their loved one, emphasizing the feeling of emptiness and loneliness that they feel. 

The chorus is a plea for answers, with the narrator asking if they should move forward or backwards, and still not finding any answers. This serves to emphasize the feeling of confusion and helplessness that the narrator is experiencing.

The second verse continues the theme of the ticking of clocks, with the narrator pleading for the departed to come back and look for them when they are lost. This serves to highlight the feeling of longing for their loved one, as the narrator needs their presence to be able to find their way.

The song's lyrics offer a vivid representation of the grief and loneliness that comes with the loss of a loved one, as the narrator searches for answers and a way to cope with their loss. Through its use of imagery, metaphor, and repetition, Coldplay manages to capture the raw emotions of the narrator and create a powerful and poignant song.

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song uses a combination of metaphors, imagery, and repetition to convey the emotions of the narrator. The emotions of the narrator range from sorrow and confusion to longing and hope, making it a powerful and emotional song. The song also uses a lot of imagery to express the narrator's feelings, with the ticking of clocks being used to symbolize the passing of time and the fading memories of their loved one. 

Overall, A Whisper by Coldplay is a powerful and emotionally charged song that captures the raw emotions of grief and loneliness that come with the passing of a loved one. Through its use of imagery, metaphor, and repetition, Coldplay manages to create an emotionally charged and poignant song that resonates with listeners.
