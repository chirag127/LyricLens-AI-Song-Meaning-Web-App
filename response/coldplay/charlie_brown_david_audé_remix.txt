Charlie Brown (David Audé Remix) by Coldplay is a lively, energetic song with a catchy chorus and uplifting lyrics. The song begins with the chorus, “We'll run wild, we'll be glowing in the dark,” which sets the tone for the rest of the song and hints at its uplifting message. The verses tell the story of a young person who has taken a car downtown and is trying to break free from their current situation. The song encourages them to “be a bright red rose, come bursting through concrete” and to “light a fire, a spark, light a fire, a flame in my heart.” The chorus reinforces these messages of hope, with the line “we'll be glowing in the dark” suggesting that even when things seem dark, there is hope. 

The lyrics of the song are filled with symbolism and metaphors, making it ripe for interpretation. For example, the “scarecrow dreams” line could refer to the idea of being stuck in a rut and the need to break free. The “bright red rose” line could refer to the beauty and hope that can be found in even the most difficult of times. The chorus could symbolize the idea of perseverance and hope in the face of adversity. 

Linguistically, the song is full of positive words and phrases, such as “stole a key,” “lost boys,” “smash my heart,” “bright red rose,” “cartoon heart,” and “glowing in the dark,” which all suggest a sense of hope and renewal. The use of repetition in the chorus further reinforces the message of hope and resilience. 

In conclusion, Charlie Brown (David Audé Remix) by Coldplay is a song about breaking free from the constraints of life and finding hope in the dark. The lyrics are full of symbolism and metaphors, and the use of positive language and repetition in the chorus invokes a sense of hope and perseverance.
