The song "Red Wedding" by Coldplay tells the story of the infamous Red Wedding massacre that took place in George R. R. Martin’s popular fantasy series A Song of Ice and Fire. The song then goes on to detail the events of the massacre, with the singer describing a gruesome scene of bloodshed, heads being severed and a wedding bed that will never be seen. The song ends with the same warning it began with, urging the listener to “don’t mess around with Walder Frey”.

The song is a darkly humorous take on the Red Wedding massacre, with the lighthearted music and playful lyrics masking a tragedy that would become known as one of the most infamous events in Westerosi history. The lyrics serve to both inform the listener about the event, as well as to provide a commentary on the nature of the Red Wedding itself.
