"St. Stephen" by Coldplay is a melancholic and contemplative song that delves deep into the artistic soul of the band. The song reflects on the struggles of life, the search for redemption, and the desire to find peace. 

The song begins by introducing the idea of a silver road of only sin, a metaphor for the difficult and tumultuous journey of life. As the song progresses, we can hear the narrator reflecting on their past mistakes and their desire to find redemption. The narrator pleads for a light to guide them on their journey, a reminder of hope for a brighter future. 

The chorus of the song brings in the titular character, St. Stephen. The lyrics suggest that St. Stephen is a figure of hope, and the narrator is searching for his light to bring them out of their darkness. The song ends with a plea for things to never be the same again, a longing for a new beginning. 

The lyrical content of this song is very meaningful and thought-provoking, with the band using a variety of metaphors and symbols to express their ideas. The language used is mostly somber and reflective, with the band expressing the struggles of life, the pursuit of redemption, and the desire for a new beginning. 

Overall, "St. Stephen" by Coldplay is a powerful and poignant song that speaks to the human experience. It reflects on the struggles of life, the search for redemption, and the desire to find peace. The lyrics are thoughtfully written and the band's use of metaphors and symbols helps to convey the message of the song.
