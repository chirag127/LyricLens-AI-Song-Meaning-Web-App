Coldplay's "Another's Arms" is a deeply emotional song about longing for a lost love and the pain of being without them. The song begins with the singer missing his former partner, wishing for them to be beside him and for their arms to be around him. The second verse finds the singer still longing for his lost love and questioning if anyone is there to reach him, to find him. The chorus then focuses on the pain of being without the person he loves, wishing for another's arms to be around him. 

The song conveys a strong sense of longing and sadness, with the singer asking for someone to pull him close, to pull him through the pain he is experiencing. The imagery of being alone late at night watching TV while his former partner is not there with him is especially heartbreaking. 

Linguistically, the song utilizes a lot of repetition to emphasize the singer's longing and emotions. The repeated phrases of "Another's arms" and "When the world means nothing to me" serve to draw attention to the loneliness and pain of the situation. The imagery of being alone late at night, combined with the repetition of the chorus, serves to emphasize the sadness and emptiness of the singer's situation.
