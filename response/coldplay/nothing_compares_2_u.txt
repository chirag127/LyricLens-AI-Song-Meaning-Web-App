Nothing Compares 2 U by Coldplay is a melancholic and somber love song that conveys the feeling of being left alone and longing for the love of another. In the song, the narrator recounts the time since their lover left, describing how they have been unable to move on and how nothing can compare to the love they had. 

The song starts by describing how long it has been since the narrator’s love left them. It has been seven hours and fifteen days since the love was taken away. The narrator then goes on to say that since the love was taken away, they go out every night and sleep all day, unable to move on from the love that has left them. 

The chorus of the song is the narrator’s realization that nothing can compare to the love that has been taken away. Nothing can take away the blues and nothing can ever compare to the love that has been taken away. 

The verse after the chorus further conveys the sadness and loneliness felt by the narrator. It has been lonely without the love of another, like a bird without a song. The narrator further expresses the sorrow they feel in their heart, describing how nothing can stop the lonely tears from falling. 

The song ends with the narrator asking what they did wrong and why their love left them.

In summary, Nothing Compares 2 U is a song about the feeling of loss after a lover leaves and how nothing can replace the love that has been taken away. The song conveys the sadness, loneliness, and longing felt by the narrator after their love was taken away. 

Linguistically, the song is filled with emotion and sorrow. The lyrics focus on the loneliness and sorrow felt by the narrator and the language used conveys a sense of hopelessness. The use of repetition in the chorus emphasizes the narrator’s realization that nothing can compare to the love that has been taken away. 

Overall, Nothing Compares 2 U is a beautiful and powerful song about love, loss, and longing. The emotion conveyed in the lyrics is raw and powerful, making it an emotional and moving song.
