Postcards from Far Away by Coldplay is a beautiful and heartfelt song that speaks to the longing of a long-distance relationship. The lyrics tell the story of a couple separated by distance, but still connected through postcards sent to each other. The song is filled with emotional imagery and longing, and speaks to the power of love and the strength of a bond that can withstand even the greatest of distances. 

Verse 1:

The first verse starts with the narrator speaking of postcards they have sent to one another, describing them as "poems of a broken heart". This is a powerful metaphor that speaks to the heartache of being separated from the one you love, and how it is manifest in physical objects like postcards. The narrator then speaks of how they are both "looking for a better way to be" and how they are "trying to keep their love alive" even though they cannot be together. 

Verse 2:

The second verse speaks of how the distance between them is only "seventy miles", and how the narrator longs to take that journey and be with the other person. The imagery of the "open sky" is a beautiful way to express the longing of being apart and the hope of one day being together. This verse also speaks to the power of love and how it can "hold us together" even when we are miles apart. 

Verse 3:

The third verse speaks of the power of the postcards they have sent, and how they will keep their love alive even when they are apart. The narrator speaks of how they are "writing down the words" and "sending out the love". This speaks to the power of words, and how even though they are apart, they can still stay connected through the words they write. 

Verse 4:

The fourth and final verse speaks of the hope of being reunited one day, and how the narrator will "come running" when that day comes. This speaks to the hope of one day being together, and how the postcards will keep them connected until that day. 

Summary:

Postcards from Far Away by Coldplay is a beautiful and heartfelt song about the power of love and the strength of a bond even when separated by distance. The song speaks to the longing of being apart and the hope of one day being together. Through the use of powerful imagery and metaphors, the narrator speaks of how postcards can keep them connected and how they will come running when that day arrives. 

Review:

Postcards from Far Away is an incredibly powerful and emotional song that speaks to the power of love and the strength of a bond that can survive even the greatest of distances. The use of powerful imagery and metaphors makes this song incredibly moving and touching, and speaks to the connection that two people can have even when miles apart.
