The song “Harmless” by Coldplay is an exploration of the pain and suffering that can come from feeling helpless and out of control. The lyrics speak to the idea that no matter how much we try to be in control, sometimes we can’t help but feel powerless. Through the use of metaphors, the lyrics are able to convey a sense of helplessness and despair, as well as a hope for a way out. 

The first verse speaks to the idea of calling out for help and feeling as though nothing can be done. The narrator notes that the “self-esteem is low” and that “it was harmless, it couldn’t hurt you.” This is a reference to the fact that even though our attempts to help ourselves can feel pointless, we still have to try. 

The second verse speaks to the idea of feeling out of control and being overpowered by certain emotions or situations. The narrator notes that “chemicals are cold” and that “you sit down on a chemical you cause.” This is an allusion to the idea that we can often be overwhelmed by our own emotions, and that we can become trapped in this cycle of feeling helpless. 

The chorus speaks to the idea of hope, as the narrator reminds us that “I know you’ll get through.” This is a reminder that no matter how bad things may seem, there is always hope for a way out.

In summary, “Harmless” is a song about feeling helpless and out of control, and the hope that comes from knowing that we can still get through it. The lyrics use metaphors to explore these themes, and paint a vivid picture of the pain and despair that can come from feeling powerless. 

The linguistic analysis of the song lyrics suggests a range of emotions and feelings, including hopelessness, despair, and a longing for control. The metaphors used in the song are effective in conveying a sense of helplessness and despair, while still maintaining a sense of hope that things can get better.  Overall, “Harmless” is a powerful reminder that even in our darkest moments, there is still hope for a brighter future.
