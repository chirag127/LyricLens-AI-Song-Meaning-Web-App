"Old Friends" by Coldplay is a nostalgic ballad that reflects on the importance of friendship and how it can be a source of comfort and strength even in the toughest of times. The song begins with the line, "Tony was a friend of mine / We were together all the time," introducing the protagonist's close and long-lasting friendship with Tony. This relationship is further emphasized in the second verse, where the protagonist recounts a time when Tony "really saved [their] life". 

The chorus of the song serves as a reminder of the power of friendship and how it can help us through even the most difficult of times. The lyrics, "Time just deepens, sweetens, and mends old friends / We all melt back into the picture / Raindrops back into the water," paint a poignant image of how friendships can be like a balm, healing and softening the wounds of life. The song ends with a simple "Amen" that serves to emphasize the power and importance of strong friendships. 

The lyrics of "Old Friends" are full of emotion and sentimentality, conveying a deep sense of appreciation for the power of friendship. The lyrics express both the beauty and the fragility of the bond between friends, emphasizing how the love and support of old friends can be a source of comfort and strength even in the darkest of times. 

The song is a touching tribute to the importance of friendship, and is sure to resonate with anyone who has ever had a strong and supportive friendship. It is a reminder that strong friendships can last a lifetime, and that the love and support of old friends can be a source of strength even in times of difficulty.
