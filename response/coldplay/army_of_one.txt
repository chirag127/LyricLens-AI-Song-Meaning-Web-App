"Army of One" by Coldplay is a song about the power of love and the lengths one is willing to go to for the one they love. The song starts off with the singer expressing his experience of travelling around the world and looking for someone like the person he loves. He mentions the wonders of the world he has seen, such as the pyramids and Babylon, and how none of them can compare to the beauty he sees in his beloved. The singer expresses his dedication and commitment to the one he loves, declaring that he would never say die, never be untrue, and never be so high as when he is with them. He also states that there is no fire he wouldn't walk through for them, thus demonstrating the strength of his love. 

The chorus of the song brings an empowering feeling, as the singer declares that he has an "army of one" that will fight for his beloved. The lyrics also mention a storm, likely symbolizing the difficult times the couple will face, and the singer's commitment to standing by his partner's side, no matter what. The song ends with the singer declaring that his heart is his gun, his only weapon, and that his "army of one" will call it through.

Overall, this song is filled with powerful imagery and symbolism that conveys the strength of a powerful love. The lyrics focus on the singer's commitment to his beloved, and the lengths he is willing to go to for them, showcasing the power of love in the face of adversity. A linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals the emotions of dedication, loyalty, and love expressed throughout the song. The song is a powerful reminder of the power of love and the deep connection that can be formed between two people.
