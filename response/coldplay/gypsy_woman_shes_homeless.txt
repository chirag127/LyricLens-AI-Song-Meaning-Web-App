"Gypsy Woman (She's Homeless)" by Coldplay is a thoughtful, emotive song that speaks to the struggles of homelessness and the power of resilience. The song begins with a description of a woman, who wakes up each morning to do her hair and makeup. Through this, the listener is immediately aware of the woman's determination, even in the face of adversity. 

The chorus presents the woman as a symbol of resilience, singing "Ladadeeladada" in hopes of finding a better life. The lyrics of the bridge emphasize her plight, with the repeated phrase "Oh why though? Oh why though?" conveying her pain and confusion. 

The song's main message is one of resilience; despite the hardships of homelessness, the woman stands strong and keeps singing, never giving up hope. This theme of resilience is further emphasized by the instrumental breakdown at the end of the song, which builds to a triumphant conclusion. 

From a linguistic point of view, the lyrics of "Gypsy Woman (She's Homeless)" contain many emotional elements. The repetition of certain phrases and words, such as "Oh why though? Oh why though?", evoke feelings of sadness and despair, while the triumphant conclusion of the song conveys a sense of hope and optimism. Additionally, the use of imagery and metaphor, such as the description of the woman's morning routine, helps to create a vivid picture of her life and struggles. 

Overall, "Gypsy Woman (She's Homeless)" by Coldplay is an emotive and powerful song that speaks to the struggles of homelessness and the power of resilience. The song's thoughtful lyrics and triumphant conclusion make it a moving and inspiring listen.
