The song "Shiver" by Coldplay is a heartfelt and emotional piece about unreciprocated love. The song opens with the narrator expressing his frustrations about being ignored by the one he loves, asking "do you" and "don't you" to emphasize his question. The narrator then goes on to express his commitment to loving this person, saying he will be there from the moment he wakes until the moment he sleeps, no matter what. He then expresses an understanding of why his love isn't reciprocated - that he needs to change - and that he is willing to do so if it will make the other person happy.

The chorus of the song is the narrator's plea for the object of his love to acknowledge him, singing "don't you shiver, sing it loud and clear, I'll always be waiting for you." He expresses that he knows how much he needs them, even though they don't seem to understand. The song ends with the narrator repeating the chorus, emphasizing his commitment to loving them and waiting for them, no matter how long it takes.

The lyrics of the song provide a nuanced exploration of unrequited love, expressing the narrator's emotions of frustration, longing, and hope. Linguistically, the song conveys a sense of desperation and desperation in its use of repetition and questioning. The narrator's repeated requests for acknowledgement are evocative of his desire to be recognized and loved. Additionally, the chorus conveys a sense of hope and optimism, as the narrator is willing to wait for the other person to reciprocate his feelings. 

Overall, "Shiver" is an evocative and heartfelt exploration of unreciprocated love, conveying the narrator's emotions of frustration, longing, and hope. The lyrics provide a nuanced exploration of the narrator's feelings, while the use of repetition and questioning conveys a sense of desperation and hope. It is a beautiful and moving song that speaks to the pain and heartbreak of unrequited love.
