The song "Viva La Vida" by Coldplay is an anthem for those who have lost power and glory and are now living in the shadows of their former greatness. He speaks of the fear in his enemies’ eyes and the singing crowds that used to celebrate him, but now the old king is dead, and the walls of his castle have been brought down by a wicked and wild wind. He reflects on how he was once a ruler, but now he is a puppet on a string. 

The lyrics of the song are a reflection on the narrator's loss of power. He laments that his castles were built upon pillars of salt and sand and that people no longer believe in him. He turns to God for guidance, as he hears Jerusalem bells ringing and Roman cavalry singing. He calls on God to be his mirror, his sword, and his shield. The chorus of the song paints a picture of the narrator's former power, as he acknowledges that there was never an honest word when he ruled the world.
