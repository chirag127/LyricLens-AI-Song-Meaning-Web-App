"Something Just Like This" by Coldplay is a song about yearning for a love story to call your own. The lyrics tell the story of someone searching for that special someone and longing for a fairy tale romance. The song begins with the narrator reflecting on famous characters from mythology and literature, suggesting that they have something that he does not. 

The chorus then expresses the narrator's desire for a love that is real, not some kind of superhuman or unrealistic fantasy. He wants something just like this, something that he can turn to and someone he can kiss. The song then reprises the same chorus and ends with the narrator singing about how he wants something just like this.

Linguistically, the song is built around the idea of longing for something that is not in the narrator's life. The use of repetition and the chorus in particular highlight the narrator's desire for something that he can connect to and feel. The use of metaphors, such as the Greek and comic book characters, emphasize the idea of a fantasy versus something that is real or attainable. 

Overall, "Something Just Like This" by Coldplay is a song about yearning for a real and meaningful love. Through the use of repetition and metaphors, the song conveys the idea of wanting something that is attainable and real, not some kind of fantasy or superhero story. It is a beautiful and heartfelt song that captures the emotions of longing for someone special.
