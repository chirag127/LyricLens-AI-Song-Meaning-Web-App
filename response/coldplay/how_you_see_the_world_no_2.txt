"How You See the World No. 2" by Coldplay is a song that explores the complexities of the world and the powerlessness of feeling unable to make a difference. The lyrics paint a vivid picture of a world that is in chaos, with the “clowns” in control and the rest of us running in circles trying to make sense of it all. The speaker laments the lack of control they have over the situation, and reflects on how they want to “get it right” but can’t seem to be able to. 

The song is driven by the chorus, which repeats the line “That’s how you see the world”. This line serves as a reminder to the listener that the world can be seen in a variety of ways, and that our own view is not necessarily right or wrong. The chorus also encourages the listener to not be afraid and to remain resilient in the face of adversity. 

The lyrics also contain a few themes of powerlessness and helplessness. The first verse talks of the world being “put in a tin can” and “sliced and packaged”, which could refer to the way that the world is often manipulated and controlled by those in power. The second verse reflects on how people are often “mantrapped” and “shrinkwrapped”, which could be a metaphor for being trapped in a system that is out of our control. 

Overall, “How You See the World No. 2” by Coldplay is a reflective and thoughtful song about the complexities of the world and the powerlessness of feeling unable to make a difference. The lyrics are powerful and thought-provoking, and the chorus provides a message of resilience and hope. Lyrically, the song encourages us to take a step back and reflect on the world around us, and to not be afraid to stand up and make a difference.
