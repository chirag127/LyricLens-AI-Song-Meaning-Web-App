"Up With the Birds" by Coldplay is an uplifting, optimistic song about chasing dreams and having faith in the future, even when things seem dark and difficult. The lyrics then talk of how hard it is to let go of something, and how the spark of hope can break through the darkness. 

The chorus of the song talks of how the speaker is going to take a risk and go somewhere where no one knows their name, and how even though life may be hard and their armor may rust, they will still keep going and have faith in the future. The bridge of the song then speaks of a simple plot and having faith that good things will come.

The lyrics have a very positive tone, and the message of having faith in yourself and your dreams is one that resonates with many. The chorus also speaks of taking risks and embracing the unknown, which is something that many people can relate to.

In summary, "Up With the Birds" by Coldplay is an uplifting and inspiring song that speaks of having faith in the future and taking risks. The song's lyrics are filled with emotion and optimism, and its message of hope and determination is sure to inspire listeners.
