"Yellow" by Coldplay is a reflective and contemplative song about love and gratitude. The lyrics are simple, but powerful, and they express a deep appreciation for the person they are singing to. The lyrics begin by looking at the stars and expressing how they shine for the person, and how everything they do is "yellow." This is a metaphor for the person's importance in the singer's life, and how the singer is in awe of them. The singer then expresses how they wrote a song for the person, and how it was called "Yellow," showing that their appreciation for the person is so strong that it has become a song. 

The song then moves on to talk about how the singer has done things for the person, and how the person is "all yellow," again showing the strength of the singer's admiration. The singer also expresses how their skin and bones turn into something beautiful, showing how the person transcends the physical and has an inner beauty. The singer also expresses how they would bleed themselves dry for the person, showing a deep commitment to the person and to their love. 

The chorus of the song looks at the stars again and expresses how they shine for the person and all the things they do. This is a beautiful sentiment that encapsulates the song's message of love and appreciation. 

Overall, "Yellow" is a beautiful song that expresses a deep appreciation for the person it is addressed to. The simple yet powerful lyrics have a strong emotional impact and make the song a timeless classic. The use of metaphor and imagery helps to bring the feelings of the song to life, and the chorus encapsulates the message perfectly. The song is a wonderful celebration of love and admiration, and its powerful lyrics make it a timeless classic.
