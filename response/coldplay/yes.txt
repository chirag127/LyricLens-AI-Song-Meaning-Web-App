The song "Yes" by Coldplay is a deeply reflective and introspective track that contemplates the struggles of being in a relationship and longing for the other person to say yes. The song starts off with a subtle and melancholic instrumental, setting the stage for the contemplative lyrics that follow. 

The first verse paints a picture of a relationship that started off with high hopes, but has since fallen into a state of frustration and tension. The lyrics suggest that the speaker is stuck in a limbo, longing for the other person to take the initiative and make a decision, but they remain stuck in their indecision. 

The second verse paints a metaphorical image of the speaker being lifted up, only to be let go and fall back to the ground. This could metaphorically represent the speaker's feelings of being emotionally lifted up by the other person, only to be let down and feel even more alone. 

The chorus is a plea to the other person to make a decision, with the speaker expressing their exhaustion from the loneliness they feel in waiting. 

The song’s lyrics are full of emotion and longing, making it a great example of what it feels like to be in a relationship in which the other person is unable to make a decision. The song’s subtle yet meaningful lyrics deliver a powerful message about the struggles of being in a relationship. 

Upon linguistic analysis, the lyrics of "Yes" contain several emotions and feelings, such as loneliness, frustration, and longing. The lyrics also demonstrate a sense of hope, as the speaker pleads for the other person to make a decision and end their suffering. Additionally, the imagery in the lyrics helps to convey the speaker's feelings of being emotionally lifted up and then let down. 

In summary, "Yes" by Coldplay is a reflective and introspective track that paints a powerful picture of the struggles of being in a relationship and longing for the other person to make a decision. The lyrics contain several emotions and feelings, and the imagery also helps to convey the speaker's feelings of longing and frustration.
