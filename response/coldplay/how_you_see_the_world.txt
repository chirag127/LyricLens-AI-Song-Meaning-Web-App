The song "How You See the World" by Coldplay is a captivating exploration of the difficulty of life, loneliness, and the struggle to make sense of the world. The song opens with a question, asking if the listener is missing something, looking for something, and tired of everything. This sets the tone for the song, as it immediately establishes a sense of restlessness and a desire for something more.

The lyrics then explore the idea of being scared, running in circles, and finding it hard to relate to situations due to their complexity. This further emphasizes the difficulty of the situation, as the narrator struggles to make sense of the world.

The chorus then reiterates the theme of the song - that life is difficult and confusing, and that it can be hard to trust what one hears. The song then goes on to explore the idea of being alone, and needing conversation and information. This further emphasizes the struggles and loneliness that the narrator is experiencing. 

The song then finishes with a reminder that, despite all of the difficulties, the narrator has the power to decide how they see the world. This is a powerful message, as it implies that the listener has the power to decide how they view their own life and the world around them.

In summary, the song "How You See the World" by Coldplay is a captivating exploration of loneliness, difficulty, and finding a sense of control in life. Through its thoughtful lyrics and powerful message, the song encourages the listener to take control of their own life.

From a linguistic analysis, the song has a melancholic tone, as it focuses on the difficulty of life and the loneliness of being alone. The song also has a strong sense of empowerment, as it reminds the listener that they have the power to decide how they view their own life. This is a powerful message which can be useful for anyone struggling with difficult times.
