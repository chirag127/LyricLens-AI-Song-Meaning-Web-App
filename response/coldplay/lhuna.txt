"Lhuna" by Coldplay is a beautiful and melancholic song, featuring lead singer Chris Martin's gentle and soulful vocals, combined with Kylie Minogue's dreamy and whimsical background vocals. The song tells the story of a man who is desperately longing for his lost love, Lhuna. Linguistically, the lyrics of "Lhuna" are full of emotion, conveying a sense of longing, despair, and confusion. There is a great deal of repetition, which helps to emphasize the protagonist's feelings and create a sense of longing. The lyrics also feature a lot of imagery, such as "the smell of her, the touch and the taste of her," focusing on physical senses in order to illustrate the intensity of the protagonist's longing for Lhuna. 

In summary, "Lhuna" is a beautiful and melancholic song that tells the story of a man longing for his lost love.
