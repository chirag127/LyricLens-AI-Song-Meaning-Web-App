The song "Church" by Coldplay is a heartfelt ballad about finding solace in a higher power when feeling hurt and isolated. The lyrics tell a story of faith and hope, as the protagonist finds peace in God, represented by the concept of a church.

The first verse begins with the narrator expressing their admiration of the person they are with and how they make them feel "walking on air." They then recognize the power of this person, who is an answer to their prayers, and express their desire to be closer to them, like a seventh sea.

The chorus introduces the concept of going to church to be comforted when feeling hurt and vulnerable. This is juxtaposed with the phrase "vocalizing in Arabic," sung by Norah Shaqur. This line serves as a reminder that faith and hope are universal, and can be found in all religions and cultures.

The bridge further emphasizes the universality of faith, with Norah Shaqur singing in Arabic about God's power and love. This is followed by the narrator's declaration that they will always worship in their church, and praise the Lord seven days a week.

In summary, "Church" by Coldplay is a beautiful song about faith and hope in a higher power. It emphasizes the universality of faith, and how it can be found in all cultures and religions. Linguistically, the song conveys a sense of solace and comfort, with a message of hope and resilience in difficult times.
