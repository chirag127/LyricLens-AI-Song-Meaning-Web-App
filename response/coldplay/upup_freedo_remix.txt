The song "Up&Up (Freedo Remix)" by Coldplay is an inspiring, uplifting anthem about striving for better and never giving up on your dreams. The lyrics tell the story of individuals working hard to make something of themselves, and of the hope and strength to be found in each person, no matter their circumstances. 

The first verse of the song speaks directly to the idea of getting it together, and working hard to make something of yourself. The lyrics mention a car, a search for water, and an orange field - all of which can be interpreted as symbols of hard work, resilience, and success. The chorus paints a picture of a pearl forming a diamond in the rough, and of a bird soaring high above the flood - symbols of hope, beauty, and freedom. 

The second verse speaks to the struggles of life, and the need to embrace the difficult times. The lyrics describe the pain of suffering and the heartache of being apart from those we love. They also speak to the need to find healing and growth, and to find strength in difficult times.

The final chorus speaks of getting it together, of having the strength to float, and of reaching for the sky. The chorus is an uplifting anthem of resilience and hope, and a reminder that no matter the struggles, success and happiness are possible.

In summary, "Up&Up (Freedo Remix)" is a song of hope and resilience. It speaks to the strength and potential of each individual, and of the need to keep striving for better, no matter the circumstances. The song is inspiring and uplifting, and overall a great reminder of the power of hope and hard work.

In terms of linguistic analysis of the song lyrics, the words used in the song are largely positive and full of hope. The use of words such as “flower”, “soar”, “diamond”, and “heal” evoke a feeling of optimism, resilience, and determination. Additionally, the use of repetition throughout the song - for example, in the chorus - serves to emphasize the message of never giving up and continuing to strive for better. All of these elements contribute to the overall message of the song and help to create an uplifting and inspiring atmosphere.
