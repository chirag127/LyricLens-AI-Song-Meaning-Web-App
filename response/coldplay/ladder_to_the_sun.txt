The song “Ladder to the Sun” by Coldplay is a poignant reflection on taking risks and the uncertainty that comes with them. This is a powerful realization and serves as a reminder of the importance of taking risks. 

The song ends with the singer reflecting on the risks he has taken and the consequences of those risks.
