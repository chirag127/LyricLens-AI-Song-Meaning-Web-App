The lyrics express a strong desire for understanding and connection, while also highlighting the need for strength and control in order to have healthy relationships.

The use of repetition emphasizes the importance of these themes and the strong desire for genuine relationships. The lyrics also express a sense of hope, with a reminder to not forget the rest of us and a call to open one’s eyes to the world.
