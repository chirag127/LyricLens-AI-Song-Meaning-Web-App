The song "Lost@ - Live at the United Center, Chicago, IL" by Coldplay is a song about the struggles of life and the need to remain hopeful and resilient in the face of adversity. In the first verse, Coldplay uses imagery to describe the feeling of being lost, as if one is trying to cross rivers but every door is locked and the shine is wearing off. The chorus then speaks of how even if one is a big fish in a small pond, they can still be lost, and their guns will fire off until the firing stops. 

The lyrics of the song are full of emotion, with the imagery of rivers and locked doors providing a strong sense of being trapped with no way out. This feeling of helplessness is further reflected in the chorus, where being even the “biggest fish” is not enough to protect one from the difficulties of life. The use of the metaphor of a gun firing off speaks of the intensity of the emotions felt when facing these challenges. 

The overall message of the song is one of resilience and perseverance, no matter how difficult the situation may seem. Coldplay encourages us to keep going and never give up, even when all the odds seem to be against us. 

The lyrics of this song are rich in emotion and provide a powerful message of hope in the face of adversity. The imagery used throughout helps to bring the message to life, and the chorus provides an uplifting reminder of the power of perseverance. This song is sure to provide inspiration and strength to anyone struggling to stay afloat in life.
