"See You Soon" by Coldplay is a song about loss and redemption. This is further emphasized by the repeated line "Don't break your back," which suggests that the narrator is determined to make things right.

This conveys a sense of optimism that even if the narrator is far away from those they care about, they will still be able to find a way to reconnect.

This resilience is further emphasized by the use of the phrase "bullet proof vest," which suggests that the narrator is determined to stand up for themselves and their beliefs, even in the face of adversity.
