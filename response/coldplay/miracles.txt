The song "Miracles" by Coldplay is a beautiful and uplifting ode to the beauty of life and the power of faith. The lyrics express a sense of awe, wonder, and hope, while also delving into the more difficult aspects of life. The song begins with the narrator reflecting on the beauty of the world, heard through the words of angels. He finds solace in the stars and in another person's eyes, which bring him a sense of comfort and peace. In the chorus, the narrator sings about believing in miracles, and how his faith can help him to rise above the troubles of the world. 

The lyrics of the song present a mix of positive and negative emotions. While the narrator is filled with awe and hope, there is also a sense of pain and suffering, particularly in the line "send your storm and your lightning to strike me between the eyes and cry." This is juxtaposed with the chorus, where the narrator finds solace in his faith, believing in miracles and rising above the troubles of the world.

Linguistically, the song has a poetic feel, with a mix of metaphors, similes, and imagery that create a vivid picture of the narrator's emotions and feelings. The lyrics are filled with emotion and imagery, creating a powerful and moving song.

Overall, "Miracles" by Coldplay is a beautiful and inspiring song, with thoughtful and moving lyrics that express a range of emotions. The song is filled with powerful imagery and emotion, which help to create a vivid and powerful song. By believing in miracles, the narrator finds hope and solace in the midst of pain and suffering.
