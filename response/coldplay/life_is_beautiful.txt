"Life is Beautiful" by Coldplay is a song that reflects the beauty of the world, even in the face of adversity. The lyrics explore themes of unity, hope, and resilience, reminding us that even in hard times, there is beauty to be found.

The song starts off with a reference to an “old song” coming on the radio, which could be interpreted as a metaphor for the past and its struggles. The lyrics then discuss the need to “stop feeling for the refugees” and the dismissal of “kiss their kids off to war”, which could be interpreted as a call to accept and embrace those who are different than us.

The chorus of the song is the most uplifting section, with the lines “it's a beautiful beautiful world” and “riches will rain on the poor”. This could be interpreted as a message of hope, that even in the face of adversity, beauty and prosperity can be found.

The song also contains a message of unity, with the lines “we're all born equal under the same sun” and “you take the noise or you make the noise”. This could be interpreted as a call to come together and make the world a better place for everyone.

Linguistically, the song employs a variety of techniques to convey its message. It utilizes repetition of the phrase “beautiful beautiful world” to emphasize the beauty of the world, as well as a call and response structure with the lines “all of the boys they rock and roll” and “all of the girls they're all over the world” to emphasize the message of unity.

In summary, "Life is Beautiful" by Coldplay is a song that celebrates the beauty of the world, even in the face of adversity. It is a message of hope, unity, and resilience, reminding us that even in hard times, beauty and prosperity can be found.
