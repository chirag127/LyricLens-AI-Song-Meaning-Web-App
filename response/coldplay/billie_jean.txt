"Billie Jean" by Coldplay is a song about a man trying to deny his relationship with a woman he believes is lying about her pregnancy. In the first verse, he describes her as a "beauty queen from a movie scene" and is confused by her claims that he is the father of her child. He then goes on to say that everyone was looking at them when they danced on the floor. In the second verse, the man denies that he is the father of her child and reminds himself to "always think twice" before getting involved with someone. He then describes how the woman came to his baby's room and how his baby was crying, showing that he was not the father. In the chorus, the man repeatedly denies being the father of the woman's child, and in the third verse, he tells her "you know what you did". 

The song has a simple, yet powerful message. It speaks to the importance of being careful with one's relationships, and reminds us that we must always think twice before we get involved with someone. It also speaks to the importance of being honest, as the woman in the song lies about the paternity of her child. 

The lyrics of the song are very effective in conveying its message. Through the use of repetition, the song emphasizes its themes of honesty, caution, and responsibility. The use of imagery is also effective in helping us understand the man's predicament, as he is "dancing on the floor in the round" with a woman he believes is lying to him. Additionally, the lyrics contain several examples of figurative language, such as similes, metaphors, and personification, which help to create vivid images and further emphasize the song's message. 

In summary, "Billie Jean" by Coldplay is a song about being careful with one's relationships and being honest about them. The lyrics are effective in conveying this message, as they contain several examples of repetition, imagery, and figurative language. The message in the song is timeless, and is still relevant today.
