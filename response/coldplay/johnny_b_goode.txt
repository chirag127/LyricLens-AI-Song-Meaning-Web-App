"Johnny B. Goode" by Coldplay is a catchy, upbeat song that tells the story of a country boy with dreams of becoming a great musician. The song is written in the key of E major, and follows a simple verse-chorus structure. The first verse introduces the character, Johnny B. Goode, who is a country boy who cannot read or write but can play the guitar "just like ringing a bell". The chorus follows, with the lyric "Go, go, go, Johnny go, go, go, Johnny go, go, go, Johnny go, go, Johnny B. Goode". The second verse provides more detail about Johnny's character and his dreams of greatness. The chorus is repeated once more and then the bridge starts, with the lyrics "Maybe someday your name will be in lights, saying Johnny B. Goode tonight". The song then ends with the chorus repeating one last time.

The lyrics of the song illustrate the struggles of Johnny B. Goode, a young country boy with dreams of becoming a great musician. The song paints a picture of a young man who is determined to make something of himself, despite the odds being stacked against him. Coldplay uses language such as "train drivers made" and "people passing by" to create an immersive atmosphere, allowing the listener to feel as if they are in the same place as Johnny.

The song is also filled with feelings of hope, ambition, and courage. The lyrics suggest that Johnny will eventually "make it" and become a successful musician, despite the fact that he cannot read or write. This message of hope and determination is inspiring and relatable, making "Johnny B. Goode" an uplifting and motivational song.

Linguistic analysis of the song's lyrics reveals an abundance of positive emotion and words, such as "just like ringing a bell", "man", "leader", and "lights". These words indicate a sense of ambition and hope, which is a powerful theme throughout the song. Additionally, the use of repetition reinforces the message of hope and determination, as the chorus is repeated several times throughout the song. This creates a powerful and uplifting energy, inspiring the listener to pursue their dreams despite the odds.

In summary, "Johnny B. Goode" is an uplifting and motivational song which tells the story of a young man with big dreams. The lyrics are filled with positive emotion and ambition, making the song an inspiring and powerful anthem for anyone with a dream.
