The song “Only 1” by Ariana Grande is a heartfelt ballad about love and longing. In the first verse, the narrator expresses their desire for their partner, noting that their presence in their life feels “too good to be true”. They recognize that there is “competition that’s after you”, but they still hope they can be the one that gets to be with their beloved. In the pre-chorus, the narrator expresses their wish for a “fairy tale” life with their partner, free of malice and lies. 

The chorus reiterates the narrator’s feelings, expressing that they can’t be the only one in their partner’s life because their partner is “twice as good” as anyone else they have ever met and their love is “three times better”. The longing in the song is further emphasized in the second verse, where the narrator expresses their amazement that their partner has remained by their side. 

The song “Only 1” is an emotional and heartfelt ballad about recognizing that someone is too good for you, but still wanting to be the one they choose. The lyrics express the narrator’s wish for a perfect relationship, free of malice and lies, and their longing for their partner to be by their side. The song is a beautiful expression of the power of love and how it can make us feel. 

From a linguistic analysis perspective, the song is filled with emotions and feelings of longing, love, and admiration. The narrator expresses feelings of admiration for their partner and their longing for a perfect relationship. The language in the song is filled with imagery, such as “competition that’s after you” and “fairy tale”, which help to emphasize the narrator’s emotions.
