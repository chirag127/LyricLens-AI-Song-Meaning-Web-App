In the song "December" by Ariana Grande, the singer expresses a longing for romantic connection and closeness despite the winter cold. The song opens with a plea to be wrapped up and taken home with her partner, suggesting a desire to be protected and cared for. The chorus then follows with a promise to love and provide whatever her partner needs - a sentiment reinforced by the pre-chorus which speaks of keeping her partner warm through the winter and giving them something to remember through the summer.

The lyrics of the song emphasize the idea of giving, both in terms of physical gifts and emotional support. The singer is willing to “give you the fire I keep inside” as well as providing a nightlight and the “sweetest kiss”. The use of the word “unwrap” further reinforces the idea of the song being a gift, and the singer’s desire to be given a chance.

Linguistic analysis of the lyrics of the song reveals that the singer is expressing a deep longing for connection and a desire to be seen and appreciated. The use of words such as “wrap” and “unwrap” expresses the idea of a physical and emotional closeness, while the words “fire” and “lights” evoke a sense of passion and intensity. Furthermore, the use of the phrase “whatever is on your list” implies a willingness to do whatever it takes to make her partner happy, suggesting a deep commitment and care.

In summary, “December” by Ariana Grande is a song that speaks of love and the desire to be both physically and emotionally close to someone. The lyrics express a longing for connection and a willingness to do whatever it takes to make the other person happy. The song is an emotional and passionate plea for love and understanding.
