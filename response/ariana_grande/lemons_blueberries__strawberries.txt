Lemons, Blueberries & Strawberries by Ariana Grande is a song that is full of love and warmth. In this track, Ariana Grande talks about the feeling of love and how it is like a heavenly fruit that cannot be compared to anything else. The song also makes the listener think about the good times that are shared together, and that no matter what, the love between them will never break apart.

The lyrics of the song have a strong sense of love and affection. The chorus repeats the phrase “lemons, blueberries and strawberries” to symbolize the sweetness and wholesomeness of the love shared between them. The lyrics also include references to some of the places they have shared together like Memphis, Nashville and Tennessee. These references give the song a more personal touch, as it is not just a generic love song.

The song also has a very upbeat and happy tone. The lyrics are full of joy and happiness, which can be seen in the references to parties and wine. This cheerful tone emphasizes the positive aspects of the relationship shared between them.
