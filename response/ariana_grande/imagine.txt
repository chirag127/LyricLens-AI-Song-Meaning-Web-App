"Imagine" is a song by Ariana Grande that talks about the singer and her partner enjoying a carefree, spontaneous and intimate relationship. In the first verse, the singer and her partner are out in the car, staying up all night, ordering food and enjoying each other's company without worrying about anyone else knowing. The second verse talks about how the singer knew the person was perfect for her after their first kiss and how their relationship feels like it has always been meant to be.

In the pre-chorus, the singer talks about posting pictures of their relationship online and showing off their love. The chorus repeats the phrase "Imagine a world like that" and talks about how the singer loves how her partner makes her feel. The outro repeats the phrase "Can you imagine it?" to emphasize the idea of a perfect, carefree relationship.

Overall, the song is a light, romantic and playful celebration of love and intimacy. The lyrics are simple and repetitive, but they effectively convey the joy and happiness that the singer feels in her relationship. The song is well-suited to Ariana Grande's upbeat and energetic vocal style, and it is likely to be a hit with fans of her music.
