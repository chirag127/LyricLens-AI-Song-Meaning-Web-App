The song "Ghostin" by Ariana Grande is about the pain of loving someone who is still grieving the loss of another person. In the first verse, the narrator expresses her sadness and need for comfort from her partner, but also acknowledges that he is dealing with his own pain. In the pre-chorus, she admits that she is causing him pain by constantly crying and being emotionally unstable, and expresses guilt for putting him through this. The chorus repeats the idea that her crying over someone else hurts her partner's heart.

In the second verse, the narrator wishes that the person she is grieving for was still with them, but also acknowledges that this person only visits her in her dreams. She reflects on the struggles they have been through together, and the hope for a better future. The pre-chorus and chorus repeat, emphasizing the narrator's love for her partner and her hope that they can move past this difficult time together.

The song overall is a raw and emotional expression of the pain of loving someone who is still grieving the loss of another person. It captures the complicated feelings of guilt, regret, and love that can arise in these situations. As a review, the song is beautifully written and performed, with a poignant message that will resonate with many people.
