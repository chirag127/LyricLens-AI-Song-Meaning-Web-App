"Far Away" by Ariana Grande is a soulful and melancholic ballad about the difficulty of letting go of a relationship and the pain of being apart from someone you love. The second verse follows the same tone, with Grande singing of the difficulty of being in a long-distance relationship and the loneliness she feels when her partner is away. The chorus follows with Grande reflecting on the pain of being apart and the difficulty of letting go.
