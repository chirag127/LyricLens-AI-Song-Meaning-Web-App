In her song “Fake Smile (Live)”, Ariana Grande reflects on the struggles of being a celebrity and the difficulty of maintaining a positive attitude in the face of criticism and judgement. The song begins with the chorus: “After laughter comes tears, Nananana / After after laughter comes tears, yeah”. This is a reference to the idea that even when you’re laughing and having a good time, eventually your emotions will catch up with you and you’ll have to face the underlying reality of your situation. This is a theme that Grande continues to explore throughout the song. 

The verses of the song focus on Grande’s personal experiences as a celebrity. She talks about the difficulty of having to leave parties early to go somewhere else, of being starved for attention, and of the criticism that she’s faced from the media. Grande expresses her frustration at the idea of having to fake a smile and act like everything is alright even when inside she’s hurting. She emphasizes the importance of being honest and not lying to yourself, and she also talks about how grateful she is for the people who support and love her. 

Overall, “Fake Smile (Live)” is an honest and heartfelt reflection on the struggles of fame and the importance of being open and honest about what you’re going through. Linguistically, the song is filled with vivid imagery, such as “arms crossed with the attitude” and “lips pouted”, which help to convey Grande’s emotions and feelings. Additionally, the song features a catchy chorus and a soulful melody, making it an enjoyable and uplifting listen. 

In summary, “Fake Smile (Live)” is an uplifting and honest song about the struggles that come with fame and the importance of being honest with yourself and those around you. Through vivid imagery, catchy melodies, and a soulful message, the song is sure to make an impact on the listener.
