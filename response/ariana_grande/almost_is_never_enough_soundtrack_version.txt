The song “Almost Is Never Enough” by Ariana Grande with Nathan Sykes is a song about two people who almost had a relationship, but never fully committed, which left both of them feeling frustrated and unfulfilled. In the second verse, Nathan Sykes sings about how if he could change the world overnight, there would be no such thing as goodbye and they would get the chance they deserve. He also repeats the pre-chorus line about denying it as much as they want, but in time their feelings will come out.

The chorus brings the two verses together by repeating the line “almost is never enough”, which reflects the sentiment of the song. The chorus also states that if they had known that they both wanted each other, they wouldn’t be two worlds apart, suggesting that if they had acted on their feelings earlier, they could have had a relationship. 

The song is a reflection on the feeling of almost having something, but not fully committing and missing out on the opportunity. The song expresses the feeling of regret and longing for something that could have been, but never was.
