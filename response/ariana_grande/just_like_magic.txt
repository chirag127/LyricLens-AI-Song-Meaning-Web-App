"Just Like Magic" by Ariana Grande is a song about manifesting one's desires and dreams with positive intentions and energy. In the chorus, Grande sings about using her "magnetic" power to manifest what she wants, and snapping her fingers to make it happen. In the first verse, Grande talks about her day-to-day routine and her pursuit of success, and how her positive attitude and hard work enable her to manifest her desires. The second verse touches on the idea of self-love and confidence, while also discussing the idea of disconnecting from her phone and connecting with herself through reading books. The bridge of the song speaks to the idea of creating new habits and being intentional with one's time.

The song is characterized by a bright, upbeat tone and catchy melodies. Lyrically, Grande's words are uplifting and empowering, encouraging listeners to manifest their own desires by believing in themselves and having a positive attitude. Through her lyrics, Grande conveys the importance of manifesting one's desires with intention and positivity.

In the linguistic analysis of the song, the lyrics convey a sense of optimism and confidence. Grande's use of figurative language, such as "magnetic" and "snap it," conveys the idea of manifesting her desires and dreams with intention and power. Additionally, Grande's use of repetition and alliteration, such as in the phrase "just like magic," emphasizes the uplifting and empowering message of the song. 

Overall, "Just Like Magic" is an upbeat, empowering anthem about manifesting one's desires and dreams with intention and positivity. Through her uplifting message and catchy melodies, Grande encourages listeners to believe in themselves and have faith in their own power.
