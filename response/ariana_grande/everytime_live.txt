The song “Everytime (Live)” by Ariana Grande is a song of longing and regret. In it, Grande expresses her desire to move on and let go of someone, yet finds herself drawn back to them despite her best efforts. The lyrics portray a sense of resignation, as if the singer knows she will never truly be free of this person. 

The song begins with the singer expressing her frustration with her partner's lack of commitment and control. She acknowledges that others are telling her to let go, but she feels she cannot do so. This creates a sense of entrapment and helplessness.

In the pre-chorus, Grande flips between trying to fight her feelings and giving in to them. This creates a tug-of-war between her head and her heart. She acknowledges that she gets weak when the person is not around and that it feels like something out of Shakespeare - a grand, tragic romance.

The chorus is an anthem of resignation. Grande acknowledges that she gets “high and call[s] on the regular”, indicating she is addicted to the person, and she “fall[s] like a teenager”, suggesting her emotions are overwhelming her. Despite this, she still questions why God keeps bringing her back to them. 

The post-chorus is a repetition of the chorus, emphasizing Grande’s struggle with her feelings and her inability to let go.

In summary, “Everytime (Live)” is a song of longing and regret, in which Ariana Grande expresses her desire to move on and let go, yet finds herself drawn back to her partner despite her best efforts. Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals emotions of frustration, entrapment, helplessness, and resignation. The song paints a picture of a grand, tragic romance, in which the singer is hopelessly ensnared.
