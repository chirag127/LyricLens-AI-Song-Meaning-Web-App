"Sweetener" is a song by Ariana Grande in which she sings about the positive impact a loved one has on her life. The lyrics describe how this person, the "sweetener," makes difficult times easier to bear and adds joy to her life. In the chorus, Grande sings about how this person makes her say "oh" in amazement and delight.

In the first verse, Grande sings about how the sweetener "lick[s] the bowl" and "touches [her] soul." She also says that this person lifts her up to "heights unknown." In the pre-chorus, Grande repeats the line "when life deals us cards / make everything taste like it is salt" to emphasize the difficulties she faces without the sweetener.

The second verse continues the theme, with Grande singing about how the sweetener's mother sent them horoscopes and how the sweetener makes her smile. In the bridge, Grande sings about how she and the sweetener spend time together, talking and enjoying each other's company.

In summary, "Sweetener" is a love song in which Ariana Grande sings about the positive impact a loved one has on her life. The song's catchy chorus and relatable lyrics make it a standout track on the album. As a fan of Ariana Grande's music, I thoroughly enjoyed "Sweetener" and would recommend it to others.
