She then speaks about how she has been sharing her personal stories with her fans since day one and that she will not be changing that.

The final verse of the song speaks to the need for women to support each other, and for the media to do the same. Grande states that women who choose to behave like full human beings should be celebrated, not treated as a joke, and that they need their sisterhood to get through the challenges they face.
