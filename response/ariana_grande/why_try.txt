"Why Try" by Ariana Grande is a song that speaks to the tumultuous nature of relationships and the power of love. In the first verse, Grande sings of being surrounded by both devils and angels, but being in the same boat as the person she’s in a relationship with. The chorus goes on to talk about the pain of the relationship and yet, why the two of them still try. The bridge emphasizes this point even further, with Grande singing about loving the pain and never wanting to live without it. 

The lyrics of the song can be analyzed from a linguistic perspective to better understand the emotions, social tendencies, and language style which helps analyze emotions and feelings that musical artists express in their songs. The song is full of repetition, from the chorus to the bridge, which emphasizes the idea that despite the pain, the two will still try. Grande also uses metaphors throughout the song, such as “living with devils and angels,” “living like angels and devils,” and “loving like angels and devils” to emphasize the struggle of the relationship and why they still try despite the pain. 

In summary, "Why Try" is a song about the power of love and the struggle of maintaining relationships. Grande speaks to the pain and yet the determination to try and make it work. Through the use of metaphors, repetition, and powerful lyrics, Grande conveys her story of love and the determination to make it work.
