Break Free by Ariana Grande is a powerful anthem about breaking away from a difficult relationship and embracing one's own strength and identity. In the first verse, she sings "If you want it, take it, I should've said it before / Tried to hide it, fake it, I can't pretend anymore." She is letting go of the facade she had created and revealing her true self. In the pre-chorus, she expresses her fear of dying at the hands of a broken heart, but also her determination to never let that happen. 

In the chorus, she sings "This is the part when I say I don't want ya / I'm stronger than I've been before / This is the part when I break free / 'Cause I can't resist it no more." She is asserting her independence and rejecting her partner's hold over her. In the second verse, she acknowledges the power the relationship had over her, singing "You were better, deeper, I was under your spell." 

In the bridge, she expresses her longing for the relationship, singing "No more, baby, 'Ooh, ooh, ooh' / Thought of your body, I came alive." She is coming to terms with the fact that she is no longer in control of her emotions, and that she must break free in order to move on. The chorus repeats again, emphasizing her newfound strength and resilience. 

The song is a testament to the power of self-love and the courage it takes to break away from a difficult relationship. The lyrics are emotionally charged and lyrically articulate the pain and difficulty of letting go. The song also has a strong beat and a catchy chorus that make it enjoyable to listen to. 

Linguistically, the song is full of positive language, including words like "stronger," "break free," and "alive." These words are indicative of the empowering message of the song and show the artist's determination to reclaim her autonomy. Additionally, the use of repetition and direct address in the song helps the artist to emphasize her newfound power and strength. 

Overall, Break Free by Ariana Grande is a powerful and uplifting song about breaking away from a difficult relationship and embracing one's own strength and identity. Its emotionally charged lyrics and catchy beat make it an enjoyable and empowering anthem.
