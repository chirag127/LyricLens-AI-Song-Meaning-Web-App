Break Your Heart Right Back by Ariana Grande is a sultry R&B/pop song that tells a story of a woman who is fed up with a man who has cheated on her. The song starts off with Ariana Grande singing, “I shoulda saw it coming, I shoulda saw the signs/But I wanted to believe you, trust you/You said you wouldn’t lie, but baby you were so good, so good/You had me going blind.” In this verse, Grande is expressing her anger and frustration at being deceived and taken advantage of by the man she thought she could trust. 

The pre-chorus of the song features the line, “What goes around comes around/And if it goes up it comes down,” which is a warning to the man to be aware of the consequences of his actions. In the chorus, Grande sings, “Now that it’s over/I just wanna break your heart right back, right back/I’m coming/Want you to cry me a river out/I just want to break your heart right back.” This line is a direct reference to the man’s actions, as Grande is saying that she will now do to him what he did to her.

The bridge of the song features a guest verse from Childish Gambino, who sings, “You got a man but your man ain’t loyal/Don’t understand how to spell it out for ya/Yes I’m a G from the A and they ask why/Sorry had to tell you I’m a nice guy/Naw I’m a sick guy money too much/I step on stage and girls move too much.” This verse is Gambino’s way of warning the man that he should not take advantage of women because he will eventually be held accountable for his actions.

In summary, Break Your Heart Right Back is a song about a woman taking revenge on a man who has cheated on her, and features a warning from Childish Gambino about the consequences of taking advantage of women. The song has a mellow, sultry tone and a unique blend of R&B and pop. Linguistically, it conveys a message of empowerment and strength in the face of betrayal, and a warning to men that they will be held accountable for their actions.
