"Problem (Wayne G Radio Edit)" by Ariana Grande featuring Iggy Azalea is an upbeat, empowering anthem about leaving a toxic relationship. In the song, Iggy raps in the first verse about the hardships of being in a relationship with someone who doesn't understand her and her decision to break away from that person. Ariana's pre-chorus and chorus explain the relief that comes from not having that problem anymore and how she is now able to focus on herself. The song is an anthem of self-love and independence, emphasizing the importance of recognizing when something isn't good for you and having the courage to make the necessary changes. 

The song starts off with Iggy rapping about the difficulties she is facing in her relationship. She speaks about the lack of understanding and support from her partner and how she has to rely on herself to get through it. Iggy then goes on to explain that she knows she will be better off without this person in her life. This verse sets the tone for the rest of the song, as it shows how much strength and resilience is needed to make the difficult decision to leave this person behind.

Ariana then takes over in the pre-chorus and chorus, singing about the freedom and joy that comes from being on her own. She declares that she has "one less problem" and is no longer weighed down by the burden of a toxic relationship. This is an empowering message, as it shows that Ariana is taking control of her life and making positive changes.

The song also includes a linguistic analysis of the lyrics, which helps to interpret the emotions and feelings that the musicians are expressing. Throughout the song, there is a sense of empowerment and determination, as both Ariana and Iggy are taking control of their lives and making the decision to leave a negative situation. This is further emphasized through their use of powerful words such as "realize," "wiser," and "breathless."

In summary, "Problem (Wayne G Radio Edit)" by Ariana Grande featuring Iggy Azalea is an empowering anthem about taking control of your life and leaving toxic relationships behind. Through their use of powerful language, Ariana and Iggy express the courage and resilience needed to make difficult decisions and ultimately find freedom and joy in being on their own.
