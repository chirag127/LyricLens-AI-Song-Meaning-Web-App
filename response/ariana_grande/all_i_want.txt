"All I Want" by Ariana Grande is a song about yearning for love and companionship. The lyrics convey the feelings of loneliness and longing for someone to share one’s life with. The song starts off with a slow, sombre tone as Grande sings, “All I want is love, all I need is love from you, from you”. This line sets the mood for the whole song, as Grande expresses her desire to have someone to love and lean on. 

The chorus of the song further emphasizes this need for love and companionship, as Grande sings, “All I want is somebody to understand me, somebody to hold me, somebody to comfort me when I'm feeling down”. Here, Grande is expressing her desire to be understood, cared for and comforted by someone she loves. The song also talks about the struggles of being alone and the hope of finding someone to share one’s life with. 

In the bridge of the song, Grande sings, “All I want is someone to be there, when I cry in the middle of the night, somebody to love me”. Here, we can see her feelings of loneliness and her desire to have someone to turn to in times of need. The song conveys a sense of yearning and desperation, as Grande expresses her desire for love and understanding. 

Overall, "All I Want" is a powerful and emotional ballad about the need for love and companionship. The lyrics are poignant and heartfelt, and the song conveys a deep sense of loneliness and longing. Through her lyrics, Grande expresses her desire for someone to love and lean on, and her hope of finding someone to share her life with.

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song includes many words associated with sadness, such as “cry”, “alone”, and “lonely”. These words are used to express Grande’s feelings of loneliness and longing for companionship. Additionally, the song also includes words associated with hope and yearning, such as “love”, “understand”, and “comfort”. These words are used to express Grande’s desire for love and understanding. 

Overall, "All I Want" is a touching and heartfelt song about the need for love and companionship. The lyrics are powerful and emotive, expressing the feelings of loneliness and longing for someone to share one’s life with. Grande’s use of language is effective and evocative, conveying her message in a clear and poignant manner. The song is a beautiful and moving ballad, and it is sure to strike a chord with many listeners.
