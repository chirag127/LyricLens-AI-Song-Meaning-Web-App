The song "You'll Never Know" by Ariana Grande speaks of the regret one can feel when a relationship falls apart and it's too late to do anything about it. The lyrics tell the story of a woman who was in a relationship with a man who never expressed his feelings for her. The chorus is a heartbreaking expression of regret, as the woman laments that she will never know what could have been had he decided to open up and tell her how he really felt.

The verses begin with the woman reflecting on the past, and how the man never provided her with the emotional support she needed. She suggests that had he been more honest and open with her, things might have been different. The second verse speaks of the woman's current situation, as she is now with somebody else and is unwilling to jeopardize that relationship.

The song is a powerful expression of regret, as the woman is left to ponder what could have been had her partner been more forthcoming with his feelings. The lyrics are an honest and raw depiction of the pain and sorrow that can come with the end of a relationship.

The song is an emotionally powerful expression of regret and longing. The lyrics are both emotionally powerful and poetic, and Ariana Grande's powerful vocal delivery helps to bring out the emotion in the song. Musically, the song is a mid-tempo pop ballad, with simple instrumentation, allowing the focus to remain on the lyrics and vocals.

When analyzing the lyrics of the song, there are many emotions at play. The lyrics are filled with sorrow and regret, as the woman looks back on the relationship she could have had. There is also a sense of longing, as she wishes she could have the chance to experience it again. Additionally, there is a sense of frustration and anger, as the woman feels that her partner wasted their time together by not being honest with her.

Overall, "You'll Never Know" is a powerful expression of regret and longing, with emotionally charged lyrics that are further elevated by Ariana Grande's powerful vocal delivery. The song is a beautiful and honest take on the heartbreak of a failed relationship, and it serves as a reminder that it is important to be honest with the ones we love.
