Be Alright (Demo) by Ariana Grande is a hopeful and uplifting song that encourages listeners to stay optimistic and keep pushing forward even when times are tough. The song speaks to the idea that if you stay focused and trust that things will eventually be alright, no matter how dark the times may be.

The song starts off with Ariana singing about the darkness of the night and how difficult it can be to find love in such circumstances. She then transitions into a chorus of encouragement, singing "we're gonna be alright, you know that we gon' be alright." This chorus is repeated throughout the song and serves as a reminder that no matter what happens, things will be alright in the end.

The verses of the song focus on overcoming difficult times and staying focused. Ariana sings about how things are not always easy, but that there is hope and light at the end of the tunnel. Quavo's rap verse adds a layer of energy to the song, while also reinforcing the theme of the song.

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song is filled with positive language and hopeful statements. This can be seen in the refrain where Ariana sings "Baby don't you know, you know, all of them tears gon' come and go, you know, baby you just gotta make up your mind, your mind that every little thing is gonna be alright". The chorus also reinforces this idea by repeating the phrase "We're gonna be alright".

Overall, Be Alright (Demo) by Ariana Grande is an uplifting and positive song that encourages listeners to stay focused and trust that things will be alright. The song's lyrics and production create a sense of hope and optimism that is sure to bring a smile to the listener's face.
