Side to Side (Phantoms Remix) by Ariana Grande is a song about being in a relationship with someone who has a bad reputation, but still being tempted by them despite this. The song begins with Ariana Grande singing about how her friends are telling her to give up on the relationship, but she can't hear them because she is too tempted. In the pre-chorus, she mentions that she is making deals with the devil, and that she knows it will get her into trouble, but as long as the other person knows that she is there for them, it's worth it. 

The chorus repeats the phrase “I’ve been here all night, I’ve been here all day, and boy got me walkin’ side to side”. This suggests that she is putting all of her time and energy into this relationship, but it is not going anywhere. 

The song features a verse by Nicki Minaj, where she brags about her success in the rap world and how she is the queen of rap. This is a contrast to the more vulnerable and insecure sentiments of the rest of the song. 

Overall, Side to Side (Phantoms Remix) is a song about the struggle between desire and reason when it comes to relationships. Ariana Grande is expressing her feelings of being tempted by someone despite knowing that it may not be the best decision. The lyrics of the song offer insight into her conflicted emotions, and the contrast between her part of the song and Nicki Minaj's verse provides an interesting perspective on the situation. Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals that the song is filled with emotions of insecurity and desire, as well as a sense of defiance in the face of the criticism of her friends.
