In Ariana Grande's song, "Bad Decisions," she reflects on her own impulsivity. In the song, Grande acknowledges the potential danger of her relationship, but is drawn to it regardless. The lyrics of the song portray a roller coaster of emotions and feelings, as Grande indulges in her feelings while also being aware of the risks of her choices. 

In the first verse, Grande alludes to the thrill of her relationship, singing “Boy, you know that you drive me crazy/ But it’s one of the things I like.” Here, Grande is admitting that her relationship is both exciting and dangerous, yet she is still drawn to it. In the chorus, Grande furthers this thought by singing “We got that hood love/ We got that good love/ We got that I don’t give a what love.” This is Grande’s way of saying that she is aware of the potential consequences of her choices, but is choosing to ignore them. The second verse reinforces this message, as Grande acknowledges that people may not understand her relationship, but she doesn’t care.

The third verse brings the song to a climax, as Grande sings “Don’t you know I ain’t fuckin’ with them good boys?/ Know you love me like ain’t nobody could.” This is Grande’s way of saying that she is choosing the thrill of the bad decisions over the safety of the good ones. The song ends with the same chorus, emphasizing the idea of Grande choosing to live in the moment and indulging in the bad decisions she is making. 

The song is a reflection on the tumultuous nature of relationships. Through her lyrics, Grande conveys the idea that bad decisions can be thrilling and exciting, yet also dangerous. She acknowledges the potential consequences of her choices, but still chooses to indulge in the thrill of the moment. The song is a reminder that, while bad decisions can be exciting and lead to thrilling experiences, they can also have potentially dangerous consequences. 

From a linguistic analysis of the song, it is clear that Grande is expressing a range of emotions, from thrill and excitement to apprehension and fear. Grande conveys these emotions through her use of imagery, alliteration, and repetition. Her use of repetition emphasizes the idea that she is aware of the risks of her decisions, yet is still drawn to them. The imagery she uses also conveys the tumultuous nature of her relationship, as she acknowledges the potential danger while still being drawn to it. 

Overall, "Bad Decisions" is an emotionally charged song that reflects on the thrill of risky relationships. Through her lyrics, Grande conveys a range of emotions, from thrill and excitement to apprehension and fear. The song is a reminder that bad decisions can be thrilling, yet also potentially dangerous.
