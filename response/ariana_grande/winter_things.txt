The song "Winter Things" by Ariana Grande is a catchy, upbeat pop song about the desire to feel the winter season even though the weather outside is not cold. The song opens with the narrator wishing it was cold outside, feeling like it's mid-July despite the fact that winter is supposed to be a season of cold and snow. She longs to enjoy the winter season with her special someone and do things that she cannot do in the warm weather.

The chorus of the song then talks about how the narrator wants to pretend that they are at the North Pole, turning the heat into an icy cold holiday just for them. She then talks about how they will go ice skating downtown, even though it is 00 degrees outside. They will also pretend to build snowmen even though there is no snow.

The bridge of the song talks about how the narrator imagines herself and her special someone laughing in a cabin by the fireside, even though the sun outside is still blazing. This serves as a metaphor for how they can be anywhere they want to be if they just use their imagination.

In summary, "Winter Things" is a song about wanting to experience the joys of winter even when the weather outside is still hot. It serves as a reminder to take the time to enjoy and appreciate the moments we have with our loved ones, no matter what time of year it is.

Linguistically, the song is filled with imagery and metaphors that evoke a sense of nostalgia and warmth. The lyrics paint a vivid picture of the narrator's desire to experience winter, and the use of repetition in the chorus helps to emphasize this point. Additionally, the use of language such as “mid-July”, “North Pole”, and “ice cold holiday” helps to create a contrast between the warmth of summer and the cold of winter, emphasizing the narrator's longing for winter. This contrast is further emphasized by the use of personification in the lyrics, as the narrator talks about the “heat” turning into an “ice cold holiday”. 

Overall, "Winter Things" is a fun and uplifting song that captures the feeling of wanting to experience winter even when the weather is hot. Through its use of imagery and metaphors, the song is able to evoke a sense of nostalgia and warmth, and its catchy chorus and upbeat rhythm make it an enjoyable and memorable tune.
