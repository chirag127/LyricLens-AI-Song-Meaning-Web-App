The song "Side to Side (Live)" by Ariana Grande featuring Nicki Minaj is a song about having fun and letting go of inhibitions. The chorus talks about how the two have been out all night and they don't need to think about anything. Ariana then sings the verses with her signature R&B-style voice, singing about how she's been trying to hide her feelings but it's no use, because she's drawn to her lover like a moth to a flame. In the pre-chorus, Ariana sings about how her friends keep warning her to stay away, but she can't hear them because she's too busy feeling her lover. The song then ends with a rap from Nicki Minaj, which further emphasizes the theme of letting go and having fun.

The song is both upbeat and catchy, and it conveys the message of being unapologetically yourself and not worrying about what anyone else thinks. The lyrics are simple and repetitive, but they create an infectious energy that helps to drive the song. The song also features some clever wordplay, as when Nicki raps, “Rappers in they feelings ’cause they feelin’ me/What I give zero and I got zero chill in me.”

The song also speaks to the power of self-love and confidence. Ariana sings about knowing her worth and not letting anyone else define her. The song encourages listeners to embrace their inner wild side and not be afraid to let go and have fun.

In terms of a linguistic analysis of the song, some of the key emotions expressed in the lyrics are confidence, power, and freedom. The lyrics also reflect a feeling of liberation and empowerment. The song celebrates being yourself and not caring what other people think. The lyrics also contain social messages, such as the value of self-love and self-confidence.

Overall, "Side to Side (Live)" is an upbeat and energetic song that encourages listeners to let go and have fun. The song speaks to the power of self-love and confidence and celebrates being unapologetically yourself. The clever lyrics and catchy beat make it a great song to dance and sing along to.
