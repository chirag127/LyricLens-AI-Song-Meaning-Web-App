The song “Still Hurting” by Ariana Grande is a melancholic ballad that speaks of the pain of a break-up. The song’s lyrics depict the narrator’s heartache and longing as they come to terms with the fact that their relationship with ‘Jamie’ is over. In the first verse, the narrator reflects on Jamie’s decision to move on and their own inability to process this new reality. The chorus serves as an interrogation of Jamie’s actions, questioning the truth of their promises and the finality of their break-up. 

The second verse focuses on Jamie’s determination to leave and the narrator’s fear of being left behind. The bridge between the second and third verses serves as a reminder that the narrator should do the same - leave and find something better. The third verse then speaks of the narrator’s grief and confusion as they grapple with the fact that Jamie is gone for good. 

The song’s lyrics are filled with emotions of grief, heartache, and confusion, as the narrator struggles to come to terms with their loss. Through the use of imagery and metaphors, Grande is able to convey the full depth of the narrator’s pain. The song is a powerful exploration of the difficulty of letting go of a past relationship and the universal experience of heartbreak. 

In conclusion, “Still Hurting” is an emotive and melancholic ballad that speaks to the pain of a break-up. Through its lyrics, the song is able to explore the full range of emotions felt by someone going through the process of letting go. The song is a powerful exploration of the universal experience of heartache and serves as a reminder that everyone has their own way of coping with loss.
