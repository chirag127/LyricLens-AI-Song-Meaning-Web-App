In her song "Ain't About What You Got", Ariana Grande sings about the importance of not basing relationships on material possessions. The song starts with her singing about her partner, and how she heard some bad things about him. She thought the stories were just gossip, but it turns out that he was taking the "sugar" from other women. The chorus of the song then repeats the message that it is not about what you have, but what you do with it. 

The second verse continues the story of her partner, and how her friends warned her to open her eyes and see that he was playing her. She then talks about how someone can look sexy on the outside, but still be shady on the inside. The song then leads into the hook, where she repeats the message of the chorus. 

The song features a catchy melody and fun beats, and the lyricism is clever and direct. It carries a strong message about avoiding superficial relationships, and encourages listeners to focus on substance, not appearance. Through her lyrics, Ariana Grande conveys the idea that it is not what you have that matters, but what you do with it. The song also has an upbeat tone, which helps to make the message more accessible and enjoyable. 

Linguistically, "Ain't About What You Got" contains a mix of positive and negative sentiment. The verses are mainly negative, as they explore the idea of a partner who is not faithful. The chorus, however, is more positive, as it emphasizes the importance of not judging people based on their possessions. The repetition of the chorus throughout the song also helps to drive home this message, and its upbeat tone helps to make it more memorable. 

Overall, "Ain't About What You Got" is a catchy and powerful song that encourages listeners to focus on substance rather than appearances. Through her clever lyrics and catchy beats, Ariana Grande conveys a strong message about the importance of focusing on what people do rather than what they have.
