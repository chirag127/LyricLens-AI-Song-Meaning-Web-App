Ariana Grande’s song “Let It Rain” is an upbeat, empowering anthem about letting go of the past and moving on. She is determined to make the best of her situation and is not afraid to face whatever comes her way.

The chorus of the song then reiterates the message of the verses: no matter what comes her way, Grande is determined to let it rain and let the sun shine down on her. She is ready to accept whatever comes her way and is not afraid to face it head-on.

The overall message of the song is one of resilience and determination. The song also celebrates the power of letting go, as Grande is ready to accept whatever comes her way and is not afraid to face it head-on.
