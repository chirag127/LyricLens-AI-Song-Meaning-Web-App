The song "Positions (Clean)" by Ariana Grande is a heartfelt, upbeat love song about longing for a connection with someone and being willing to switch up the traditional roles in a relationship. In the first verse, Ariana sings of being "heaven sent" to her partner, and expresses her hope that she won't "repeat history" by making the same mistakes she has in the past. In the chorus, Ariana expresses her desire to meet her partner's mother on a Sunday and make a "lotta love" on a Monday, emphasizing the significance of the relationship. In the second verse, she sings about being willing to "switch up the positions" for her partner and do anything for them; this could be interpreted as a willingness to break from traditional gender roles and do whatever it takes to make the relationship work. In the bridge, Ariana expresses her willingness to do things she doesn't usually do for her partner, emphasizing her deep commitment to the relationship. 

The song's lyrics provide an optimistic outlook on relationships, emphasizing the importance of breaking from traditional norms and doing whatever it takes to make a relationship work. The lyrics also emphasize the importance of deep commitment and trust in a relationship and how these are essential for a successful relationship. 

From a linguistic analysis perspective, the song has a positive, hopeful tone and the lyrics make use of many metaphors and figures of speech to express Ariana's love and commitment to her partner. The use of repetition emphasizes the importance of her emotions and her willingness to go above and beyond for her partner. The use of vivid imagery such as "cookin' in the kitchen" and "jumpin' through hoops" helps to bring the lyrics to life and further emphasize the power of Ariana's love for her partner. 

Overall, "Positions (Clean)" is an upbeat, uplifting love song that emphasizes the importance of breaking from traditional roles in a relationship and doing whatever it takes to make it work. The lyrics are vivid and powerful, and the use of repetition and figures of speech helps to convey the emotions of Ariana's love for her partner.
