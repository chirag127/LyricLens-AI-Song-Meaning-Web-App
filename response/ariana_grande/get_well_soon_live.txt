Ariana Grande’s song “Get Well Soon” is a powerful, uplifting anthem about finding strength in the midst of hardship. The song starts off with a chorus of “you can feel it, feel it”, which sets the tone for the rest of the song. Throughout the verses, Grande conveys the idea of being overwhelmed by her own thoughts and worries, and the need to take care of oneself. She sings about how it’s important to “unfollow fear” and “put the little lights on your phone”, and how it takes both her and her listener to “make us” in order to achieve success. In the chorus, Grande sings that “if it ain’t one thing, it’s another” and that she’ll be “right there just to hug you” and to remind her listener that they “shouldn’t be alone”. 

The lyrics of “Get Well Soon” are filled with emotion, as Grande conveys her message of hope and resilience, and encourages her listeners to keep fighting and pushing forward. The song is written in a personal way, which helps to convey the message of self-care. The use of repetition and the chorus also help to emphasize the idea of perseverance and resilience, as Grande sings about how it’s important to keep going and to never give up. 

Linguistically, the song has several features that help to convey Grande’s message. The use of repetition in the chorus helps to emphasize the idea of perseverance and resilience, while the use of personal pronouns like “you” and “me” helps to convey the message of self-care. Additionally, the use of imperatives like “unfollow fear” and “put the little lights on your phone” helps to convey the idea that it’s important to take care of oneself and to keep moving forward. 

In summary, “Get Well Soon” is a powerful, uplifting anthem about finding strength in the midst of hardship. The lyrics are filled with emotion, as Grande conveys her message of hope and resilience, and encourages her listeners to keep fighting and pushing forward. The use of repetition, personal pronouns, and imperatives help to emphasize Grande’s message, making for an emotionally powerful song.
