The song “Woman Races” by Ariana Grande is a powerful anthem about female empowerment and strength. The song celebrates the strength and resilience of women, and encourages them to stand up and fight for themselves.

The song’s chorus is repeated throughout the song, emphasizing the importance of female strength and resilience.
