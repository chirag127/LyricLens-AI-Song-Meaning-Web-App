"Thinking Bout You" by Ariana Grande is a heartfelt, melodic mid-tempo song that reflects on missing a past lover and longing for them to come back. In the second verse, the singer reflects on how her lover left her and how she's been trying to make it through the night without them. The pre-chorus and chorus repeat, then the song closes with the singer expressing her need for her lover and admitting that she can't help but think about them.
