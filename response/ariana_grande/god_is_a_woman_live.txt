The song “God is a Woman” by Ariana Grande is a powerful and uplifting anthem about female power and strength. In the song, Grande sings about how, no matter what people may think, she is confident in who she is and in the power of being a woman. The lyrics paint a vivid picture of her self-assuredness and the belief that God is a woman. 

The first verse of the song starts out with Grande singing about how she loves the way she moves and how she touches people, and how when all is said and done, they will believe that God is a woman. This is a strong statement of female empowerment, conveying the message that women can be powerful and strong, and are to be respected. 

The second verse of the song talks about not wanting to waste any time, and how Grande has the power to have things any way she likes. This conveys the idea of taking control of one’s own life and not allowing anyone else to dictate how one should live. 

The third verse of the song talks about Grande telling people all the things they should know, and how they can be saved by her. This could symbolize how Grande is saving people from their own doubts and misconceptions, and by believing in her they can be saved. 

The fourth verse of the song talks about how Grande is telling people the way she likes it and how she wants it. This could symbolize her strength and her determination to have things her own way and not be dictated by what others may think. 

The chorus of the song reiterates the main theme of the song: that God is a woman. Grande is confidently proclaiming her belief in female empowerment and strength, and how no matter what people may think, they will come to believe that God is a woman. 

In summary, the song “God is a Woman” by Ariana Grande is an uplifting anthem about female power and strength. Through her lyrics, Grande conveys her self-assuredness and belief that women are to be respected. She proclaims her confidence in her own abilities, and encourages others to take control of their own lives and believe in themselves. The song is a powerful reminder of the importance of female empowerment and strength. 

From a linguistic analysis of the song lyrics, it is apparent that Grande is expressing a range of emotions, from confidence to determination to strength.
