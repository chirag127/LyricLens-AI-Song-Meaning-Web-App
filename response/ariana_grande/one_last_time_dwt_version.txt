The song "One Last Time" by Ariana Grande is a powerful ballad that speaks to the heartache of letting go of someone you love. In this song, Grande talks about her feelings of guilt and regret for not being able to give her lover the things he needs, and the pain of having to let him go. The first verse of the song speaks to the feelings of guilt and regret, as Grande sings about how she should have done better and how she was a liar and gave into the fire. The chorus then shifts to the pain of having to let go, with the lyrics “so one last time I need to be the one who takes you home, one more time I promise after that I’ll let you go.” The song speaks to the pain of having to move on from a relationship, and the guilt and regret that comes with it. 

The language used throughout the song is very poetic and emotive, with Grande using vivid imagery to describe her feelings. The lyrics are full of emotion, expressing the heartache and sadness of the situation. This can be seen in the chorus, where she sings “all I really care is you wake up in my arms one last time”, which captures the desperation and sorrow of having to let go. 

When it comes to a linguistic analysis of the song, the predominant emotion expressed is sadness. The lyrics are full of grief and regret, as she reflects on her mistakes and the pain of having to let go. There is also a sense of acceptance and resilience in the lyrics, as Grande acknowledges the pain of the situation but also finds strength in the idea of letting go and moving on. 

In summary, "One Last Time" is a powerful and emotive song that speaks to the heartache of letting go of someone you love. The lyrics are full of emotion, expressing the grief and regret of the situation, as well as the strength that comes with acceptance. The language used throughout the song is poetic and evocative, making it a perfect example of Ariana Grande's powerful and emotive songwriting.
