In the song "God is a Woman" by Ariana Grande, the singer explores the idea that the divine and feminine are intertwined. She sings about how she can show her partner a good time and make them believe in the power of femininity. In the chorus, she repeats the line "You'll believe God is a woman" to emphasize this point.

In the first verse, Grande talks about how she knows how to please her partner and that they should take things slow. In the pre-chorus, she sings about how she can be everything her partner tells her not to be and that she will flourish despite any criticism. She also hints at the idea that the universe is within her.

In the second verse, Grande sings about how she wants to tell her partner all they need to know in order to save their soul. She encourages them to confess and promises that they will be blessed if they do. In the final pre-chorus and chorus, she repeats the idea that she can make her partner believe that God is a woman through her touch and presence.

Overall, "God is a Woman" is a confident and empowering song that celebrates femininity and the divine. In my opinion, the song is a great addition to Grande's discography and showcases her vocal abilities and artistic vision.
