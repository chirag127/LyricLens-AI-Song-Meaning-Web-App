Santa Tell Me is a song by Ariana Grande in which she pleads with Santa to tell her if the person she loves will be around next year. The song has an upbeat, pop feel to it, with a twinkling instrumental backing that gives it a festive, seasonal vibe.

The chorus then has Grande pleading with Santa to tell her if he's really there, and if the person she loves really cares. She doesn't want to give her heart away unless she knows he'll be around next year. 

The second verse finds Grande reflecting on how she's been down this road before and has fallen in love on Christmas night only for the person not to be around the next day. Now she needs someone to hold her and be her fire in the cold, but it's hard to tell if this is just a fling or if it's pre-true love that he thinks of so that she won't be alone next Christmas. 

The song ends with Grande pleading with Santa to tell her if he's really there and if the person she loves really cares.

Santa Tell Me is a sweet and catchy pop song about the uncertainty of falling in love and the hope of finding true love.
