"thank u, next" is a song by Ariana Grande in which she reflects on her past relationships and the lessons she has learned from them. In the first verse, she mentions three of her exes: Sean, Ricky, and Pete. She reflects on how each relationship ended and how she has grown from them. In the chorus, she thanks her exes for teaching her about love, patience, and pain, and for helping her become the person she is today. In the second verse, she mentions meeting someone new, named Ari, and how she is happy with where she is in life. The song is a pop-ballad with a catchy beat and relatable lyrics. Many fans have praised the song for its empowering message and catchy melody. Overall, "thank u, next" is a well-written and well-received song.
