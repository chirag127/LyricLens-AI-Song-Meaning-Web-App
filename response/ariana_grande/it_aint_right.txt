The song "It Ain't Right" by Ariana Grande is a powerful anthem about heartbreak, betrayal, and the recognition of one's own self-worth. In the first verse, Ariana expresses her feelings of frustration and anger at being taken advantage of by her lover. She describes how her lover has brought her to her "melting point" and how their lack of love leaves her feeling "paranoid". She questions how she can win when her lover wears her out and spreads her thin. 

The chorus is a defiant proclamation of self-love, strength, and autonomy. Ariana recognizes her lover's faults and intentions, and decides that it is time for her to let go. She repeats the refrain "it ain't right" to emphasize her feelings of disappointment and betrayal. This is further reinforced by the lyrics "all I ever did was love" and "I know I did it all for love". 

The second verse is a repetition of the first, emphasizing the same feelings of anger, betrayal, and frustration. Ariana emphasizes her newfound understanding of her lover's true intentions, and her desire to reclaim her autonomy. 

The bridge is a climatic moment of strength and independence. Ariana recognizes the fact that she can no longer depend on her lover, and decides to take matters into her own hands. She claims her own power, declaring that she "took off too fast" and "jumped overboard" in order to finish last, and that she is "shoving [her lover] down". 

In summary, "It Ain't Right" is a powerful and emotional song about the discovery of one's own self-worth and independence after a period of heartbreak and betrayal. The song is an anthem of strength, resilience, and self-love.

Linguistically, the song is an expression of defiance. Through the repetition of the refrain "it ain't right", Ariana conveys her feelings of disappointment and betrayal. Additionally, the lyrics "all I ever did was love" and "I know I did it all for love" emphasize the strength and power of her love, and her recognition of her own worth. 

Overall, "It Ain't Right" is a powerful and emotional song about resilience, strength, and self-love. The song is a powerful anthem for anyone feeling betrayed and heartbroken. Ariana's powerful lyrics and defiant refrain resonate with the listener, conveying her message of self-love, autonomy, and resilience.
