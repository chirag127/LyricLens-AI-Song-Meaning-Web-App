Ariana Grande's song "You'll Never Know (Live)" is a lamentation about a relationship that ended too late. In the first verse, the narrator expresses her frustration that words came too late; all the drama has been too much, and the other person has been "replaced" in her life. In the pre-chorus, she challenges the other person to "man up" and tell her how he really feels, but it's too late and she's already with somebody else. In the chorus, she reflects on the relationship and what it could have been if he had spoken up earlier. 

The song is a powerful expression of longing and regret. The narrator is disappointed that she never got to experience the relationship she wanted, and the listener is struck with a sense of what could have been. Through the use of clever wordplay and clever metaphors, Ariana Grande effectively conveys the emotions of the situation.

Linguistically, the song employs a range of emotions, from frustration and anger in the first verse to sadness and regret in the chorus. The narrator's tone shifts from disbelief in the first verse to disappointment in the chorus, and her words become more direct and emotive as the song progresses. The use of repetition in the chorus highlights the narrator's feelings of regret and longing. 

Overall, "You'll Never Know (Live)" is an emotionally powerful song that expresses the pain of unfulfilled potential. The lyrics are cleverly written and effectively convey the narrator's emotions, and the music offers a fitting backdrop to the story.
