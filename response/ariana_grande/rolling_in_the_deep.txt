"Rolling in the Deep" by Ariana Grande is an emotionally charged song about the pain of a breakup. The song begins with Grande emphasizing the hurt of the breakup while she sings "There's a fire starting in my heart, reaching a fever pitch and it's bringing me out the dark". The lyrics then go on to explain how the breakup has left her feeling betrayed and heartbroken. 

The chorus of the song emphasizes the pain and regret of the breakup as Grande sings "You're gonna wish you never had met me, rolling in the deep. Tears are gonna fall, rolling in the deep". In each verse, Grande reflects on the love they once shared and how it is now gone. This is further emphasized in the bridge when she sings "You had my heart inside of your hands, and you played it to the beat".

Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals Grande's intense emotions of hurt and betrayal. The repetition of certain phrases such as "you're gonna wish you never had met me" and "we could have had it all" further emphasize her feelings of regret. The song also contains several metaphors, such as "make a home down there" which reflects Grande's feelings that her heart is now no longer her own.

In summary, "Rolling in the Deep" is an emotionally charged song about the pain of a breakup. Grande's lyrics convey her intense emotions of hurt and betrayal and emphasize her feelings of regret.
