Ariana Grande's song "Zero to Hero" follows the story of the titular character Hercules, a young lad who was initially considered a nobody, but quickly rose to fame and glory. In the first verse, the song paints a picture of Hercules as a person of the week in the Greek opinion polls, and someone who is able to take on difficult challenges. In the second verse, we see Hercules become wealthier and more famous, so much so that people line up just to witness his feats. The third verse introduces the idea that Hercules is not only strong, but also sweet and undefeated, which only furthers his popularity. 

The fourth verse introduces the idea that Hercules is a gladiator, and someone whose daring deeds are great theatre. The fifth verse emphasizes that Hercules is not only bold, but also sweet, making him a favorite amongst the crowds. The chorus of the song expresses how Hercules rose from being a nobody to a hero in no time at all, and is now riding high. 

The lyrics of "Zero to Hero" offer a positive message about the power of hard work and perseverance. It is a reminder that anyone can rise from the bottom to the top if they stay focused and determined. The song also paints a vivid picture of the rise of Hercules, and the excitement that comes with his newfound fame and glory. 

From a linguistic analysis standpoint, it is interesting to note that Ariana Grande uses a lot of alliteration in the song, such as "person of the week" and "sweet and undefeated". This helps to create a sense of flow and rhythm to the song, and emphasizes the idea that Hercules has become a hero in the eyes of the people. Furthermore, the repetition of the phrase "zero to hero" in the chorus reinforces the idea of Hercules' meteoric rise. 

In summary, Ariana Grande's song "Zero to Hero" is a powerful and uplifting anthem about the power of hard work and determination. Its vivid lyrics and use of alliteration make it an enjoyable and captivating song that sends a positive message about achieving greatness.
