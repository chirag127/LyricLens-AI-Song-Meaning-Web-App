"Side To Side" is a song by American singer Ariana Grande featuring rapper Nicki Minaj. In the song, Grande sings about a romantic relationship that has left her feeling physically exhausted. The chorus repeats the line "I've been here all night, I've been here all day / And boy, you got me walkin' side to side" to emphasize this feeling of physical strain. In the verses, she describes the temptation and excitement of the relationship, and how it makes her ignore the advice of her friends.

Nicki Minaj adds a rap verse in which she brags about her own success and sexual prowess. She references her "fresh type of flow" and "ride dick bicycle" line, which became popular memes after the song's release.

The song is upbeat and catchy, with a danceable beat and sing-along chorus. It was a commercial success, reaching the top 10 on the Billboard Hot 100 chart and being certified double platinum by the RIAA.

In terms of a review or opinion of the song, "Side To Side" is a fun and energetic track that showcases both Ariana Grande and Nicki Minaj's talents. The lyrics are relatable and the beat is infectious, making it a great addition to any party playlist.
