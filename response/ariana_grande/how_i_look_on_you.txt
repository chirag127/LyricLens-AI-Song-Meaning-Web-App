"How I Look on You" by Ariana Grande is a song that speaks about the struggles of being in the public eye and being loved for who you are rather than for the image that people might have of you. In the first verse, Ariana talks about how she wants someone to love her for who she is, despite the fame and attention she has. She doesn't want to be judged for how she looks, but instead for who she is.

In the second verse, Ariana talks about how her fame has changed the dynamics of her relationship. She admits that her partner isn't around as much as he used to be because of her success and popularity, and that she only sees him on the big screen.

The bridge of the song brings both of these themes together, asking her partner if he likes her for who she is or just for her image in the public. The chorus then reiterates this sentiment, repeating "just how I look on you" as a reminder of what people might think of her.

Overall, "How I Look on You" is a thoughtful and introspective song that speaks to the difficultly of navigating relationships while in the public eye. The song speaks to the fragility of relationships and how fame can change the dynamics of them. The lyrics are poetic and the production is beautiful. Linguistically, the lyrics are full of emotion, with the words "just how I look on you" being repeated as a reminder of what people might think of her.

This is an impactful and meaningful song that speaks to the struggles of fame and the power of being loved for who you are rather than for your image. It is an important reminder to appreciate the people in our lives for who they are and to make sure we're not caught up in the glamour of fame.
