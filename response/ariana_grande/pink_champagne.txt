 "Pink Champagne" by Ariana Grande is an upbeat, empowering song about living life to the fullest and embracing its ups and downs. In the first verse, she speaks of working hard with no end in sight and then deciding to make the most of it by painting the city in pink champagne. In the pre-chorus, she encourages listeners to express their feelings and be free. The chorus emphasizes the idea of partying and having fun while letting the world know their names. 

The second verse speaks to those who may not be able to get into a party and suggests creating their own instead. In the pre-chorus, she again encourages listeners to express themselves, this time to the point where it cannot be contained. The chorus is repeated with the same message of partying and letting the world know their names. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song contains emotional and empowering language, with words such as "grinding," "bubble," and "trouble," which all connote movement and suggest a desire to keep going and make the most of life. Additionally, the use of "paint" implies a level of creativity and a willingness to make a statement, while "screaming" suggests a sense of power and strength. 

Overall, "Pink Champagne" is an upbeat, empowering song about living life to the fullest and embracing its ups and downs. With its creative and empowering lyrics, it encourages listeners to make their own fun and be themselves while also letting the world know their names.
