In the song "Dangerous Woman" by Ariana Grande, the artist speaks about taking control of her life and being a confident and independent woman who is not afraid to take risks. In the first verse, Grande declares her independence and her decision to test her limits. The chorus reiterates this message, emphasizing her determination and self-assurance. She proclaims that she has nothing to prove and is bulletproof in her decisions. 

The pre-chorus speaks to the desire to savor and enjoy the moment, and the post-chorus speaks to the admiration of the "bad girls" who have the confidence and freedom to do as they please. The bridge speaks to the admiration of a boy, possibly the object of her desire, who is making her feel like a dangerous woman. 

Overall, the song speaks to the power of women and their confidence and strength. It emphasizes the importance of living life to the fullest and being brave enough to take risks. The song is both an empowering and uplifting anthem for women everywhere. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song contains numerous positive words and phrases such as "test my limits," "control," "focused," "taste the flavor," and "savoring." These words and phrases contribute to the empowering sentiment of the song and emphasize the artist's message of self-assurance and confidence. Additionally, there is a sense of freedom and independence in the lyrics which can be interpreted as a metaphor for female empowerment. 

In conclusion, "Dangerous Woman" is an inspiring and empowering song about women taking control of their lives and having the courage to take risks. The lyrics contain numerous positive words and phrases and the overall message of female empowerment is clear. The song is both an anthem for women and a reminder for everyone to live life to the fullest.
