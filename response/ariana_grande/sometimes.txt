Ariana Grande's song "Sometimes" is a romantic ballad about the strength of a committed relationship. In the song, Grande speaks of how she used to be cautious and reckless, but that all of her emotions are now tied to her partner. She expresses her reluctance to ever let go of this relationship, as it has become a part of her very being. 

The song's lyrics speak to the depth of the connection between Grande and her partner, and how it has become a tattoo on her mind. She expresses her fear of being without her partner and the difficulty of breathing when they are not around. 

The overall message of the song is one of commitment, loyalty, and deep connection. Grande conveys a strong sense of security in the relationship and an understanding that no matter what, they will always be together. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song conveys a sense of security, comfort, and reliability. The use of repetition in the chorus conveys a sense of certainty, while the lyrics also speak to the importance of the relationship and the strength of the bond between them. 

Overall, "Sometimes" is a romantic and heartfelt song about the strength of a committed relationship. It conveys a strong sense of security and loyalty, and speaks to the importance of the relationship and the bond between the two people.
