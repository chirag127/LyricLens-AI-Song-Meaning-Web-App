In Ariana Grande's song "Bad Idea," she reflects on her own decisions to engage in a relationship with someone even though she knows it's not the best thing for her. In the first verse, she expresses her longing for someone to save her from her current situation, and her hesitance to get involved in a relationship. The chorus of the song then talks about her bad idea of taking a little bit of time away from the person she's in a relationship with and forgetting about him. 

In the second verse, Grande acknowledges that she knows it's not the right thing to do and that she needs someone else to help her numb the pain. The second chorus is a reiteration of the bad idea of taking a little bit of time away and forgetting about the person she's with. 

The song's lyrics effectively convey the emotions and feelings of a person in an unhealthy relationship. Through the use of repetition, Grande emphasizes the internal struggle between wanting to break away from the relationship and, at the same time, wanting to stay in it. The song's lyrics also demonstrate the need for self-control in order to make the right decision, as Grande expresses her desire to take control of the situation. 

The song is an effective way of conveying the difficulties of being in an unhealthy relationship and the internal struggle of trying to make the right decision. Grande's lyrics effectively capture the emotions and feelings of the situation and allow the listener to relate to her story. Additionally, the song's chorus serves as a reminder to take a step back and assess the situation before making a decision. 

Through linguistic analysis of the song's lyrics, one can detect emotions such as longing, desperation, and hesitance. The lyrics also showcase Grande's internal struggle between wanting to stay in the relationship and wanting to break away from it. Furthermore, the lyrics demonstrate social tendencies such as the need for companionship and the desire to make the best decision for oneself. 

In summary, Ariana Grande's song "Bad Idea" is an effective way of conveying the emotions and feelings associated with being in an unhealthy relationship. Grande's lyrics effectively capture the internal struggle of wanting to stay in the relationship and wanting to break away from it, as well as the need for self-control and making the right decision. The song is an excellent reminder to take a step back and assess the situation before making a decision.
