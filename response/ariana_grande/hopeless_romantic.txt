"Hopeless Romantic" by Ariana Grande is a song about trying to find love, but finding it difficult to find someone who will love her back. The song starts off with the line "I thought that I was a hopeless romantic", which shows her disappointment and frustration in not being able to find a romantic partner. The next verse goes on to explain how she thought it would be easy to find love like her grandparents, but she has been unsuccessful in her search.

The chorus of the song emphasizes this idea of being a "hopeless romantic" by repeating the line "I thought that I was a hopeless romantic" before transitioning into the next verse. The second verse of the song further expresses her difficulty in finding love, as she sings "I don't wanna fall in love just to fall out again". This line conveys the fear of being hurt and her hesitation in entering a relationship.

The third verse of the song is a plea to find someone who will understand her and love her for who she is, as she sings "I'm begging you, don't settle for someone who can't see you". This highlights her desire for a special kind of relationship, one where she can be herself and be accepted for who she is.

The song ends with a reprise of the chorus, as she sings "I thought that I was a hopeless romantic". This line is a reminder of the pain she has experienced in trying to find love, and how it has not been easy for her to find someone who will love her.

In summary, "Hopeless Romantic" by Ariana Grande is a song about the difficulties and frustrations of trying to find love. The lyrics convey a sense of sadness and longing, as she expresses her desire to find someone who will love her for who she is. Linguistic analysis of the song reveals emotions of disappointment and hurt, along with a strong desire to find someone she can trust and share her life with. The song is a powerful ode to the ups and downs of love, and is sure to strike a chord with many listeners.
