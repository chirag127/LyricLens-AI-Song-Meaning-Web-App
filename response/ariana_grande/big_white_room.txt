The song "Big White Room" by Ariana Grande is an emotional and powerful anthem of self-reflection and resilience. The lyrics tell the story of someone sitting in a "big white room" alone, feeling young, broken, and scared. They long for freedom and to be able to run away from their pain, but feel stuck in the moment. 

In the verses, Grande speaks to the loneliness and despair of being in a situation that feels inescapable and overwhelming. She expresses a sense of helplessness and desperation, as well as a desire to reclaim her own power. The chorus provides a sense of hope and resilience, as Grande sings of her determination to stay in control and fight her way out of the dark situation. 

The underlying message of the song is one of perseverance and strength in the face of adversity. Grande conveys the idea that no matter how hard the situation may be, there is still a way out and you can fight your way to freedom.

The lyrics of the song contain numerous linguistic features that help convey its message. For example, the repetition of words such as "crazy," "losing," and "room" creates a sense of urgency and desperation. The use of similes and metaphors, such as "feel the wind in my hair," helps to create vivid images that bring the lyrics to life. Additionally, there are several allusions to religion, such as "clench my fist and try to stay strong," which may be used to evoke feelings of hope and faith in the listener. 

Overall, "Big White Room" is an emotionally powerful song that conveys a message of hope and resilience in the face of adversity. The lyrics are filled with vivid imagery and poetic devices that help to bring the story to life, creating a stirring and memorable experience for the listener.
