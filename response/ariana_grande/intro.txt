Ariana Grande's "Intro" is a song about committing to a relationship, no matter the cost. The first verse speaks to the idea of giving all you have and not looking back. The singer acknowledges that it's not an easy road ahead, but that they will stay together and be strong no matter what. The chorus then reiterates this idea of commitment, as the singer promises to stay with their partner no matter what.

The song's lyrics are emotionally charged, as they speak to the idea of making a long-term commitment and promises of never leaving the other person. The language is also quite poetic and romantic, as the singer speaks of a "road to the sky" and of them and their partner being "shining every night". This conveys a sense of optimism and hope for the future.

In terms of its linguistic analysis, the song is packed with emotion. The use of words like "promise", "love", and "road" all convey strong feelings of commitment and hope for the future. The use of repetition throughout the song also reinforces the idea of never leaving the other person, as the singer repeats their promises multiple times.

In summary, "Intro" is a song about making a commitment to a relationship and never looking back. The lyrics are poetic and romantic, and the language conveys a strong sense of emotion and hope for the future. This is a beautiful song that speaks to the power of love and commitment in relationships.
