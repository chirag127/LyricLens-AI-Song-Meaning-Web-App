The song "Somewhere Over the Rainbow (Live from Manchester)" by Ariana Grande is a beautiful and heartfelt song about hope and dreams. In the first verse, Grande sings about a land she has heard of in a lullaby, suggesting that this land is a place of comfort and peace. In the refrain, she expresses her desire to one day wish upon a star and wake up in a place where troubles have melted away like lemon drops, a place far away from the chimney tops. In the second verse, Grande asks why, if bluebirds can fly over the rainbow, she can't do the same. 

The song speaks to the power of dreams and the hope of a brighter future. It encourages listeners to look beyond their current circumstances, to find a place of peace and joy. The song's lyrics are filled with imagery of a beautiful, distant land and suggest a longing for a better future.

Grande also uses repetition of the refrain to emphasize the power of her hope. Additionally, the use of "why" in the second verse suggests a feeling of frustration and helplessness in the face of her current situation.
