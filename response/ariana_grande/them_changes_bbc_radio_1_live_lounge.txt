The song "Them Changes" by Ariana Grande is a poignant and heartfelt examination of the difficulty of navigating a broken relationship. The song begins with the lyrics “so nobody move, there’s blood on the floor and I can’t find my heart, so where did it go”. These lines set the stage for a tale of a person’s heart being taken away, and the loss and confusion that accompanies this. 

The second verse takes a deeper dive into the emotions of the song, expressing the difficulty of coping with this loss. The lines “did I leave it in the cold, so please give it back ‘cause it’s not yours to take” highlight the helplessness of the situation, as the person is unable to get their heart back. The chorus then further emphasizes this emotional state, as the person begs for help in the line “somebody help ‘cause I can’t find my way”.

The third verse shifts focus, as the person reflects on the relationship that led to this pain. The lines “why in the hell would I give my heart to you just to watch you throw it in the trash” express the anger and frustration of being betrayed and abandoned. The bridge then serves as the climax of the song, as the singer expresses the emptiness and despair of the situation with the words “now I’m sitting here with a black hole in my chest, a heartless broken mess”.

In summary, the song “Them Changes” by Ariana Grande is a powerful and emotional look at the pain and loss of a broken relationship. The lyrics are able to effectively convey a range of raw emotions, from confusion and helplessness to anger and despair. The song also shows the importance of self-love and care, in order to ensure that one does not succumb to the darkness of a broken heart.

From a linguistic perspective, the song has many features that help to convey the emotions of the singer. The repetitive use of the pronoun “I” helps to emphasize the personal nature of the song, while the use of simple language helps to capture the raw emotion of the lyrics. Additionally, the use of metaphors, such as “blood on the floor” and “a black hole in my chest”, helps to bring the lyrics to life and provide a vivid image of the situation.

Overall, “Them Changes” is a powerful and emotionally charged song that is sure to resonate with many listeners. The song’s ability to capture such raw emotions in such an effective manner is a testament to Ariana Grande’s abilities as a songwriter.
