The second verse then goes on to explain why the person is dangerous. It talks about the chill he gives her inside, and how nothing else scares her as much as him. This is followed by the chorus again, reinforcing the notion that the person is dangerous. 

The song concludes with a bridge that repeats the phrase "he's a dangerous boy", emphasizing the overall theme of the song.
