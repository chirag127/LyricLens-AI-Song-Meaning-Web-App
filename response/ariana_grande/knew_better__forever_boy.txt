The song "Knew Better/Forever Boy" by Ariana Grande is a romantic ballad that talks about the love that can be found when two people come together and the potential for it to last forever. In the first part of the song, Ariana expresses her doubts about her current relationship, saying that even though the guy can "turn her on," she doesn't believe what he says and she is not feeling him anymore. She then goes on to say that if he "knew better," he would do better, and that if he loved her like she loves him, they could be together. 

The second part of the song takes a more hopeful tone, as she talks about how being with this person has made her feel happy, and how she knows all of his likes and dislikes and what makes him cry. She then expresses her emotions by saying that he should be "forever mine" and that he should be hers "all the time." 

The song is a beautiful ode to the power of love, and it speaks to the idea that when we find someone who truly understands us and makes us feel special, we should never let them go. The lyrics also contain a lot of emotion, from the doubt and uncertainty in the first part to the hope and joy in the second. 

In terms of a review, this song is a great representation of Ariana Grande's musical style and her ability to convey emotion through her music. Her voice is powerful, and it carries the emotion of the song very well. The lyrics are also very catchy and memorable, making the song enjoyable to listen to. All in all, this is a great song and definitely worth a listen.
