Ariana Grande's "Tour Guide" is a powerful anthem of strength and resilience, as the singer proclaims her independence and self-empowerment. The song begins with the lines "I'm every woman, Vogue" which sets the tone of the song as a celebration of self-love and womanhood. In the chorus, Grande declares "I'm my own tour guide, I'm gonna show the way," emphasizing her determination to forge her own path and take control of her own destiny.

The verses of the song tell the story of Grande's journey towards self-discovery and independence. She sings of "honeymoon tours," "dangerous woman tours," and "sweetener world tours," alluding to her own experiences with different types of relationships and explorations of her own identity. The lyrics back up her message of self-reliance as she sings "Leave me lonely reprise, no need to apologize" and "Love me harder, breathin', right there, you'll never know, break your heart right back," demonstrating her strength and her ability to take control of her own life.

Overall, Grande's "Tour Guide" is a powerful song that speaks to the strength and resilience of womanhood. Through her lyrics, Grande celebrates her own self-empowerment and independence, and encourages other women to do the same. On a linguistic level, the song is imbued with a sense of strength and confidence, as well as a sense of defiance and determination, which is highlighted in lines such as "Leave me lonely reprise, no need to apologize" and "Love me harder, breathin', right there, you'll never know, break your heart right back." This creates an anthemic feel that is both uplifting and inspiring, ultimately making for a powerful song that speaks to the power of womanhood.
