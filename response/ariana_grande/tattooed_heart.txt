The song "Tattooed Heart" by Ariana Grande is a love song that speaks to the desire to be with someone for as long as possible, even if it doesn't last forever. In the first verse, the singer expresses her desire for the person's love, saying that all she needs is their love to get her blood rushing. She then references the old-fashioned notion of going steady, saying that she wants to be the name on their tattooed heart.

In the chorus, the singer makes it clear that she is not asking for too much, as she doesn't want to make her partner crazy. She just wants the person to call her if they want her and show her how much they want to be with her. She then asks to be wrapped up in their jacket and to be kissed in the moonlight, wanting to trace the lines of their tattooed heart.

The song's lyrics offer a deep insight into the sentiment of love and longing. The repetition of the phrase "tattooed heart" throughout the song emphasizes the idea that the singer wants to be a part of the person's life for as long as possible. The use of imagery like the moonlight and jackets draws the listener in, creating a dream-like atmosphere.

The song's lyrics can also be analyzed for their use of language to express emotions. The singer conveys a deep longing for love with words like "lovin'" and "crazy," and her desire for closeness with phrases such as "wrap me in your jacket" and "trace the lines on your tattooed heart." The singer's tone also suggests a deep yearning for lasting love, which is further underscored by her insistence that her name be on the person's tattooed heart.

In conclusion, "Tattooed Heart" by Ariana Grande is a heartfelt love song that speaks to the desire for lasting love. The song's lyrics offer an emotive journey through the singer's longing for closeness and her desire to be with the person for as long as possible. Through her use of imagery, repetition, and language, the singer conveys a powerful sentiment that resonates with the listener.
