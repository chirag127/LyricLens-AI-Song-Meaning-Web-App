The song “R.E.M.” by Ariana Grande is a dreamy and romantic song that tells a story of two people who find love in a momentary escape from reality. The third verse is about her wanting to take the person away from the world, and how she will never forget them. 

The song overall is about finding a connection with someone, and wanting to escape from reality and stay in the moment for as long as possible. The song is written in a way that is dreamy and romantic, and it captures the feeling of being in love.
