"Safety Net" by Ariana Grande is a song about taking a chance on a new relationship and letting down your guard. Throughout the song, Ariana conveys a sense of vulnerability and insecurity as she warns her love interest that she's never been as scared before, but despite her hesitancy, she still finds herself drawn to him.

The first verse introduces the premise of the song, with Ariana singing about how quickly her feelings for her love interest have grown. She wonders how it all happened so fast, but she finds herself coming back for more. The pre-chorus finds Ariana coming to terms with her feelings and realizing that, despite the risks, she wants to take the chance on this new relationship.

The chorus is the key moment of the song, as Ariana sings about being "tripping falling with no safety net." This metaphor alludes to the risks associated with opening up and taking a chance on a new relationship, and the feelings of vulnerability and insecurity that come with it. The chorus is then repeated in the second verse, with Ty Dolla Sign joining in to add his own perspective on the situation.

The bridge is a moment of respite, as Ariana and Ty Dolla Sign sing about the comfort and security of being together. They urge each other to never run away, and to always be there for one another. The chorus is repeated one final time, reinforcing the message of the song and tying the narrative together.

Overall, "Safety Net" is a song about taking a chance on a new relationship, and all the fears and insecurities that come with it. It's a heartfelt and vulnerable ballad, and Ariana and Ty Dolla Sign's duet adds an extra layer of emotion and depth to the song. Linguistically, the song relies heavily on metaphors to convey its message, with the safety net metaphor being the key element of the song. The use of this metaphor to represent the risks associated with a new relationship adds an extra layer of depth and emotion to the song and helps the listener connect to the story being told.
