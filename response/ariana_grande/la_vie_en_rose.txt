The song "La Vie En Rose" by Ariana Grande is a romantic ode to her beloved. In the chorus, Grande sings of living "la vie en rose"—a French phrase meaning "life in pink," or life in a happy and contented state. The use of the phrase "la vie en rose" also serves to emphasize the happiness she feels in the relationship.
