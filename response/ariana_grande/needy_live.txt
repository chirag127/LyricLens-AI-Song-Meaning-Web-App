"Needy" by Ariana Grande is a poignant and emotionally charged song about the complexities of relationships and the vulnerabilities that come with love. In the first verse, the singer describes her feelings of longing and desperation for her partner, and admits that she doesn't know how she will react if they take too long to respond. The song culminates with an expression of gratitude to her partner for understanding her "needy" tendencies, and for providing her with the love and care she needs. 

In summary, "Needy" is a heartfelt and emotive song about the struggles and vulnerabilities of love, and the importance of having a partner who can provide support and understanding. The song's lyrics are a reflection of the singer's emotional journey as she comes to terms with her neediness and her need for love and companionship.
