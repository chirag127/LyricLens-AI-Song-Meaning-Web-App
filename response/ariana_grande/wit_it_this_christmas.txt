Ariana Grande’s song “Wit It This Christmas” is an upbeat and cheerful holiday themed love song. In the first verse, Grande expresses her desire for a romantic kiss and her willingness to keep her love interest warm without the need for a fireplace. In the pre-chorus, she invites her love interest to join her in some holiday treats and promises to be the only drum he'll play. The chorus emphasizes the importance of love during the Christmas season and asks her love interest if he is “wit it”.

In the second verse, Grande proposes to forego physical presents and instead focus on the love between them, comparing herself to a candy cane or gingerbread. Once again, in the pre-chorus, she invites her partner to partake of some sweet treats while emphasizing the importance of love this Christmas season. The chorus repeats and adds emphasis to the importance of love during the Christmas season, and to the question of if her partner is “wit it”.

The song is a cheerful and lighthearted holiday love song. The lyrics convey the joy of being with the one you love during the Christmas season and the importance of spending time together. With its upbeat tempo, catchy chorus, and positive message, “Wit It This Christmas” is sure to become a holiday classic.

From a linguistic analysis perspective, the song conveys a sense of joy and optimism. The lyrics emphasize the idea of togetherness and loving one another. Grande uses the metaphor of sweet treats and the idea of being a “drummer boy” to convey the idea of being in love, while the repeated chorus of “are you wit it” emphasize the importance of being with the one you love this holiday season.
