The song “Be Alright”, a Justin Bieber cover by Ariana Grande, is a beautifully written and performed song that is full of emotion and meaning. The song is about the struggles of being in a long distance relationship, and how even though it is hard, everything will be alright in the end.

In the first verse, Grande sings about the physical distance between her and her partner, and how it is starting to affect their relationship. She sings about how she misses her partner’s face and how she needs to look into his eyes. In the second verse, she talks about the emotional distance between them, and how she is waiting for his phone call and would walk a thousand miles just to be in his arms.

The chorus of the song is where Grande really emphasizes her message of hope and reassurance. She sings that even though there will be sorrow and fights, everything will be alright in the end.

The song also has a great message of love, with Grande singing about how she cares for her partner and will always be there for him. This powerful message is further reinforced in the bridge, where she sings that they can make it through anything together.

Overall, “Be Alright” is a beautiful and powerful song that speaks to the struggles of a long distance relationship while also providing hope and reassurance that everything will be alright in the end. The lyrics are full of emotion and speak to the resilience of love and the power of two people to make it through tough times together. The song is both uplifting and inspiring, and is sure to resonate with many people.
