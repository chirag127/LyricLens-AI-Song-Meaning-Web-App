The song "Problem" by Ariana Grande featuring Big Sean and Iggy Azalea is an upbeat pop anthem that celebrates freedom from heartache. The song's lyrics are heavily rooted in the idea of overcoming obstacles and finding strength in yourself instead of relying on someone else. The first verse is sung by Ariana and focuses on how she is struggling to let go of someone she loves despite knowing that it is for the best. She expresses her inner conflict by singing about how she wants to love them, but also how she hates them. The pre-hook is where Ariana acknowledges that she should be wiser and realize that the person is not worth her time or energy. The hook, sung by Big Sean and Ariana, reinforces this sentiment by declaring that one less problem is without this person. 

The second verse, sung by Iggy Azalea, builds upon the theme of freedom in the song by emphasizing the idea that it is better to be without someone than to be with them if they are not worth it. Iggy underscores this point by singing that there are "a million yous" and that it is best to move on. The song then returns to the hook once more, with Ariana repeating "one less, one less problem". 

The overall message of the song is that it is better to be without someone if they are not worth your energy or time. It is a message of self-empowerment and encourages its listeners to be strong and independent even in the midst of heartache. 

The song is lyrically and musically compelling, with a catchy hook and memorable lyrics. The production is upbeat and light, with bright synths and a twerk-style beat. The song is both fun and empowering, making it the perfect anthem for anyone looking to move on from their past.

Linguistically, the song is full of statements of strength and self-empowerment. The lyrics focus on the idea of overcoming obstacles and not relying on someone else to make you feel complete. The use of repetition serves to emphasize the main theme of the song, and the upbeat production helps to further this message. Additionally, the use of Iggy's verse further drives home the idea that it is better to be without someone than to be stuck with them if they are not worth it. 

Overall, "Problem" is an uplifting and empowering anthem that celebrates freedom from heartache. The song is musically and lyrically compelling, and the message of self-empowerment is one that can resonate with many people.
