The song “Problem (Solo Version)” by Ariana Grande is a song about a difficult relationship between two people. The lyrics express the singer’s frustration with her partner and her desire to get out of the relationship, while also reflecting on the difficulty of breaking away from someone she still loves.

The song starts off with the lines “Hey baby even though I hate ya, I wanna love ya, I want you.” This expresses the internal conflict the singer feels between her feelings of love and her feelings of anger and resentment towards her partner. She continues by saying “Even though I can’t forgive ya, I really want to, I want you.” This expresses her desire to try and make things work, despite the fact that she can’t forgive her partner for whatever wrong was done.

The chorus of the song is the most powerful and poignant part of the lyrics. The line “I got one less problem without ya” expresses the singer’s realization that the relationship is no longer working and that she is better off without it. This is followed by the lines “I should be wiser and realize that I’ve got one less problem without ya.” This expresses the singer’s newfound understanding that the relationship was not healthy and that she is better off without it.

The song is a powerful expression of the difficulty of breaking away from a relationship that is no longer working. The singer is able to express her feelings of love and anger, and her ultimate realization that she is better off without the relationship. The lyrics are a testament to the difficulty of letting go of someone you still care about.

Linguistic analysis of the song lyrics reveals a strong presence of negative emotion, particularly in the lines “I hate ya,” “I can’t forgive ya,” and “I got one less problem without ya.” This shows the singer’s frustration and anger towards her partner and her desire to break away from the relationship. Additionally, the lyrics also reveal a sense of relief and resolution in the lines “I should be wiser and realize that I’ve got one less problem without ya.” This shows that the singer has come to terms with the fact that the relationship is no longer working and that she is better off without it.

In conclusion, “Problem (Solo Version)” by Ariana Grande is a powerful song that expresses the difficulty of breaking away from a toxic relationship. The lyrics are emotionally charged and powerful, expressing the singer’s conflicting feelings of anger, love, and finally resolution. The song serves as a reminder that sometimes it is necessary to let go of someone you still care about in order to move forward.
