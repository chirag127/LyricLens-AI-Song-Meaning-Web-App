Ariana Grande’s “Honeymoon Avenue” is a song about two people in a relationship struggling to find their way back to the bliss of the early days. In the verses, Grande paints a picture of two lovers stuck in a seemingly never-ending cycle of fighting and making up. The chorus offers a glimmer of hope in the suggestion that they can find their way back home to the “honeymoon avenue” of the beginning of the relationship. 

The song begins with a verse in which Grande looks back in the rear view mirror and realizes that the past makes much more sense than the present. She is ready to turn around and avoid a crash before it’s too late. In the next verse, Grande talks about how her partner is able to drive through rain, but chooses not to make a change. This implies that the relationship is stuck in a stagnant place and going the wrong way home. This is a metaphor for the couple being stuck in a rut and unable to find a way out. 

The chorus of the song provides a glimmer of hope, as Grande suggests returning to the “honeymoon avenue” of the beginning of the relationship. She acknowledges that it is foolish to fall in love, however, she knows that they can find their way home. In the bridge, Grande sings about being stuck in bumper-to-bumper traffic, which implies that they are in a difficult spot in the relationship, but they can still find a way out. 

Overall, “Honeymoon Avenue” is a powerful song that captures the struggles of a relationship and offers a glimmer of hope in the suggestion that they can find their way back home. Through her lyrics, Grande conveys a range of emotions, including love, frustration, and longing. The song also contains elements of social commentary, as it explores the idea of relationships being stuck in a cycle of fighting and making up. Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a sense of desperation, as well as a hope for a better future.
