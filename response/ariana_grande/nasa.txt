The song "NASA" by Ariana Grande is a powerful anthem about being independent and in charge of one's own destiny. The bridge of the song shifts focus to her partner, and explains how difficult it is for them to be apart. She acknowledges how hard it is for her partner to let her go, and how they don't want to leave each other, but that she needs to take the time to self-discover.
