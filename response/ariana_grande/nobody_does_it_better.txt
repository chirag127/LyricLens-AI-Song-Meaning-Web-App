Nobody Does It Better by Ariana Grande is a song about a woman who has been wronged by her former lover, who has chosen someone else over her. The chorus reiterates this sentiment, emphasizing that nobody can do better than her. The second verse furthers the song's themes of heartache and betrayal, with Ariana telling the man not to write his heart out in a letter as she has already read his mind. The bridge of the song is a warning to the man to remember that nobody can do better than her.

The song also features a catchy chorus, making it easy to remember and sing along to.
