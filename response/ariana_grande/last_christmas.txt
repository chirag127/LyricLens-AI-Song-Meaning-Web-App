Ariana Grande’s song “Last Christmas” is a melancholic tale of heartbreak and regret. In the first verse, the narrator expresses her pain at being reminded of her ex-lover’s betrayal of her trust. She wishes she could forget what happened, but the memory still haunts her. The chorus then reflects on the narrator’s desire to find someone new to give her heart to and to save her from her pain. In the second verse, the narrator reflects on how her ex had convinced her that they were meant to be together, only for him to break her heart once again. The third verse is a reminder of the betrayal, with the narrator asking how her ex could leave her on Christmas morning with no warning. The chorus is repeated once again, and the song ends with the narrator wishing she could forget what happened.

The song paints a vivid picture of a broken heart and the pain of betrayal. It conveys the narrator’s feelings of regret and sadness but also her determination to move on. The song’s lyrics also display a range of emotions, such as anger, disbelief and sadness. Through the use of poetic language and vivid imagery, the song conveys a sense of longing and emptiness.

Overall, “Last Christmas” is a powerful and emotive song that captures the pain of a broken relationship. With its vivid imagery and poetic language, it conveys a strong sense of longing and regret. It is a reminder that time heals all wounds, and that even in the darkest of times there is hope for a brighter future.
