Goodnight n Go, by Ariana Grande, is a song about the singer's desire to be with her crush, despite knowing that it would be better if they just said goodnight and went their separate ways. The song starts off with Grande expressing her feelings for her crush, telling them why they have to look at her the way they do and how it affects her. She then suggests a scenario, where her crush would miss their train and come stay with her, and they would have drinks and talk all night. 

The chorus is where Grande tells her crush that they should just say goodnight and go, as it would be better for both of them in the long run. She then sings about how she knows what he wants and how he can't have what he desires, before the song enters its bridge, which consists of a repeating chorus of "tadadadadadadadadadadadada".

The lyrics of Goodnight n Go provide an interesting insight into the emotions and feelings of the singer. Throughout the song, Grande expresses her desire to be with her crush, despite knowing that it would be better for both of them if they just said goodnight and went their separate ways. This dichotomy reflects the inner struggle many people feel when it comes to relationships, where the heart wants one thing and the head another.

The song also reflects the emotions of frustration and longing that come with unrequited love. Grande is clearly attracted to her crush, but knows that they can't be together, which leads to a sense of frustration and longing.

Ultimately, Goodnight n Go is an emotionally charged song that reflects the inner struggles of unrequited love. Grande's heartfelt lyrics and melodic delivery provide a powerful insight into the emotions and feelings associated with such a situation. It is a song that will resonate with many people, and one that will certainly leave a lasting impression.
