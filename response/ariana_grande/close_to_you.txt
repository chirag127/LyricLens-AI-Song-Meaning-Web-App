In her song “Close to You”, Ariana Grande reflects on her own faith and the love she feels for someone else. In the song, she acknowledges the doubts and difficulties in her own faith but expresses her desire to be close to her loved one. 

The song starts with Grande reflecting on her own faith, admitting that she isn’t “devastated” and that her mind is running “underneath warm jets.” This could be interpreted as a metaphor for the doubts and difficulties she encounters in her faith. She then expresses her desire to reach out to her loved one, singing “Baby, let my hands through what’s left.” Here, she is acknowledging the fact that she and her loved one are getting older and that their time together is limited. 

In the chorus, Grande expresses her desire to be close to her loved one, singing “Just like mine versions of these belong to you.” Here, Grande is suggesting that her faith and desire to be close to her loved one are connected. She then expresses her hope that her loved one will feel the same way, singing “Just like me, they long to be close to you.” 

The main theme of the song is a desire to be close to someone despite the doubts and difficulties in her own faith. Grande expresses her belief that her faith and her love for her loved one are connected. She also acknowledges the fact that their time together is limited and expresses her desire to make the most of it. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song has a melancholic tone, which expresses Grande’s doubts and fears about her faith. The lyrics also contain a lot of repetition, which emphasizes Grande’s desire to be close to her loved one. The song is a beautiful expression of love, faith, and hope. 

Overall, “Close to You” is a beautiful and heartfelt song that expresses Grande’s desire to be close to her loved one, despite the doubts and difficulties in her own faith. The song is a powerful expression of love, faith, and hope, and is sure to move listeners.
