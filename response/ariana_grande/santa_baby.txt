The song Santa Baby by Ariana Grande and Liz Gillies is a humorous take on the classic Christmas song. The lyrics tell the story of a young woman who is asking Santa for extravagant gifts. The first verse sees her asking for a sable, a ‘54 convertible, and for Santa to hurry down the chimney so she can wait up for him. The second verse has her asking for a yacht, a deed to a platinum mine, and for Santa to trim her Christmas tree with decorations from Tiffany’s. The final verse sees her asking for a ring, and for Santa to hurry down the chimney. 

The song is a lighthearted take on the original song, with the protagonist cheekily asking for expensive gifts, yet still staying true to the original song’s message of believing in Santa and being a “good girl”. The lyrics are also cleverly written, as they still manage to fit the original melody of the song. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song is filled with playful language, with words like “yacht”, “convertible” and “Tiffany’s” being used to create a humorous and lighthearted tone. Additionally, the use of the word “ring” in the last verse could be interpreted to mean a marriage proposal, which adds an extra layer of humor to the song. 

Overall, Santa Baby by Ariana Grande and Liz Gillies is an entertaining and humorous take on the classic Christmas song. The cleverly written lyrics and playful language help to create a lighthearted atmosphere, making it an enjoyable song to listen to during the festive season.
