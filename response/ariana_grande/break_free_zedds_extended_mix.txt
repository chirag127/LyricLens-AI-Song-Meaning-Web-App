Ariana Grande's song "Break Free (Zedd's Extended Mix)" is a powerful anthem of self-empowerment and liberation. The song's lyrics narrate the story of a person breaking out of a relationship that is unhealthy or oppressive, and finding their strength and freedom in the process. 

The first verse paints a vivid picture of a person's emotional state prior to their liberation, as they desperately try to "hide it/fake it" and deny their true feelings. The chorus is a powerful declaration of their newfound freedom and strength, as they proclaim that they "can't resist it no more" and will no longer be bound by a relationship that is holding them back.

The second verse further emphasizes this journey to self-empowerment by describing the sense of being "better/deeper" under the spell of the relationship, as well as the realization that this is not a healthy place to be. The bridge of the song further emphasizes this journey of liberation, as the person finds the strength within themselves to "break free" from what is holding them back.

Linguistically, the song contains many phrases that emphasize the idea of liberation, such as "break free" and "I'm stronger than I've been before". This language conveys the idea of breaking out of an oppressive situation and finding strength and freedom in the process. 

Overall, the song is a powerful ode to self-empowerment and liberation. Its vivid lyrics and powerful declarations of strength and freedom make it an inspiring and uplifting anthem that celebrates the strength of the human spirit.
