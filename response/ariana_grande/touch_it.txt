"Touch It" by Ariana Grande is a song about wanting more connection and intimacy in a relationship. In the first verse, the narrator expresses confusion over why their partner is not physically present, asking why they want them and then telling them they won't come in. The chorus is a plea for the narrator's partner to take them away, to "touch it" and explore the possibilities of their connection.

The second verse is a direct plea to the object of the narrator's affections, asking them to remember why they are taking a break and wondering why they don't just commit to their relationship and fall in love. The chorus repeats, conveying a sense of longing for the connection the narrator craves.

The lyrics of "Touch It" reflect Ariana Grande's signature sound, with a mixture of classic R&B and pop. Throughout the song, the narrator expresses a desire for connection, with a focus on the physical, such as wanting to "feel" the other person on their skin and wanting to "touch it". This expresses a longing for a physical connection and for a deeper, more intimate connection between the two people.

The language used in the song is also indicative of the narrator's longing for intimacy, with words such as "remember", "danger", and "fall in love" being used to convey the narrator's desire to reconnect with their partner. As the song progresses, the narrator's longing becomes more and more intense, expressing a desire to take the relationship further and to explore the possibilities of their connection.

The overall message of "Touch It" is one of longing and hope, conveying the narrator's desire to take their relationship to the next level and to explore the possibilities of a deeper connection. The song is a powerful example of Ariana Grande's ability to craft lyrics that capture the emotions of the listener, expressing her own desire for deeper connection and intimacy.
