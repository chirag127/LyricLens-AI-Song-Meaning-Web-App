Ariana Grande's song "Bad Idea (Live)" is an upbeat, empowering anthem that speaks to the struggles of letting go of past relationships and making the conscious decision to move on. The bridge finds Grande expressing her desire to let go and move on, and in the final chorus she expresses her bad idea of taking time away again and forgetting about him and her.
