"Everyday" by Ariana Grande is an upbeat love song that speaks to the power of unconditional love and commitment. The pre-chorus follows with her acknowledging the “good shit” her partner gives her that keeps her motivated and dedicated. The second verse dives into the body and soul connection between the two partners with Grande singing of her partner’s love “running through her veins.” The pre-chorus follows with her again reiterating the “good shit” that her partner gives her that keeps her hopeful and positive. The chorus follows with a repetition of the sentiment of her partner giving her love every day. 

The bridge follows with Grande expressing her dedication to her partner with her singing of her “fighting for the things [her partner] believes in” and “putting [her] body in drive.” The chorus follows with a repetition of the sentiment of her partner giving her love every day. 

Overall, “Everyday” is a powerful love song that speaks to the power of unconditional love and commitment. The lyrics are full of emotion and detail, which is further highlighted by Grande’s powerful vocal performance.
