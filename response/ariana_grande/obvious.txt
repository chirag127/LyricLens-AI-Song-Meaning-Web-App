In her song "Obvious," Ariana Grande explores the power of love and how it can make us feel and behave. The chorus of the song is a plea for her partner to stay and be with her, as she desperately declares her love and loyalty and asks if she could be more "obvious." This is a plea for her partner to recognize the deep feelings she has for them.

In the second verse, Ariana expresses her belief that her partner will never leave her, and her hopes that they will stay with her for the afternoon. She also expresses her commitment to their relationship, expressing that she is willing to tell all of her "dirty truths" in order to keep them together.

The song contains a variety of emotions, from love and joy to desperation and fear. Through her lyrics, Ariana conveys her deep feelings of love and commitment to her partner, while also expressing her fears and insecurities about their relationship. Additionally, the song conveys a sense of longing and hope, as Ariana pleads with her partner to stay and recognizes the power of true love.

Overall, "Obvious" is a beautiful and heartfelt song that explores the power of love and conveys the emotions of both love and fear. Ariana's lyrics are sincere and touching, and her delivery of the song is passionate and powerful. It is a testament to the power of love and how it can make us feel and behave.
