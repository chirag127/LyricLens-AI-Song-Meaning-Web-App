 
"7 Rings (Live)” by Ariana Grande is an upbeat, empowering anthem about living life to the fullest and buying what you want. In the song, Grande celebrates the idea of spoiling one's friends with riches, having a closet full of designer clothes, and buying extravagant jewelry. The song is a reminder to enjoy the moment and not worry about the future. 

The song begins with an introduction in which Grande talks about changing costumes and jokes about a jacket she is wearing. In the first verse, Grande talks about going to Tiffany's for breakfast and having bottles of bubbles. She also mentions girls with tattoos who like getting into trouble and wearing diamonds and ATM machines. This verse speaks to the idea of living life to the fullest and treating yourself. 

In the pre-chorus, Grande talks about having been through some bad times and how she has become a "savage" in response. This line speaks to the idea of rising above hard times in order to be successful. In the chorus, Grande celebrates buying whatever she wants and having the money to solve her problems.

In the second verse, Grande talks about wearing a ring but not being tied down. She also talks about spoiling her friends with her riches and retail therapy being her new addiction. She also mentions her smile being beaming, her skin being gleaming, and her hair being bought. This verse speaks to Grande's idea of treating herself and her friends.

In the bridge, Grande celebrates her success by talking about her receipts looking like phone numbers, her black card being her business card, and not having enough money to pay her respect. This bridge speaks to the idea of Grande's success and how she's been able to turn her life around.

In summary, “7 Rings (Live)” by Ariana Grande is an upbeat, empowering anthem about living life to the fullest. Grande celebrates buying whatever she wants, having the money to solve her problems, and spoiling her friends with her riches. The song is a reminder to enjoy the moment and not worry about the future.

This song displays a combination of positive, confident, and proud emotions. The lyrics are full of references to money and wealth, which speaks to the idea of materialism. Grande also expresses a sense of independence and freedom, which motivates the listener to strive for personal success. The combination of these emotions makes for an uplifting and inspiring song.
