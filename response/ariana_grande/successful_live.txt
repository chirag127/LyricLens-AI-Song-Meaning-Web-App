Ariana Grande's song "Successful (Live)" is an upbeat and inspiring anthem about the joys of achieving success. The song starts out with a question to the audience, asking them how they are feeling. This sets the tone for the song, which is about the celebration of success and the encouragement of others to achieve their own success. Through the lyrics, Grande expresses her own joy and excitement at having achieved her goals, and she encourages others to do the same.

The lyrics of the song are full of positive imagery, as Grande talks about the feeling of being on top of the world and the feeling of being “so young and have this fun and be successful”. She also speaks of how “girl you too” can find success and how she “can’t imagine what [she’s] 'bout to say” in regards to her success.

A linguistic analysis of the lyrics of the song reveals Grande’s positive emotions and enthusiasm. The use of words such as “baller” and “beneath” convey a sense of power and confidence, while other words such as “fun” and “young” express a sense of joy and excitement. The lyrics also contain a sense of optimism, as Grande speaks of the success that she has achieved and encourages others to follow in her footsteps. 

In summary, Ariana Grande’s song “Successful (Live)” is an inspirational anthem about the joys of achieving success. Through her lyrics, Grande conveys her own enthusiasm and optimism, and she encourages others to find their own success. The song is uplifting and encourages listeners to strive for the best and be proud of the success they have achieved.
