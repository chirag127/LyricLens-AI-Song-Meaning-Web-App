"Everytime" by Ariana Grande is a song about the power of love and the inescapable cycle of seeking it out. The song begins with the line "back to you back to you back to you back to you back to you back to you I go back to you back to you back to you every time." This line sets the tone for the song, as it expresses the inevitability of returning to the one we love no matter what we try to do to escape it. 

The chorus further expresses this idea, as the narrator talks about how they have tried to fight the energy between them, but they always end up back with their lover. The bridge makes this idea even more explicit, as the narrator wonders why God keeps bringing them back to the same person.

The lyrics of the song also express a range of emotions, from joy and excitement to sadness and despair. Through the use of metaphors and imagery, the song paints a vivid picture of the highs and lows of being in love. The lyrics also express a deep longing for connection and understanding, as the narrator yearns to be understood and accepted by their lover.

When it comes to a musical analysis of the song, the instrumentation consists of a simple acoustic guitar, a string section, and some synths to provide atmosphere. The production is minimalistic and allows the lyrics and vocals to take center stage.

Overall, "Everytime" is a beautiful and emotionally-charged song that speaks to the power of love and the inescapable cycle of seeking it out. It is a powerful reminder that, no matter how much we struggle against it, we often find ourselves drawn back to the same person. Lyrically and musically, it is an impressive piece of work that speaks to the human experience.
