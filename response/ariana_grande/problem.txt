The song "Problem" by Ariana Grande featuring Iggy Azalea is a song about a relationship in which the singer is trying to move on from a problematic partner. The song starts off with Grande singing about being "head in the clouds" and having "no weight on her shoulders," and how she should be wiser and realize that she has a problem. She then sings about how she knows her partner won't wake up and she needs to give up, but she can't help but be drawn to him. The chorus then follows with Grande and Azalea singing about how they "got one less problem without ya" and that there are a million other people out there, so they shouldn't be so attached to the partner. The second verse sees Grande trying to convince herself to forget the partner, while Azalea raps about how she should be better off without them and how she "finally learned her lesson." The song ends with Grande and Azalea repeating the chorus one last time. 

The song is a powerful anthem about the power of moving on from a problematic relationship and letting go of the person who is causing the problem. It is a song of resilience and strength, and it is a reminder that one should never stay stuck in a problematic situation. The lyrics are cleverly written and make use of metaphors to convey the point of the song. Additionally, the song has a great beat and catchy hooks, making it an enjoyable listen. 

A linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals that the song is full of positive emotions, such as confidence, resilience, and strength. The use of words like "breathless," "wiser," and "lesson" suggest that the singer is learning and growing from her experiences and is determined to move on. Additionally, the use of the word "problem" throughout the song is a clever way of conveying the message that the singer is trying to solve the problem and move on. The lyrics also make use of repetition, which helps emphasize the main theme of the song and create a powerful anthem-like effect. 

Overall, "Problem" by Ariana Grande featuring Iggy Azalea is a powerful anthem about the power of moving on from a problematic relationship. It is an uplifting and catchy song with clever lyrics that convey the idea of resilience and strength in the face of adversity. It is a song that is sure to resonate with many people and help them find the strength to move on.
