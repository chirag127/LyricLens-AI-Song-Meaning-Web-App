In her song “Too Close”, Ariana Grande reflects on the complex emotions of wanting to be with someone, but knowing that it may not be the right decision. The song starts off with Grande confessing her feelings for the person, singing, “I want you, oh, I want you / You know what I want, I know what you don’t.” She acknowledges that she should be the one her love is given to, but due to the long-standing friendship between them, she is hesitant to pursue it. In the chorus, Grande states, “I've been thinking about you, boy, it's killing me / And I got to let you know, baby, tell me, do you feel it like I do? / 'Cause we both know what could go down if we get too close.” She expresses the internal conflict she is facing between wanting to be close to this person, and knowing that it could lead to potential heartache. 

The bridge of the song finds Grande addressing her female audience directly, singing, “All my ladies, all my ladies, all my ladies, make noise.” This could be interpreted as a call to action for her female fans to take control of their own lives and not let anyone else determine the course of their relationships.

In summary, “Too Close” is a song about the difficult decision of whether or not to take a chance on a relationship, even if it could end in heartbreak. The lyrics are an honest reflection of the inner turmoil that comes with wanting to be close to someone, but knowing it could have negative consequences. Linguistic analysis of the song can reveal that Grande is portraying a range of emotions, from desire and longing to fear and hesitation. She encourages her female fans to take control of their own lives, and make their own decisions about relationships without allowing anyone else to influence them.
