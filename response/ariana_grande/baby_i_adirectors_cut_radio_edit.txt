"Baby I (A-Director's Cut Radio Edit)" by Ariana Grande is a song about the difficulty of expressing love for someone. Throughout the song, Grande struggles to find the words to express her feelings, and instead chooses to focus on the actions she can take to show her love instead.

The second verse focuses on the commitment she has to the other person. She expresses her loyalty and dedication, and how she has been feeling for them even before she knew what love was. In the pre-chorus and chorus, she expresses the same sentiment as before - that words only complicate her feelings and are not enough to express them. 

The song ends with Grande saying that she loves the person more than words can say, and that it is better not to try to explain it.

Overall, "Baby I (A-Director's Cut Radio Edit)" is a song about the difficulty of expressing love - how words can only do so much, and how actions speak louder than words. Through her lyrics, Grande conveys her commitment and dedication to the person she loves, and her frustration at not being able to express her feelings adequately. The song also has a powerful message of being able to express love through actions, rather than relying on words.
