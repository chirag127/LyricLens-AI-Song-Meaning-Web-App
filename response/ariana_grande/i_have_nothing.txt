"I Have Nothing" by Ariana Grande is a song about the importance of love and the pain of being without it. In the first verse, Ariana sings about how she is willing to be taken for who she is and that she won't change her colors in order to please someone else. In the pre-chorus, she expresses her desire to remain close to the person she loves, and her fear that she might have to go on without them. The chorus emphasizes the fact that she has nothing if she doesn't have them, and that the strength of their love helps to break down her walls. 

The second verse further explores the idea of not being able to live without the person she loves, and her vow to remember their love forever. This is repeated in the refrain, where she begs them not to walk away from her and expresses the fear that she will have nothing if they do. 

The song's lyrics express the deep emotion that comes with being in love, as well as the pain of being without it. It is a powerful reminder of the importance of love, and of how difficult it can be to move on when it is gone. 

From a linguistic perspective, the song's lyrics are full of emotion. Throughout the song, Ariana expresses her feelings and her fears in a very honest and heartfelt way. The lyrics also show the power of love and how it can bring down walls and make us feel strong and invincible. By using repetition and specific word choices, Ariana conveys her emotions in a way that is both powerful and poignant.
