"Better Days" by Ariana Grande and Victoria Monét is a powerful song about the need for change in the world. In the opening verse, the singers tell us of a war outside their window and how they will be alright if they have each other. This sets the tone for the song, as it is a plea for peace in the face of violence and hatred.

In the second verse, the singers express the need for better days, days where things are better and the violence, hurting, and hatred can be stopped. They call for everyone to come together and make a change, for the sake of future generations.

The chorus communicates the persistent hope for better days, that things will be better and everyone can have a better life. The lyrics are simple and straightforward, yet they carry an emotional weight that is undeniable.

The song carries a powerful message of hope, peace, and unity. Through the lyrics, the singers convey the importance of coming together and making a positive change. The song has a very uplifting tone, which encourages people to strive for a better future for themselves and for the world.

From a linguistic analysis of the song's lyrics, it is clear that the singers are expressing strong emotions through their words. The words they use communicate feelings of hope, determination, and unity. The repetition of the phrase "better days" is especially effective in conveying the message of the song and emphasizing the need for change.

Overall, "Better Days" is an inspiring and powerful song that speaks to the heart and encourages us to strive for a better world.
