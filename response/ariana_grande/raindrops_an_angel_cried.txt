"Raindrops (An Angel Cried)" by Ariana Grande is a heart-wrenching ballad about the pain of a lost love. The song begins with the sound of rain falling, symbolizing the sadness that the singer is feeling in the wake of the breakup. The first verse gives a vivid description of the moment that the singer realized that their relationship was over: "When raindrops fell down from the sky, the day you left me, an angel cried, oh she cried, an angel cried, she cried". 

The chorus of the song tells a story of a love that was so strong that even an angel was moved to tears. The angel's tears are a metaphor for the pain of the singer's heartbreak and the fact that it was so deep that an angel wept for them. This chorus also serves as a reminder that although love can be painful, it can also be a powerful, beautiful thing. 

The second verse of the song details the singer's feelings of loneliness and despair in the aftermath of the breakup. The lyrics "I'm so scared of being lonely, I don't want to be the only one" convey the singer's fear of being alone and her longing for her lost love. 

The bridge of the song serves as an emotional climax, as the singer realizes that although the relationship is over, their love will never truly die. The lines "But I'm still here, still your angel, still here, still your love" are a powerful reminder that even though the relationship is over, the love that the two people shared will always be a part of them. 

The song ends with the sound of thunder, a metaphor for the storm of emotions that the singer is feeling in the wake of the breakup. 

In summary, "Raindrops (An Angel Cried)" by Ariana Grande is a heartbreaking ballad about the pain of a lost love. The song uses vivid imagery and powerful lyrics to convey the emotions of heartbreak, loneliness, and longing that come with the end of a relationship. The song is emotionally moving and captures the intensity of a love so strong that it could move even an angel to tears. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song is filled with emotional language and powerful imagery. The singer uses metaphors and vivid descriptions to convey the intensity of her emotions. Additionally, the use of repetition and the sound of thunder at the end of the song serve to emphasize the emotional impact of the singer's words. 

Overall, "Raindrops (An Angel Cried)" is a beautiful and powerful song about the pain of a lost love. The song is filled with emotion and powerful imagery, making it an emotional and moving experience for listeners.
