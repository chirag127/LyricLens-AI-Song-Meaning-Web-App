In Ariana Grande's song "Honeymoon Avenue (Demo)", she describes the struggles of a relationship that is stuck in a rut. The lyrics explore the idea of a love that has been lost and the longing to return to the way things were when they first started. 

The song starts with the narrator looking in her rear-view mirror and recognizing that the relationship isn't going anywhere. She is ready to make a change before they both "crash and burn", indicating the potential danger of continuing on the same path. 

The chorus captures the longing for the relationship to return to the way it was when they first fell in love. She sings of being "on honeymoon avenue" and wanting to get "back to the way it was". The imagery of a "yellow light" suggests that the relationship is stuck in limbo, unable to move forward or backward. 

The second verse reflects on the idea that "only fools fall in love", suggesting that the narrator and her partner are not being smart in the way they are handling their relationship. Despite this, the narrator still has faith that they can find their way back home. 

The song is a powerful reminder that relationships take effort and that sometimes it can be difficult to get back to the way things were. On a more hopeful note, it suggests that by recognizing the problems and making the effort to address them, a relationship can be saved. 

The lyrics of "Honeymoon Avenue (Demo)" are filled with emotion and vulnerability, making it a relatable song for many people. Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals that the song expresses a range of emotions, including longing, sorrow, hope, and faith. Through her words, Ariana Grande helps to foster a sense of understanding and compassion for struggles in relationships. 

In summary, Ariana Grande's "Honeymoon Avenue (Demo)" is a powerful reminder of the power of love and the importance of working together to save a relationship. The song's emotional lyrics provide a relatable and heartfelt message about the struggles of getting "back to the way it was".
