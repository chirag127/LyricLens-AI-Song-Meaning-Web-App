The song "Love Is Everything" by Ariana Grande is an upbeat, soulful anthem that celebrates the power of love. The musical arrangement of the song is also very uplifting. The combination of upbeat drums and soulful vocals create an optimistic atmosphere that conveys the message of love and togetherness. The chorus is particularly uplifting, with its catchy melody and chorus of voices.
