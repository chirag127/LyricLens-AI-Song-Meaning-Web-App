Put Your Hearts Up by Ariana Grande is an uplifting pop song that encourages listeners to use their love to make a positive change in the world. In the first verse, Grande encourages listeners to use their small actions to make a difference, such as giving a quarter to a stranger or a wink and a kiss to show care. The chorus emphasizes the power of collective action, with Grande singing, “If we give a little love, maybe we can change the world.” The bridge of the song encourages listeners to not give up in the face of struggle, and to remember that the only thing they have is love. 

The song’s lyrics are full of positive messages of hope, such as “you don’t have to be a billionaire/you don’t have to have much to show to show much care.” Through this line, Grande encourages listeners to recognize that even small acts of kindness can make a difference. Additionally, the song’s uplifting message of perseverance in the face of adversity is inspiring and empowering. 

Linguistic analysis of the song’s lyrics reveals a positive overall tone, with words such as “love,” “change,” and “together” giving the song a hopeful and uplifting feel. Additionally, the repetition of the phrase “put your hearts up” serves to emphasize the collective power of love and encourages listeners to come together in order to make a positive impact on the world. 

In conclusion, Put Your Hearts Up by Ariana Grande is an inspiring and uplifting pop song that encourages listeners to use their love to make a positive change in the world. The song’s positive messages of hope, perseverance, and collective action are empowering and inspiring, and its uplifting and hopeful tone makes it a great anthem for anyone looking to make a difference.
