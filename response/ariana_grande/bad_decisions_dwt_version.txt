Bad Decisions [DWT Version] by Ariana Grande is an upbeat love song that speaks to the thrill of making bad decisions with someone you love. The song starts off with Ariana singing about how her love drives her “crazy” but that it is one of the things she likes. She then encourages her love to let people keep talking, as it doesn’t matter what they say.

The pre-chorus speaks to the wildness she feels in her love that has become her favorite sin. The chorus then speaks to the kind of love they share, which is one that doesn’t care what anyone else thinks. This is followed by the repeated phrase of “bad boy you make me make bad decisions.”

The second verse follows the same pattern as the first, beginning with the pre-chorus and then leading into the chorus. However, this time Ariana takes the opportunity to sing directly to her love, affirming that she does not “fuck with them good boys” and that her love is something that “ain’t nobody could.”

Overall, Bad Decisions [DWT Version] by Ariana Grande is a fun and upbeat love song that speaks to the freedom of being able to make bad decisions with someone you love without worrying about what anyone else thinks. The lyrics are packed with emotion, and the production is upbeat and catchy. Linguistically, the song is full of repetition and alliteration, which helps to emphasize the feelings of freedom and excitement in the lyrics. As a song, Bad Decisions is a great ode to the thrill of making bad decisions with the one you love, and is sure to be a hit with any fan of Ariana Grande.
