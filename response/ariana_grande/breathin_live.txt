"Breathin" by Ariana Grande is a powerful, uplifting song about managing anxiety and finding peace in difficult times. The song opens with an invitation to the listener to join her in singing, as she sings about feeling overwhelmed and as if the whole world is spinning around her. She acknowledges that sometimes things can get complicated and she can overcomplicate them, but she finds solace in the reminder that she just needs to see her loved one's face in order to feel better. 

The chorus follows with Grande urging the listener to “keep on breathin’” and to try to find their way up into the clouds in order to tune out the noise. The song ends with her singing about not being able to control her mind, but still finding a way to keep on breathin’.

The lyrics of the song contain many elements of linguistic analysis such as imagery, metaphors, and repetition. Grande uses imagery to describe her feelings and struggles, such as her “blood running” and the “sky falling”. The metaphors she uses to describe her feelings of being overwhelmed can be interpreted as a way of expressing her need for escape and for freedom from her worries. The repetition of the phrase “keep breathin’” serves as an encouragement to the listener to keep going and to not give up.

Overall, "Breathin" is an inspiring anthem of hope and courage in the face of anxiety and difficulty. The song encourages the listener to keep on breathing and to find solace in the little things in life. The lyrics are powerful and meaningful, and the song is sure to connect with many people who are struggling to cope with difficult times.
