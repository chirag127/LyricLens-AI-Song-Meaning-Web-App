"Hope Is The Song" by Ariana Grande and Jim Carrey is a powerful and uplifting anthem about finding hope and strength in difficult times. The song starts with a question from Ariana, asking what to do when there is no one to turn to and it is hard to cope. Jim Carrey then responds with the chorus "Hope is the song, when the world is a menace, hope is an open door, maybe getting back up again is what falls are for." This chorus serves as a reminder of the power of hope and how it can help us in times of trouble.

The verses provide further insight into the meaning of the song. Ariana sings about how, even in the darkest times, there is still something to hold onto. She encourages the listener to learn, to make a change, and to find hope in order to get back up again. Jim Carrey follows with a message of perseverance and determination, singing about how we have to keep fighting, no matter what life throws our way. 

The song has a powerful message of hope and resilience that can be applied to any situation. It encourages the listener to find hope even in the darkest of times and to never give up. The lyrics are well-crafted and the melody is both uplifting and inspiring. The collaboration between Ariana Grande and Jim Carrey is also a highlight of the song, as the two voices come together to create a powerful anthem of hope. 

When examining the lyrics of "Hope Is The Song" through a linguistic analysis, it is clear that the song conveys a message of hope and resilience. The use of positive words such as "hope", "open", and "learn" are used to emphasize the idea of finding strength even in difficult times. There is also a strong focus on the idea of never giving up and of having the courage to move forward. The repetition of the chorus also serves to emphasize the power of the message. 

Overall, "Hope Is The Song" is a powerful and uplifting anthem that carries a message of hope and resilience. The collaboration between Ariana Grande and Jim Carrey is a highlight of the song, and the lyrics are well-crafted and thought-provoking. The song encourages the listener to find hope even in the darkest of times and to never give up.
