In the song "Leave Me Lonely" by Ariana Grande, the singer paints a vivid picture of a tumultuous relationship, describing the roller coaster of emotions brought on by a dangerous love. The song begins with Macy Gray singing a hook of "Dangerous love, oh / You're no good for me, darling / Ahahah / Yeah, you turn me away," setting the tone for the track with its infectious hook and empowering lyrics. 

In the first verse, Grande delivers a powerful message of resilience, singing "Is it love when so easily you said goodbye? / Is it love when we've given up before we tried? / Mmm, is it love when you stole my peace of mind? / Is it love when you cry and cry and cry?". These lyrics serve as an exploration of the complexities of the relationship and an attempt to reconcile the situation. 

Grande continues her story in the pre-chorus, singing "So when you walk out that door / Don't you come back no more / My heart has had enough of the give and take / And as much as I want you to stay". In this section, Grande expresses her frustration with the situation, and her desire to finally be done with the relationship. 

The chorus finds Grande delivering an anthemic hook of "You're a dangerous love, ooh / Maybe you're no good for me, darling / Ahahah / 'Cause if you're gonna love me and leave me hanging here / Then I'd rather you leave me lonely". This hook serves as a declaration of independence from the relationship and a statement of her own self worth. 

The bridge sees the return of Macy Gray, singing a reprise of the hook, further emphasizing the message of the song and solidifying its impact. 

Overall, "Leave Me Lonely" is an empowering song that serves as a reminder of the strength of the human spirit. Through her lyrics, Grande conveys a message of resilience and self-worth, and expresses the power of letting go of a toxic relationship. Her words are a powerful testament to her own strength, and the song has powerful implications for anyone who has ever found themselves in a similarly difficult situation. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the lyrics of the song can be interpreted as expressing feelings of anger, sadness, and frustration. The use of repetition of the hook serves to emphasize the song's message, while the use of rhetorical questions serves to emphasize Grande's feelings of confusion and uncertainty. All of these elements combine to create a powerful and impactful song that expresses both the hurt and the strength of a dangerous love.
