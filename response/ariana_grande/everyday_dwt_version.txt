The song "Everyday" by Ariana Grande is a love song about the joys of being with someone who loves you unconditionally. The lyrics detail the narrator's appreciation for the special person in their life and all the ways they make them feel. The song is full of positive energy, with a catchy chorus and upbeat, danceable production.

The first verse begins with the narrator expressing their loneliness and longing for someone special, who they know can make them feel better. The pre-chorus then introduces the "good shit" that this person is giving them, symbolizing the love, care, and attention they provide. In the chorus, the narrator sings that they are given this "good shit" every day, showing how important and consistent this person's presence is in their life.

The second verse of the song sees the narrator expressing their appreciation for their partner—they feel their love in their veins, and no matter what they do, they have the reassurance of knowing they are taken care of. The pre-chorus and chorus then repeat, emphasizing how important it is to the narrator to be with this person every day.

The bridge of the song sees the narrator expressing how they want to reciprocate the love and care they have been given. They want to put all their energy and effort into making the other person happy, and they are willing to do whatever it takes to make that happen.

Overall, "Everyday" by Ariana Grande is a beautiful love song about the joys of being with someone who loves you unconditionally. The lyrics are full of positive energy and describe the narrator's appreciation for their partner and all the ways they make them feel. The chorus is particularly catchy and upbeat, making it a great song to dance to. Linguistically, the song conveys a sense of happiness, contentment, and appreciation, as well as a strong feeling of love and care between two people.
