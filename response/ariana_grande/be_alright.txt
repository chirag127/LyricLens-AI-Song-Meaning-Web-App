"Be Alright" is a song by Ariana Grande, released in April 2020. The song is about finding hope and strength in difficult times, and how love can be a powerful source of comfort. The lyrics of the song are divided into three verses, with a refrain repeating between them. 

The first verse starts with midnight shadows and a battle for love. This suggests that the narrator is facing a situation in which love is hard to find, but the refrain of "we're gonna be alright" is a promise of hope that better times are ahead. The refrain is repeated again at the end of the first verse.

The third verse is a direct call to action, with the narrator encouraging themselves to make up their mind that "every little thing is gonna be alright." This suggests that the narrator is taking control of their own destiny and finding the strength to go on. The refrain is repeated again at the end of the verse. 

The song is a powerful reminder that difficult times can lead to better days, and that we can find strength and hope in the people around us. The refrain of "we're gonna be alright" is a mantra of strength and encouragement that can be used in any situation.
