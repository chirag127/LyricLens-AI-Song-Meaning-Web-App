"Jason's Song (Gave It Away)" by Ariana Grande is a powerful anthem of self-empowerment and strength. In the song, Grande laments being taken advantage of by a former partner, and ultimately decides to take her power back. The song begins with Grande expressing her frustration at being lied to and taken for granted. She recognizes that it is time to move on, as she has been mistreated and taken advantage of. The chorus follows, with Grande asserting that she is reclaiming the power she has given away and is no longer going to be taken advantage of.

The second verse follows, with Grande asserting that she is no longer going to be treated as a "blow up doll" or a "free for all". She has taken control and is no longer going to be a slave to anyone else's decisions. The pre-chorus sees Grande asserting that she was used as a "fragment of someone else's grand design" and that she no longer has to accept this treatment. 

The final chorus repeats the message of the first chorus, that she is reclaiming the power she has given away and that she is now taking it right back. This is a powerful message of self-empowerment and strength, and a reminder that we all have the power to take control of our lives and decide who we want to be.

The song contains a lot of emotion and anger, which is reflected in the language and tone of the lyrics. Grande uses strong language to express her frustration and anger at being taken advantage of and mistreated. Additionally, her use of repetition helps to emphasize the message of the song, that she is taking back the power she has given away.

Overall, "Jason's Song (Gave It Away)" is a powerful anthem of self-empowerment and strength. Grande's lyrics are emotive and angry, and her use of repetition helps to emphasize her message and make it even more powerful. By reclaiming the power she has given away, Grande is sending a message to all of us that we can all take control of our lives and decide who we want to be.
