The song "Perfectly Wrong" by Shawn Mendes is a reflection on a relationship that is doomed to fail, despite the narrator's attempts to make it work. In the first verse, the narrator speaks of the "poison" from their partner's lips and how their love is "drunken in," suggesting that despite the negativity in the relationship, they are still deeply enamored with each other. The pre-chorus emphasizes the inevitability of the relationship's failure, as the narrator knows the truth but it is "much too late," and in the chorus, the narrator finally admits that their partner is "perfectly wrong for me." 

In the second verse, the narrator further reflects on the toxicity of the relationship, noting that it hurts them every time their partner says they hate them, but then they're "making love" and it makes it all worthwhile. The bridge finally allows the narrator to admit that they can't resist the urge to be with their partner, despite knowing it will only end in heartbreak. 

The lyrics of "Perfectly Wrong" can be interpreted as an exploration of the complex and conflicting feelings of being in an unhealthy relationship, with the narrator struggling to let go despite knowing that they should. Through linguistic analysis, the lyrics demonstrate a mix of conflicting emotions, as the narrator is both filled with longing and desire for their partner, but also knows that it is ultimately wrong for them. 

Overall, "Perfectly Wrong" is a powerful and heartfelt song that does an excellent job of conveying the complex emotions of being in an unhealthy relationship. Shawn Mendes' masterful writing and emotive vocals help to bring the lyrics to life and make it a truly memorable track.
