In the first verse of "Mercy" by Shawn Mendes, the narrator is expressing their vulnerability and dependence on the person they are singing to. They know that the person has a strong hold over them and they are powerless to resist. Despite their efforts to maintain a sense of independence, they find themselves falling whenever they are around this person. The narrator pleads for the person to show them compassion and not hurt them, but instead, the person continues to cause them pain.

In the chorus, the narrator repeats their request for mercy and asks the person to take it easy on their heart. They acknowledge that the person may not intentionally mean to hurt them, but the end result is the same - their heart is being torn apart.

In the second verse, the narrator shows their dedication to the person, saying they would drive through the night just to be near them. They open up their heart and plead with the person to be honest with them and not make them doubt their own sanity. The narrator admits that they are not asking for much, just some basic honesty and respect.

The bridge of the song intensifies the narrator's emotional state as they describe the physical effects of the person's actions on their body. They feel as though they are suffocating and that their very skin is being ripped off. Despite the pain, the narrator is willing to sacrifice their life for this person and even says they would do it twice.

In the final chorus, the narrator begs the person to show them mercy and to set them free from the emotional hold they have on them. They plead for the person to have compassion on their heart and to stop causing them pain.

Overall, "Mercy" is a song about being in a vulnerable and difficult situation where the person you love is causing you pain. The narrator pleads for the person to show them compassion and to end the cycle of hurt. In my opinion, the song is well-written and captures the intense emotions of being in a tumultuous relationship.
