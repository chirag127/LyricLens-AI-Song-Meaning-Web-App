There's Nothing Holdin' Me Back is a song by Canadian singer Shawn Mendes. The song is about a person who is in a relationship with someone who makes them feel free and uninhibited. The first verse describes the narrator's desire to follow this person wherever they go and how they think about them constantly. In the pre-chorus, the narrator admits that the other person keeps them guessing and maybe they should confess their feelings for them.

The chorus repeats the idea that the other person takes away all of the narrator's inhibitions and that there's nothing holding the narrator back from fully expressing their feelings. The second verse describes the other person as being fearless and spontaneous, which the narrator finds attractive. The bridge talks about how even if they were to go too far in their relationship, they would be okay because they have each other.

Overall, the song is a declaration of love and the freedom that comes with being in a relationship with someone who makes you feel comfortable and accepted. It's a catchy and upbeat song with relatable lyrics that many people can identify with.
