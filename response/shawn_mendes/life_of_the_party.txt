"Life of the Party" is a pop song by Shawn Mendes. The song is an upbeat, danceable anthem encouraging the listener to let go of their inhibitions and be the life of the party.

In the first verse, Mendes sings about how he loves it when his partner doesn't care about what others think and just lets loose, dancing and doing what they want. He encourages them to be fearless and not worry about the opinions of others.

In the chorus, Mendes repeats the refrain "be the life of the party," urging the listener to take risks and make mistakes, because they don't have time to be sorry. He also warns that hearts may be broken in the process, but that's okay.

In the second verse, Mendes sings about how he and his partner can let go together and dance and fall in love as the club music plays. Again, he emphasizes that they don't care about what others think.

The bridge of the song repeats the refrain "come out tonight" and encourages the listener to take the opportunity to be the life of the party and not let others stand in their way.

The song is an infectious, upbeat anthem that encourages listeners to let go of their fears and be the life of the party. It is a fun and energetic song that is sure to get people dancing.
