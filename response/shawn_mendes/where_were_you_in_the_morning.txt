In the song "Where Were You in the Morning?" by Shawn Mendes, the narrator is expressing frustration and disappointment with someone who had shown interest in getting to know them, but then disappeared without explanation. In the first verse, the narrator describes the other person's initial interest and the promise of spending the night together. However, when the narrator wakes up, the other person is gone. In the refrain, the narrator is confused and hurt by the sudden change, wondering how the other person could have made them believe there was something between them.

In the second verse, the narrator reflects on how the other person had made it seem like they were really into each other, talking about plans for the next morning. But now, the other person has disappeared without a trace. In the chorus, the narrator repeatedly asks where the other person was in the morning, expressing frustration at not being given any explanation or warning. The post-chorus includes the refrain of "How do you just walk away?"

In the bridge, the narrator wonders if the other person had a good reason for leaving, but also admits that they know the other person had a good time. They are looking up at the ceiling, unable to stop thinking about what happened. The song ends with the chorus and post-chorus repeating.

Overall, the song is about the disappointment and confusion that comes with being left without explanation by someone who had previously shown interest. The narrator is unable to understand why the other person would just walk away without any warning or explanation.

As for a review of the song, the lyrics are relatable and capture the emotions of being left without explanation. The melody is catchy and the repetition of the chorus and post-chorus helps to emphasize the narrator's frustration. Overall, it is a well-written and emotionally resonant song.
