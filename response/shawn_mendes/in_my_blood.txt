"In My Blood" is a song by Canadian singer Shawn Mendes. The song is about feeling overwhelmed and anxious, and the struggle to overcome those feelings.

In the first verse, the narrator describes feeling "overwhelmed and insecure" and laying on the bathroom floor, feeling "nothing". They are looking for something to ease their mind and make them feel better, but nothing seems to work.

In the pre-chorus, the narrator pleads for help, saying that it feels like the walls are caving in and they sometimes feel like giving up. They mention that no medicine is strong enough to help them, and they are "crawling in [their] skin".

The chorus repeats the phrase "it isn't in my blood" to emphasize the narrator's determination to keep going, even when they feel like giving up.

In the second verse, the narrator describes feeling anxious and afraid to be alone. They are trying to find a way to calm down, but can't seem to catch their breath. They ask if there is anyone who can help them.

The bridge of the song finds the narrator pleading for someone to help them, saying "I need somebody now".

Overall, the song is a powerful expression of the struggles with anxiety and the determination to keep going even when it feels overwhelming.

In terms of a review or opinion of the song, "In My Blood" is a well-written and emotive song that many people can relate to. Its message of perseverance in the face of anxiety and other struggles is inspiring and uplifting. Shawn Mendes' vocals are strong and emotive, perfectly capturing the emotions of the song. Overall, it is a great addition to his discography and a must-listen for fans of his music.
