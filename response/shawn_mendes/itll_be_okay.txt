It'll Be Okay is a song by Shawn Mendes that explores the uncertain and potentially painful nature of a relationship. In the first verse, the singer asks if the relationship is going to work out, expressing fear that it may end up causing pain. The pre-chorus imagines a world where the two people don't collide, but this thought makes the singer sick. The chorus, however, suggests that even if the relationship doesn't work out, the singer will be okay and will still love the other person.

The second verse talks about the future they once dreamed of, which is now fading away. The pre-chorus repeats the idea of imagining a world without the other person, but again, the thought is painful. The chorus repeats the message of acceptance and love, regardless of what happens to the relationship.

In the bridge, the singer repeats the message of loving the other person, even if the end of the relationship is sweet or bitter. The outro repeats the line about the future fading to black, emphasizing the uncertain nature of the relationship.

Overall, the song is about dealing with the potential pain and uncertainty of a relationship, and accepting that it may not work out but still loving the other person. It's a poignant and emotional song, and Shawn Mendes does a great job of capturing the complex emotions involved in relationships.
