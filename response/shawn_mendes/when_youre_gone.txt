The song "When You're Gone" by Shawn Mendes is a heartfelt ballad about the hurt of losing someone you love. In the first verse, Mendes sings about the pain of realizing that you had something good and now it's gone. He reflects on the past, wondering if he had known the joy of being with the person before they left, he would have taken more time to appreciate it.

In the pre-chorus, Mendes expresses his desire to hold onto the relationship, even though he knows it's time to let go. He's trying to accept the situation and move on, but it's hard for him to let go.

The chorus finds Mendes reflecting on the sadness of knowing what it's like when the person he loves is gone. He knows that if he lets go, he won't be able to go back to being happy again. He expresses his longing for the person and how he didn't know how happy he could be with them until they had gone. 

In the second verse, Mendes expresses his need to learn how to cope without the person, and how he is trying to protect himself but only the person he loves knows how to. 

The bridge brings the song to a climax as Mendes reflects on the pain of knowing that the person he loves no longer needs him. He is struggling to accept the situation and believes that it's all for the better, but he is still missing the person deeply. 

The chorus repeats, emphasizing Mendes's longing for the person and his wish to hold onto the relationship. 

In conclusion, "When You're Gone" is a beautiful and heartbreaking song about the pain of losing someone you love. The song expresses the sadness and longing of having someone gone, and the difficulty of accepting the situation. Mendes's heartbreaking lyrics and beautiful melody convey the emotion of the song perfectly. 

Overall, this is an excellent song that captures the pain of heartbreak and loss perfectly. The lyrics are heartfelt and honest, and Mendes's voice conveys the emotion of the song perfectly. This song is sure to touch the hearts of many listeners.
