The song "Lost in Japan" by Shawn Mendes is a love song in which the narrator expresses his desire to be with the person he is singing about. In the first verse, the narrator imagines being in the same time zone as this person and looking through their social media timeline, seeing all the happy moments they have shared. He then has an idea and admits that it may sound crazy, but he wants to see this person and asks if they have plans that night.

In the chorus, the narrator reveals that he is currently a couple hundred miles from Japan and proposes flying to the person's hotel to be with them because he can't get them off his mind. He repeats this sentiment in the second verse, where he describes feeling a tension between them and sensing that there is more to their relationship than just friendship. He asks the person not to fall asleep and promises that it will only be for a few hours before he has to leave.

In the bridge, the narrator repeats his desire to be with this person and suggests that they "get lost" together tonight. He admits that he can't seem to get them off his mind. In the final chorus, he repeats his proposal to fly to their hotel and spend the night together, and again expresses how much he is thinking about them.

Overall, the song is a romantic and passionate expression of the narrator's desire to be with the person he is singing about. It conveys the intensity of his feelings and his willingness to take bold actions to be with them.

As for a review of the song, it is a catchy and well-written love song with a strong chorus and relatable lyrics. The use of the imagery of being "lost" in Japan adds a unique and intriguing element to the song. Overall, it is a solid addition to Shawn Mendes' catalog.
