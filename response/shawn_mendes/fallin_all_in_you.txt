In "Fallin' All in You" by Shawn Mendes, the singer describes falling in love with someone and the way that person has changed him. In the first verse, he describes waking up with this person and feeling the beginning of a new relationship. The pre-chorus talks about how he has been alone for a while and has been wrong about love in the past, but since meeting this person he is starting to see things differently. The chorus repeats the line "fallin' all in you" and describes how the person is bringing out a new side of him and making him feel free.

The second verse picks up with the couple a couple years in the future, and talks about the ups and downs of their relationship. The bridge reinforces the idea of how much this person has changed the singer's life, and how he never wants to wake up from the dream of being with them.

Overall, the song is a romantic and hopeful portrayal of falling in love and the ways it can change us. It's a sweet and catchy tune with relatable lyrics that many listeners will enjoy.
