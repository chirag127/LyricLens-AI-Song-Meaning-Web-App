If I Can't Have You is a song by Shawn Mendes in which he sings about his feelings for someone who he cannot be with. In the first verse, Mendes is in Toronto and has a nice view, but he feels lonely and consumed by thoughts of this person. In the pre-chorus, he admits that he is good at keeping his distance, but he knows that this person is the feeling he is missing. In the chorus, he repeats the line "Everything means nothing if I can't have you."

In the second verse, Mendes apologizes for his timing and admits that he cannot move on if they are still going to talk. He wants all of this person, not just half. In the pre-chorus, he repeats the lines about keeping his distance and missing this person.

In the bridge, he sings about trying to move on and forget this person, but he still holds on. He repeats the chorus again at the end of the song.

The song is a powerful and emotional ballad about the pain of wanting someone who you cannot have. Mendes' vocals are strong and convey the raw emotion of the lyrics. Overall, If I Can't Have You is a well-written and moving song.
