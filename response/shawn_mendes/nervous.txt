In the song "Nervous" by Shawn Mendes, the singer describes meeting someone who makes him feel anxious and excited. In the first verse, he sees this person in a café and is immediately drawn to them. He starts to feel his heart race and his hands shake as he thinks about talking to them. In the second verse, he gets the chance to spend more time with them and they go for a walk in the rain. The singer admits that he is not usually so nervous around people, but this person has a special effect on him.

The chorus repeats the phrase "I get a little bit nervous around you" and describes some of the other feelings the singer experiences when he thinks about this person. He gets stressed out, excited, self-conscious, and even starts talking too much. The bridge of the song finds the singer preparing to see this person again, even though he doesn't know why he is so nervous.

Overall, the song is about the butterflies that come with falling for someone new. The singer is caught off guard by the intense feelings he has for this person and is unsure of how to handle them. Despite his nerves, he is excited to see where things might go.

As for a review of the song, it is a catchy and relatable love ballad that showcases Shawn Mendes' smooth vocals. The repeated chorus makes it easy to sing along and the lyrics capture the excitement and anxiety that comes with falling for someone new.
