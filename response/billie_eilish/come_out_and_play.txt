The song “Come Out and Play” by Billie Eilish is a powerful anthem of self-expression and encouragement. The lyrics tell the story of a person who has something inside them that they are afraid to show to the world, but who is being encouraged to come out and reveal their true self. The verses focus on contrasting the feelings of fear and insecurity with the courage and strength that is needed to overcome them. 

The first verse starts off with the line “hmmmm, wake up and smell the coffee” which sets the tone for the song. It is a reminder that we all have to wake up and accept our current situation, despite any fears or anxieties we may have. The line “is your cup half full or empty when we talk” speaks to the power of communication, and how even in the most difficult of situations, having a conversation can provide clarity and direction. The line “you say it softly but I love it when you’re awfully quiet” speaks to the power of silence. It acknowledges that it can be difficult to speak up, but that the silence can still be beautiful and powerful. 

The chorus is a plea for the person to take a chance and “come out and play”. It is a reminder that even in the face of fear, there is strength and courage to be found in taking risks and showing the world who you truly are. The line “you’ll never know until you try it” speaks to the idea that sometimes the only way to find out what you’re capable of is to take that first step and try. The lyrics “don’t hide, don’t hide, too shy to say but I hope you stay, don’t hide away, come out and play” are a powerful reminder that even though it can be hard to take that first step, it is worth it in the end. 

The second verse speaks to the idea that it can be easier to stay hidden and protected, but that sometimes it is worth it to take that first step and push yourself out of your comfort zone. The line “look up out of your window, see snow, won’t let it in though” speaks to the idea that even when things seem cold or daunting, you can still be resilient and find the strength to overcome them. 

The song’s lyrics provide an inspiring message of self-expression and empowerment. It is an anthem for those who are struggling to find the courage to take that first step towards self-discovery, and a reminder that it is worth it in the end. Linguistically, the song’s lyrics focus on imagery and metaphor, speaking to the power of taking the plunge and revealing one’s true self. The song’s message of acceptance and courage is one that resonates with many, making it an inspiring and empowering anthem for self-expression.
