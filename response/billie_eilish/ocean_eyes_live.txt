"Ocean Eyes" by Billie Eilish is an emotive song about the power of love and attraction. The lyrics tell a story of a person who is watching and admiring another person, and who is scared of falling in love with them. The imagery of "burning cities and napalm skies" is used to describe the intensity of their feelings, while the "fifteen flares" represent the spark between them.

The lyrics "No fair/You really know how to make me cry/When you give me those ocean eyes/I'm scared I've never fallen from quite this high" express the protagonist's fear of the unknown and their vulnerability.

In summary, "Ocean Eyes" is a powerful song about the power of love and attraction.
