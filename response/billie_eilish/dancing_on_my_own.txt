The song “Dancing on My Own” by Billie Eilish is a heartfelt track about being in love with someone who is unavailable to you. In the first verse, the narrator is talking about seeing the person they love with someone else, and the pain that comes with that realization. The narrator is in the corner, watching them kiss, and is unable to make themselves be seen. The chorus of the song is a powerful declaration of not allowing the heartache to keep them down, as they keep “dancing on my own” despite the pain they feel. 

In the second verse, the narrator is still in the corner, watching the person they love with someone else. The pain is still present, as they are unable to make themselves be seen and unable to be the one taken home. Despite this, they still keep “dancing on my own”.

The song is about heartache and unrequited love, but also about resilience and refusing to give in to the pain. Through the use of poetic imagery, the song conveys the raw emotion of the narrator, and the determination to stay strong in the face of disappointment.

Linguistically, the song is filled with vivid imagery, creating a sense of loneliness and heartache. From the “big black sky” to the narrator being “in the corner”, the lyrics convey a sense of despair and longing for love. Additionally, there is a strong sense of resilience in the chorus, as the narrator is determined to keep “dancing on my own”. 

Overall, “Dancing on My Own” is a powerful and emotive song that conveys the pain of heartache and the determination to stay strong. With vivid imagery and poetic language, Billie Eilish conveys the emotions of the narrator in a heartfelt and captivating way.
