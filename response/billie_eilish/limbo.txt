The song “Limbo” by Billie Eilish is a reflection on a broken relationship and the frustration that comes with it. The lyrics depict a situation in which the protagonist is stuck in limbo, desperately wanting the other person to stay and make things work, but ultimately knowing that they can’t. 

The first verse paints a picture of a relationship in which the protagonist is trying to pretend that everything is okay, even though it’s not. She is not crying on the phone when the other person calls and she is not trying to make things work anymore. This is an indication of the level of frustration and resignation that the protagonist is feeling. 

In the second verse, we see the protagonist expressing her desperation for the other person to stay and make things work. She wants them to give her their sweater, hold her hand tight, and make her feel better. However, she also knows that it’s futile and that she has nothing in common with the other person at this point, nor does she have anything left to lose. 

The chorus reinforces the idea of the protagonist being stuck in limbo and her feelings of resignation. She is prepared to give up her weapons if the other person loves her enough to stay, but she knows that won’t be the case. The idea of broken glass is used to further highlight the fragility of the relationship and how it is on the brink of shattering. 

The final verse sees the protagonist finally accepting the situation and realizing that she needs to let the other person go and leave limbo. She knows that nothing can make her feel better, and that there are too many problems between them to fix. 

Overall, the song “Limbo” is an honest and heartbreaking reflection on a relationship that has gone wrong. The lyrics effectively capture the feelings of desperation, frustration, and resignation that come with being stuck in limbo. The song also contains several layers of emotion and meaning that can be interpreted in different ways.

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song has been described as being “emotionally charged” and “raw”. The words used in the lyrics, such as “limbo”, “broken glass”, and “arms akimbo” all have a strong emotional connotation. This helps to convey the sense of frustration and resignation that the protagonist is feeling. The repetition of certain words and phrases also helps to create an atmosphere of desperation and hopelessness. 

Overall, “Limbo” is a powerful and emotionally charged song that captures the feelings of despair and resignation that come with a broken relationship. The lyrics are vivid and the sentiment is strong, making this an impactful and captivating song.
