The song “Paparazzi Instagram Monologue” by Billie Eilish is an honest and intense reaction to an encounter with paparazzi, who she has a long-standing policy of not signing items for, as they often sell them. In the song, Eilish expresses her frustration at being booed and called a “little bitch” for not signing items, noting that she had willingly gone out of her way to say hello and hug the fans she encountered. 

The song is a powerful reflection on the power dynamics of fame and how those who are famous are often treated poorly by those who are after their fame and money. Eilish conveys her anger and hurt at being treated this way, noting that the paparazzi who reacted so negatively to her not signing items were grown men. She also shows her understanding of the situation, noting that she probably will lose her Grammy nominations, but that the nomination itself is the important thing. 

The language used in the song reflects Eilish’s emotions, with the lyrics being full of exclamations and expletives that convey her anger and shock. The repeated use of the words “little” and “bitch” further illustrate her feelings of being belittled and disrespected. The song also shows a strong sense of self-respect, with Eilish asserting her own policy of not signing items, and her refusal to be disrespected by those who are after her fame and money. 

Overall, “Paparazzi Instagram Monologue” is a powerful and intense reflection on the power dynamics of fame and how those who are famous can be treated by those who are after their fame and money. The song conveys Eilish’s anger and hurt at being treated this way and also shows her understanding of the situation and her strong sense of self-respect.
