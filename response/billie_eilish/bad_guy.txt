"Bad Guy" is a song by American singer Billie Eilish. The song is about a relationship where the narrator is the "bad guy" and the person they are with is the "tough guy".

The lyrics describe the narrator's behavior in the relationship, including causing bruises on their knees and not saying thank you or please.

The chorus repeats the line "I'm the bad guy" and the post-chorus adds "duh" to emphasize the narrator's self-awareness of their own behavior.

In the second verse, the narrator talks about letting their partner take control and play a dominant role in the relationship. They also mention their mother singing with them, but not wanting to sing this particular song because of its lyrics.

The bridge adds another layer to the song, with the narrator liking when their partner gets mad and wearing their cologne. This suggests a desire for the partner's attention and a longing for the relationship to continue.

The song is a dark, atmospheric pop track with a catchy chorus and clever lyrics. Eilish's haunting vocals add to the eerie feel of the song, emphasizing the narrator's manipulative behavior.

Overall, "Bad Guy" is a thought-provoking and well-written song that showcases Eilish's talent as a singer and songwriter.
