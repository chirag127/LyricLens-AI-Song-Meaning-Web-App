The song "Ocean Eyes (Jengi Beats Remix)" by Billie Eilish is a beautiful, dreamlike ode to the power of love. The lyrics tell a story of a person who has been watching someone for some time, captivated by their ocean eyes, and is now falling in love. The song contains multiple metaphors, such as burning cities and napalm skies, fifteen flares, and a diamond mind. 

The first verse paints a vivid picture of someone who is enraptured by another person's eyes. The speaker is comparing the beauty of the person's eyes to a city burning, and the stars in the sky to fifteen flares. The imagery of burning cities and flares is interesting, as it can be seen as a metaphor for the intensity of the feelings the speaker is experiencing. 

The chorus further emphasizes the speaker's feelings for the person, as the speaker is scared of falling from such a high, yet still falls into the person's ocean eyes. The use of the ocean as a metaphor for love is particularly effective, as oceans can be seen as both tranquil and peaceful, as well as powerful and intense. 

The second verse talks about a world gone blind, and how the speaker is so captivated by the person that they can't stop thinking about their diamond mind. The metaphor of a diamond mind is particularly evocative, as diamonds are associated with strength, beauty, and permanence. 

In summary, "Ocean Eyes (Jengi Beats Remix)" is a beautiful love song that tells the story of someone captivated by another's eyes and mind. The use of metaphors and imagery to describe the intensity of the speaker's feelings is effective in conveying the powerful emotions the speaker is experiencing. The song also displays linguistic analysis of the lyrics, with emotive language used to express the strength of the feelings of love and admiration the speaker has for the other person. Ultimately, "Ocean Eyes (Jengi Beats Remix)" is a beautiful and captivating love song that is sure to draw listeners in and evoke a sense of nostalgia and emotion.
