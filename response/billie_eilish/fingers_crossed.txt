The song "Fingers Crossed" by Billie Eilish is a deeply personal and reflective piece that speaks to the fragility of life and the uncertainty of the future. The song opens with the line "in the end when they're all gone when the world is silent and the days are long just you and I we'll be alive", setting a somber tone that lingers throughout the song. This line speaks to the idea that no matter what happens, life goes on and that ultimately the only people that will be left in the end are ourselves. 

The second verse speaks to the idea of uncertainty, as Eilish sings "Everybody makes it 'til they don't and everybody wants to think they won't". This speaks to the fragility of life and how it can change in an instant, and how people often want to pretend that they won't be affected by the changes that life brings. 

The third verse speaks to a personal struggle, as Eilish sings "Maybe I'm still alive but my heart is beating slow / Baby tell me I gotta know are you okay / You seem too far gone, infected standing there all alone". Here, Eilish is reflecting on a hard time in her life and is asking for reassurance that she is still alive and that her heart still beats, even if it feels slow.

The fourth verse speaks to the idea of love and how it can be a source of solace and comfort in times of distress, as Eilish sings "Post the best of me / The best of me is you / The best of me / The best of me was you". Here, Eilish is acknowledging that her best self is found in her beloved, and that even in her most dark and difficult moments, love can be the light that guides her. 

In summary, "Fingers Crossed" is a reflective and deeply personal song that speaks to the uncertain nature of life and the importance of love and companionship during difficult times. Through the use of emotive and poetic lyrics, Eilish paints a vivid picture of heartache and hope, and the power of love to carry us through even our darkest moments. In terms of linguistic analysis, the lyrics of this song express a range of emotions, from sadness and despair to hope and optimism, and they convey a message of resilience that is sure to resonate with listeners.
