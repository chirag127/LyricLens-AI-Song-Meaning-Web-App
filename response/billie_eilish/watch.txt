In Billie Eilish's song "Watch," she reflects on a relationship that has come to an end, detailing the pain and resentment she feels towards the person who caused her so much suffering. In the first verse, she describes how her heart skips a beat every time she thinks of the person, and how if they were meant to be, it would have happened by now. The chorus follows with her expressing her anger, saying that she will watch as the person's car, and her heart, burn with the fire they started. The second verse follows a similar theme, with Eilish describing how the person's love feels so fake and she won't accept it. In the bridge, she reflects on how the person never did the same for her, and questions if they fantasize about her. The song concludes with her asserting her freedom and claiming that she will never let the person back to put out the fire they started. 

The lyrics of "Watch" reflect a strong sense of anger and resentment towards the person who hurt her. The song is full of imagery of fire and burning, which is used to symbolize the pain and anger she feels towards the person. The song has a strong sense of self-empowerment, with Eilish asserting her freedom and making a statement that she will never let the person back into her life. 

Linguistically, the song uses a range of emotive language. There are a lot of words which are used to express anger and resentment, such as "fake," "burn," and "nothin'." The use of repetition also emphasizes the words and emotions in the song, with the phrase "I'll sit and watch your car burn" being repeated in the chorus. This repetition highlights the anger and resentment that Eilish feels towards the person and helps to create a powerful atmosphere. 

In summary, "Watch" is a powerful song about anger and resentment towards someone who has hurt the singer. The use of emotive language and imagery of fire helps to express the strong emotions that Eilish feels in the song.
