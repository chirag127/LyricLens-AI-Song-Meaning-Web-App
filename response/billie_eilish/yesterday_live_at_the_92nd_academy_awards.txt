The song “Yesterday” by Billie Eilish is an emotional exploration of heartache and longing. In the first verse, Eilish reflects on how her troubles have become a permanent fixture in her life and expresses her yearning for the past. The second verse is a lament for a lost love, as Eilish is left wondering why her partner had to go and if she said something wrong. The chorus then reiterates the sentiment that love was an easy game to play in the past, and now she needs a place to hide from her troubles. 

The song’s lyrics lend themselves to a deep linguistic analysis. Eilish’s use of words like “suddenly”, “shadow”, and “long” evoke feelings of surprise, sadness, and isolation. The word “game” in the chorus can be interpreted as a metaphor for the relationship, and the idea of needing to hide suggests that the pain of the breakup is too much to bear. All in all, the song is a powerful reflection on the pain of heartbreak and longing for the past. 

The song’s powerful lyrics and emotional delivery make it a standout performance. The sparse instrumentation allows Eilish’s voice to be the main focus, and her vocal performance is hauntingly beautiful. The song’s production is also top-notch, with subtle touches like the echoing backing vocals adding to the atmosphere. All in all, “Yesterday” is an emotionally powerful song that will stay with you long after you’ve finished listening.
