The song “You Don’t Get Me High Anymore” by Billie Eilish is a melancholy reflection on the loss of a feeling. The lyrics portray a person who is unable to escape their feelings and struggles to find solace in the things that used to give them pleasure. The song starts with the singer reflecting on their current state, feeling “stoned in the backseat from a dream where my teeth fell out of my head”. The dream serves to reflect the feeling of being out of control and unable to escape their own thoughts. The chorus conveys the idea that nothing is fun anymore, as the singer laments “you don’t get me high anymore, it used to take one now it takes four”. This could be interpreted as a reference to drug use, but could also be seen as a metaphor for the singer’s inability to find pleasure in anything anymore. 

The verses of the song are full of imagery that reflects the singer’s feelings of hopelessness and despair. They describe the feeling of running through emergency rooms and spinning wheels and ceiling fans, symbolizing the feeling of being stuck in a cycle of pain and emptiness. The bridge builds on this idea of being unable to escape, as the singer reflects on their own feelings of helplessness, asking “do you feel like letting go, I wonder how far down it is”. 

The overall tone of the song is one of sadness and despair, as the singer reflects on their inability to find joy in things that used to bring them pleasure. It is a melancholic reflection on the loss of a feeling, and the struggle to find a way out of the darkness.

In summary, “You Don’t Get Me High Anymore” by Billie Eilish is a sad reflection on the loss of a feeling. The lyrics portray a person who is unable to escape their feelings and struggles to find solace in the things that used to bring them pleasure. The song is an exploration of the feeling of hopelessness and despair and the struggle to find a way out.

The song's lyrics provide an interesting insight into the artist's emotions, with a range of vivid imagery used to illustrate their feelings of helplessness and despair.
