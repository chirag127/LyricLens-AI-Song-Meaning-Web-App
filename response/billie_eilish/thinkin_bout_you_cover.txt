"Thinkin Bout You (Cover)" by Billie Eilish is a song about longing for a past love. The song opens with Eilish describing a tornado that has passed through her room as a metaphor for her emotions. She reflects on how her eyes "don't shed tears, but boy they bawl" when she thinks about the person she misses. The chorus sees Eilish asking her former lover if they still think about her, and if they are thinking about the future with her.

The next verse sees Eilish admitting that she thought the other person was cool enough to "kick it" with her, but then revealing that they don't feel the same way and she's not sure if they are thinking about the future with her. The chorus repeats, and Eilish then sings about how she remembers how the person made her feel and she wants to keep that feeling alive.

The lyrics of this song provide insight into the emotions Eilish is feeling. The song is filled with feelings of longing, sadness, and regret. The metaphor of the tornado also reflects Eilish's inner turmoil and the chaos she is feeling in her mind and heart. The song is an honest reflection of the pain of missing someone, and the hope that the person might still think about her.

The song is a powerful expression of Eilish's emotions and is crafted in a way that allows the listener to relate to and empathize with her. The lyrical analysis further reveals the depth of her emotions, and her longing for the person she is missing. The song has a strong message of hope, even in the midst of sadness and regret.

In summary, "Thinkin Bout You (Cover)" by Billie Eilish is a powerful and emotionally resonant song about longing for a past love. The lyrics are filled with feelings of sadness and regret, but also hope. The lyrical analysis of the song reveals the depth of Eilish's emotions and her longing for the person she is missing. The song is a powerful and relatable expression of her emotions, and is sure to connect with listeners everywhere.
