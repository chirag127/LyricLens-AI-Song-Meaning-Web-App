The song "My Boy" by Billie Eilish is a powerful and heartbreaking exploration of toxic relationships and failed promises. The song follows the narrative of a woman whose boyfriend has failed to keep his promise of love and devotion. The lyrics paint a vivid picture of the narrator's feelings of betrayal and anger, with the chorus repeating the line "My boy, my boy, my boy / Don't love me like he promised".

The song opens with a haunting and repetitive melody, which serves to emphasize the feeling of emptiness and betrayal that the narrator feels. The lyrics then build up to the chorus, which serves as a direct and powerful statement of the narrator's feelings. The verses then explore the narrator's inner turmoil and her attempts to try and make sense of the situation. The final verse then moves from the narrator's inner emotions to outwardly addressing her partner and the consequences of his betrayal.

When analyzing the lyrics of "My Boy", it is clear that the narrator is feeling betrayed, frustrated, and angry. The lyrics also show a deep sense of longing for the relationship, as the narrator is still hoping for the partner to fulfil their promise of love and devotion. On a linguistic level, the song uses powerful imagery and metaphors to emphasize the narrator's emotions, such as the use of "shadow" to describe her partner and the use of "knives" to describe the pain caused by his betrayal.

Overall, "My Boy" is an emotionally powerful song that speaks to the pain of a failed relationship. The lyrics are vivid and evocative, and the song serves as a powerful statement of the narrator's feelings of betrayal and anger.
