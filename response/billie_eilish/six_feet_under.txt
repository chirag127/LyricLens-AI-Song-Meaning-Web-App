"Six Feet Under" by Billie Eilish is a hauntingly beautiful song about the deep and lingering pain of a lost love. The song opens with the narrator reflecting on their sense of loss, asking for help as they find themselves “lost again” and remembering their now-distant lover. The chorus speaks to the finality of the relationship, with the narrator acknowledging that their love is “six feet under” and wondering if their gravesite was “watered by the rain” and if roses could bloom again. The sadness is further emphasized by the narrator's imagery of themselves “retrac[ing] [their] lips and eras[ing] [their] touch” and wishing that their lover would “blow away like smoke in air” from their memories. 

The song paints a vivid picture of the narrator's pain and regret, and the refrain of “six feet under” serves as a reminder of the finality of the relationship. The imagery of the roses blooming again serves as a metaphor for the hope that the narrator still holds of healing and finding love again. The song ends on a hopeful note, with the sounds of the music “laying them down” and the clouds “crying them back to life” despite the narrator's lover being “cold as a knife.” 

The lyrics of "Six Feet Under" are incredibly powerful, conveying the narrator's sense of loss and loneliness in an incredibly evocative way. The imagery of the roses blooming again speaks to the hope for healing and for finding love again, which serves as an inspiring message for listeners. Musically, the song is haunting and beautiful, with the lyrics and instrumentation coming together to create a powerful and emotive experience. Overall, "Six Feet Under" is a stunningly beautiful and evocative song that speaks to the depths of human emotion.
