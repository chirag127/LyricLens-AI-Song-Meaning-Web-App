"True Blue" is a song by Billie Eilish that tells a story of heartache and longing. The lyrics paint a vivid picture of a person struggling to move on from a past relationship.

The song opens with the singer expressing her feelings of loneliness and despair since her partner is not around. She tries to cope by counting her tears instead of sheep as she sleepwalks in the night. The next verse introduces a sense of hope as she finds herself on the street where her partner used to live. She knocks on the door but leaves when no one answers, acknowledging that things are not the same anymore.

The chorus emphasizes the narrator's attempt to move on, as she tries to live in black and white, but finds that she is still blue. Despite her efforts, she still has not been able to get over her former partner. The second verse paints a picture of the narrator's current life, as cold days and a frozen lake in the park represent her inner state of numbness.

The bridge of the song brings a sense of resolution as the narrator acknowledges that her bad dreams may not mean anything, and that her ex-partner may not need anything from her anymore. However, the chorus follows up with a reminder that, despite the narrator's efforts, she is still so blue, and not yet over her partner.

The song is a powerful and emotional exploration of the difficulty of moving on from a past relationship. Lyrically, it captures the conflicting emotions of wanting to move on and yet still feeling drawn to a person. The language is simple yet evocative, and the imagery of black and white contrasts with the narrator's true blue feelings to create a vivid and emotionally resonant picture. This song is a perfect example of Billie Eilish's skill in expressing complex emotions in her music.
