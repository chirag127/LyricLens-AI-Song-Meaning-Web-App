"8" by Billie Eilish is a song about disappointment and heartache. The chorus of the song further emphasizes her feelings of hurt and confusion, as she sings that her partner said not to treat her badly, but did so sadly. The song ends with the singer repeating the chorus and emphasizing the futility of her love, as she acknowledges that she will never be loved by her partner. 

The lyrics of "8" are reflective of the emotions of disappointment and heartache that come with unrequited love. The singer expresses her feelings of hurt and confusion as to why she can't please her partner, as well as her desire to move on.
