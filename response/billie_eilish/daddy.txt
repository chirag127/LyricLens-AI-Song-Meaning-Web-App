"Daddy" by Billie Eilish is a song that tells the story of a young woman's relationship with her father. The song opens with her expressing her sadness and loneliness without her father, and then she goes on to describe how much she misses him and how she wants to be close to him again. The chorus emphasizes the intensity of her emotions as she yearns for her father's love, and she expresses her desire to be with him and to hear his heartbeat. 

The lyrics of the song are filled with emotion and longing, and they capture the pain of being separated from a loved one. Billie Eilish's lyrics also demonstrate her understanding of what it means to be apart from someone you care about and her own experience of loneliness. The words are filled with imagery and metaphors that convey her feelings of sadness and longing.

In terms of the song's overall message, Billie Eilish is trying to express her sadness and loneliness without her father and her desire to be close to him again. The song also communicates her feelings of longing and an intense desire to be with her father, and her wish to hear his heartbeat when they are apart.

Linguistically, the song's lyrics demonstrate a deep understanding of the emotions associated with being apart from a loved one. The metaphors and imagery are powerful and effective in conveying the sadness and longing of the situation. Additionally, the repetition of certain words and phrases helps to emphasize the intensity of the feelings and the desire for connection with her father. 

Overall, "Daddy" is a powerful and emotive song that conveys the intense emotions associated with being apart from a loved one. Billie Eilish's lyrics demonstrate a deep understanding of the emotions associated with the situation, and her use of imagery and metaphors helps to convey her sadness and longing. The song is a powerful reminder of the importance of relationships, and it is sure to be an emotional experience for anyone who listens.
