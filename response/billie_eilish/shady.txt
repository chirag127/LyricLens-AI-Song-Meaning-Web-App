The song Shady by Billie Eilish is a melancholic yet empowering ballad about self-awareness and accepting yourself for who you are. The song opens up with the line “Strange boy, you’re acting shady / Never change, always stay the same”, a line that speaks to how one can become comfortable in their individual identity and embrace themselves for who they are, despite what others may think. The chorus of the song further reinforces this message, with the lyrics “I’m not tryna follow the trend / I’m just tryna be me / I’m not tryna follow the trend / Just tryna be free”. These lyrics illustrate the singer's freedom to be who they are despite societal pressures, and are a rallying cry to all who wish to express themselves without judgement or criticism.

The song's verses then go on to address the idea of self-acceptance and the consequences of criticism. In the first verse, Billie Eilish sings “People say what they want to / But I don’t mind at all / Cause I been doin’ me since the start / And I ain’t gonna stop for y’all”. This lyric speaks to how, despite what others may think or say, the singer is committed to staying true to themselves. The second verse further emphasizes this idea, with the lyrics “I ain’t gonna take no more / I’m just gonna be me / I ain’t gonna take no more / Just gonna be free”. This verse is a reaffirmation of the singer's commitment to self-expression, and serves as a reminder to all those who may feel confined by external pressures.

The song ends with a powerful and uplifting message of self-acceptance, with the lyrics “I don’t care what people say / I know who I am / I know who I am”. These lyrics offer a sense of empowerment to the listener, as they remind us that despite what others may think or say, each of us must ultimately find our own truths and express them without fear or judgement.

In summary, Shady is a powerful and inspiring song about self-acceptance and embracing one's individual identity despite external pressures. Through its emotive lyrics, Billie Eilish encourages us to be true to ourselves, and to find our own truths without fear or judgement.

From a linguistic analysis of the song lyrics, it is clear that the singer is conveying a strong sense of self-empowerment and resilience. Throughout the song, the singer emphasizes the importance of self-expression, and conveys a sense of independence and freedom from external pressure.
