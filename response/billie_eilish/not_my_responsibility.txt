 "Not My Responsibility" by Billie Eilish is a powerful anthem of self-acceptance and self-love. The song speaks to the idea that one's worth should not be based on the opinion of others. In the song, Eilish questions why people make assumptions about someone based on their size or clothing and why those assumptions should define their worth. 

The first verse of the song, Eilish sings of the judgement and opinions she faces from others. No matter what she does, she feels that someone is watching and judging her. Eilish questions how those opinions would affect her if she were to live by them, and she wonders if her clothing or size would be met with more approval. 

The chorus of "Not My Responsibility" is a declaration of self-worth and independence. Eilish acknowledges that she is not responsible for the opinion of others and that her value should not be defined by them. The song is a powerful reminder that one's self-worth should come from within, not from others.

The second verse of the song deals with the idea of self-acceptance and self-love. Eilish questions why she should feel ashamed of her body and her clothing choices, whether she is wearing more or less. She acknowledges that while people may judge her, she is not responsible for their opinion of her. 

The song is a powerful reminder to love ourselves and to accept ourselves as we are, regardless of what others may think. Through her lyrics, Eilish encourages her listeners to embrace their worth and to stand up for themselves. 

The lyrics of "Not My Responsibility" are full of emotion and power. Through her use of language, Eilish conveys her message of self-love and self-acceptance in a way that resonates with the listener. The song is an anthem of independence and self-worth that encourages its listeners to stand up for themselves and to embrace their value regardless of what others may think.
