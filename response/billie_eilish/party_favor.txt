Party Favor by Billie Eilish is a song that explores the concept of relationships, particularly the pain of letting go. The song begins with Eilish asking her former partner to call her back so they can talk, but quickly realizes that it’s too late and that the relationship is over. In the chorus, Eilish emphasizes her point by repeating the line “I’m not your party favor,” making it clear that she is not willing to be taken advantage of anymore. 

The first verse of the song deals with the longing and regret of a lost relationship. Eilish sings about the “weather” that has come and gone, a metaphor for their relationship. She also mentions books and mishearing her songs, further emphasizing the fact that something has changed between them. 

The chorus of the song is where Eilish makes her point clear: “I'm not your party favor.” This can be interpreted as her saying that she’s not going to be taken for granted anymore and that she won’t be someone’s “party favor” that can be used and disposed of. 

The song ends with a reminder of the pain of the lost relationship. Eilish sings “It’s not you, it’s me and all that other bullshit,” emphasizing the fact that the relationship ended because of her own choices and decisions. 

Overall, Party Favor is an emotionally powerful song that explores the pain of letting go of a relationship. The lyrics are full of metaphors and poetic language that effectively convey Eilish’s feelings and emotions. The song is a powerful reminder of the pain of a lost relationship and the importance of making your own choices and decisions.
