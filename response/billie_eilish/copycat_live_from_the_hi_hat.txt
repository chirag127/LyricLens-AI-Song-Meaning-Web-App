The song "Copycat (Live from The Hi Hat)" by Billie Eilish is a modern take on the classic theme of someone copying another person's style. The lyrics paint a vivid picture of a person who is tired of being imitated and is determined to protect their own originality. 

The chorus of the song is a warning to a copycat, telling them that they have been uninvited from the game and that they should not expect to get anything from the original. The lyrics emphasize the idea that the copycat is trying to "cop" the original's manner and glamor, but they will not be successful in this endeavor. 

In the first verse, the lyrics describe the copycat as committing a crime against the original by trying to imitate them. It paints a picture of a push-and-pull dynamic between the two, with the original having their "finger on the trigger" even though it is the copycat's trigger finger. 

The second verse shifts the focus to the copycat, describing them as being "calloused" and "cold" in comparison to the original, who is described as "bold". The lyrics also suggest that the copycat better love the original, as they are "just a clone" in comparison. 

The final verse is a warning to the copycat, telling them that they have crossed a line and run out of time. The lyrics emphasize the idea that the original will not stand for the copycat's attempts to steal their manner and glamor. 

In summary, "Copycat (Live from The Hi Hat)" by Billie Eilish is a warning to copycats who try to imitate someone else's style. The lyrics emphasize the idea that the original will not stand for the copycat's attempts to steal their manner and glamor, and that the copycat will not be successful in doing so. 

Linguistically, the song uses strong language to emphasize the idea that the copycat will not succeed in their attempts to imitate the original. The lyrics also contain a lot of repetition, which helps drive home the point that the copycat is not welcome and will not be successful in their endeavors. 

Overall, "Copycat (Live from The Hi Hat)" is an excellent song that captures the frustrations of the original person when they are copied by another. It is an important reminder that everyone should strive to be original and to respect the originality of others.
