The song “Bellyache (Marian Hill Remix)” by Billie Eilish is a dark and brooding song about being betrayed by a lover and struggling to understand why. Additionally, the line “What an expensive fake” implies that her lover was not genuine, and she was fooled into believing that their relationship was real. 

The second verse focuses on the consequences of the betrayal, as Eilish sings about her friends coming over to look for her and her biting her nails in fear of going to jail. This paints a picture of a young woman who is feeling overwhelmed by the situation and unable to cope with the reality of what happened. 

The third verse finds Eilish singing about being betrayed by her lover and trying to make sense of it all. She repeats the line “Maybe it’s in the gutter”, suggesting that her mind is still trying to piece together what happened.

The song’s lyrics provide a vivid picture of the emotional turmoil and confusion that is associated with being betrayed by a loved one. In her lyrics, Eilish conveys a sense of loneliness, confusion, and fear that is common among those who have been betrayed in a romantic relationship. The song explores a range of emotions, from anger and sadness to confusion and despair.
