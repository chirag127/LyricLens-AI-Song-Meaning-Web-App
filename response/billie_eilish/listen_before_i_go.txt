"Listen Before I Go" by Billie Eilish is a song that dives into the depths of emotional pain and suffering. In the first verse, Billie expresses her desire to escape to a place of peace and serenity, asking to be taken to a rooftop to be able to see the world. This can be seen as a metaphor for her wanting to escape the pain she is feeling, as well as a plea for help. In the chorus, Billie expresses her feelings of helplessness, knowing that this escape is not possible. She sings of being sorry and of having no way out, and of being “down”, likely referring to her emotional state. 

In the second verse, Billie sings of the emotional toll that her pain has taken on her, expressing that it has caused her to cry salty tears. She also expresses her feelings of powerlessness, that no matter how much she wants to be okay, she can’t escape the pain. She expresses her desire for someone to understand her and acknowledge her feelings, but also her understanding that this is not possible. 

The bridge of the song sees Billie calling out to her friends, telling them that she loves them and will miss them, yet still not being sorry for the choices she has made. This can be seen as a reflection of her understanding that her pain and suffering are a part of life, and that it is okay to feel these emotions, even if it causes love and hurt to those around her.

Overall, "Listen Before I Go" is a song of self-reflection and acceptance of pain. Billie conveys her feelings of helplessness, powerlessness, and sorrow, yet also her understanding that it is okay to feel these emotions. Linguistically, the song conveys a sense of sadness and hopelessness, with words such as “leavin’” and “down” being repeated throughout. This conveys the idea that pain and suffering are a part of life, and that it is important to accept and understand these emotions.
