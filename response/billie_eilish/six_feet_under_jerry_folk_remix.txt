"Six Feet Under" by Billie Eilish is a song about the death of a relationship and the grief and sadness that comes with it. The song starts with the line "help I lost myself again", which speaks to the narrator's feelings of being lost in the aftermath of the relationship ending. The next line "but I remember you don't come back" speaks to the feeling of helplessness in the situation, as the narrator recognizes that the relationship is over and that the other person is not coming back. The following lines "it won't end well but I wish you'd tell me too" convey the idea that the narrator is hoping for closure and resolution, but knows that it is unlikely to happen. 

The chorus of the song is the narrator reflecting on their relationship, musing on what their love would have been like had it been given the chance to flourish, and asking if the love they shared would have been enough to make roses bloom. This speaks to the idea that despite the fact that the relationship is over, the narrator still holds onto hope that it could have been something meaningful and beautiful.

The song's lyrics also contain a sense of sadness and regret in the lines "retrace my lips, erase your touch, it's all too much for me" which speaks to the idea that the narrator is overwhelmed by the pain and sadness of the situation. The final lines of the song, "our love is six feet under, I can't help but wonder, if our grave was watered by the rain, would roses bloom, could roses bloom" reiterate the idea that the narrator is still holding onto hope that their love would have been enough to make something beautiful.

In summary, "Six Feet Under" by Billie Eilish is a song about the death of a relationship, and the pain and grief that comes with it. The narrator is filled with sadness and regret, but still has a sense of hope that their love could have been something beautiful.

The lyrics of the song demonstrate a range of different emotions. The first verse speaks to feelings of being lost and helpless, while the chorus reflects on the love that the two people shared and the hope that it could have been something beautiful. The bridge speaks to feelings of sadness and regret, while the chorus speaks to a sense of hope. This range of emotions creates a powerful and poignant story, and speaks to the narrator's inner turmoil and struggle.

The song also contains a range of linguistic devices. The repetition of the phrase "our love is six feet under" is used to emphasize the idea of the death of the relationship and the narrator's grief. The use of imagery such as "smoke in air" and "roses bloom" also helps to bring the story to life. 

Overall, "Six Feet Under" by Billie Eilish is a powerful and poignant song about the death of a relationship and the sadness and grief that comes with it. The range of emotions in the lyrics combined with the use of powerful imagery and repetition makes it a moving and thought-provoking song.
