The song “Six Feet Under” (Ninski & Mickey Valen Remix) by Billie Eilish is a somber, reflective track that speaks to the feelings of loss and regret that come with a relationship’s end. The song begins by introducing the idea of a person losing themselves in the midst of a breakup, but being reminded that the other person is no longer around. Despite the somber tone, there is still a strong glimmer of hope that the person being spoken to might come back, even though the narrator knows that this wouldn’t end well.

The chorus of the song speaks to the finality of the situation, with the narrator reflecting on the love they shared being “six feet under”, and wondering if the rain pouring on their grave could bring back the love they shared. This is followed by the narrator trying to erase the other person’s touch and presence, as they process the difficult feelings of loss and regret. The outro of the song speaks to the carelessness with which the other person died, leaving the narrator feeling overwhelmed by the suddenness and finality of the breakup.

The song speaks to the emotional toll that breakups can take on people, and how it can leave them feeling lost and confused. The lyrics are reflective of the deep emotions that are felt in these situations, and the sentiment of wishing that the other person would come back even though it wouldn’t end well conveys the desperation that can be felt. The imagery of the rain on the grave symbolizes the hope that can be found in the midst of tragedy, and the carelessness of the other person’s death emphasizes the suddenness of the breakup.

Overall, “Six Feet Under” (Ninski & Mickey Valen Remix) by Billie Eilish is a powerful track that speaks to the emotional toll of breakups, and speaks to the complex emotions that can be felt in these situations. The imagery and symbolism in the lyrics help to convey the feelings of loss and regret, while also conveying a sense of hope that can be found in the midst of tragedy. The song is a powerful reflection on the emotional toll that breakups can take on people, and is an excellent example of Billie Eilish’s songwriting talent.
