"LA Girls" is a song by Charlie Puth that explores the theme of longing for a past relationship. In the song, Puth sings about how he misses his ex-partner and regrets the mistakes he made that led to the end of their relationship. He also reflects on how he attempted to fill the void left by his ex by dating other women, but ultimately realized that no one can replace her.

The song begins with Puth singing about how he has moved on to bigger and better things, but the truth is written all over his face – he is still haunted by his ex. He then goes on to mention several women he has dated since their breakup, but admits that there is only room for his ex in his world. Despite the fact that his ex accuses him of being fickle and changing like the weather, Puth realizes that he made a mistake by letting her go.

In the chorus, Puth repeats the line "I miss my baby" as he reflects on the good times they shared together. He wishes he could go back and make things right, but knows it's too late.

Overall, "LA Girls" is a heartwarming and relatable song about the pain of losing a loved one and the regrets that come with it. Puth's emotive vocals and catchy melodies make the song a standout track on his album.
