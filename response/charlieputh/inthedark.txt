The song "In The Dark" by Charlie Puth is a romantic track that expresses the singer's desire to be with a certain girl. In the first verse, Puth sees a girl sitting alone at a crowded bar and notes that she is looking at someone else, but he believes that they would be a perfect match. He encourages her to give him a chance, asking why she is playing hard to get.

In the chorus, Puth sings about how they don't have much time and encourages the girl to put her lips on his. He also suggests that they think of all the things they could do together if they were in the dark, hinting at the possibility of a sexual encounter.

The second verse delves into the girl's current situation, where she is spending her nights alone and receiving multiple calls from other guys. Puth suggests that she is looking in the wrong direction and that she should rearrange her plans to be with him instead.

Overall, the song is a playful and seductive track that showcases Puth's smooth vocals and catchy melodies. The lyrics are simple but effective in conveying the singer's desire for the girl. As a whole, "In The Dark" is a fun and enjoyable track that is sure to be a hit with fans of Charlie Puth's music.
