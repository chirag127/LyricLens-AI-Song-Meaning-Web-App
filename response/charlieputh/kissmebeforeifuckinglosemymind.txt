"Kiss Me Before I Fucking Lose My Mind" by Charlie Puth is a song about a person who has been hiding their feelings for someone else for a long time, but is now at the point where they can't take it anymore and need to tell the other person how they feel. The song is about the fear of rejection and the anxiety of putting yourself out there. The singer is asking the other person to kiss them before they lose their mind from all the built-up emotions. The song is upbeat and catchy, with relatable lyrics and a strong vocal performance from Charlie Puth. Overall, it's a great addition to his discography and a song that many people can relate to.
