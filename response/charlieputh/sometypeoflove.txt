"Some Type Of Love" by Charlie Puth is a love song that explores the idea of a lifelong, committed relationship. In the lyrics, the singer expresses his desire to grow old with the person he loves, and to always have them by his side through every moment, good and bad.

In the first verse, the singer imagines a future where he is old and grown, and he won't have to sleep alone because the person he loves will be there with him. He also says that every moment will fade into them, indicating the deep emotional connection he has with this person.

The chorus repeats the line "That's some type of love," emphasizing the intensity and depth of the singer's feelings for the person he loves. He sees their relationship as something special and unique, and he is willing to commit to them for the rest of his life.

In the second verse, the singer imagines a future where they are old and gray, and their faces have changed, but their love for each other remains the same. He also mentions that they could continue to "pretend" that their love will never fade, even when the stories of other relationships have ended.

The song's chorus repeats the idea that the singer's love for this person is "some type of love," indicating that it is a love that is strong, enduring, and unique. He is willing to give himself completely to this person, and to stand by them no matter what.

Overall, "Some Type Of Love" is a sweet and romantic love song that explores the idea of a lifelong, committed relationship. The singer's dedication to the person he loves is evident in the lyrics, and the song's catchy melody and upbeat rhythm make it a joy to listen to.
