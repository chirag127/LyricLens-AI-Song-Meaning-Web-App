"Heart Go Crazy" by Charlie Puth is a love song about being completely enamored with someone and feeling like your heart is going crazy with desire and infatuation. In the first few lines, the narrator expresses his desire to spend more time with the object of his affection and how he can't stop thinking about them. He then goes on to say that he would be willing to wait forever for them, as long as they don't tell him "never" and shut down the possibility of a relationship.

In the chorus, the narrator sings about how being with this person makes him feel like he could touch the sky, and how just looking into their eyes makes his heart go crazy. The song has a upbeat, pop-infused sound that matches the feelings of excitement and desire expressed in the lyrics.

Overall, "Heart Go Crazy" is a catchy and relatable love song that captures the feeling of falling hard for someone and wanting to be with them all the time.
