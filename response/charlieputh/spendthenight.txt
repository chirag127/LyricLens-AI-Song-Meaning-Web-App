"Spend The Night" by Charlie Puth is a romantic and longing ballad about the desire to spend more time with a loved one. In the first verse, Puth sings about how he misses his partner on the long drive home from work on Monday nights, and how he wishes he could be with them instead. He reminisces about the good times they had together, such as staying home and watching TV shows like "Friends".

The chorus repeats the refrain "Come spend the night here with me up in Boston / Don't leave me here all alone / And I wonder if you'll feel the same way tomorrow / If you see me calling your phone," expressing Puth's hope that his partner will agree to spend the night with him and not leave him alone. He wonders if they still feel the same way about him and if they will answer his calls.

In the second verse, Puth sings about the intimacy and comfort of lying in bed together until the early hours of the morning. He expresses his willingness to do anything to experience that feeling again.

Overall, "Spend The Night" is a sweet and heartfelt ballad that captures the longing and vulnerability of love. Puth's emotive vocals and relatable lyrics make it a powerful and relatable track that will resonate with many listeners.
