"That's Not How This Works" is a song by Charlie Puth that deals with the complexities of a relationship. The lyrics describe a situation where one person tries to end the relationship, but the other person continues to hold on and try to make things work. The singer expresses frustration with the other person's indecision and their tendency to change their mind about the relationship. The song is a commentary on the difficulties of navigating romantic relationships and the ways in which people can hurt each other without intending to.

In the first verse, the singer describes the moment when their partner disappears and they think the relationship is over. However, they later see their partner with someone else, and they assume this is the end of the relationship. However, the partner still tells the singer that they need them, leaving the singer confused and unsure of what is happening.

The chorus repeats the line "that's not how this works," emphasizing the singer's frustration with their partner's indecision. They tell their partner that they can't say they hate them and then call them when they're hurt, and that they can't walk away and then come back to what they were. The singer makes it clear that they are fed up with the inconsistency and lack of communication in the relationship.

In the second verse, the singer refers to past memories of the relationship, such as staying up late and watching TV together. However, they also mention that their partner is constantly reminding them of these memories, which the singer believes is just another excuse to try and hold on to the relationship. The singer expresses frustration with their partner's inability to make up their mind and move on.

The bridge of the song features the line "don't say that you're in love with me / cause I know it's not what you mean." This line emphasizes the disconnect in the relationship and the lack of genuine emotion from the partner. The singer knows that their partner's words are not sincere and that they are just trying to manipulate the situation.

Overall, "That's Not How This Works" is a relatable and emotional song about the complexities of romantic relationships. The lyrics capture the confusion and frustration that can arise when one person wants to end a relationship but the other person is not ready to let go. The song's catchy chorus and catchy melody make it a standout track on Charlie Puth's album.
