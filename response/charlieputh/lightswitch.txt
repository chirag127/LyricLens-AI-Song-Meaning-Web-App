"Light Switch" by Charlie Puth is a song about a relationship that has become toxic and emotionally manipulative. The narrator describes how their partner always seems to call them late at night, looking for a hookup, but never wants to commit to a real relationship. Despite this, the narrator keeps going back to them, unable to resist their charms.

In the chorus, the narrator compares their partner's ability to seduce them to flipping a light switch, suggesting that they have a strong physical attraction to each other, but no real emotional connection. The song suggests that the narrator is aware of the unhealthy nature of their relationship, but they are unable to break free from it.

Overall, the song is a commentary on the dangers of getting involved with someone who is emotionally manipulative and unreliable. The catchy chorus and upbeat tempo make it a fun listen, but the lyrics offer a cautionary tale about the pitfalls of getting involved with the wrong person.
