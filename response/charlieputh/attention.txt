"Attention" by Charlie Puth is a pop song about a relationship that has ended and the former partner's desire for attention. The song's narrator accuses their ex of throwing dirt on their name and going to parties in Los Angeles just to make sure that the narrator sees them. The narrator suggests that the ex only wants attention and doesn't actually want their heart. In the chorus, the narrator sings, "You just want attention / You don't want my heart / Maybe you just hate the thought of me with someone new / Yeah, you just want attention / I knew from the start / You're just making sure I'm never getting over you."

The song has a catchy beat and Puth's smooth vocals make it an enjoyable listen. The lyrics are relatable for anyone who has been in a relationship where their partner only wanted attention and not a real connection. Overall, "Attention" is a solid pop song that captures the frustration of a toxic relationship.
