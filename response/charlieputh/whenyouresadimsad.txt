"When You're Sad I'm Sad" is a song about a relationship where one person is trying to move on but keeps being drawn back in by the other person's emotional manipulation. The lyrics describe the singer trying to end things with their partner, but feeling guilty and taking them back whenever they cry or beg for them to stay. The singer realizes that they are being used and that the relationship is not healthy, but they still cannot fully let go.

The song is a relatable and emotional exploration of the struggles of breaking up with someone who is not good for you. Charlie Puth's powerful vocals and heartfelt lyrics make the song a poignant and moving listening experience. Overall, "When You're Sad I'm Sad" is a well-written and emotionally impactful song.
