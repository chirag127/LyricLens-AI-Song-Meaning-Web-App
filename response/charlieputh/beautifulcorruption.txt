"Beautiful Corruption" by Charlie Puth is a song about a person who is struggling to move on from a relationship that has ended. The song starts with the narrator reflecting on their current situation, feeling lonely and unsure of why they even came to the place they are in. The chorus repeats the main theme of the song, with the narrator expressing their fear of being self-destructive when they are near their ex-partner.

The second verse describes a specific scene where the narrator is at a party with their ex, and they are feeling overwhelmed by the memories and emotions that are coming back to them. The chorus repeats once again, emphasizing the narrator's fear of falling back into their old patterns.

Overall, the song has a melancholy tone and the lyrics are well-written, capturing the confusion and fear that can come with trying to move on from a difficult relationship. The chorus is particularly powerful, with the line "I'll be self-destructing when your beautiful corruption touches me" highlighting the pull that the ex-partner still has over the narrator. It is a relatable and emotional song that will resonate with anyone who has struggled to move on from a past relationship.
