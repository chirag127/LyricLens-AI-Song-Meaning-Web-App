"Life's Good" by Charlie Puth is a motivational and uplifting song about perseverance and not giving up. The lyrics describe a person who is feeling down and unsure of themselves, but is encouraged by someone else to keep going and not give up. The chorus repeats the refrain "if at first you don't succeed and you fall down to your knees / oh, you gotta pick yourself back up and try again / if it don't go like it should, just remember life is good / and you gotta pick yourself back up and try again." This message of resilience and determination is a powerful one, and the catchy chorus and upbeat melody make the song an enjoyable and inspiring listen. Overall, "Life's Good" is a positive and encouraging song that encourages listeners to keep pushing through tough times and never give up.
