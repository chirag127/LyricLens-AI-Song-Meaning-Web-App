"Seventeen" by Charlie Puth is a nostalgic and reflective song about the memories and emotions of a young love affair. In the first verse, Puth sings about the excitement and thrill of sneaking out of the house with his underage partner and driving around together in his Chevy. He mentions how they try to avoid getting pulled over by the police and how they have to find a place to sleep if they can't find a motel.

The chorus repeats the refrain "It's not right to hold you through the night / You put up no fight when I put words in your mouth," expressing Puth's guilt and confusion about his relationship with a girl who is only 17 and not yet of legal age. He sings about how she tells him that she is proud to be 17, but he can't help but feel that it is wrong for them to be together.

In the second verse, Puth sings about the playful and carefree moments they shared, such as throwing grass at each other and kissing in the moonlight. He also reflects on the bittersweet feeling of waking up in the middle of the night on a deserted highway, realizing that their relationship is not meant to be.

Overall, "Seventeen" is a poignant and evocative song that captures the emotions and memories of young love. Puth's nostalgic lyrics and mellow vocals make it a relatable and introspective track that many listeners will be able to connect with.
