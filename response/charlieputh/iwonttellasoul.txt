"I Won't Tell a Soul" by Charlie Puth is a song about a forbidden romance between two people who are already in relationships with other people. Despite knowing that what they're doing is wrong, the singer and his partner can't help but continue their secret affair.

In the first verse, the singer talks about how both of them are aware that their relationship isn't right, but they can't seem to let go of each other. Every time one of them tries to end it, the other holds on tighter.

The chorus repeats the sentiment of the singer offering to keep their relationship a secret if his partner stays with him for the night. He says he won't tell anyone about their time together.

In the second verse, the singer's partner admits that someone is waiting for her at home, and she knows she shouldn't be with the singer. However, she continues to kiss him and they decide to lock all the doors and draw the blinds to keep their secret safe.

The song ends with the chorus repeating, with the singer encouraging his partner to stay with him and keep their love a secret.

Overall, "I Won't Tell a Soul" is a sensual and alluring song about the temptation of forbidden love. Charlie Puth's smooth vocals and catchy melody make it a standout track.
