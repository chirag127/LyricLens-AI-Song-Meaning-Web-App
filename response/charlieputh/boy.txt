"BOY" by Charlie Puth is a song about a relationship in which the other person is treating the singer like a child. In the song, the singer is expressing frustration at being treated as though he is not mature or serious enough for the other person, despite giving them what they wanted and putting in effort in the relationship. The singer is asking for the other person to take him seriously and not treat him like a boy.

In the first verse, the singer describes how he is used to moving on from relationships easily, but for the first time, he wants to commit and make it work. However, the other person has been in more relationships than him and is not taking the relationship seriously.

In the chorus, the singer expresses confusion and hurt at being treated like a child and not being taken seriously. He asks how the other person can love him and then not want to stay with him. He also points out that he is not too young and has given the other person what they wanted.

Overall, "BOY" is a song about feeling undervalued and dismissed in a relationship. The singer is asking for the other person to take him seriously and treat him with respect.
