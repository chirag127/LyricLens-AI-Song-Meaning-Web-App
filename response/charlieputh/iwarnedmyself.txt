"I Warned Myself" by Charlie Puth is a song about a person who has been cheated on and is warning themselves not to fall into the same situation again. In the song, the narrator reflects on their relationship and the lies their partner told them. They warn themselves not to trust the person again and to be careful with their heart. The song is a cautionary tale about the dangers of falling for someone who has cheated in the past.

In the first verse, the narrator admits that they were wrong to trust their partner and that they should have known better. They express regret for getting involved with someone who has proven to be untrustworthy. The chorus repeats the line "I warned myself that I shouldn't play with fire / But I can tell that I'll do it one more time / Don't trust in myself, no good for my health / You messed with my heart, now you're the reason why." This line reflects the narrator's inner conflict between their desire to trust their partner and their fear of getting hurt again.

The second verse focuses on the lies the partner told the narrator. The narrator remembers being told that their partner's relationship with their ex was over, only to find out later that it wasn't true. They question why their partner would lie about such a important detail and express frustration at being kept in the dark.

The final chorus reiterates the warning to not trust the partner again. The narrator has learned from their mistake and knows that they shouldn't put themselves in a vulnerable position again.

Overall, "I Warned Myself" is a relatable song about the difficulties of moving on from a relationship that has been marked by infidelity. The song's catchy chorus and relatable lyrics make it a standout track on Charlie Puth's album "Voicenotes."
