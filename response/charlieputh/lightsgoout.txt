The song "Lights Go Out" by Charlie Puth is about a person who is trying to catch the attention of someone they are interested in. They are trying to convince the person to spend time with them, and they promise to take care of them and never leave them feeling cold. The song has a catchy beat and the lyrics are relatable, making it a enjoyable listen. The chorus is particularly memorable, with the repeated line "when the lights go out, you know it's going down" capturing the excitement and anticipation of a potential romantic encounter. Overall, "Lights Go Out" is a fun and upbeat song that many people can enjoy.
