The song "Left Right Left" by Charlie Puth is about overcoming challenges and moving forward in life. In the lyrics, Puth sings about being down on his luck and feeling like he's been through tough times. He talks about how he's trying to keep up with the changing times and how he's climbing daily to reach the top. Puth also sings about being brokenhearted and feeling lost without someone by his side, but he encourages the listener to keep pushing forward and not give up.

In the chorus, Puth sings about taking one more step towards achieving their goals and moving on without any regrets. The repeated refrain of "left, right, left" could symbolize the steps one takes towards progress, or the constant decision-making and adjustments needed to move forward in life.

Overall, the song is an upbeat and motivational anthem about perseverance and not giving up on your dreams. The catchy chorus and energetic instrumentals make it an enjoyable and uplifting listen.
