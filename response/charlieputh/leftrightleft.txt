"Left Right Left" by Charlie Puth is a song about overcoming struggles and adversity in life. In the first verse, the singer expresses their feelings of hurt and misfortune. In the final chorus, the singer repeats the refrain of "left, right, left, right" and encourages themselves and their partner to keep moving forward, one step at a time. They acknowledge that the journey may be long and difficult, but they're determined to reach their goals and overcome their struggles.

Overall, "Left Right Left" is a motivating and inspiring song about perseverance and resilience. The lyrics are relatable and uplifting, encouraging listeners to keep pushing through even when things are tough.
