"Tangerine Dreams" by Charlie Puth is a nostalgic and emotional ballad about the aftermath of a difficult breakup. In the first verse, Puth sings about how he listens to his ex-partner sing themselves to sleep at night, feeling a sense of longing and regret. He opens up the curtains to listen to the rain, hoping to wash away their pain.

In the chorus, Puth expresses his desire to go anywhere with his ex-partner, but admits that the tangerine dreams of their relationship will not go away easily. He tries to rebuild and mold these dreams out of clay, but they continue to haunt him.

In the second verse, Puth apologizes for taking so long to get back to his ex-partner, and admits that he has not been himself since their breakup. He knows he will not be able to see them on Commonwealth again, and this thought makes him suffer in silence.

Overall, "Tangerine Dreams" is a powerful and relatable ballad that captures the pain and longing of a difficult breakup. Puth's emotive vocals and raw lyrics make it a powerful and moving track that will resonate with many listeners.
