"Look At Me Now" is a song by Charlie Puth that celebrates his journey to success. In the song, Puth reflects on the times when he was doubted and underestimated by others. He talks about how he has overcome those obstacles and is now in a position where he can look back and prove all of his doubters wrong.

The song starts off with Puth talking about how he has always been determined to succeed, even when others said he couldn't. He says that he's going to show everyone that he can do everything they said he couldn't and more.

The chorus of the song is where Puth really celebrates his success. He says that he's "kickin' in the door now" and that he's going to be unstoppable. He also mentions that he's going to be "stronger, a little bit taller, [and] on top" in the future.

The second verse of the song is where Puth reflects on the people who doubted him in the past. He says that he's going to call all of the girls who turned him down and tell them that he's "just too busy" to talk to them now because he's too focused on his success.

The song ends with Puth repeating the chorus, where he continues to celebrate his success and urge his doubters to "look at me now."

Overall, "Look At Me Now" is a powerful and triumphant song that celebrates overcoming obstacles and achieving success. Puth's confident vocals and uplifting lyrics make for a truly inspiring track.
