"When She Kissed Me" by Charlie Puth is a romantic song about falling in love with someone who unexpectedly surprises and impresses the narrator. The song describes the narrator's experiences with this person, from their first date to their first kiss. Throughout the song, the narrator expresses amazement at the feelings they have for this person and their determination to hold onto these feelings.

In the first verse, the narrator describes taking the person out to dinner and being surprised by their personality and charm. The chorus then describes the moment of their first kiss, which the narrator says knocked them off their feet and made them feel like they had found the person they had been looking for.

In the second verse, the narrator describes spending more time with this person, including a trip to the beach and a movie date. They express their admiration for this person and their excitement at the possibility of a relationship with them. The song ends with the narrator pledging to never let this person go.

Overall, "When She Kissed Me" is a sweet and romantic song that captures the feeling of falling in love and the excitement of a new relationship. The song is well-written and catchy, with a catchy chorus that sticks in the listener's head. The song is a good choice for fans of romantic pop music.
