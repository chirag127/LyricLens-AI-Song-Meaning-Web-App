"If You Leave Me Now" is a song by Charlie Puth featuring Boyz II Men. In the song, the narrator is pleading with his partner not to leave him. He admits to making mistakes and promises to change in order to save their relationship. He expresses his fear of being alone and begs his partner not to say goodbye. The chorus of the song repeats the phrase "baby, if you leave me now," emphasizing the narrator's desperation.

Overall, the song is a heart-wrenching ballad about the pain of losing someone you love. The addition of Boyz II Men's harmonies adds to the emotional impact of the song. Charlie Puth's vocals are strong and emotional, perfectly capturing the despair of the narrator. This is a powerful and moving song that will resonate with anyone who has experienced the pain of a failed relationship.
