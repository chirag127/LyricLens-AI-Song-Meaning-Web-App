"Ride to Melrose" by Charlie Puth is a melancholy and introspective song about the aftermath of a breakup. In the first verse, Puth sings about how his partner left him and how he is coping with the pain and loneliness. He mentions carving her name into his car seat and feeling the need to escape the city and clear his mind.

In the chorus, Puth sings about taking a road trip to Melrose with a friend, where they drink and try to forget their sorrows. They talk about their dreams and aspirations, and try to find some solace in each other's company.

The second verse and the rest of the song consists of Puth's internal monologue, as he grapples with the emotional turmoil of the breakup and the memories it brings up. He reflects on his feelings of inadequacy and the sense of loss and longing he experiences. He also expresses a desire to reconnect with his partner and make things right, but ultimately accepts that their relationship is over.

Overall, "Ride to Melrose" is a raw and emotional song that captures the pain and confusion of a breakup. Puth's introspective lyrics and vulnerable vocals make it a relatable and moving track that many listeners will be able to connect with.
