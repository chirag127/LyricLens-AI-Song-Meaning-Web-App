The song "Attention (Bingo Players Remix)" by Charlie Puth is about a person who is seeking attention from their ex-partner. The narrator accuses their ex of trying to ruin their reputation and showing up at parties in order to try and get their attention. The narrator also accuses their ex of not really wanting their heart, but just wanting to make sure they are never able to move on.

The first line of the song, "You've been runnin' 'round, runnin' 'round, runnin' 'round / Throwin' that dirt all on my name," sets the tone for the song, with the narrator accusing their ex of trying to ruin their reputation. The line "You've been going 'round, going 'round, going 'round / Every party in LA / 'Cause you knew that I, knew that I, knew that I'd / Be at one (be at one), oh," further accuses the ex of trying to get their attention by showing up at parties where they know the narrator will be.

The chorus repeats the main theme of the song, with the narrator stating that their ex "just want[s] attention." The lines "You just want attention / You don't want my heart / Maybe you just hate the thought of me with someone new / Yeah, you just want attention, I knew from the start / You're just making sure I'm never gettin' over you (over you), oh" highlight the narrator's frustration and anger at their ex's behavior.

Overall, the song is a catchy and relatable anthem for moving on from an ex who is only interested in getting attention. The lyrics are well-written and the melody is engaging, making it a great song to listen to if you are dealing with a similar situation in your own life.
