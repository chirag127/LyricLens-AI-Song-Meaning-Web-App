The song "Next to You" by Charlie Puth is a love song in which the singer expresses his desire to be close to his romantic partner. The lyrics describe the singer's infatuation with the partner and the feeling of being "lost in [their] eyes." The song also includes themes of longing and regret, as the singer wishes he had acted on his feelings sooner.

Musically, the song is upbeat and poppy, with a catchy chorus and a memorable melody. Puth's vocals are smooth and emotive, capturing the emotional intensity of the lyrics.

Overall, "Next to You" is a fun and relatable love song that showcases Puth's ability to craft catchy and emotional tunes. It is a great addition to his repertoire and a testament to his talent as a singer and songwriter.
