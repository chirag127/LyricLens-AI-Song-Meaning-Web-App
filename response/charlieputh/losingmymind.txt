"Losing My Mind" by Charlie Puth is a song about the feeling of being overwhelmed and overworked, and the struggle to find balance and happiness in a fast-paced and demanding world. In the lyrics, the singer reflects on how he thought that money and success would bring him fulfillment, but he has now realized that there is more to life than just chasing material goals.

In the first verse, the singer describes how he has "got just exactly what he wants," but he feels a sense of emptiness and disconnection from the things he has achieved. He admits that he will try to have fun and find fulfillment at some other time, because he is currently too focused on his goals and ambitions.

The chorus repeats the line "I'm losing my mind," emphasizing the feeling of being overwhelmed and overworked. The singer admits that he has so much to do and not enough time, and that he is losing his mind because of the pressure and stress of trying to achieve everything he wants.

In the second verse, the singer apologizes to the person he is singing to, and admits that he has not been able to give them the attention and support they deserve. He says that he will think about them and their relationship at some other time, when he is not so consumed by his goals and ambitions.

The song's chorus repeats the idea that the singer is losing his mind because of the pressure and stress of trying to achieve everything he wants. He admits that he has been making things harder for himself by not taking the easy road, and that he is now feeling the consequences of his choices.

Overall, "Losing My Mind" is a powerful and relatable song that explores the struggles of trying to find balance and happiness in a demanding and fast-paced world. The singer's emotive and impassioned vocals add to the intensity of the song, making it a compelling and moving listen.
