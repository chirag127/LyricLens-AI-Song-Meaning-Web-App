The song "Loser" by Charlie Puth is about a person who is regretting losing their significant other and feeling like a loser because of it. In the lyrics, the person expresses their sadness and longing for their ex, saying that they will never recover from losing them. They also mention trying to distract themselves from their thoughts of their ex by getting drunk and avoiding sleep, but still can't escape their feelings.

In the chorus, the person repeats the line "Oh, I'm such a loser" as they reflect on how they lost the love of their life and how they can't believe they let them go. They express their belief that they must have been out of their mind to let their significant other go, and they know they'll never be able to recover from the loss.

Overall, the song is a heart-wrenching and relatable portrayal of the pain and regret that comes with losing a loved one. The lyrics are emotionally raw and evocative, and the melody is haunting and memorable.
