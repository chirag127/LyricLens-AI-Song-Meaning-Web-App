"Cheating on You" is a song about regret and longing after a breakup. The lyrics describe a relationship that started off casually but eventually became serious. The narrator expresses regret for ending the relationship and admits that they miss their ex. Despite trying to move on and date someone new, the narrator can't shake the feeling that they are cheating on their ex whenever they touch the new person.

They mention that the relationship wasn't serious at first but eventually they caught feelings and things got "real." However, the narrator admits that they thought the relationship was too good to be true and they ended it.

The chorus expresses the narrator's regret for ending the relationship and their longing for their ex. They wonder if they can still make their way back to their ex and fix things. In the second verse, the narrator reflects on their cocky attitude and their belief that they could get their ex back at any time. They regret not realizing how much they loved their ex and the hurt they caused by ending the relationship. The narrator mentions missing their ex "bad" and realizing that they don't know what they had until it's gone.

The final chorus repeats the sentiments of the first chorus, expressing the narrator's regret and longing for their ex. Despite trying to move on and date someone new, the narrator can't shake the feeling that they are cheating on their ex. They admit that no one else's lips compare to their ex's and they can't move on.

Overall, "Cheating on You" is a powerful and emotional song about regret and longing after a breakup.
