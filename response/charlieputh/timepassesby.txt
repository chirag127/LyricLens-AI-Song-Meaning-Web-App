The song "Time Passes By" by Charlie Puth is a melancholy ballad in which the singer reflects on the passing of time in a relationship. The lyrics describe the singer's loneliness and longing for his partner, as well as his regret at letting their relationship slip away.

Musically, the song has a haunting and somber feel, with a simple piano accompaniment and Puth's emotive vocals taking center stage. The lyrics are introspective and relatable, capturing the feeling of longing and regret that comes with the end of a relationship.

Overall, "Time Passes By" is a poignant and well-written ballad that showcases Puth's ability to craft emotional and introspective songs. It is a powerful addition to his repertoire and a testament to his talent as a singer and songwriter.
