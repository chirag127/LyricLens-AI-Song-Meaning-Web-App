"I Don't Wanna Hurt You Baby" by Charlie Puth is a song about a relationship that has been on and off for a while. The singer is expressing his feelings about not wanting to keep hurting his partner, and how it's best for both of them to move on.

In the first verse, the singer talks about seeing his partner with all of her friends, and how he remembers what she said to him in the past. He also mentions feeling okay about the ups and downs in their relationship, but he can't help coming back to her.

The chorus repeats the sentiment of not wanting to hurt his partner anymore, and how it's best that they move on from each other.

In the second verse, the singer admits to making mistakes in the relationship and acknowledges that staying together isn't good for either of them. He also expresses his regret for hurting his partner and causing her pain.

The song ends with the singer repeating the chorus, emphasizing his desire to end the relationship in order to stop causing his partner any more hurt.

Overall, "I Don't Wanna Hurt You Baby" is a heartfelt and emotional song about the difficult decision to end a relationship in order to protect the other person. Charlie Puth's smooth vocals and catchy melody make it a standout track.
