"River" by Charlie Puth is a song about a relationship that is on the brink of ending. The singer is pleading with his partner not to give up on their love and to let it flow like a river. In the song, the singer describes his partner as playing cool and pushing him away, but he assures her that he is not going to give up on their love. He encourages her to let go of her fears and to fall in love with him again, just like a river would flow.

Throughout the song, the singer uses the metaphor of a river to represent their relationship. He compares the ups and downs of their relationship to the ebb and flow of a river. He also uses the imagery of a river to describe how he feels about his partner, saying that she is the home for his heart and that she should not be running away from their love.

Overall, "River" is a beautiful and emotional love song that captures the feelings of someone who is trying to hold on to a relationship that is on the brink of ending. The use of the river metaphor adds a poetic and powerful element to the song, making it a standout track on Charlie Puth's album.
