"Dangerously" by Charlie Puth is a song about the risks and consequences of being in a passionate and intense relationship. In the lyrics, the singer reflects on the pain and heartache that comes from loving someone too much and ignoring the warning signs that the relationship was not healthy.

In the first verse, the singer admits that he blames himself for the pain and hurt he is feeling because he ignored the truth about the relationship and allowed himself to be "drunk off that love." He says that there is no forgetting the person he loved, and that they have left a lasting impact on him.

The chorus repeats the line "I loved you dangerously," emphasizing the intensity and recklessness of the singer's feelings for the person he was in a relationship with. He loved them more than the air he breathed, and he was willing to risk everything for them, even if it meant crashing and being ruined by the explosion of their passion.

In the second verse, the singer describes how the dynamic of the relationship shifted, and how the person he loved became the one with the power, while he was left vulnerable and exposed. He describes being tied to the track as a train approaches, symbolizing the feeling of being helpless and unable to escape the destructive force of the relationship.

The song's chorus repeats the idea that the singer loved the person he was in a relationship with dangerously, and that he was willing to risk everything for them. He admits that he ignored the warning signs and the consequences of the relationship, and that he is now left with nothing because of the explosion of their passion.

Overall, "Dangerously" is a powerful and emotive song that explores the risks and consequences of being in a passionate and intense relationship. The singer's raw and emotional vocals add to the intensity of the song, making it a compelling and moving listen.
