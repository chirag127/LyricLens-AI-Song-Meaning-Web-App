"My Gospel" is a song by Charlie Puth, in which he sings about the lengths he would go to in order to win over the love of someone he is deeply infatuated with. In the song, Puth mentions various scenarios in which he would be willing to do anything, even if it means appearing crazy or reckless, just to show this person how much he loves them. For example, he sings about stealing a ski mask and robbing a bank, burning money on someone's front lawn, and singing karaoke in the rain.

The song is a declaration of love and commitment, with Puth singing about how he is not afraid to be vulnerable or to make a fool of himself in order to make the person he loves happy. The lyrics are raw and emotional, and the song's catchy chorus makes it easy to sing along to. Overall, "My Gospel" is a fun and upbeat love song that captures the intensity of being deeply in love.
