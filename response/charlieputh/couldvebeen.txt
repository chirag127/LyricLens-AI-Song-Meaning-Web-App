"Could've Been" is a song about missed opportunities and regret in a relationship. The lyrics describe a relationship that didn't work out, despite the narrator's feelings for their partner. The narrator reflects on how things could have been different and wonders what their partner's true feelings were. Despite the pain of their failed relationship, the narrator knows that they can't dwell on what could have been.

In the first verse, the narrator expresses confusion and uncertainty about their relationship. They ask for answers and wonder if their relationship was ever the right answer. Despite telling themselves that their relationship wasn't meant to be, the narrator admits that they still wonder how their partner felt about them.

The chorus describes the narrator's regret and longing for their ex. They think about their ex when they are alone and admit that the part of them that cared is gone. Despite their feelings, the narrator knows that they can't dwell on their failed relationship and get caught up in the past.

The second verse repeats the sentiments of the first verse, expressing the narrator's regret and longing for their ex. They admit that they tried to pretend that their relationship was still working, but deep down they knew it wasn't. The narrator wonders if their ex still dreams and thinks about what their relationship could have been.

Overall, "Could've Been" is a poignant and emotional song about regret and missed opportunities in a relationship. The lyrics are relatable and capture the complex feelings that can arise after a relationship ends. The song is a reminder that sometimes we can't change the past and we must move on from failed relationships.
