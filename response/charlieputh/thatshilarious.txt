"That's Hilarious" by Charlie Puth is a song about a man who is confronted by his ex-girlfriend who is trying to win him back. In the song, the man reflects on the pain and heartache his ex caused him during their relationship, and how she didn't appreciate him while they were together. Now, he sees her attempts to win him back as "hilarious" and is not interested in getting back together with her.

In the first verse, the man talks about how the tables have turned in their relationship and how his ex is now the one reaching out to him. He also mentions how she is "only making it worse" by continuing to call him even though he has made it clear that he is not interested in getting back together.

In the second verse, the man talks about how his ex's behavior has been a "lesson" for him, and how he won't be giving his heart to someone who still has a "broken" one. He also reflects on the fact that he has lost a year of his life to their failed relationship and can never get that time back.

In the chorus, the man expresses his disbelief at his ex's audacity to think that he would still want her after everything she put him through. He sees her attempts to win him back as "hilarious" and is not interested in giving her a second chance.

Overall, "That's Hilarious" is a song about moving on from a failed relationship and not allowing oneself to be manipulated by an ex who didn't appreciate them while they were together.
