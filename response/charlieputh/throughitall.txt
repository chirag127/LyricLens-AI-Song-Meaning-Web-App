"Through It All" is a song by Charlie Puth that reflects on his life and experiences. The song begins with Puth acknowledging that he has lived a "crazy" life and that it has made him question his faith. He then reflects on the passage of time and how quickly it has gone by.

In the chorus, Puth sings about the challenges he has faced in his life, including heartbreak and falling down. Despite these challenges, he has always gotten back up and continued on his own path. He asserts that he has been through it all and won't be seen crying if something were to happen to him.

The second verse of the song delves deeper into Puth's experiences growing up in a "wild city" where he had to fight to survive. He mentions that this has made him fearless and that his recklessness has sometimes gotten him into trouble. However, he doesn't regret living his life the way he has.

Overall, "Through It All" is a reflective and empowering song about overcoming challenges and living life on one's own terms. It showcases Puth's strong vocals and catchy melodies, making it a solid addition to his discography.
