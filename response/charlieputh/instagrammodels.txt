The song "Instagram Models" by Charlie Puth is a reflection on the singer's experiences with superficial and unreliable relationships. The lyrics describe a scenario in which the singer is drawn to an "instagram model" who is only interested in superficial connections and leads him on. The singer expresses frustration with this person and their behavior, and ultimately decides to move on from the situation.

The song uses a catchy beat and catchy hooks to convey its message, and Puth's vocals are strong and emotive. Overall, the song is a relatable and engaging reflection on the challenges of modern dating and the pitfalls of superficial connections. It serves as a cautionary tale for those who may be drawn to the glitz and glamour of the Instagram world, and encourages listeners to seek out meaningful and authentic connections instead.
