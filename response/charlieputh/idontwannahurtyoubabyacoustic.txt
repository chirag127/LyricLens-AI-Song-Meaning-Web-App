The song "I Don't Wanna Hurt You Baby (Acoustic)" by Charlie Puth is a ballad in which the singer expresses his reluctance to continue hurting his romantic partner. The lyrics suggest that the relationship has been tumultuous and that the singer has caused the partner pain in the past. However, the singer also expresses a desire to move on and end the cycle of hurting each other.

In the chorus, the singer repeats the line "I don't wanna hurt you baby" to emphasize his sincere desire to end the relationship on a positive note. The acoustic arrangement of the song adds to its emotional tone, with Puth's vocals taking center stage.

Overall, the song is a heartfelt and relatable portrayal of the difficulty of ending a toxic relationship. It showcases Puth's ability to craft emotional and introspective lyrics and deliver them with raw vulnerability.
