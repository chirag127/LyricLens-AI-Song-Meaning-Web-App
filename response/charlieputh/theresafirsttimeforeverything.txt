"There's a First Time for Everything" is a song by Charlie Puth that reflects on a person's first experience with love and the lasting impact it can have. In the first verse, Puth describes feeling hesitant to get close to someone he is attracted to, but ultimately succumbing to the temptation. In the chorus, he expresses surprise at how much he misses and wants to be with this person, even though it is their first time being in love.

In the second verse, Puth describes meeting this person and spending time with them, leading to a defining moment that changes their relationship. He notes that even after they go their separate ways, he can't forget about them and the feelings they had for each other.

Overall, the song is a nostalgic and emotional reflection on the power of first love and the way it can change a person. It is a relatable and well-written song that showcases Puth's vocal ability and songwriting skills.
