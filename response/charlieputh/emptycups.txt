"Empty Cups" by Charlie Puth is a song about two people who want to be alone together at a party. The lyrics describe the feelings of excitement and anticipation that come with the possibility of a secret rendezvous. The singer expresses his desire to slip away from the party with the girl he's interested in and spend some time together in private. He sings about how they can dance and get close to each other without anyone noticing.

The song has a catchy beat and the lyrics are fun and playful. The chorus, with its repeated refrain of "Oh oh oh oh" is particularly catchy and easy to sing along to. Overall, "Empty Cups" is a lighthearted and enjoyable song that captures the feeling of being young and carefree at a party.
