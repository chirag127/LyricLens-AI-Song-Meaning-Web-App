"How Long" by Charlie Puth is a song about a man who has been caught cheating on his girlfriend. In the song, the narrator admits that he was wrong and tries to explain his actions to his girlfriend. She is hurt and wants to know how long the cheating has been going on, and the narrator is forced to face the consequences of his actions.

In the first verse, the narrator admits that he was wrong and tries to explain that he was drunk and not thinking clearly. He tells his girlfriend that there were no feelings involved and promises that it only happened once.

In the second verse, the girlfriend confronts the narrator and asks him if the cheating was real or just for show. She tells him to save his apologies and says that she just wants to know the truth.

In the chorus, the girlfriend repeats her question: "How long has this been going on?" She accuses the narrator of "creeping 'round" on her and acting shady, and says that she has been feeling it lately.

Overall, the song is a heartfelt and emotional depiction of the aftermath of infidelity. The narrator expresses regret and remorse, while the girlfriend struggles to come to terms with the betrayal. The song is relatable and captures the complex emotions involved in a situation like this.
