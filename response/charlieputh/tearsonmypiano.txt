"Tears on My Piano" is a song by Charlie Puth in which he expresses his heartache and sadness over a past relationship. In the bridge, Puth admits that he knows who his ex is and that he won't say their name, but he just wants them to feel the same heartache that he does. He admits that every song he writes is about wanting them back and that it's "so fucking sad."
