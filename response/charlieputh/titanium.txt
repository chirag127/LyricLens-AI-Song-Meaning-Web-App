The song "Titanium" by Charlie Puth is about resilience and strength in the face of adversity. The lyrics describe a situation where the singer is being criticized or attacked, but they remain unshaken and refuse to be brought down. The lyrics also refer to the idea of being "bulletproof" and having "nothing to lose," suggesting that the singer is unafraid of the challenges they face.

In the first verse, the singer describes their opponents as shouting, but their words are inaudible. The singer responds by talking loudly and confidently, but not saying much, indicating that they are not interested in engaging with their attackers. The second verse continues this theme, with the singer being "cut down" and their opponents "having further to fall." This suggests that the singer is unafraid of the consequences of standing up to their attackers, and that they believe they will ultimately come out on top.

The chorus repeats the phrase "I am titanium," emphasizing the singer's unshakeable strength and resilience. This phrase is also reinforced by the lyrics "you shoot me down, but I won't fall," which imply that no matter what challenges the singer faces, they will remain standing.

Overall, "Titanium" is a powerful and uplifting song that celebrates resilience and strength in the face of adversity. The lyrics are clear and straightforward, and the chorus is particularly memorable and catchy. Overall, the song is a positive and inspiring anthem for anyone who has ever felt like they were being held back or attacked.
