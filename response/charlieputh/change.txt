"Change" by Charlie Puth is a song about the need for unity and understanding among people. The song begins with Puth asking why people look down on each other and why there is not enough love to go around. He then goes on to lament the fact that people are often divided and argues that the world would be a better place if everyone could just get along and treat each other with love and respect.

Throughout the song, Puth repeats the refrain "Why can't we just get along?" and implores listeners to make a change in their own lives and in the world. He argues that loving one another is not wrong and that it is necessary for people to come together and understand each other in order to make the world a better place.

In terms of its musical style, "Change" is a pop-infused ballad with a catchy melody and a driving beat. Puth's emotive vocals and powerful lyrics make for a compelling and thought-provoking listening experience.

Overall, "Change" is a timely and important message that encourages listeners to strive for unity and understanding in a world that can often be divided and hostile. Puth's powerful vocals and thought-provoking lyrics make for a compelling and moving listening experience.
