"Smells Like Me" by Charlie Puth is a song about a relationship that has ended and the lingering effects that the past relationship has on the person. In the first few lines, the singer talks about how his ex-partner still wears his cologne and uses his laundry detergent, indicating that she is holding on to the memories of their relationship. He then questions if she ever thinks of him while she is with her new partner.

In the chorus, the singer expresses his hope that the memories of their relationship are painful for his ex and that she is unable to forget about him. He also wonders if she feels the same way with her new partner as she did with him.

The second verse continues on the theme of the lingering effects of the past relationship, with the singer mentioning that his ex still uses the same candles that he did and questions why she hasn't moved on yet.

Overall, the song is a heartbreak ballad that explores the emotions and feelings of a person after a relationship ends. The song is well-written and relatable, making it a great listen for anyone who has experienced the pain of a breakup.
