In "Hard On Yourself," Charlie Puth sings about a person who is struggling and is hard on themselves. The lyrics suggest that the person is comparing themselves to others and trying to be someone else. Puth encourages them to find love again and to remember that they are perfect just the way they are. The song is a reminder to be kind to oneself and to not compare oneself to others. It is a powerful message about self-acceptance and self-love.

In terms of the song's review, it is a well-written and relatable track with a catchy melody and Puth's signature soulful vocals. The lyrics are relatable and the message is powerful, making it a standout track on Puth's album. Overall, "Hard On Yourself" is a strong and emotional song that will resonate with many listeners.
