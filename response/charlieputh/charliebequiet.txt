"Charlie Be Quiet!" is a song about the fear of falling in love and being rejected. In the song, the narrator is telling someone named Charlie to be quiet because they are in love with someone who doesn't reciprocate their feelings. The narrator is worried that if the object of their affection finds out how they feel, they will run away.

The lyrics suggest that the narrator has been hurt before and is hesitant to open up and be vulnerable again. They are trying to play it cool and not reveal their true feelings, but the feelings are getting stronger despite their attempts to suppress them.

Overall, the song is a relatable and emotional take on the fear of falling in love and being rejected. The chorus is catchy and the lyrics are well-written, making for a strong and enjoyable track.
