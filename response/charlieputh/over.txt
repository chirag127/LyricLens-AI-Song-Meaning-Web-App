"Over" by Charlie Puth is a heartbreak ballad about the pain of a breakup and the singer's hope that their relationship is not truly over. In the first verse, Puth describes seeing his partner crying and feeling devastated by their sadness. He reflects on happier times they shared, when they would lie beneath the stars and feel like their love was unstoppable.

The chorus repeats the refrain "It's not over," expressing Puth's determination to keep fighting for their relationship, despite the difficult situation. He pleads with his partner to not give up on them and to come back to him.

In the second verse, Puth expresses his heartache and the feeling of being all alone without his partner. He repeats that his heart belongs to them and begs them not to forget about him. The song ends with the line "Letting you go hurt me," emphasizing the emotional pain of the breakup.

Overall, "Over" is a powerful and emotional ballad that captures the feeling of heartache and the hope of reconciliation after a breakup. Puth's heartfelt vocals and relatable lyrics make it a poignant and moving song that will resonate with many listeners who have experienced similar struggles in their own relationships.
