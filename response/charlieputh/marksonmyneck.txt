"Marks On My Neck" by Charlie Puth is a song about the aftermath of a break-up. The protagonist laments the physical and emotional scars left behind after their partner let them go. Despite trying to move on and cover up the pain, they are unable to forget the hurt and the lingering presence of their ex. The song is a relatable and poignant depiction of heartbreak and the difficulty of moving on. The use of physical marks on the neck as a metaphor for the lingering effects of the relationship adds a unique and striking element to the song. Overall, the song is a strong and emotive exploration of the aftermath of a break-up.
