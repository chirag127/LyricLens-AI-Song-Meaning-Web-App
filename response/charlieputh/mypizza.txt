The song "My Pizza" by Charlie Puth is a humorous and light-hearted track that celebrates the joy of finally getting to eat a well-deserved pizza after a long wait. The song begins with Puth singing about how he was the only one at the dinner table who wasn't hungry, and how his anticipation for his pizza order to arrive is growing. In the chorus, Puth sings about how he is not sharing his pizza with anyone and how he has waited for so long to enjoy it. In the second verse, he playfully sings about not wanting to eat the vegetables on his pizza, and how he is so excited to eat his pizza that he might even throw up. Overall, "My Pizza" is a fun and catchy song that celebrates the simple pleasures in life, like enjoying a delicious pizza.
