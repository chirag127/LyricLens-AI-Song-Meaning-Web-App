The song "One Call Away (Remix)" by Charlie Puth is a ballad about the singer's willingness to be there for someone whenever they need him. In the first verse, Puth sings "I'm only one call away / I'll be there to save the day / Superman got nothing on me / I'm only one call away," emphasizing that he is always available and ready to help whenever his friend needs him. In the chorus, Puth encourages the listener to reach out to him and not be afraid, telling them that they are not alone.

In the second verse, guest artist Tyga adds a rap verse, in which he talks about how he and the person he is singing to have been through tough times together, but have always stuck by each other. He also mentions that he is only a phone call away, just like Puth.

Overall, the song is a heartwarming ode to friendship and the comfort of knowing that someone is always there for you. The catchy melody and uplifting lyrics make it a feel-good, empowering track that is sure to resonate with many listeners.
