"Left and Right" is a song by Charlie Puth that explores the lingering memories of a past relationship. The song starts by describing how memories of the ex-partner follow the narrator everywhere they go, and how they can still feel their presence in every corner of their mind. The song continues by discussing how the narrator's dreams are all the same, filled with memories of the past relationship. The chorus repeats the line, "memories follow me left and right," emphasizing the narrator's inability to escape these memories.

Overall, the song is a relatable and emotional exploration of the lingering effects of a past relationship. Puth's smooth vocals and catchy melodies make the song enjoyable to listen to, and the lyrics capture the feelings of longing and nostalgia that often come with a break-up. The song is a great example of Puth's ability to craft relatable and emotionally impactful pop songs.
