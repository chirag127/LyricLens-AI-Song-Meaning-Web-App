"Left And Right" is a pop/R&B song by American singer Charlie Puth. The song is about the memories of a past relationship that continue to haunt the narrator, even after the person has left. The song is about the difficulty of moving on from a break-up and the longing for the person who got away.

In the first verse, Charlie Puth sings about how memories of the person follow him everywhere and take up every corner of his mind. He reflects on the pain of the break-up and the inability to forget the person. He also talks about how he tries to distract himself with alcohol and focusing on himself, but the memories still remain.

In the second verse, Charlie Puth talks about how the person was the one that got away and how he is still not okay after the break-up. He also talks about how his dreams are still about the person, even when he closes his eyes.

The chorus repeats the line "Memories follow me left and right" and talks about how the person's love stays with him day and night.

The song has a emotional and longing tone, with a catchy beat and relatable lyrics. Overall, "Left And Right" is a powerful and honest exploration of the difficulty of moving on from a break-up.
