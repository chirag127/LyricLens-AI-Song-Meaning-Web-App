"As You Are" is a song by Charlie Puth that expresses his love and dedication to his partner, despite their past mistakes. In the first verse, Puth reflects on the early days of their relationship when they were happy and in love. However, he admits to letting his partner down at some point. In the chorus, he promises to always love and accept them as they are, without judging them.

In the second verse, Puth emphasizes the depth of their connection, stating that his partner knows him inside and out. He thanks them for their love and forgiveness, and declares that he would climb Mount Kilimanjaro a thousand times for them. In the final verse, featuring Shy Carter, Puth reaffirms his commitment to their relationship and declares that he is devoted to his partner.

Overall, "As You Are" is a love song that showcases the strength and resilience of a relationship, even after facing challenges. Puth's vocals and the song's catchy melody make for a heartfelt and emotional listening experience.
