The song "Two Months" by Charlie Puth is about a relationship that has ended after only two months. The narrator misses the good times they had together, but also feels angry and betrayed by their partner's behavior. They talk about the other person's success and self-absorption, and wonder why they ever fell for them in the first place. The song has a sad and bitter tone, reflecting the narrator's feelings of regret and hurt. Overall, the song is a poignant exploration of the aftermath of a short-lived relationship.
