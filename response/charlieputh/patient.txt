"Patient" by Charlie Puth is a heartfelt ballad about a person asking for their partner's patience and understanding. The song's lyrics describe the mistakes the singer has made in their relationship and their desire to make things right. The singer admits to being immature and causing their partner to feel insecure, but pleads for a chance to change their behavior and make things work.

The song's chorus includes the repeated refrain, "Please be patient with me," as the singer asks for their partner to give them another chance. The song also includes the lines, "Is it too much that I'm asking for/Can we start over, can we end this war," which show the singer's willingness to put in the work to fix their relationship.

Overall, "Patient" is a vulnerable and emotional song that showcases the singer's remorse and determination to make things right. The song's simple, stripped-down production allows the focus to remain on the singer's heartfelt vocals and the emotional weight of the lyrics.
