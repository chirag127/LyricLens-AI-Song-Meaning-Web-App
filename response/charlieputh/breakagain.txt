"Break Again" is a song about heartbreak and the pain of losing someone you love. The lyrics describe the narrator crying and feeling like they are dying after their relationship ends. They express their frustration that the person they loved is no longer there and they don't know if they can move on. The narrator also expresses their anger and disappointment that the person they loved never changed and continued to hurt them.

In the first verse, the narrator describes their feelings of sadness and despair after their relationship ends. They mention trying to stand by their partner, but the relationship was over before it could really begin. The narrator also mentions trying to convince themselves that everything will be alright and that their ex will eventually come back to them.

The chorus describes the narrator's desire to move on from their ex and the pain they feel. They don't want to hear love songs or see their ex because they can't afford to have their heart broken again. They express their disappointment that their ex never changed and continue to hurt them.

In the second verse, the narrator describes the impact their heartbreak has had on their daily life. They sleep their life away to avoid thinking about their ex and the pain they feel. The narrator also expresses their confusion and uncertainty about how to move on and feel happy again.

The final chorus repeats the sentiment of the first chorus, expressing the narrator's determination to move on and not let their ex hurt them again. They declare that they have had enough and can't let their ex break their heart again.

Overall, "Break Again" is a powerful and emotional song about the pain of heartbreak and the struggle to move on from a lost love. The lyrics are relatable and capture the raw emotions of someone going through a difficult breakup. The song is a reminder that healing from heartbreak is possible, even though it may take time and effort.
