"Mother" is a song by Charlie Puth, in which he sings about a secret relationship with a girl whose mother doesn't approve of their relationship. The song describes how the couple sneaks around and tries to keep their relationship a secret from the girl's mother. The narrator expresses his fear that if the mother found out about their relationship, she would keep them apart.

In the song, the narrator also hints at the possibility of the girl's father being upset and potentially wanting to harm him if he found out about their relationship. The song speaks to the feelings of fear and excitement that come with being in a secret relationship.

Overall, "Mother" is a catchy and relatable song that captures the thrill of forbidden love. The song's upbeat tempo and catchy chorus make it a fun and enjoyable listen.
