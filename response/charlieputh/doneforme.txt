"Done For Me" by Charlie Puth is a song about a relationship where one person feels like they are doing everything for the other person, but not getting the same level of effort in return. In the first verse, Puth sings about the other person wanting to leave and finding someone else, but he won't beg them to stay or apologize for something he didn't do. In the chorus, Puth and guest artist Kehlani both sing about the things they have done for their partner and ask them to tell them what they've done in return.

The song has a catchy beat and a relatable message about the give and take in relationships. The chorus is particularly memorable with its repetition of the line "tell me what you've done for me." Overall, it's a well-written and performed song that captures the frustration and disappointment of feeling unappreciated in a relationship.
