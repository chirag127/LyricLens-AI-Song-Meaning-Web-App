"I Don't Think That I Like Her" is a song about a man who has had a change of heart about a girl he was previously interested in. The song begins with him pursuing her and finding common ground, but as the relationship progresses, he realizes that she is not the right fit for him. The chorus repeats the line "I don't think that I like her anymore" and explains that all girls are the same and only want to break his heart. The song is a reflection on a failed relationship and the disappointment that comes with it. The lyrics are relatable and the melody is catchy, making for a well-written pop song. Overall, the song is a solid entry in Charlie Puth's discography.
