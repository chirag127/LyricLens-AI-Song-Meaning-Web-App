"Up All Night" by Charlie Puth is a song about a tumultuous relationship and the rollercoaster of emotions that come with it. The narrator is unable to shake the other person from their mind, despite trying to move on and "shake them out" of their head. The other person has a powerful hold over the narrator, and the narrator finds themselves drawn back in despite the games and mixed signals the other person plays. The chorus repeats the line "it's a crime" and "up all night" to emphasize the tumultuous nature of the relationship. The song is well-written and relatable, with a catchy chorus and captivating vocals from Charlie Puth. Overall, it is a solid addition to Charlie Puth's discography.
