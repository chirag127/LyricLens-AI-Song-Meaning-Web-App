"Attention (Remix)" by Charlie Puth is a song about a person who is seeking attention from their ex-partner. The song features rapper KYLE, who adds a verse about his own experiences with a person who is constantly seeking attention.

The first verse, sung by Puth, accuses the ex of trying to ruin their reputation and showing up at parties in order to try and get their attention. The chorus repeats the main theme of the song, with the narrator stating that their ex "just want[s] attention."

In the second verse, KYLE adds his own perspective on the situation, saying that the person seeking attention is "searchin' for attention in my mentions / Like talking shit's the only way to get some." He also states that he is not interested in giving them any of his attention.

The song has a catchy melody and the lyrics are well-written, making it a relatable anthem for moving on from an ex who is only interested in getting attention. The addition of KYLE's verse adds a fresh perspective to the song and makes it stand out from other similar tracks. Overall, it is a great song to listen to if you are dealing with a similar situation in your own life.
