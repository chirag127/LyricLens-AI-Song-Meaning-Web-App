Love on Top by Beyoncé is an anthem of self-love and self-confidence that encourages listeners to take control of their lives and trust their own judgement. The song is about a woman who has been through difficult times but is still able to find joy and contentment in her own life. The song has a strong rhythm and catchy melody that is sure to get stuck in your head. 

The verse-by-verse breakdown of the lyrics starts with the introduction, "Bring the beat in / Love on top on top on top on top / Ooh love on top on top on top on top / Ooh love on top on top on top." This sets up the theme of the song, with Beyoncé asking her listeners to embrace their love and the power within.

The first verse deals with the narrator's journey to finding self-love. She speaks of seeing the stars and feeling the sun, reflecting on how she can feel the warmth of love despite the difficult times in her life. She mentions fighting tears and coming out strong and confident, knowing that her partner has put her first.

The chorus then reveals the narrator's newfound confidence, as she sings of how her partner has made everything stop and put her love on top. She encourages her listeners to love themselves and trust their own judgement. 

The second verse reflects on the narrator's journey and how it has changed her life for the better. She speaks of feeling the wind and dancing in the night, feeling empowered by her partner's love and support. 

The refrain then repeats the chorus, reminding listeners to always put their love on top.

In summary, Love on Top is an empowering song that encourages listeners to take control of their lives and trust their own judgement. Through its catchy melody and strong rhythm, the song speaks of self-love and resilience, and how even in the darkest of times, love can still be found.
