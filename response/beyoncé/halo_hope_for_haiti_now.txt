The song “Halo (Hope for Haiti Now)” by Beyoncé is a powerful and emotional ballad that speaks of hope, salvation, and a brighter future. The song ends with the chorus “Everywhere we lookin’ now I’m surrounded by your embrace/Haiti, we can feel your halo/You know you’re my savin’ grace”, which serves as an uplifting reminder of the power of love and hope.

The song’s message of hope and salvation is timeless and powerful, and reminds us that even in the darkest of times, there is always light.
