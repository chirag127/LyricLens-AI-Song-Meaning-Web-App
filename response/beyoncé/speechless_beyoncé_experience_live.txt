Beyoncé's song "Speechless" is a heartfelt, passionate love song about being so deeply in love that words are not enough to express one's feelings. The song starts with Beyoncé singing “You’ve got me, you’ve got me, you’ve got me, you’ve got me, you’ve got me, you’ve got me, you’ve got me… speechless”, setting the tone of the song and illustrating the feeling of being lost for words. The lyrics go on to describe the anticipation of waiting for one's lover to arrive and the feeling of being in the presence of the one they love. 

The chorus, “You’ve got me, you’ve got me, you’ve got me, you’ve got me, woh ooh oh, you’ve got me, you’ve got me, you’ve got me speechless”, is repeated throughout the song, emphasizing the feeling of being lost for words due to the intensity of the love shared between the two. The verses then describe the pleasure and intimacy of being in the embrace of one's lover, and how their love drives them crazy. 

The song overall conveys a strong sense of intimacy and passion, as well as a feeling of being overwhelmed by love and unable to express it in words. Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals emotions of passion, excitement, and desire, as well as a strong sense of longing and anticipation. Additionally, the use of repetition in the chorus serves to emphasize the feeling of being speechless and unable to express one's feelings. 

In summary, Beyoncé's song "Speechless" is a passionate and heartfelt love song that conveys the feeling of being overwhelmed by love and unable to express it in words. The lyrics contain emotions of passion, excitement, and desire, as well as a strong sense of longing and anticipation. The use of repetition in the chorus emphasizes the feeling of being speechless and unable to express one's feelings.
