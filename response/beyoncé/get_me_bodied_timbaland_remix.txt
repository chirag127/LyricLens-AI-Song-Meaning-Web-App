The chorus of the song is the most memorable part of the track. Beyoncé sings about wanting to be herself and let it out, asking her partner to get her bodied. The chorus is repeated twice more in the song and is the main hook of the track.

The bridge of the song brings a different energy to the track, introducing a more relaxed feeling. Beyoncé sings about coolin’ off and doing an old school dance while she “winds it back”. The outro of the song brings the energy back up as Beyoncé sings about touching each other’s bodies and getting bodied. 

In summary, “Get Me Bodied” (Timbaland Remix) is a high energy, empowering song that encourages listeners to let loose and have fun on the dancefloor.
