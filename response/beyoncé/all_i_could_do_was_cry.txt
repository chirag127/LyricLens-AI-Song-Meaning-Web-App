The song “All I Could Do Was Cry” by Beyoncé is a heartbreaking ballad about a woman’s pain and anguish as she watches her former lover marry another woman. The song begins with the narrator hearing church bells ringing and a choir singing, a traditional sign of a wedding. The narrator then sees her former lover walk down the aisle with another woman and place a ring on her finger. This is the moment where the narrator realizes that her love is gone and the pain sets in. 

The chorus of the song is a simple yet powerful line: “All I could do was cry…I was losing the man that I love, and all I could do was cry.” This line serves to highlight the narrator’s anguish and despair, as she is unable to do anything but cry at the loss of her former love. 

The second verse of the song speaks of the end of the wedding, with the rice being thrown over the couple’s heads and their life beginning while the narrator’s life is ending. This serves to further emphasize the narrator’s heartbreak and pain.
