The song "Check on It" by Beyoncé features lyrics that focus on self-confidence, sexuality, and the power of choice. The song starts off with a verse from Swizz Beatz and Slim Thug, who both rap about the importance of being independent and not playing around with “clowns and wankstas”. They go on to encourage the listener to take control of their life and make their own decisions, emphasizing the importance of being assertive and confident. 

Beyoncé then takes center stage and continues this theme of self-assuredness and empowerment. She speaks to a potential partner, telling them that they should be confident in their own skin, and that she will let them “work up on it”. In this verse, Beyoncé is telling her partner to take the initiative and make the first move, while also reassuring them that it is okay to be confident in their own skin. 

The second verse from Beyoncé further emphasizes the message of self-confidence, as she speaks of taking control of her own life and not allowing others to take advantage of her. She goes on to tell her partner that they must be patient and understanding in order to truly be with her. 

The song then transitions to a verse from Bun B, who speaks about the power of choice and how it should be exercised wisely. He also speaks about the importance of remaining independent and not allowing anyone to control your life. 

The chorus of the song brings everything together as Beyoncé sings about being confident and in control of their own life and destiny. She tells her partner that they should come over and “check up on it”, emphasizing the importance of taking the initiative and being assertive. 

Overall, the song "Check on It" by Beyoncé is a powerful anthem about self-confidence, independence, and the power of choice. Through her lyrics, Beyoncé encourages her listeners to take control of their lives and be confident in their own skin, while also illustrating the importance of being patient and understanding when it comes to relationships. Linguistically, the song is characterized by its use of repetition and alliteration, which serve to emphasize the main themes of the song. Additionally, there is a strong focus on self-affirmation and the power of one’s own decisions, which is further highlighted by the heavy use of repetition and catchy melodies. 

In summary, “Check on It” by Beyoncé is a powerful song about self-confidence and taking control of one’s own destiny. The lyrics are characterized by their focus on self-affirmation and the power of choice, and are further highlighted by the heavy use of repetition, alliteration, and catchy melodies. This song is an inspiring anthem about taking the initiative and being assertive in life and relationships.
