This is followed by a powerful chorus, which brings the song to a close with Beyoncé declaring that her partner's love has her looking so crazy in love.

Linguistically, this song is full of emotion and speaks to the passionate and overwhelming nature of love. Beyoncé's use of repetition and her strong and powerful delivery of the lyrics make the song an emotionally charged one that speaks to the deep and consuming nature of love.
