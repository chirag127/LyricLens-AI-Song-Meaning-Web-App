"Ave Maria" by Beyonce is a breathtakingly beautiful piece of music, which expresses the power of faith and love. The song begins with Beyonce singing about feeling alone, even when surrounded by friends. This is a feeling many of us can relate to, and it is one that Beyonce expresses with her raw and powerful vocals. 

The chorus of the song is a reference to the traditional Catholic prayer, "Ave Maria." The title of the song is symbolic of Beyonce's faith and her hope that her prayer will be heard by a higher power. 

The lyrics of the song express a deep longing for a sense of belonging and acceptance, a hope that even when the lights go out, there will still be someone there to provide comfort and security. This message is further emphasized in the bridge of the song, where Beyonce sings that she will always be able to hear a voice inside, singing "Ave Maria." 

From a linguistic standpoint, "Ave Maria" is an incredibly powerful and emotionally charged song. The lyrics are filled with imagery and symbolism, and Beyonce's passionate delivery serves to further emphasize the emotion and power of the words. 

In summary, "Ave Maria" is an intensely personal and moving piece of music. The song is a powerful expression of faith, love, and longing, and Beyonce's raw and passionate vocals make it an incredibly moving experience.
