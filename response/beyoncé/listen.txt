In the song "Listen" by Beyoncé, the singer is struggling to find her own voice and autonomy after being oppressed and pushed aside by someone she trusted. The song is structured as a call to action, urging the listener to take notice and pay attention to her story. 

The first verse of the song introduces the concept of a "melody" that the singer is trying to start but can't complete. This speaks to her feeling of being unable to express herself properly and of being stifled in her attempts to make her voice heard. The line "listen to the sound from deep within" further emphasizes this feeling of being unable to find the words to express her innermost emotions. 

In the pre-chorus, the singer pleads for her dreams to be heard and for her voice to not be pushed aside or turned into someone else's. This is a direct reference to the way she has been silenced and disregarded by the person she trusted. 

The chorus is the most powerful part of the song. Here, the singer expresses her frustration and anger towards the person who has not been listening to her. She is no longer willing to be taken for granted and expresses her determination to find her own voice and autonomy. 

The second verse speaks to the singer's journey to finding her own voice and strength, as she acknowledges that there was someone deep inside of her that she thought had died a long time ago. This speaks to her ability to persevere through hardships and find the courage to rise up and fight for herself. 

In the bridge, the singer expresses her determination to keep moving forward and find her own path, even if the person she trusted does not listen. This is her way of affirming her autonomy and her commitment to finding her own voice. 

Overall, "Listen" is an empowering and inspiring anthem of self-expression and resilience. It speaks to the importance of being heard and of finding strength and courage to fight for one's own voice and autonomy. Through her lyrics and powerful delivery, Beyoncé encourages listeners to stand up for themselves and to never let anyone silence them. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song’s lyrics are filled with emotional language and imagery that reflects the singer's frustration and determination. Words like "screaming", "believing", and "dreams" help to convey the emotional intensity of the song, while the chorus’s repeated use of "listen" helps to emphasize the singer's need to be heard. The song also contains several references to struggle and overcoming adversity, which speaks to the singer's resilience in the face of her own struggles. 

Overall, "Listen" is an inspirational and empowering song that speaks to the importance of being heard and of finding the courage to fight for one's own autonomy. Through her powerful delivery and emotional lyrics, Beyoncé encourages listeners to stand up for themselves and to never be silenced.
