Diva (Live) by Beyoncé is an empowering song that celebrates female independence and strength. It talks about how a "diva" is a female version of a hustler and promotes the idea that women don't need men to succeed. The lyrics are assertive and confident and are backed up by a strong beat and powerful vocal performance.

Verse-by-Verse Breakdown:

Verse 1: Beyoncé introduces herself as the number one diva in the game, and speaks confidently about her success and wealth.

Verse 2: The chorus is a proclamation of her independence and strength, and she calls on other women to join her in being empowered individuals.

Verse 3: She speaks about pulling up in her car and making sure she has money to spend. This verse promotes the idea of female hustlers and speaks to the need to have money to be successful.

Verse 4: Here, she talks about how other people talk shit and act like they are better than her, but she knows she is the queen.

Verse 5: In this verse, Beyoncé talks about stick-ups and needing bags of money. She talks about how women are getting money and don't need men to be successful.

Verse 6: The last verse is a call to action for women to take their success to the next level and not be held back by anyone.

Summary:

Diva (Live) by Beyoncé is a powerful song that celebrates female independence and strength. Beyoncé speaks out confidently about her success and wealth and encourages other women to do the same. She speaks about hustling and getting money in order to be successful and calls on women to take their success to the next level without being held back.
