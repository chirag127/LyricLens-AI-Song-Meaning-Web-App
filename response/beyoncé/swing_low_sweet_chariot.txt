Swing Low, Sweet Chariot is a song by Beyoncé that speaks of the hope of returning home after a long journey. The song is full of imagery and metaphors, with the lyrics representing a longing for a homecoming. In the song, the narrator is looking for a chariot to bring them home and is surrounded by a band of angels ready to carry them away. 

The song starts with the chorus of “swing low, sweet chariot, comin’ for to carry me home”. This is a reference to the biblical story of Elijah, who was taken up to heaven in a chariot of fire. The lyrics also evoke a sense of hope, as the narrator is looking for a way home and is surrounded by a band of angels coming to bring them home. 

The second verse says “I looked over Jordan and what did I see, comin’ for to carry me home, a band of angels comin’ after me, comin’ for to carry me home”. This is a reference to the Jordan River, which is a biblical symbol for the journey between life and death. The “band of angels” is a metaphor for the hope that the narrator is feeling, as they are surrounded by hope and comfort. 

The third verse says “if you get there before I do, comin’ for to carry me home, tell all my friends I’m comin’ too, comin’ for to carry me home”. This is a plea to the angels to take the narrator home and to bring their friends with them. This speaks to a need for companionship and a longing for a safe place.
