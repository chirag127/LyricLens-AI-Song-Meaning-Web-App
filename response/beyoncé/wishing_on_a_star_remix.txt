In her song “Wishing On A Star (Remix)”, Beyoncé reflects on her journey from poverty and obscurity to becoming a successful, respected artist. Throughout the song, she expresses a deep longing to return to the simpler days of her childhood, and to reconnect with her old friends who were left behind in the hustle of her newfound success.

The first verse of the song sees Beyoncé reminiscing on her past, detailing her humble beginnings with lines such as “no watches to glisten, no V’s with hot systems,” and reminiscing on the days when “we told wonderful lies on how we would live forever.” She acknowledges the challenges of her past, but expresses her determination to “get our shit together and move it along” as she pursues her dreams.

The second verse of the song sees Beyoncé reflecting on the impact of her newfound success, noting that things have changed for her and for her old friends. She acknowledges the fact that she is “too deep for” her old friends, and that the distance between them has grown. However, she expresses her hope that they can still be connected “like Doug E Fresh and Ricky D,” and that they can “reach for the stars now, jigga.”

The final verse of the song is a call to action, as Beyoncé encourages her listeners to join her in her mission to reach the stars, and to “wish on all the rainbows that [they] see.” She also encourages them to “pray[ ] on tomorrow,” expressing her hope that they can all find success, and that they can “wish on all the loving we’ve ever done.”

In summary, “Wishing On A Star (Remix)” is an uplifting song that celebrates the journey from poverty to success, and expresses the hope that all of us can find success if we work hard and remain true to our dreams. The song is a powerful reminder that we should never forget our past, or the people who have helped us along the way.

The lyrical content of the song is both emotionally and socially charged, touching on themes of poverty, success, ambition, and friendship. The song has an uplifting message, expressing optimism for the future and encouraging listeners to strive for greatness. The language style is conversational and informal, with Beyoncé speaking directly to her listeners in a relatable way. 

Overall, “Wishing On A Star (Remix)” is an inspiring and empowering song that is sure to motivate and uplift its listeners. Beyoncé’s powerful lyrics and passionate delivery make this song an excellent addition to her discography.
