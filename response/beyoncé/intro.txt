The song "Intro" by Beyoncé is a powerful and moving introduction to the artist's I Am...World Tour album. The song is an empowering anthem of self-love and self-acceptance. The lyrics of the song serve to create a sense of strength, resilience, and determination.

The bridge of the song serves to bring the message of the whole song full circle. Beyoncé speaks of the courage she has gained from the experience and how she is now ready to take on the world. This serves to emphasize the journey she has taken and her newfound strength and determination.
