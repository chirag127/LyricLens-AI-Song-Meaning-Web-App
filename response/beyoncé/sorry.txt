"Sorry" by Beyoncé is a powerful anthem that speaks to the universal experience of leaving a relationship that no longer serves us. Through the lyrics, Beyoncé expresses her feelings of disappointment, anger, and ultimately, freedom. 

The song starts with Beyoncé declaring that she is “sorry” but not sorry for her decision to leave the relationship. This sets the tone for the rest of the song—an honest and direct expression of her feelings. The chorus emphasizes this point with the line, “middle fingers up, wave it in his face, tell him boy bye.”

The second verse explores the idea of the other person trying to “roll me up” and Beyoncé’s refusal to be drawn back into the relationship. The pre-chorus and chorus then repeat, highlighting the idea that Beyoncé is done with the relationship and not looking back. 

The third verse delves deeper into the idea of the other person trying to make amends and Beyoncé’s refusal to accept them. The pre-chorus and chorus then repeat again, highlighting Beyoncé’s resolve.

The fourth verse examines the idea of the other person trying to make up for the past but Beyoncé’s refusal to accept their apologies. The pre-chorus and chorus then repeat a third time, emphasizing the idea that Beyoncé is done with the relationship. 

The song ends with Beyoncé declaring that she and her “baby” will be alright and that the “big homie better grow up.” This serves as a reminder that Beyoncé is taking control of her life and will no longer be held back by the relationship. 

In summary, "Sorry" is a powerful anthem of self-empowerment and freedom. Through her lyrics, Beyoncé expresses her feelings of disappointment, anger, and ultimately, freedom. The song speaks to the universal experience of leaving a relationship that no longer serves us, and serves as a reminder that we should never be held back by something that doesn’t make us happy.

Linguistically, Beyoncé uses a range of emotion-evoking and powerful words to convey her message, such as “sorry”, “wilding”, and “suicide”. The repetition of the chorus further reinforces the idea that Beyoncé is done with the relationship and is taking control of her life. The song serves as a reminder that we should never be held back by something that doesn’t make us happy.
