In her song "Cards Never Lie," Beyoncé explores the idea that one's destiny is manifest in the cards laid out before them. The song starts with her reflecting on her life, questioning what she was thinking to get to the point she is at now and that her time is unforgiven. She then suggests that one must respect the visions from the cards and that divine intervention can come and snatch up one's dreams from behind. She sings about the power of love and how it can make one feel divine, and she encourages listeners to let people know their dreams and not let any moment slip away. 

The chorus speaks of Beyoncé's reluctance to accept her fate, as she sings "No, no, no, no, I don't wanna, I don't wanna, I don't wanna go," suggesting that she wants to stay and live her dreams. Rah Digga, Carmen, and Wyclef provide commentary, with Rah Digga stating that one should not listen to the cards as they have deceit and Wyclef suggesting that the cards never lie and it is someone else's turn to try to reach her goals before she reaches the sky. 

The song ends with Beyoncé repeating the chorus, reinforcing her idea that she does not want to accept her fate, but rather wants to stay and live her dreams. 

In this song, Beyoncé is reflecting on her own life and experiences, and is challenging the idea that destiny is predetermined and there is no room for change. She is suggesting that one should take control of their own life and that divine intervention can help in achieving one's dreams. Her lyrical style is deeply emotional and reflective, as she speaks of her own experiences and expresses her desire to challenge fate and live her own life. 

The song is overall uplifting and empowering, as it encourages people to take control of their own lives and not be limited by the cards laid out before them. It is a reminder that destiny is not predetermined, and that one can shape their own future if they have the courage to take control of their life. In this way, the song speaks to the power of love and the strength of the individual. 

In conclusion, Beyoncé's song "Cards Never Lie" explores the idea that destiny is not predetermined and encourages one to take control of their own life and not be limited by the cards laid out before them. Through her reflective and emotional lyrical style, Beyoncé speaks to the power of love and the strength of the individual, ultimately creating an uplifting and empowering song.
