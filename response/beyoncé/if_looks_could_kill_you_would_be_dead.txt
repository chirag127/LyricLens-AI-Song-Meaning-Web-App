In Beyoncé's song "If Looks Could Kill (You Would Be Dead)", she talks about her beauty and her ability to attract attention from men. Through her lyrics, Beyoncé paints a vivid picture of herself as an empowered woman who is confident in her own beauty, and uses her looks to her advantage. She speaks of her hair as "like forever" and her eyes as "like the ocean", and proclaims that she is not one to be messed with and that she won't settle for anything less than she deserves. 

The verses are filled with clever wordplay, using double entendres to express her view of her power and her unwillingness to settle for anything less than what she wants. In the chorus, Beyoncé sings, “Yo, I'm tryin' to get with you shorty, but I'm not feelin' you. But I'm an officer shorty, my looks are killin' you. Murder one will get you under the covers, game talker or manslaughter, I ain't feelin' you brothers.” Here, Beyoncé is warning any potential suitors of her power and her unyielding standards.

The song is a fun, upbeat tune that celebrates Beyoncé's confidence and her rejection of anything that does not meet her expectations. The melody is catchy and the lyrics are clever and thought-provoking. Lyrically, the song explores the power that beauty can have and how it can be used to set the standards in a relationship. Musically, the song is upbeat and has a strong beat that makes it enjoyable to listen to.

Overall, "If Looks Could Kill (You Would Be Dead)" is a vibrant, fun song that celebrates the power of beauty. Beyoncé's clever wordplay and upbeat tempo make it an enjoyable song to listen to and a great example of how beauty can be used to set the standards in a relationship. Additionally, the song contains a range of linguistic features that help to express the emotions and feelings that Beyoncé is conveying. Through the use of imagery, double entendres, and clever word play, Beyoncé creates an empowering story of a confident woman who won't settle for anything less than she deserves.
