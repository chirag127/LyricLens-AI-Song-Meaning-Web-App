"One Night Only (Disco)" by Beyoncé is a song about the desire to have a passionate romance that is limited only to one night. The lyrics speak of wanting to have all of someone's love and devotion, but not having the time to provide it. The song is about the bittersweet feeling of knowing that the romance will end soon and that the moments shared will be fleeting. 

The song starts off with an upbeat rhythm and a catchy chorus, setting the tone for the rest of the song. Beyoncé sings about wanting to share her love and devotion, but being limited by time. She pleads with her lover to make the most of their time together, since they only have until dawn. The chorus is repeated and the song ends on a somber note, emphasizing the bittersweet nature of the romance.

Linguistically, the song is filled with imagery and symbolism. Beyoncé speaks of wanting to give her love and devotion, but being limited by time. This is a metaphor for the fleeting nature of the romance, and the chorus emphasizes this idea with the line “we only have 'til dawn”. Additionally, the use of repetition adds to the poignancy of the song and emphasizes the feeling of urgency. 

Overall, "One Night Only (Disco)" by Beyoncé is a poignant and heartfelt song about the bittersweet nature of a fleeting romance. The song is filled with imagery and symbolism, and the lyrics speak of wanting to have all of someone's love and devotion, but being limited by time. The use of repetition in the chorus also adds to the poignancy of the song, emphasizing the urgency of the situation.
