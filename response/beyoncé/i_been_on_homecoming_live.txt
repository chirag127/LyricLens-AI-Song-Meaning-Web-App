The song "I Been On (Homecoming Live)" by Beyoncé is an anthemic celebration of her accomplishments and success. The lyrics of the song focus on Beyoncé's self-confidence and the pride she has in her achievements. The message of the song is clear: no matter the obstacles, she will always stay true to her identity and continue to be "on". 

The song begins with a call and response chorus in which Beyoncé proclaims she is “on” and then challenges the listener to find someone who can take her off. The phrase “on” serves as a metaphor for her unshakeable self-confidence and her refusal to let anyone take her down. 

In the first verse, Beyoncé celebrates her lavish lifestyle, describing herself as “rolling high, leather and wood”. She is also unafraid to show her love for her mother, and she is unapologetic about her partying and “popping bottles in the club”. She also suggests that she is not afraid to stand up to those who challenge her, “smack[ing] that trick” when necessary.

In the bridge and chorus, Beyoncé again emphasizes her refusal to be taken down, proclaiming that she is “bigger than life” and that her name is “in the lights”. She also makes reference to her nickname, “the Capital B”, as a symbol of her success.

Overall, "I Been On (Homecoming Live)" is a song of self-empowerment and pride in one's successes. Through her lyrics, Beyoncé is sending a message of resilience and determination, and her anthemic chorus is a reminder to never give up on one's dreams. 

The song's lyrics are filled with emotion and optimism, and the language used is both direct and powerful. Beyoncé's use of metaphors and vivid imagery helps convey her message of self-confidence, and her lyrics are full of positivity and ambition. This is further emphasized by her vocal delivery, which is both passionate and powerful. 

In conclusion, "I Been On (Homecoming Live)" is an uplifting and empowering song that celebrates one's successes and encourages listeners to be proud of their accomplishments. The lyrics are filled with emotion and determination, and Beyoncé's message of resilience and optimism is clear.
