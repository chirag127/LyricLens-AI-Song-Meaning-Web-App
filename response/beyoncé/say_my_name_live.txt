Say My Name (Live) by Beyoncé is a powerful song about love, heartbreak, and betrayal. The song starts off with the lyrics “say my name, say my name / you actin’ kinda shady / ain’t callin’ me baby / better say my name”. These lyrics depict a sense of longing, as Beyoncé is asking her partner to say her name. This is further shown by her asking “why the sudden change” and questioning if “somebody’s there then tell me who”, conveying a sense of hurt and betrayal. 

The following verse continues to explore the theme of heartbreak and betrayal as it talks about how her partner’s behavior has changed and how he is not being truthful. The chorus further expresses her feelings of hurt and betrayal with the lyrics “say my name, say my name / if no one is around you say baby I love you / if you ain’t runnin’ game say my name”. Here, Beyoncé is pleading for her partner to be honest with her and to say her name if no one else is around.

The interlude of the song is a call back to the group Destiny’s Child, demonstrating Beyoncé’s roots in the R&B genre. This interlude also serves to further connect the themes of heartbreak and betrayal to the group’s earlier music. 

Overall, Say My Name (Live) by Beyoncé is an emotionally charged song about love, heartbreak, and betrayal. The song’s lyrics are full of emotion and convey Beyoncé’s feelings of hurt and betrayal. The song is both powerful and moving, and is sure to resonate with many listeners.
