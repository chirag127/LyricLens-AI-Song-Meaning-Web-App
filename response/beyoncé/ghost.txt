"Ghost" by Beyoncé is a powerful song about the struggles of modern life and how it can weigh on the individual. The song opens with Beyoncé singing about how she is "drifting off on knowledge", implying a feeling of being overwhelmed by the world and its complexities. She then goes on to sing about how the women around her have become solemn, and how she could sing a song for a mon or salamander. This verse serves as an example of Beyoncé's clever lyricism, as the imagery of a monarch and a salamander is used to describe the struggles of the modern woman.

The chorus is where Beyoncé really begins to express her feelings about the world, singing about how she is "looking off the edge" and her "gut can't help but ignore it". She is commenting on how, in a world filled with distractions, it can be difficult to stay focused and true to oneself. The chorus also contains the song's titular lyric, "What goes up, ghost around", which can be interpreted as a metaphor for how quickly people and things can come and go. The song's hook then adds to this idea, with Beyoncé singing "soul not for sale, probably won't make no money off this, oh well, reap what you sow". This speaks to how, in the modern world, money and material possessions can never buy true satisfaction.

The overall message of the song is that, despite the struggles of modern life, it is still possible to find joy and peace within oneself. This is a message that has resonated with many, as the song has become a popular anthem for those who have struggled with the difficulties of life. In terms of lyrical analysis, the song is filled with imagery and metaphors that express Beyoncé's feelings about the world. Additionally, the song also features a unique production style, with its funky synth-heavy sound complementing the lyrical themes.

In summary, "Ghost" by Beyoncé is an incredibly powerful song that speaks to many of the struggles of modern life. The song's clever lyricism and unique production style make it a standout track, and its message of resilience and finding joy within oneself has resonated with many. It is an anthem for those who are looking to make the best out of difficult circumstances, and is a reminder that we all can make it through the toughest of times.
