Beyoncé’s song “Denial” is a powerful and emotive exploration of the difficulties of a relationship, and the lengths one may go to in order to salvage it. The song utilizes a poetic narrative to convey its message, with each of the verses detailing a different attempt the narrator makes to try and save their relationship. 

The first verse begins with the narrator expressing a desire to change in order to please their partner. They close their mouth more, try to be softer and less awake, and fast for sixty days. This is followed by the narrator wearing white, abstaining from mirrors and sex, and not speaking another word. The narrator then explains how their hair grew past their ankles and how they slept on the floor. 

The second verse continues to explore the narrator’s attempts to save the relationship, this time delving into more extreme measures. They swallow a sword, levitate, confess their sins and are baptized in a river. They get on their knees and ask for dominion, throwing themselves into a volcano, drinking the blood and wine. They sit alone and beg and bend at the waist, crossing themselves in thought and seeing the devil.

The third verse further explores these drastic measures, with the narrator growing thickened skin on their feet and bathing in bleach. They then plug their menses with the pages from the holy book, only to be left with the need to know: “Are you cheating on me?” 

The song is a powerful and emotive exploration of the struggles of a relationship, and the lengths one may go to in order to save it. The song’s narrative structure, combined with its emotive lyrics and poignancy, provides a powerful insight into the narrator’s struggles. 

Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a deeply emotive and evocative story, with intense imagery and emotions such as fear, desperation, and sadness. The narrator’s use of repetition and the extended metaphor of “swallowing a sword” to illustrate the depths of their desperation, further conveys the intensity of their feelings. 

Overall, “Denial” is a powerful and emotive song, with powerful lyrics and imagery that provide an insight into the narrator’s struggles. It is a deeply moving song, and one that will linger in the memory for some time.
