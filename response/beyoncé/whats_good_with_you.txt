In Beyoncé's song "What's Good With You", she speaks of the importance of honesty and communication when it comes to relationships. The song begins with Beyoncé asking the person she is addressing to be upfront about their intentions and to be honest from the start. She suggests that if they are serious about wanting to be with her, they should go all the way. The chorus of the song is a direct question to the person - "What's Good With You?", asking them to reveal what they have been up to and to be honest with her. 

The second verse speaks of the need to be direct and not play mind games, and to make up one's mind right away. She also states that there is no need to waste time if the person is not being honest. The third verse continues this theme, as Beyoncé insists that the person not try to play with her mind and instead be honest about their feelings. 

The song is a powerful reminder of the importance of honesty and being upfront in relationships. Beyoncé insists that her potential partner be honest about their intentions and not try to play games with her. This is an important message for anyone in a relationship, and it serves as a reminder to communicate openly and honestly with one another.

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song is full of direct commands, questions, and statements, as well as imagery and emotive language that conveys Beyoncé's feelings. The song is also full of power and strength, as Beyoncé stands her ground and demands honesty from her partner. This is a reminder that we should all strive for honesty and openness in our relationships, and that we should not be afraid to stand up for ourselves and demand honesty from the people we love.

Overall, "What's Good With You" is an empowering song that speaks to the importance of communication and honesty in relationships. Beyoncé's strong message of self-worth and demand for honesty is a powerful reminder that we should all strive for open and honest communication in our relationships.
