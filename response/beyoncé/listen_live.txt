"Listen (Live)" by Beyoncé is a powerful anthem of self-determination and self-empowerment. This is a powerful expression of the need to break away from someone who has limited or held back the singer's progress and find her own way. 

The second verse continues the story, describing how the singer has been reminded of the strength inside her after having felt like it had been lost. The chorus then returns, reinforcing the message of having to find her own way and step away from someone who doesn't recognize her value. The song ends with the same opening line, "listen to the song here in my heart a melody I start but I will complete," signifying the singer's determination to reclaim her own power and complete her journey. 

The song is a powerful expression of the need to break away from those who try to limit us and find our own courage, strength, and power. The lyrics are filled with emotion, and the music carries a sense of urgency and determination, making it a powerful anthem of self-liberation. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song has a number of features that could be considered. For example, the use of repetitive phrases such as "listen" and "you should have known" emphasises the message of the song, while the use of the first person narrative helps to draw the listener into the story and empathise with the singer. Overall, "Listen (Live)" is an emotionally powerful song about self-liberation and reclaiming one's own power. With its strong message, emotive lyrics, and impassioned delivery, it is a powerful anthem of self-determination and strength.
