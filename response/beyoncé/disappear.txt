"Disappear" by Beyoncé is a song about the struggle of feeling connected to someone who isn't present in your life. The song starts with Beyoncé asking herself if begging or crying could change the sky and if she should wait for her loved one to call. She acknowledges that she was never really there for them, and as a result, she was unable to share as much of herself with them as she would have liked. She then sings about how she missed all the signs of her loved one's readiness for her to be in their life, but now she is ready to show them her love. 

The song is a reflection on the feelings of longing and loss that come with missing a loved one, and Beyoncé's lyrics illustrate the pain of not being able to have them in her life. The song's lyrics also show a sense of hope, as Beyoncé acknowledges that her loved one is still out there and that, perhaps, one day they will be able to reconnect. 

Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a deep sense of longing and regret in the song. Beyoncé's use of words such as "cried," "light," "hopeless," and "drifting" express a sense of despair at the absence of her loved one. She also uses words such as "faithful," "right," and "ready" to express her hope that the two can eventually be reunited. 

Overall, "Disappear" is an emotionally powerful song about the difficulty of being separated from a loved one. Beyoncé's lyrics capture the complex emotions of longing, regret, and hope that come with missing someone, making it an incredibly poignant and relatable song.
