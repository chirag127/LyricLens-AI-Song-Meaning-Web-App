The song "Psycho In My Head" by Skillet appears to be about the narrator's struggles with their own mental health. In the first verse, they express feelings of paranoia and uncertainty, questioning whether their reality is a lie and whether they are "sick inside." The chorus introduces the idea of a "psycho in my head," representing the internal conflict and struggles the narrator is experiencing.

In the second verse, the narrator describes living in a state of hysteria, with nothing making sense. They express fear and a lack of trust in themselves, and mention the "chaos in my mind." The chorus repeats, emphasizing the destructive presence of the "psycho" in their head.

In the third verse, the narrator returns to the idea of feeling paranoid and unsure of their reality. They express a desire to believe in themselves and see the good in themselves, but struggle to do so due to the presence of the "psycho" in their head. The chorus repeats once more, with the narrator declaring that they are not crazy, but acknowledging that they may be.

Overall, the song appears to be a powerful and emotionally charged exploration of the internal struggles that come with mental health issues. The use of the term "psycho" in the chorus, while potentially triggering for some, effectively conveys the feeling of being overwhelmed and consumed by one's own thoughts and emotions.
