The song "Champagne Problems" by Taylor Swift is an introspective look at what it means to be a young adult navigating the complexities of modern love. The bridge of the song is a reflection on the past, with Swift singing about a Midas touch, a Chevy door, and a November flush. She then sings about a group of friends who will never say that word again, referring to the failed relationship. The outro then repeats the chorus, with Swift singing about how they will never remember all the champagne problems.

The song "Champagne Problems" is an emotional exploration of the complexities of modern love. Through the lyrics, Swift reflects on the idea that sometimes relationships can be difficult, even if they involve champagne and luxury.
