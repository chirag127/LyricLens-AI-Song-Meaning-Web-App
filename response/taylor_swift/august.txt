The song “August” by Taylor Swift is an emotionally charged ballad about reminiscing on a summer romance that never fully blossomed. The song is composed of three verses, a bridge, and a chorus. 

The first verse of “August” sees Swift exploring the nostalgia of a summer romance- “Salt air, and the rust on your door, I never needed anything more”. She touches on the feeling of uncertainty that often accompanies new relationships - “Whispers of ‘Are you sure?’, ‘Never have I ever before’”.

The chorus of the song is a reflection of the longing Swift feels for a relationship that was never hers - “But I can see us lost in the memory, August slipped away into a moment in time, ‘Cause it was never mine. And I can see us twisted in bedsheets, August sipped away like a bottle of wine, ‘Cause you were never mine”.

The second verse finds Swift further exploring the longing she feels for someone who was never officially hers - “Your back beneath the sun, Wishin’ I could write my name on it, Will you call when you’re back at school? I remember thinkin’ I had you”.

The bridge of the song sees Swift reminiscing about the time before she realized that the relationship was never meant to be - “Back when we were still changin’ for the better, Wanting was enough, For me, it was enough, To live for the hope of it all, Cancel plans just in case you’d call, And say, ‘Meet me behind the mall’ So much for summer love and saying ‘us’, ‘Cause you weren’t mine to lose, You weren’t mine to lose, no”.

The song “August” is an exploration of the emotions felt when a summer romance fades away. The lyrics are filled with a sense of longing and hope, and Swift’s emotionally charged delivery of the song’s verses and chorus adds to the sentiment. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the lyrics of “August” evoke a range of emotions from nostalgia to despair. The first and second verses paint a picture of a new relationship filled with uncertainty and longing, while the bridge highlights the feelings of hope and loss that come with the realization that the relationship was not meant to be. The chorus serves to further emphasize the idea that the relationship was never officially hers. 

Overall, “August” is a heartfelt and melancholic track that perfectly encapsulates the emotions felt when a summer romance fades away. The lyrics and Swift’s delivery of them evoke a range of emotions that leave the listener feeling reflective and melancholic.
