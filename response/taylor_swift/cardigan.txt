The song "Cardigan" by Taylor Swift is a reflective and introspective exploration of the complexities of youth and lost love. The song begins with a description of a young person going out on the town, wearing a vintage tee and brand new phone, and high heels on cobblestones. However, the lyrics suggest that while they appear to be confident, they feel “assumed to know nothing”, implying a lack of knowledge or experience in life. 

The chorus of the song introduces the theme of a lost love, with the singer describing how they “knew” the person in question, and reminiscing about the good times they shared together. The refrain of the song further elaborates on this idea, with the singer comparing themselves to an old cardigan, hidden away in someone’s bed, until the other person put it on and said it was their favorite. 

The second verse of the song introduces a new theme, that of friendship. The singer states that “a friend to all is a friend to none”, suggesting that you shouldn’t spread yourself too thin. The chorus again reiterates the idea of lost love, as the singer talks about playing hide-and-seek, giving their weekends, and having their heartbeat on the High Line.

The bridge of the song is an expression of the singer’s nostalgia for their lost love, as they reminisce about kissing in cars and downtown bars, and the other person drawing stars around the singer’s scars. The chorus again states that the singer knew the person, and the final verse of the song concludes with the singer admitting that they “knew everything when they were young” and they will miss the person in question for a long time. 

In summary, "Cardigan" by Taylor Swift is a reflective and introspective exploration of the complexities of youth and lost love. The singer reflects on their experiences, and how they knew the person they were in love with, while also expressing their nostalgia and sadness at the loss of the relationship. Linguistic analysis of the song lyrics reveals a range of emotions, including nostalgia, sadness, and regret. The song is a beautiful ode to the complexities of youth and love, and is sure to resonate with many listeners.
