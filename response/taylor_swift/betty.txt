"Betty" by Taylor Swift is an emotional song about a former love and the regret and longing the singer feels for that person. The song follows a verse-by-verse breakdown of the lyrics, beginning with the singer expressing their surprise at the beloved's decision to change homerooms and their inability to breathe when they ride by their house. The pre-chorus reveals the rumors that the singer believes are the cause of the beloved's decision, and the chorus follows with the singer's plea to be accepted back into the beloved's life. 

The second verse explores the singer's regret in more detail, as they acknowledge where things went wrong and how they avoided the beloved at the school dance. The pre-chorus and chorus repeat, emphasizing the singer's longing for the beloved and their desire to be accepted back into their life. 

The third verse brings the story full circle, with the singer standing on the beloved's doorstep and acknowledging the plan they had made to reach out. The chorus repeats for the last time, this time emphasizing that the singer is willing to do whatever it takes to be accepted back into the beloved's life.

Overall, the song is a poignant and heartbreaking exploration of regret, longing, and desire for reconciliation. The lyrics are beautifully and poetically written, and they express a deep and powerful emotion that is felt by many. The song is a reminder that even though relationships can be difficult, it is worth fighting for them and trying to make things right.

In terms of the linguistic analysis of the song, it is evident that the lyrics express feelings of regret, longing, and sadness. The use of pronouns such as “you” and “I” show the singer’s focus on the beloved, and the use of words like “trust” and “love” reveal the depth of the singer’s feelings. Additionally, the use of metaphors, such as the reference to “broken wings”, highlight the emotional pain the singer is feeling. 

In conclusion, "Betty" is a powerful song that speaks to the heart and conveys deep emotions in a beautiful and poetic way. The song is a reminder of the power of love and the importance of fighting for relationships.
