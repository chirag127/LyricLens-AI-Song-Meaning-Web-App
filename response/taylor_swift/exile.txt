Taylor Swift's song "Exile" is a song of heartbreak and sorrow. Swift's lines show her grappling with the pain of the breakup and trying to come to terms with it. The use of words like "exile" and "understudy" emphasize the feeling of being abandoned and forgotten. The repetition of the chorus, with its refrain of "I think I've seen this film before," emphasizes the helplessness and hopelessness of the situation.

A linguistic analysis of the song reveals a range of emotions, from sadness and regret to anger and bitterness. The lyrics also express a sense of resignation and acceptance, as Taylor comes to terms with the fact that the relationship is over and she can no longer rely on her former love.
