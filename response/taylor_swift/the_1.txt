The song “The 1” by Taylor Swift is a reflection on a past relationship and the nostalgia that comes with it. In the song, Swift reminisces on the good times they shared and the “roaring twenties” they enjoyed together. She also expresses regret for not having been “the one” that the person wanted, and her wish that things had been different. 

The first verse begins with Swift expressing that she is doing better and making positive changes in her life. She then talks about a moment where she thought she saw her former partner at the bus stop, but it wasn’t them. She then talks about how she is taking advantage of life and having adventures. 

The pre-chorus follows in which Swift acknowledges the uncertainty of life and the sadness of being alone. She also reflects on how if her former partner had wanted her, they should have showed it. 

The chorus follows in which Swift acknowledges the special times they shared and expresses regret for not having been the one. She also expresses regret for not having left things well enough alone. 

The second verse follows in which Swift talks about her dreams for her former partner and how they are living life with adventures. She then talks about how they never “painted by the numbers” together, but they made their time together count. 

The pre-chorus and chorus repeat with slight variations.

The bridge follows in which Swift expresses her temptation to ask what would have been different if they had been together. 

The chorus repeats with a slight variation.

In summary, the song “The 1” by Taylor Swift is a reflection on a past relationship and the nostalgia that comes with it. Swift expresses regret for not having been the one that her former partner wanted, and she also expresses a wish that things had been different. Linguistically, the song has a melancholic tone that conveys a sense of nostalgia, regret, and longing.
