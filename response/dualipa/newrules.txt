"New Rules" is a pop song by British singer Dua Lipa. The song is about setting boundaries in a relationship and not allowing oneself to be mistreated by a partner.

The first verse talks about the narrator talking to themselves at night and trying to rationalize their relationship. Despite the fact that their partner doesn't love them, they continue to hold on to the relationship. The chorus introduces the "new rules" that the narrator has set for themselves, including not picking up the phone when their partner calls and not letting them into their home or being their friend.

In the second verse, the narrator describes the pattern of their relationship and how their partner keeps pulling them back even though they are trying to move on. They reiterate the "new rules" and say that they are trying to learn them by heart and practice them.

The song has a catchy, danceable beat and relatable lyrics about the struggles of setting boundaries in a relationship. Overall, "New Rules" is an empowering and self-aware song about not allowing oneself to be mistreated in a relationship.
