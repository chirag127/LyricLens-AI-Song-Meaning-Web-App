The song "Levitating" by Dualipa is a romantic and upbeat track about falling in love and feeling like you're floating on cloud nine. In the first verse, the singer offers to take her love interest on a ride through the galaxy, mentioning a premonition that they will fall into a rhythm together. She describes the spark between them as glitter in the sky and in their eyes, shining just the way they like.

The chorus repeats the phrase "I'm levitating," emphasizing the feeling of weightlessness and joy that the singer experiences when she is with her partner. She also references the Milky Way and renegading, possibly indicating a sense of adventure and freedom in their relationship.

The second verse continues the theme of falling in love and feeling like anything is possible. The singer believes that they are meant to be together and sees their future written in the stars. She wants to go wherever and do whatever with her partner, feeling like they can never be too far from each other.

The third verse shifts to a more energetic and playful tone, with the singer comparing her love to a rocket blasting off and feeling electric as she dances. She indicates that she can't stop even if she wanted to, highlighting the unstoppable nature of her feelings.

Overall, the song is a celebration of falling in love and the joy and freedom that comes with it. The use of imagery and metaphors, such as glitter and levitation, helps to convey the emotional experience of the singer. The catchy chorus and upbeat tempo also contribute to the overall uplifting and celebratory tone of the song.
