"Don't Start Now" is a pop song by British singer Dua Lipa. The song is about moving on after a breakup and not wanting the other person to try to come back into the narrator's life.

In the first verse, the narrator describes how they have done a "full one-eighty" and changed since the breakup. They ask if the heartbreak changed them and mention that they are in a better place now. The chorus repeats the refrain "Don't start caring about me now" and tells the other person to walk away and not try to come back.

The second verse talks about the other person trying to hurt the narrator with their goodbye, but the narrator has survived and is better off without them. The chorus repeats again, emphasizing that the other person should not try to come back into the narrator's life.

The song has an upbeat tempo and catchy chorus, which reflects the narrator's confidence and determination to move on from the relationship. Overall, "Don't Start Now" is a catchy and relatable song about moving on from a past relationship and not wanting the other person to try to come back into your life.
