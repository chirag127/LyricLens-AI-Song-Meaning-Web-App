"Bodak Yellow (Latin Trap Mix)" is a remix of Cardi B's hit single "Bodak Yellow," featuring Latin trap artist Messiah. The song is a braggadocious anthem in which Cardi B boasts about her success and dismisses those who try to compete with her. She raps about her luxurious lifestyle and her ability to make money moves, while also threatening those who try to come after her. In the chorus, she declares that she is the queen of the trap and that nobody can compete with her.

The Latin trap mix features additional verses from Messiah, who adds his own braggadocious rhymes and boasts about his own success. He raps about his lavish lifestyle, his ability to make money, and his ability to attract women.

In terms of a review or opinion of the song, it is a solid remix that adds a new flavor to the original track. Cardi B and Messiah both deliver strong verses and the trap beat provides a solid foundation for their rhymes. Overall, the song is an enjoyable addition to Cardi B's discography and is worth a listen for fans of the original "Bodak Yellow" or those interested in Latin trap music.
