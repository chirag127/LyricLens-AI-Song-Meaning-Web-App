In "Everything," Cardi B reflects on the struggles she faced growing up and how she's determined to provide a better life for her family. She speaks about her past as a stripper and how people underestimated her. She goes on to talk about her goals, including buying her parents a house and car and making sure her father can retire before he turns sixty and her mother can retire before she turns fifty. The chorus of the song emphasizes the importance of her family, calling them her "everything."

The song is a powerful anthem of determination and overcoming adversity. Cardi B's lyrics are raw and relatable, and she conveys her message with passion and conviction. The song is a reminder that even when people underestimate us, it's important to keep pushing forward and striving for our goals. Overall, "Everything" is a great addition to Cardi B's impressive catalog of hits.
