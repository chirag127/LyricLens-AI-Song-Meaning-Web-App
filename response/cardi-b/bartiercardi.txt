"Bartier Cardi" is a song by American rapper Cardi B featuring 21 Savage. The song is about Cardi B's luxurious lifestyle, with references to designer brands and expensive cars. She boasts about being rich and successful, and how she can take anyone's man if she wants to. 21 Savage provides a verse in which he also boasts about his wealth and success. The song is upbeat and energetic, with a catchy beat and strong vocals from both Cardi B and 21 Savage. Overall, "Bartier Cardi" is a fun and entertaining track that showcases Cardi B's brash and confident personality.
