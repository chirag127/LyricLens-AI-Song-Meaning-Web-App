"Bodak Yellow" is a song by American rapper Cardi B. In the song, Cardi B brags about her success, wealth, and lifestyle, and dismisses anyone who tries to stand in her way. She raps about her luxury apartments and designer clothes, and says she's the hottest in the street and doesn't need to dance to make money. She also references her rise from the streets to success, and warns anyone who tries to come after her or her money.

The song is a trap-influenced banger, with a catchy hook and energetic verses. Cardi B's confident, assertive delivery and clever wordplay make the song a standout.

Overall, "Bodak Yellow" is a strong showcase of Cardi B's talents as a rapper and a reminder of why she's one of the biggest names in the game. The song is energetic, bold, and brimming with confidence, making it an enjoyable listen.
