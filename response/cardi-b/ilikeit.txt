"I Like It" is a song by American rapper Cardi B, featuring Puerto Rican rapper Bad Bunny and American rapper and singer J Balvin. The song was released as a single in May 2018 and is included on Cardi B's debut album Invasion of Privacy.

The song begins with Cardi B's signature flow, rapping about her love for money and flashy possessions. She also boasts about her success and her ability to prove doubters wrong. Bad Bunny then comes in with a verse about his own lavish lifestyle, mentioning designer clothes and expensive cars. J Balvin rounds out the song with a verse about his love for exotic women from various Latin American countries.

The song is a fun and energetic celebration of wealth and success, with each artist showcasing their own unique style and flair. Cardi B's verse is filled with her characteristic wit and braggadocio, while Bad Bunny and J Balvin add a Latino twist with their Latin-inspired verses. The chorus of the song, with its catchy "I like it like that" hook, is sure to get stuck in your head.

Overall, "I Like It" is a fun and energetic track that showcases the unique talents of Cardi B, Bad Bunny, and J Balvin. The song's catchy chorus and energetic beats make it a great addition to any party playlist.
