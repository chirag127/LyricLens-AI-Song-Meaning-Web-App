"Best Life" is a rap song by Cardi B and Chance the Rapper. The song is about the artists living their best lives, achieving success and wealth, and being happy and content with their lives. In the first verse, Chance the Rapper raps about making a lot of money with his best friends and turning his struggles into lessons. He also references the luxury car he bought with his earnings and boasts about his dreams coming true. Cardi B then takes over in the second verse and raps about her rise to fame, overcoming her struggles, and achieving success. She also takes shots at her haters and boasts about her accomplishments. In the chorus, Chance the Rapper repeats the line "I'm living my best life" to emphasize the theme of the song.

As for a review or opinion of the song, "Best Life" is a catchy and upbeat rap song that celebrates the artists' success and happiness. The beat is energetic and the lyrics are playful and boastful. Overall, the song is likely to be appreciated by fans of rap and hip-hop music.
