In the song "Get Up 10," Cardi B tells the story of her rise to fame, starting from her days as a stripper and working her way up to becoming a successful rapper. She addresses the haters who have underestimated her and talks about the struggles she has faced along the way. She also touches on her relationship with her family, her dedication to her craft, and her determination to succeed. 

In the chorus, she repeats the phrase "get up 10" to emphasize her resilience and ability to overcome obstacles. Throughout the song, she expresses confidence in her abilities and lets her critics know that she won't be held down.

Overall, "Get Up 10" is a powerful and inspiring anthem that showcases Cardi B's strong personality and determination to succeed. The song is full of attitude and confidence, and it's clear that Cardi B is not one to be underestimated.
