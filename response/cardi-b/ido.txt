In "I Do," Cardi B and SZA sing about their independence and their ability to do what they want, when they want. The song is a celebration of their strength and confidence as women. 

In the first verse, SZA sings about leaving a man on read because she feels like it, and dressing up in a Raf Simons jacket. She boasts about her financial success and explains that she does whatever she wants because she can afford to. 

In the second verse, Cardi B raps about being a boss and making her own money. She boasts about her sexual prowess and her ability to dominate men. She also encourages other women to be independent and not to depend on men for their happiness or financial security.

The chorus, sung by both artists, repeats the line "I do what I like" as a declaration of their independence and freedom. 

Overall, the song is a empowering and confident anthem for women who are in control of their own lives. The production is upbeat and energetic, matching the mood of the lyrics. Cardi B and SZA's performances are strong and assertive, adding to the song's overall message.
