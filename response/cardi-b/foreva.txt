In the song "Foreva," Cardi B brags about her toughness and willingness to confront those who try to mess with her. She warns her enemies to be careful and not to start any conflicts that they cannot finish. Cardi B also touches on her rise to fame and the hard work she has put in to achieve success.

The song is an energetic and aggressive track that showcases Cardi B's aggressive side and her willingness to stand up for herself. The lyrics are brash and unapologetic, as Cardi B asserts her dominance over anyone who tries to come for her.

Overall, "Foreva" is a strong and confident track that highlights Cardi B's determination and strength. It is a powerful anthem for anyone who has had to overcome adversity to achieve their goals.
