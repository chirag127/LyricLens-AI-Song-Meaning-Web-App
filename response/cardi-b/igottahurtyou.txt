"I Gotta Hurt You" is a song by Cardi-b where she expresses her desire to hurt her partner, despite loving them. She mentions that she might be petty or spiteful, and needs therapy for her actions. The song is about the inner turmoil she feels, and her desire to make sure her partner remembers her forever.

In the first verse, Cardi-b talks about loving her partner with all her heart, but at the same time feeling the need to hurt them. She questions if she is spiteful or needs therapy for her actions. The chorus repeats the line "I want to hurt you" several times, emphasizing her desire to cause harm to her partner.

In the second verse, Cardi-b introduces herself as Cardi-b and talks about how loneliness fulfills her sorrow. She mentions that it's easier to die than to live, but she will show her strength by using and abusing her partner, and ultimately winning in the end.

The song is a raw and emotional portrayal of the complexities of relationships and the conflicting emotions that can arise. Cardi-b's vocals are powerful and emotive, making the song an intense listening experience. Overall, "I Gotta Hurt You" is a strong and impactful track that showcases Cardi-b's talent as a singer and songwriter.
