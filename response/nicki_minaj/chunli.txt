The song "Chun-Li" by Nicki Minaj is about the rapper's success and dominance in the rap game, comparing herself to the video game character Chun-Li. In the first verse, she brags about her skills on the basketball court and her fashion sense, while also taking shots at her haters. The chorus references her wealth and success, as well as her status as a leading figure in the rap game. In the second verse, she continues to boast about her achievements and dismisses her critics. The song also includes two interludes where Minaj addresses her critics and the portrayal of her in the media.

In summary, the song is a confident and assertive anthem that showcases Minaj's skills as a rapper and her determination to succeed in the rap game.

As for a review or opinion of the song, "Chun-Li" is a standout track from Minaj's 2018 album "Queen." The beat is energetic and driving, and Minaj's confident and assertive flow is on full display. Her clever wordplay and clever references to popular culture add to the song's appeal. Overall, "Chun-Li" is a strong addition to Minaj's impressive catalog of hits.
