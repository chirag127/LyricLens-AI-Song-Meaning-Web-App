Nicki Minaj's "Super Bass" is an energetic and upbeat hip-hop/pop crossover that celebrates the joys of young love. The final chorus then takes on a more celebratory tone, with Nicki singing about the joy of being in love and the power of their connection.

Linguistic analysis of the song reveals a range of emotions, from the joy of young love to self-assured confidence. The song also contains several references to Nicki's own success and dominance, which suggests an underlying theme of self-empowerment and female agency.
