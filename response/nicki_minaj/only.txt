In the second verse, Drake joins the song to rap about his attraction to Minaj and his desire to sleep with her once her current relationship ends. He also compares her to the character Clair Huxtable from the TV show "The Cosby Show."

In the third and final verse, Lil Wayne raps about his admiration for Minaj and the impact she has had on his career. He also asserts that anyone who has slept with her has not done it properly.

Overall, the song celebrates wealth, success, and exclusivity. It is a declaration of the singer's place in the upper echelons of society and a rejection of anyone who is not on the same level.

As for my review of the song, I think it is a well-crafted and entertaining track with catchy beats and clever lyrics.
