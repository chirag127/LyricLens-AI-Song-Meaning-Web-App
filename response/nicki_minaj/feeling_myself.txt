"Feeling Myself" is a song by American rapper Nicki Minaj and singer Beyoncé. In the first verse, Nicki Minaj raps about her success and wealth, referencing her black credit card and expensive handbags. She also boasts about her sexual prowess and compares herself to a jackrabbit and a bad toss. In the second verse, she raps about her "pretty on fleek" appearance and her dominance over her male partners. The chorus, sung by Beyoncé, repeats the phrase "feelin' myself" as a declaration of self-confidence and empowerment.

In the song's bridge, Minaj and Beyoncé rap about their sexual desires and the effects of their drug use. The final verse features both artists trading lines about their wealth, success, and attractiveness.

Overall, "Feeling Myself" is a boastful and confident anthem about female empowerment and success. The song celebrates the artists' achievements and promotes self-love and self-confidence. As a review, the song is a catchy and energetic tribute to female empowerment, with dynamic performances from both Nicki Minaj and Beyoncé.
