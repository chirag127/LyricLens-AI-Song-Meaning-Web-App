"Get Me" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Kehlani. In the song, Bieber sings about how he feels a strong connection with Kehlani and how he appreciates her for understanding him. The song has a upbeat, pop sound with a catchy chorus. Kehlani adds a smooth, soulful touch to the track with her vocals. Overall, "Get Me" is a fun, lighthearted song about finding a special connection with someone.
