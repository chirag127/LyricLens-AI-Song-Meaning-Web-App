"Friends" is a song by Justin Bieber, released in 2017. In the song, Justin reflects on a past relationship and wonders if they can still be friends. He expresses curiosity about his ex's well-being and reminisces about their time together. Justin asks if the relationship has to end and if they can still be friends, even if it does.

In the first verse, Justin wonders about his ex's mother and if she's doing well. He also asks about the car she was having problems with and if she's sold it. In the second verse, Justin admits that he's been calling his ex for ulterior motives and wonders if they can still be friends. He reflects on the good times they had together and asks if they can still maintain a friendship.

In the chorus, Justin repeats the question, "Can we still be friends?" He suggests that the relationship doesn't have to end and that they can still be friends, even if it does. The song has a pop and R&B sound, with a catchy chorus and relatable lyrics about navigating the aftermath of a breakup. Overall, it's a poignant and reflective song about moving on from a relationship and maintaining a friendship.
