"Forever" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Post Malone and Clever. In the song, Justin Bieber expresses his love for a girl and asks if she will be with him forever. He sings about the hardships they have faced in their relationship and how they have overcome them together. He also talks about their future together, imagining a life of luxury and happiness. Post Malone and Clever contribute verses in which they also express their love and commitment to their partners.

The song has a pop and R&B influenced sound, with a catchy beat and smooth melodies. Justin Bieber's vocals are emotional and sincere, and his lyrics are relatable and heartfelt. Post Malone and Clever's verses add depth and variety to the song.

Overall, "Forever" is a romantic and upbeat love song that showcases Justin Bieber's vocal and songwriting skills. The catchy chorus and relatable lyrics make it a strong addition to his discography.
