"PYD" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring R. Kelly. The song is about putting a girl "down" in a romantic way, showing her love and affection. The lyrics describe various scenarios in which the singer will put the girl "down" in a loving manner, from the bedroom to the club to the parking lot. The chorus repeats the phrase "I'ma put you down" (PYD, PYD, PYD) as a way of expressing the singer's affection for the girl. R. Kelly also contributes a verse to the song, expressing similar sentiments.

The song has a catchy beat and a smooth, R&B vibe. The chorus is particularly memorable and catchy, with the repeated PYD refrain sticking in the listener's head. Overall, "PYD" is a fun and enjoyable love song that is sure to get stuck in your head.
