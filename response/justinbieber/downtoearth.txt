"Down To Earth" by Justin Bieber is a heartfelt and emotional ballad that explores the challenges and struggles of a distant and troubled relationship. In the first verse, Bieber sings about how he never thought it would be easy to maintain a relationship with someone who is far away and emotionally distant. He also mentions how the walls are closing in on them and how they are struggling to find answers.

In the chorus, Bieber sings about how they must fight through the hurt and cry in order to learn and grow. He also stresses the importance of working together and meeting in the middle in order to find their way back to each other. This suggests that the relationship is on the brink of collapse but still has a chance of being salvaged.

In the second verse, Bieber sings about how his parents were often absent and how this has affected his ability to be a "normal" person. He also expresses his pain and sadness at the distance between him and his loved ones, and how he just wants them to be there for him.

In the bridge, Bieber sings about how the relationship has fallen far from where it used to be, and how they are struggling to find their way back to each other. He also talks about the need to start over and rebuild the relationship from the ground up.

Overall, "Down To Earth" is a powerful and emotional ballad that showcases Bieber's vulnerability and raw talent as a singer and songwriter. The song's relatable and heartfelt lyrics, coupled with the singer's emotive vocals, make for an impactful listening experience.
