In "She Don't Like The Lights," Justin Bieber sings about a relationship that is struggling because his partner doesn't like the fame and attention that comes with his job as a musician. She wants him to give it up and be with her, but he can't imagine living his life without his music. The song explores the conflict between their desires and the compromises they may have to make for the relationship to work.

In the first verse, Bieber sings about how his partner doesn't like the flash of cameras and the distance that his career often puts between them. In the chorus, he sings about how she is giving him ultimatums and wants him to choose between his career and their relationship. He struggles with this decision, as his music is a big part of his identity.

In the second verse, Bieber sings about how his partner wants him to live a more private life and is getting to the point where she is ready to end the relationship because of the demands of his career. However, he loves her and doesn't want to lose her. He hopes that she will wait a little longer and see how good their life together can be.

Overall, the song is about the challenges of balancing a successful career with a romantic relationship. It shows how fame and attention can put a strain on even the strongest relationships and how difficult it can be to find a compromise that works for both partners.
