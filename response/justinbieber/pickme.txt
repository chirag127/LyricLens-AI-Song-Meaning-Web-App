In "Pick Me," Justin Bieber sings about a girl he is interested in and asks her to choose him as her partner. He describes himself as a good choice because he is everything she needs and will always be her number one fan. He also mentions the activities they could do together, such as going to the movies and the beach. In the chorus, Bieber repeats the line "You should pick me" and asks the girl if she can "dig it," which means if she likes the idea.

The song is a upbeat pop track with a catchy chorus and simple, repetitive lyrics. Bieber's vocals are smooth and confident as he sings about his love interest.

Overall, "Pick Me" is a fun and lighthearted love song that showcases Bieber's charm and charisma. It is a catchy and enjoyable track that will appeal to fans of pop music.
