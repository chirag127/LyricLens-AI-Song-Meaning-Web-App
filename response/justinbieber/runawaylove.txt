"Runaway Love" by Justin Bieber is a song about a man searching for his partner who has left him. In the first verse, the singer laments his partner's decision to leave and expresses his desire to find her before anyone else does. He vows to do whatever it takes to win her back.

In the chorus, the singer expresses his urgency to find his partner, using phrases like "I'm running out of time" and "I won't stop until I find my runaway love." He also mentions giving everything up for the relationship, but it still wasn't enough to keep his partner by his side.

In the second verse, the singer reveals that his partner struggles with staying at home by herself and needs company, even if it's not always with him. He expresses his willingness to be there for her and do whatever it takes to make her happy.

In the final chorus, the singer repeats the refrain of searching for his runaway love and expressing his determination to find her. He also expresses frustration and confusion at how he let her slip away, despite his best efforts.

Overall, "Runaway Love" is a emotional and heartfelt song about the struggle to hold on to a relationship and the lengths one is willing to go to win back a lost love. The lyrics are relatable and showcase the singer's vulnerability and deep feelings for his partner.
