"Silent Night" is a popular Christmas carol that was written by Joseph Mohr and composed by Franz Xaver Gruber in 1818. The song tells the story of the birth of Jesus Christ and the peacefulness and serenity that surrounds it. The lyrics describe the scene of the virgin Mary and baby Jesus, with the "holy infant" sleeping in "heavenly peace." The song also references the light and grace of Jesus as the "Son of God" and the "love's pure light."

Justin Bieber's version of "Silent Night" is a straightforward and faithful rendition of the classic carol. It features Bieber's smooth, clear vocals and a simple, acoustic accompaniment. The song is a fitting addition to any holiday playlist and serves as a reminder of the true meaning of Christmas.
