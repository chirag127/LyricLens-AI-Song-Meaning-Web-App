"Freedom" is a song by Justin Bieber that features the artist BEAM. The song is about the freedom that comes from being forgiven by God. Bieber and BEAM sing about the struggles and hardships of life, and how turning to God can bring relief and peace. The chorus repeats the phrase "freedom, don't it feel good?" to emphasize the feeling of liberation that comes from being forgiven. The song encourages listeners to seek out God and experience the freedom that comes from His love and forgiveness.
