The song "Love Me" by Justin Bieber is a romantic ballad in which the singer expresses his strong feelings of love and devotion for his partner. In the first verse, he talks about how his friends think he's foolish for being so in love with this person, but he doesn't care because he's "just a sucker for love." The chorus repeats the phrase "love me" several times, emphasizing the singer's desire for his partner to return his feelings and show him the same level of affection.

In the second verse, the singer addresses the negative comments and advice he's received from others about his relationship, but he ignores them because he values his time with his partner more than anything else. He also mentions how being with his partner makes him feel like everything else fades away and he never wants to let go.

Overall, the song is a declaration of love and commitment to the singer's partner, despite any challenges or doubts they may face. It's a heartfelt and emotional expression of the singer's devotion and willingness to do anything for the person he loves.

As for a review of the song, it's a catchy and well-written love song that effectively conveys the singer's feelings and emotions. Bieber's vocals are strong and emotive, and the song's simple but effective melody and production make it a relatable and enjoyable listen for fans of romantic pop music.
