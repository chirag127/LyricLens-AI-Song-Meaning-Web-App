"Love Yourself" by Justin Bieber is a song about moving on from a relationship that isn't working. In the song, Bieber reflects on the ways his partner mistreated him, and how he has come to realize that he is better off without her. He advises her to "love yourself" and move on from him as well.

In the first verse, Bieber addresses the hurtful things his partner has done, such as using his name to get into clubs and raining on his parade. He denies that she broke his heart, and asserts that he is not crying over her. He then reveals that he didn't want to write a song about her, but she kept calling him, so he decided to address the situation in a song.

In the chorus, Bieber advises his partner to "love yourself" and move on from him. He suggests that her problems are not with his friends, but with herself, and that she should focus on self-improvement rather than trying to tear him down.

The second verse finds Bieber reflecting on how his partner made him feel small and vulnerable, and how he has come to realize that he is better off without her. He repeats the advice to "love yourself" and move on from the relationship.

In the final chorus, Bieber reiterates that his partner should "love herself" and let go of him. He suggests that she is still holding on to something that is no longer there, and that she needs to move on and find self-love.

Overall, "Love Yourself" is a powerful and relatable song about moving on from a toxic relationship. Bieber's lyrics are candid and honest, and the song's message of self-love and empowerment is one that resonates with many people who have been in similar situations.
