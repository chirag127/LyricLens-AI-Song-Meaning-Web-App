"Never Say Never" is a song by Justin Bieber that was released in 2010. The song is about not giving up and pushing through difficult times. In the lyrics, Bieber sings about how he never thought he could overcome certain challenges, but he has found the strength to do so and is determined to keep going.

The first verse of the song talks about facing adversity and not backing down. Bieber sings about how he never thought he could walk through fire or take the burn, but he has reached a point where he can't turn back. He gives everything he has because it's his destiny.

The chorus repeats the phrase "never say never" as a declaration of Bieber's determination to keep going and never give up. He will fight until he makes things right, and whenever he is knocked down, he will get back up again.

In the second verse, Bieber sings about feeling powerful and free, and about being strong enough to overcome any obstacle. He compares himself to David, who defeated the giant Goliath, and to Kobe Bryant, who is known for his clutch performances in the fourth quarter of basketball games.

The song also features a guest verse from Jaden Smith, who raps about how he is "born from two stars" and is destined for greatness. He boasts about his skills and confidence, and declares that he will never give up or back down.
