"Out of Town Girl" by Justin Bieber is a song about meeting and falling for a girl who is not from his hometown. Bieber sings about being attracted to the girl's accent and her unique qualities. He expresses his desire to spend time with her and show her a good time.

In the chorus, Bieber sings about how he likes "out of town girls" and encourages others to show their support for them. He also repeats the line "all you gotta do is swag" as a call to embrace confidence and style.

Overall, the song is a light-hearted and catchy tune about falling for someone new and exciting. It celebrates the appeal of meeting someone from a different place and getting to know them. As a review, the song is enjoyable and has a fun, upbeat vibe.
