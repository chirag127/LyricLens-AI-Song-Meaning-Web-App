In the song "Mama's Boy," Justin Bieber sings about his desire to run away from his current situation but ultimately feeling guilty about leaving his mother behind. He reflects on a recent argument with his father and the comfort his mother's singing used to bring him when things were tough. In the chorus, he sings about how he can't run away and leave his mother alone because he's a "mama's boy" who loves and depends on her. The song ultimately conveys the deep love and loyalty between a mother and her child.

In terms of a review or opinion of the song, it is a heartfelt and relatable ode to the bond between a mother and child. The lyrics capture the frustration and helplessness that can come with difficult relationships, but ultimately emphasizes the unbreakable bond between a mother and her child.
