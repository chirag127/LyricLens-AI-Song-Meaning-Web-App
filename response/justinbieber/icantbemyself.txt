"I Can't Be Myself" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Jaden. The song is about Justin's inability to be himself without the presence of his significant other. In the first verse, Justin sings about how he feels amazing and how he sees the world through his partner's eyes. In the chorus, he expresses his fear of being alone and how his sweetest dreams turn into nightmares without his partner. The second verse, sung by Jaden, echoes Justin's sentiments and highlights the importance of his partner in his life. The song ends with Justin repeating the chorus and emphasizing his inability to be himself without his partner.

Overall, the song is a heartfelt expression of love and reliance on a significant other. The lyrics are emotional and relatable, and Justin's smooth vocals bring the song to life. The addition of Jaden's verse adds a different perspective and adds to the song's appeal. It is a well-written and well-performed love song.
