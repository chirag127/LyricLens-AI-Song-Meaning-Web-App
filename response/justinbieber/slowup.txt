In "Slow Up" Justin Bieber encourages his partner to take things slow and not rush into anything. He doesn't want to hurt them and wants to make sure they're on the same page before moving forward. Bieber emphasizes that rushing into things can cause problems and that it's important to take their time to get to know each other and make sure their relationship is built on a solid foundation.

The song is a slow, romantic ballad that showcases Bieber's smooth vocals and emotional lyrics. The acoustic guitar adds to the intimate atmosphere of the song.

In summary, "Slow Up" is a heartfelt song about taking things slow in a relationship and not rushing into anything. Bieber's sincere lyrics and soulful performance make for a moving and romantic listening experience.
