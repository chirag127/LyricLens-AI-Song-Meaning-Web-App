"Get Used To It" is a romantic song by Justin Bieber about committing to a relationship and wanting the other person to get used to being loved and supported by him. In the song, Bieber reflects on the progression of their relationship, from being just friends to falling in love and wanting to be together forever. He also assures the other person that he is not going anywhere and will always be there for them. The lyrics are expressed in a lighthearted and playful manner, with references to specific dates and holidays to emphasize the longevity of their love. Overall, the song conveys a sense of dedication and commitment in the relationship.
