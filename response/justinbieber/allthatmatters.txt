"All That Matters" by Justin Bieber is a love song that showcases the singer's deep feelings for his significant other. In the lyrics, Justin expresses his need for his partner to be by his side, and how their love completes him.

In the first verse of the song, Justin sings about how he doesn't pay attention to other people or texts when he's not with his partner. He explains that his significant other is all that matters to him, and that he needs them to feel complete. The chorus of the song repeats the message of how his partner is all that matters to him, and how they complete each other as a team.

The second verse continues the theme of devotion and appreciation for his partner. Justin sings about how grateful he is for their existence, and how their love is faithful no matter the distance. The bridge of the song reiterates the message of the chorus, with Justin singing about how his partner is all that matters to him.

In terms of a review of the song, "All That Matters" is a heartfelt and romantic expression of love and devotion. Justin Bieber's vocals are strong and emotive, and the song has a catchy and infectious beat. Overall, it is a love song that will resonate with many listeners who are in long-term, committed relationships.
