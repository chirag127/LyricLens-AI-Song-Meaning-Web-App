"Beauty And A Beat (Acoustic Version)" by Justin Bieber is an acoustic rendition of the original song. It follows the same structure and lyrics as the original, with the singer expressing his desire to show off his romantic partner and party like it's the year 3012. The song celebrates the singer's success and wealth, and the beauty of his partner. The chorus repeats the phrase "beauty and a beat" and describes the singer's partner as the source of his happiness. The acoustic version of the song has a more stripped-down and intimate feel, compared to the original's energetic and upbeat vibe. Overall, the song could be considered a pop or acoustic pop song.
