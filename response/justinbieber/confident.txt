"Confident" is a song by Justin Bieber that features Chance the Rapper. The song is about a woman who catches Bieber's attention because of her confidence and sexuality. In the lyrics, Bieber describes her body and the way she moves, and expresses his attraction to her. Chance the Rapper's verse adds to the description of the woman, with references to her tattoos, piercings, and love of marijuana. Overall, the song is a celebration of a confident and sexual woman.
