"Christmas Eve" is a romantic holiday song by Justin Bieber. In the song, Bieber sings about spending Christmas with his love and how the holiday season brings them closer together. He talks about the traditions of Christmas, such as hanging stockings by the fireplace and waiting for Santa to arrive. He also mentions wanting to take his love on a sleigh ride and how he doesn't need any presents because she is all he needs.

In the chorus, Bieber sings about wanting to be his love's date on Christmas Eve and how he wants to spend the holiday with her, laying his head on her and kissing her under the Christmas tree. He also promises to never do her wrong and give her all of himself each year on Christmas Eve.

Overall, the song is a sweet and romantic ode to the magic of Christmas and the joy of spending it with the one you love.
