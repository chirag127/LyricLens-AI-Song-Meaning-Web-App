"Born To Be Somebody" is a song by Justin Bieber that showcases his determination and drive to succeed. The song opens with Bieber singing about the dream and passion that burns deep inside of him, and how he knows that one day he will be able to set it free and achieve greatness.

In the first verse, Bieber sings about how he will not let anyone or anything hold him back from reaching his goals. He speaks of how he will rise above any challenges and obstacles, and will not be deterred by those who try to bring him down.

The chorus of the song features Bieber singing about how he was "born to be somebody," and that nothing will ever stop him from achieving his dreams. He declares that he will "light up the sky like lightning," and will show the world what he is made of.

In the second verse, Bieber sings about how even though life can be tough and the world can make you feel small, he will not let it defeat him. He states that he was "born to stand tall" and will go all the way in pursuit of his dreams.

The song concludes with the chorus, with Bieber singing about how he was "born to be somebody" and that this world will one day belong to him.

Overall, "Born To Be Somebody" is an empowering and motivational song that encourages listeners to never give up on their dreams and to always strive for greatness. It is a celebration of the human spirit and the determination to succeed.
