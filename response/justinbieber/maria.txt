"Maria" by Justin Bieber is a song about a girl who falsely claims to have had a child with Justin Bieber. In the song, Justin denies ever meeting the woman and refutes her claims. The chorus of the song repeats the phrase "that ain't my baby, that ain't my girl" to emphasize Justin's denial. In the verses, Justin describes the woman's behavior, calling her "crazy" and saying that she is "faking it all."

The song is a response to rumors that were circulating at the time, and Justin uses the song to clear his name and set the record straight.

As a review of the song, "Maria" is a catchy and well-written track that effectively conveys Justin's message. The repetition of the chorus helps to hammer home Justin's denial, and the lyrics are relatable for anyone who has ever been the subject of false rumors or accusations. Overall, the song is a strong addition to Justin Bieber's catalog.
