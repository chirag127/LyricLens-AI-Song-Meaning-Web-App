"Home This Christmas" is a song by Justin Bieber, featuring The Band Perry. The song is about a person who is waiting for their significant other to come home on Christmas. The lyrics describe the person's anticipation and longing for their loved one to arrive, and express their desire to spend the holiday together.

In the first verse, the singer describes looking out the window and waiting for their loved one's headlights to pull up in the driveway. They mention that it is snowing outside and express their wish that their loved one was there with them, safe and warm.

The chorus repeats the line, "I'll be waiting under the mistletoe / While you're driving here, through the winter snow / Baby, think of me / If it helps to get you home." This highlights the singer's eagerness for their loved one to come home and their hope that thinking of them will help guide them through the storm.

The second verse describes the singer pacing nervously down the hallway, trying to fight the urge to call their loved one. They mention the silence in the house, with the only sound being the ticking of the clock on the wall. This verse also repeats the line from the first verse, "And I'm scared that you won't make it through the storm / You should be here with me, safe and warm."

In the bridge, the singer expresses their desire to spend the holiday together, saying "There's nothing else that I want this year / More than just to have you here / I'll be waiting."

Overall, "Home This Christmas" is a heartfelt song about longing for a loved one to come home for the holidays. It captures the emotions of missing someone and the desire to be together during the holiday season.
