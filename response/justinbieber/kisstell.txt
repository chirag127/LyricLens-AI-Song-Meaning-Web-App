"Kiss & Tell" is a song by Justin Bieber about a potential romantic encounter with a girl. In the song, Bieber sings about how he can sense a connection with the girl and that he is interested in kissing her. However, he also expresses his concern about the girl potentially spreading rumors or gossiping about their encounter. He asks her if she is going to "kiss and tell," or if she will keep their interaction private.

In the song, Bieber uses imagery of lips and chemistry to describe the attraction he feels towards the girl. He also uses the phrase "keep it on the hush" to emphasize the importance of discretion in their potential relationship. The song has a catchy, upbeat melody and Bieber's vocals are smooth and engaging.

Overall, "Kiss & Tell" is a song about the complexities of potential romance and the need for trust and discretion in relationships. It is a relatable and enjoyable listen for fans of Justin Bieber and pop music in general.
