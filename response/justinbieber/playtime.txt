In the song "Playtime," Justin Bieber and Khalil sing about wanting to move on from a relationship that has been plagued by games and arguing. They both express that they no longer have time for such behavior and want to focus on spending time together and enjoying each other's company. The song's chorus repeats the phrase "playtime is over" to emphasize the idea that they are ready to move on from the negative aspects of the relationship.

The song is upbeat and catchy, with a pop beat and smooth vocals from both Bieber and Khalil. The lyrics are relatable and express a common sentiment of wanting to move on from a toxic relationship. Overall, "Playtime" is a fun and upbeat song that will likely resonate with listeners who have experienced similar situations in their own relationships.
