"Second Emotion" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Travis Scott. In the song, Bieber sings about meeting someone and wanting to take things further with them, asking if they feel the same way. He expresses his attraction to this person and how he can't help but be drawn to them. The chorus repeats the line "Do you second that emotion?" as Bieber asks if the other person feels the same way he does. Travis Scott also appears on the track, adding a verse about his own experiences with love and attraction.

The song is upbeat and catchy, with a bouncy beat and smooth vocals from both Bieber and Scott. The lyrics are playful and flirty, expressing the excitement and uncertainty of a new romance. Overall, "Second Emotion" is a fun and lighthearted track that captures the thrill of falling for someone.
