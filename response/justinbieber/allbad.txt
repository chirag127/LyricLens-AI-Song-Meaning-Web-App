"All Bad" by Justin Bieber is a song about dealing with negativity and haters. In the song, Bieber addresses the people who try to bring him down and spread rumors about him. He asserts that he is not all bad, despite what they may say.

 

In the first verse, Bieber sings about the "instigators" who try to stir up drama and cause trouble. He suggests that they are like putting fire on propane, creating a volatile situation. He also references the way that some people like to talk about others behind their backs.

The chorus repeats the refrain "I ain't all bad" and asserts that Bieber is not as bad as his haters make him out to be. He acknowledges that he might make people mad at times, but he is not all bad.

In the second verse, Bieber addresses the people who try to come between him and his love interest. He suggests that these people are trying to be your love interest's "best friend" in order to create conflict. He also addresses the envy and jealousy that some people feel towards him.

The bridge repeats the chorus and reinforces the message that Bieber is not all bad, despite what his haters may say.

In summary, "All Bad" is a song about dealing with negativity and haters. Bieber asserts that he is not all bad and encourages listeners to not let others change their mood or bring them down.
