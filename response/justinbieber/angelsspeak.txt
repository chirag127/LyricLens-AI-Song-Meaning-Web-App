The song "Angels Speak" by Justin Bieber is about the singer being captivated by the voice and energy of a person who he believes is an angel. In the first verse, Justin Bieber describes the person's voice as being like music to his ears and he is drawn to the way their frequencies resonate. The second verse describes the person as having a positive impact on others without trying and their boundaries keep Bieber in line. The third verse highlights the person's obvious intentions and miraculous affection, and the chorus repeats the idea that only angels speak like this person does. The bridge describes the cadence of the person's voice and how it keeps them in sync. The outro repeats the chorus.
