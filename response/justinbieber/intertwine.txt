In "Intertwine," Justin Bieber and Lil Twist sing about wanting to spend time with a potential romantic partner and offer them their company to avoid loneliness. The chorus repeats the line "What's the use of being lonely when I'm offering my company?" and suggests that the two singers want to intertwine with their partner and make time to be together.

In the first verse, Bieber sings about how the potential partner could be his lover and that he would give them attention all day and all night. Lil Twist's verse discusses how the potential partner caught his eye at a club and how he wants to get to know her better. He offers to take her out to eat and spend time with her, and mentions that she shouldn't be lonely with him around.

The song has a laid-back, R&B feel and the singers' smooth vocals add to the romantic atmosphere. Overall, "Intertwine" is a love song about wanting to spend time with a special someone and offering them companionship to avoid loneliness.
