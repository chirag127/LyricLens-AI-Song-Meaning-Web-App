"The Christmas Song (Chestnuts Roasting on an Open Fire)" is a classic holiday song written by Mel Tormé and Bob Wells. Justin Bieber, along with Usher, perform a cover of the song in their own style.

In the song, the singers describe the various sights and sounds of the holiday season. They also mention the traditional holiday elements of turkey and mistletoe, and how they help to make the season bright. The singers also mention Santa and his sleigh, and how excited children are to see if reindeer can really fly.

Throughout the song, the singers offer the phrase "Merry Christmas" as a simple but heartfelt message to everyone. They encourage people of all ages to enjoy the joy and magic of the season.

Overall, "The Christmas Song (Chestnuts Roasting on an Open Fire)" is a classic holiday song that captures the spirit and magic of the season. Justin Bieber and Usher's cover adds a fresh and contemporary twist to the song, making it appealing to a new generation of listeners.
