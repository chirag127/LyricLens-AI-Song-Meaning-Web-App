"Double Negative" is a song by Justin Bieber that explores the idea of a "double negative" relationship, where the singer is causing harm and disappointment to their significant other but also proving them right in their doubts. The lyrics describe the singer's behavior, such as showing up late, not answering calls, and constantly lying. The song also touches on the singer's fame and how it may contribute to the negative dynamic in the relationship.

In the first verse, the singer talks about not sleeping and running a lie, doing their partner wrong and proving them right each time. This is described as a "double negative" situation. The singer then talks about showing up late and not picking up the phone, further highlighting their neglectful behavior.

The chorus repeats the phrase "double negative" and describes the singer's actions as causing shame on themselves.

In the second verse, the singer talks about traveling and being away from their partner, wishing they could physically send their heart to their partner's doorstep. However, they continue to let their partner down and make them cry.

The third verse brings in the perspective of the partner, who is critical of the singer's lack of boundaries and patience. The singer then reflects on their own behavior and the negative energy surrounding them. They mention trying to shake off the negativity and acknowledging that their actions may have consequences.

Overall, "Double Negative" is a reflective and introspective song that explores the negative effects of a toxic relationship. The lyrics are relatable and thought-provoking, and the song has a catchy and upbeat melody.
