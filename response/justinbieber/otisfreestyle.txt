In the second verse, Bieber talks about his relationship with his girlfriend and how she thinks he's perfect. He also brags about his success and how he has many fans. He also compares himself to Zack Morris and says that he's saving people from hell.

In the final verse, Bieber talks about how people are envious of him and how he's a mystery. He also says that his fans are like his glasses, and without them he wouldn't be able to see clearly.

Overall, "Otis (Freestyle)" is a boastful and self-aggrandizing song where Bieber talks about his success and wealth. The lyrics and flow are not particularly impressive, but the song is still an entertaining listen.
