"Alone" by Justin Bieber is a heartfelt and emotional song that explores the effects of a busy schedule on a relationship. In the lyrics, Justin sings about how he and his partner were once inseparable, but his busy schedule and the demands of his career ended up causing a rift between them. The song explores the feelings of regret and guilt that Justin experiences as a result of this, as well as the feelings of loneliness and isolation that his partner experiences.

In the first verse of the song, Justin sings about the memories they made together and how incredible they were. However, he also mentions how their love was interrupted by his schedule, and he was unable to spend as much time with his partner as he wanted to. The chorus repeats the message of Justin's regret and guilt, with him singing about how his partner has adapted to being alone and how they've gotten used to it.

The second verse continues the theme of regret, with Justin singing about how he saw the rift between them growing but was unable to do anything about it. He expresses regret about not being able to divide his time and give his partner the attention they needed. The bridge of the song repeats the chorus, further emphasizing Justin's feelings of guilt and regret.

In terms of a review of the song, "Alone" is a powerful and emotional ballad that showcases Justin Bieber's vulnerable side. The lyrics are relatable and heartfelt, and Justin's vocals are strong and emotive. Overall, it is a song that will resonate with many listeners who have experienced the effects of a busy schedule on their relationships.
