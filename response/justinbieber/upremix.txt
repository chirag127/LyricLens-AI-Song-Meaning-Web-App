"Up (Remix)" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Chris Brown. In the song, Bieber expresses his love and commitment to his partner, promising to always be there for her and to take their relationship to new heights. He sings about making the sun shine in the moonlight and turning grey clouds into blue skies, showing that he is willing to do whatever it takes to make her happy. The chorus repeats the phrase "we can go nowhere but up," expressing the belief that their love will only grow stronger and that they will overcome any challenges that come their way.

Chris Brown's verse adds to the theme of unwavering love and support, promising to show his partner the world and to always be there for her. He also sings about believing in their love and the strength it gives them to face any obstacles.

Overall, the song is a upbeat and positive love song that celebrates the power of commitment and dedication in a relationship. It encourages listeners to believe in the potential of their own relationships and to strive for growth and success together.
