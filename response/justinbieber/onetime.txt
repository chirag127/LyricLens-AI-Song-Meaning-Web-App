"One Time" is a song by Justin Bieber, released as the lead single from his debut studio album, My World 2.0. The song was written and produced by Christopher "Tricky" Stewart and Terius "The-Dream" Nash.

In the song, Bieber sings about meeting a girl and falling in love with her. He describes the feeling of butterflies in his stomach and how their love is all they have. He also talks about how their worlds are intertwined and how he will always be there for her.

One line that stands out is "Your love's so deep / You know that it humbles me." This shows the depth of the feelings Bieber has for this girl and how she has humbled him with her love.

Overall, "One Time" is a sweet and romantic love song that showcases Bieber's vocal abilities and his talent for crafting relatable lyrics. The catchy chorus and upbeat production make it a fun and enjoyable listen.
