"Deserve You" is a song by Justin Bieber that explores the theme of self-doubt and insecurity in a romantic relationship. In the lyrics, Bieber expresses his fears that he may not be good enough for his partner and that he may revert back to his old ways. He admits that he has a hard time believing that he is the person his partner thinks he is, and he worries that he may not be deserving of their love. Despite these doubts, Bieber ultimately affirms his commitment to the relationship and expresses his need for his partner's love and support.

In the first verse, Bieber reflects on his self-doubt and insecurity, admitting that it can be difficult for him to believe that he is the person his partner thinks he is. He also reveals that he has a history of letting go of things that are important to him, but he is determined not to let that happen in this relationship.

The chorus repeats the refrain "I feel like I don't deserve you tonight," highlighting the singer's insecurities and doubts about the relationship. However, he also notes that the way his partner holds him and the things they show him help him to feel more confident and assured. He expresses his need for his partner's love and support, and reaffirms his commitment to the relationship.

In the second verse, Bieber reveals that he has a tendency to hold back and not fully open up to his partner. He admits that he needs their love and support more than he lets on, and expresses his fear that he may be too much for them to handle. He also expresses his desire to leave his past behind and to be the person his partner deserves, and he emphasizes the importance of their relationship to him.

The third verse finds Bieber in a more confident and assured state, as he reflects on the moments in the relationship that make him feel alive and happy. He describes the feeling of being "right where I belong" with his partner, and he affirms his commitment to the relationship.

Overall, "Deserve You" is a heartfelt and vulnerable song that explores the theme of self-doubt and insecurity in a romantic relationship. Bieber's emotive vocals and heartfelt lyrics make for a compelling and relatable listening experience.
