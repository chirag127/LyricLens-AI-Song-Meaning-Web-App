"Somebody To Love (Remix)" is a song by Justin Bieber, featuring Usher. The song is about needing someone to love and be with. In the lyrics, Justin Bieber and Usher sing about how they would do anything for the person they love, even if it means running a thousand miles or writing a symphony. They also express their desire for a loving relationship and how money cannot buy them the love they need.

The song is upbeat and catchy, with a strong beat and a chorus that is easy to sing along to. The collaboration with Usher adds a new dimension to the song, with his smooth vocals complementing Justin Bieber's youthful energy.

Overall, "Somebody To Love (Remix)" is a fun and catchy love song that showcases Justin Bieber's talent and versatility as an artist.
