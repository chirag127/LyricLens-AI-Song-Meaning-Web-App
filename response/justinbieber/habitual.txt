The song "Habitual" by Justin Bieber is a love song about a deep and committed relationship. In the first verse, the singer describes the natural and organic way that the relationship has grown, comparing it to the way that flowers open in sunlight and the tides change. He also talks about the physical attraction between the two, but emphasizes that the relationship goes deeper than just physical touching. The chorus repeats the phrase "my love for you is habitual," meaning that it is a constant and ongoing part of their lives.

In the second verse, the singer reflects on how their relationship has developed over time and how it has changed their perspective on life. He also mentions that their love is unconventional, but that it is perfect for them. The chorus repeats again, emphasizing the ongoing nature of their love.

Overall, the song is a celebration of a deep and lasting love that has become a habitual part of the singer's life. It is a romantic and heartfelt love song.
