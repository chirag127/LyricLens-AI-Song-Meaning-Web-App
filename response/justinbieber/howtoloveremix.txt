"How To Love (Remix)" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring rapper Lil Wayne. The song is a remix of Lil Wayne's original track "How to Love" and features Bieber singing the chorus and Wayne rapping the verses.

In the song, Wayne describes a woman who has experienced heartache and failed relationships in the past. He tells her that she needs to learn how to love and be loved in return, and offers himself as a potential partner who can help her do that. Bieber's chorus talks about taking things slow and enjoying the moment, rather than rushing into a relationship.

Overall, the song is a smooth and catchy R&B track with a message of love and support. Wayne's verses are clever and well-delivered, and Bieber's chorus adds a nice touch of pop appeal. The song is a great addition to Bieber's catalog and a solid collaboration with Wayne.
