In "There She Go," Justin Bieber sings about a girl who catches his attention with her beauty and moves. He describes how she makes him feel and how he wants to be with her. The song is upbeat and catchy, with a simple, repetitive chorus. The overall theme of the song is admiration and attraction.

In the first verse, Bieber sings about how the girl's presence makes him feel and how she catches the attention of others with her dancing and appearance. In the second verse, he talks about how he wants to take the time to get to know her better and make her his own. The chorus repeats the line, "there she go," as a way to emphasize the girl's movement and presence.

In the third verse, Bieber sings about how he wants to be with the girl and make her happy. He also mentions how he has changed for the better since meeting her. The song ends with the chorus repeating once more, highlighting the girl's presence and the singer's admiration for her.

Overall, "There She Go" is a fun and upbeat song that captures the feeling of admiration and attraction. Bieber's smooth vocals and the catchy beat make it a pleasurable listen.
