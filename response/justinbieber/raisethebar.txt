"Raise The Bar" by Justin Bieber is a romantic ballad in which Bieber sings about the special connection he has with a woman. In the first verse, he describes her eyes and how they change like the seasons. He also says that she makes him speechless, and he believes that they were meant to meet for a reason.

In the chorus, Bieber sings about wanting to raise the bar and set high standards in their relationship. He wants to take their love to the next level and make her fall in love again. He also acknowledges that trust can be difficult in relationships and promises to do his best to make her feel secure.

In the second verse, Bieber sings about wanting to teach each other how to run and fly, and to give each other everything. He repeats the chorus, emphasizing the importance of raising the bar and setting high standards in their relationship.

Overall, "Raise The Bar" is a heartfelt and romantic song that celebrates the special connection between two people and their commitment to building a strong and loving relationship.
