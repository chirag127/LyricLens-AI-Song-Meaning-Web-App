"Sorry (Latino Remix)" is a remix of Justin Bieber's hit song "Sorry," featuring J Balvin. In the song, Bieber sings about his regret for making mistakes and hurting his partner. He begs for forgiveness and a second chance, expressing his love for the person and his desire to make things right. J Balvin's verse adds a Latin twist to the song, with him singing about his own mistakes and asking for forgiveness from his partner.

In the chorus, Bieber sings, "Is it too late now to say sorry? / 'Cause I'm missing more than just your body / Is it too late now to say sorry? / Yeah I know that I let you down / Is it too late to say I'm sorry now?" The chorus conveys the feeling of desperation and regret that comes with realizing the impact of one's actions on a relationship.

In the verse, J Balvin sings, "Quisiera regresar el tiempo y cambiar errores / Sé que todo fue mi culpa y te causé dolores / Espero que de volver tú tengas intenciones / Porque hoy estoy aquí extrañándote" which translates to "I wish I could turn back time and change my mistakes / I know it was all my fault and I caused you pain / I hope you have intentions of coming back / Because today I'm here missing you." This verse emphasizes the remorse and longing that comes with losing a loved one.

Overall, "Sorry (Latino Remix)" is a heartfelt and emotional song about love, regret, and the desire for redemption and forgiveness. The addition of J Balvin's verse adds a unique and refreshing flavor to the original song.
