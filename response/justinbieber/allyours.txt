"All Yours" by Justin Bieber is a romantic and sweet love song that showcases the singer's devotion to his partner. In the lyrics, Justin sings about opening up his heart and being there for his partner, no matter what they need. The second verse continues the theme of devotion and commitment, with Justin singing about his partner's attitude and how it draws him in. He also mentions knowing their secrets and being able to keep them safe. The bridge of the song repeats the chorus, further emphasizing Justin's love and devotion to his partner.

In terms of a review of the song, "All Yours" is a sweet and romantic love song that showcases Justin Bieber's emotional and vulnerable side.
