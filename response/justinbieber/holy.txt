"Holy" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Chance the Rapper. The song is about finding true love and the feeling of being in a relationship that feels holy. In the first verse, Bieber sings about how he hears a lot about sinners but doesn't think he'll be a saint. However, he admits to feeling a deep connection with his partner and wanting to go down to the river with them. The chorus repeats the line "The way you hold me feels so holy" and describes running to the altar like a track star.

In the second verse, Bieber sings about not wanting drama or fake relationships and not believing in nirvana. However, he credits his partner with giving him life and being unable to explain the feelings they bring out in him. Chance the Rapper joins in for the third verse, rapping about the challenges of finding true love and the importance of faith in a relationship. He also references God and His role in their relationship.

Overall, the song is a romantic ballad about finding true love and the feeling of being in a relationship that feels "holy." The lyrics are emotional and the production is smooth and mellow, allowing Bieber and Chance the Rapper's vocals to shine. The song is a beautiful ode to the power of true love and the feeling of being in a relationship that feels divine.
