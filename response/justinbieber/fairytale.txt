"Fairytale" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Jaden Smith. In the song, Justin Bieber tells a romantic interest that he will be her "prince charming" and promises to provide her with the perfect wedding and a "storybook ending." Jaden Smith also contributes a rap verse, in which he expresses his love and willingness to go to great lengths to make his partner happy. Overall, the song is a lighthearted and romantic ode to love and commitment.
