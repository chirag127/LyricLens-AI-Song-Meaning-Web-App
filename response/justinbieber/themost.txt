In "The Most," Justin Bieber reflects on a past relationship and the feelings of abandonment he experienced when his partner left him. The song opens with Bieber asking where his former partner is now that he needs them the most. He recalls how he stayed by their side when they were going through a difficult time, but now that he needs support, they are nowhere to be found. In the chorus, Bieber repeats the line "where are you now that I need you" as a way of emphasizing the lack of support he is feeling.

Throughout the song, Bieber reflects on the ways in which he tried to support his partner and make them happy, but despite his efforts, they still left him. He acknowledges that he gave them his heart and his attention when nobody else was paying attention to them, and he even gave them the shirt off his back. Despite these gestures, they still left him when he needed them the most.

In the final verse, Bieber expresses his frustration and sadness at being left alone, and he repeats the line "where are you now that I need you" once again as a way of emphasizing his feelings of abandonment. Overall, "The Most" is a heart-wrenching song about the pain of being left behind by someone you love.

As a song, "The Most" is a powerful and emotional reflection on the feeling of being abandoned by someone you love. Bieber's vocals are raw and emotional, and the lyrics are relatable and thought-provoking. The chorus is particularly impactful, with the repeated line "where are you now that I need you" serving as a haunting reminder of the pain and betrayal that can come with love. Overall, "The Most" is a moving and powerful song that showcases Bieber's talent as a singer and songwriter.
