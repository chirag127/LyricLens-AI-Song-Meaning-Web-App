The song "Only Thing I Ever Get For Christmas" by Justin Bieber is a romantic ballad about the singer's desire to have a special someone as his only Christmas gift. In the first verse, Bieber sings about how this person is the only thing he wishes for and how he will be waiting for them under the mistletoe. In the chorus, he repeats the line "If you're the only thing I ever get for Christmas, then everything I wished for has come true" to emphasize his love and longing for this person.

In the second verse, Bieber describes the hustle and bustle of Christmas morning and how he doesn't care about the presents under the tree because he only wants this special someone. He then sings about how this person is the only one who can make his Christmas truly merry.

The song has a soft, romantic feel and Bieber's smooth, emotional vocals add to the sentimentality of the lyrics. Overall, the song is a sweet ode to the joy and magic of finding the right person to share the holiday season with.
