"Old School Flow" by Justin Bieber is a song that showcases the artist's ability to rap and deliver a flow reminiscent of old-school hip hop. In the song, Bieber boasts about his skills and success, comparing himself to iconic figures like Michael Jordan and Gordon Ramsay. He also addresses critics who may doubt his abilities, insisting that he writes his own lyrics and can hold his own in the rap game. The song ends with a reference to the classic film "Ferris Bueller's Day Off," with Bieber declaring himself "cooler" than the titular character.

Overall, "Old School Flow" is a confident and energetic track that showcases Bieber's versatility as an artist. The old-school flow and references to classic figures in pop culture add a nostalgic touch to the song, making it a fun and enjoyable listen.
