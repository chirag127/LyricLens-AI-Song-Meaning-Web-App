In "E.T.A" Justin Bieber sings about longing for his significant other and wanting to know when they will be together again. In the first verse, he describes how time seems to be moving slowly without them, and he's trying to distract himself to avoid going "looney" over missing them. In the chorus, he repeatedly asks for their estimated time of arrival (E.T.A) and admits that the distance between them has only made him grow fonder of them. In the second verse, he expresses his eagerness for their arrival and describes how ready he is for their return. The song has a upbeat, pop melody and reflects on the difficulties of being apart from the one you love.
