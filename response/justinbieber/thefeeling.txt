In the song "The Feeling" by Justin Bieber, the singer discusses his feelings for someone and wonders if he is truly in love with the person or just in love with the feeling of being in love. He describes the person as being a part of him and pulling him in like gravity, but also admits to being confused and not knowing what is good for him. The chorus repeats the question, "Am I in love with you? Or am I in love with the feeling?" Halsey joins in for the chorus, singing about trying to find the truth and wondering if she is crazy for being in love.

In summary, the song is about the uncertainty and confusion that can come with falling in love and not being sure if it is real or just a fleeting feeling.

As for a review or opinion of the song, it could be said that the lyrics are relatable and capture the uncertainty and confusion that can come with falling in love. The combination of Justin Bieber and Halsey's voices in the chorus adds an interesting dynamic to the song. Overall, it is a catchy and emotionally resonant track.
