In the song "Name" by Justin Bieber, the singer discusses a past relationship that has ended, with the other person moving on to someone else. The singer also reflects on the good times they shared, reminiscing about laying on a Goodwill couch and going line for line at their best friend's house. In the end, the singer admits that even after all this time, they still feel the same about the other person.

Overall, the song is a nostalgic and emotional reflection on a past relationship and the feelings that still linger after its end. The singer's pleading for the other person to call out their name shows their desire for a second chance and the hope that their feelings may still be reciprocated.

As a song, "Name" showcases Justin Bieber's vocal abilities and emotional range, making it a standout track on his album. The lyrics are relatable and poignant, capturing the feeling of longing for a past love. The song's production is also well-done, with a mellow beat and backing vocals adding to the emotional atmosphere. Overall, "Name" is a touching and well-crafted addition to Justin Bieber's discography.
