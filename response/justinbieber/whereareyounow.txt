"Where Are You Now" by Justin Bieber is a song that expresses the singer's feelings of loneliness and longing for someone who is no longer with him. In the song, Bieber asks where the person is when he needs them the most and why they are not there to support him. He also asks for guidance and help to stay strong and to be shown the right path in life.

The song has a melancholic and emotional tone, with Bieber's vocals conveying a sense of vulnerability and desperation. The lyrics are deeply personal and reveal the singer's feelings of isolation and abandonment. The song also touches on the theme of growing up and becoming independent, with Bieber asking where the person is now that he is "half grown."

Overall, "Where Are You Now" is a poignant and heartfelt ballad that showcases Justin Bieber's ability to convey emotional depth and vulnerability through his music. The song is a raw and honest expression of the singer's feelings, and is sure to resonate with listeners who have experienced similar feelings of loneliness and longing.
