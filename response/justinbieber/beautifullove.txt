"Beautiful Love" by Justin Bieber is a love song about appreciating the beauty in life and in each other. The first verse of the song talks about how people often take the beauty around them for granted, and suggests that seeing the world through the eyes of a child can help us appreciate it more. The second verse speaks about starting each day with a sense of gratitude, and recognizing that we are not perfect but that our journey is worth it. The chorus repeats the phrase "beautiful love" and describes the singer's partner as a source of beauty in their life. The bridge and outro of the song reinforce the theme of appreciating the beauty in love. Overall, the song has a positive and uplifting mood, and could be considered a pop or R&B song.
