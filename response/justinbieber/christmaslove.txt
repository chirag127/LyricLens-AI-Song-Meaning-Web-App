"Christmas Love" is a holiday love song by Justin Bieber. In the song, Bieber sings about his love for someone and how they are the best gift he could have received this Christmas. He describes his love as a "beautiful tree" that lights up the room and a "beautiful carol" that he gets lost in. He also expresses his gratitude to God for bringing this person into his life.

In the chorus, Bieber sings about being under the mistletoe with his love and telling Santa that he doesn't need any other gifts because he has this person in his life. He also encourages others to stay close to the ones they love and be thankful for the love they have.

Overall, the song is a sweet and romantic ode to the joy of being with someone you love during the holiday season. It captures the excitement and warmth of the season and celebrates the special bond between two people.
