"Heartache" is a song by Justin Bieber that describes the pain and sadness of a break-up. In the song, Bieber reflects on the moments of happiness and promise that he shared with his partner, but ultimately acknowledges that the relationship has come to an end.

In the first verse, Bieber describes the beginning of his relationship with his partner, saying that it started out "perfect" and "nothing but fun." He expresses confidence that the relationship would last forever, but is ultimately surprised when it ends. He also reflects on the feelings of loss and confusion that come with a break-up.

In the second verse, Bieber describes the feeling of being alone in his room after the break-up and wanting to "get out of here." He reflects on the promise that he and his partner made to each other and the fact that she is now with another guy.

In the chorus, Bieber sings about the pain of heartbreak and the difficulty of moving past it. He describes experiencing a headache and feeling like he can't shake the pain of the break-up. He also acknowledges that it will take time to heal, but admits that he wants to lay there and let the pain wash over him.

Overall, "Heartache" is a heart-wrenching song that captures the emotions of a break-up and the difficulty of moving past the pain. The song is reflective and introspective, and explores the feelings of loss and confusion that come with a break-up.
