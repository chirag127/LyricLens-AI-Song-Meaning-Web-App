"Friends (dvsn Remix)" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring dvsn. The song is a remix of Bieber's original song "Friends" and features additional vocals from dvsn. In the song, Bieber sings about wanting to remain friends with an ex-partner, even though their relationship didn't end well. Dvsn adds his own perspective on the situation, singing about the challenges of remaining friends with an ex and the potential for old feelings to resurface. The song has a laid-back, R&B vibe and features a catchy chorus where Bieber and dvsn both sing about wanting to remain friends. Overall, the song is a relatable and emotional exploration of the complexities of post-relationship friendship.
