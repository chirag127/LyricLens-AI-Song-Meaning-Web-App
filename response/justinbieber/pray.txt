"Pray" is a song by Justin Bieber that expresses concern and sadness about the state of the world. In the song, Bieber sings about seeing the news and feeling overwhelmed by the suffering and injustice he sees. He expresses a desire to make a change and to help those in need. The song also includes a call for prayer, asking for guidance and strength in making a positive impact on the world.

In the first verse, Bieber sings about feeling unable to sleep because he is troubled by the things he sees on TV and in the news. He mentions children crying, soldiers dying, and people without homes, alluding to the suffering and injustice that exists in the world.

In the chorus, Bieber expresses his hope for a better future, stating that he knows there is sunshine beyond the rain and good times beyond the pain. He also expresses a desire to make a difference and asks for guidance on how to do so.

In the second verse, Bieber sings about losing his appetite as he thinks about children starving and the excess food on his own plate. He expresses a vision to make a difference and to start doing so today.

The song concludes with a call for prayer, asking for help for the brokenhearted, the unborn, and those in need. Bieber also repeats his earlier call for guidance and strength in making a positive impact on the world.

Overall, "Pray" is a powerful and emotional song that speaks to the need for compassion and action in the face of suffering and injustice. Bieber's sincere and heartfelt lyrics, coupled with his emotive vocal performance, make for a moving and impactful listening experience.
