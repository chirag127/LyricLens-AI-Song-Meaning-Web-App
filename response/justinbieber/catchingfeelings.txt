"Catching Feelings" is a song by Justin Bieber in which he sings about developing romantic feelings for a friend. In the first verse, Bieber describes how this person is always on his mind, even in his dreams. He expresses uncertainty about whether this relationship is meant to be and wonders what he should do. In the chorus, he admits to "catching feelings" for this person and wonders if it could be a possibility for them to be together. In the second verse, he admits that he wants to be with this person and is willing to do whatever it takes to make it happen. However, he is unsure of how to approach the situation and whether the other person feels the same way.

In terms of a review or opinion of the song, it is a catchy and relatable tune that many people can relate to. The lyrics accurately capture the feelings of uncertainty and nervousness that often accompany developing romantic feelings for a friend. Overall, "Catching Feelings" is a well-written and enjoyable song.
