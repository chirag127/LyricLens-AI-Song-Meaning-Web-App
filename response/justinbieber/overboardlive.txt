"Overboard (Live)" is a live performance by Justin Bieber and Miley Cyrus. In the song, the two singers describe feeling lost and alone in a relationship, struggling to communicate and connect with each other. They express their need for the other person's love and support, and their willingness to work together to make the relationship work.

Justin Bieber then takes over, singing about not understanding his partner's perspective and feeling like he is doing his part in the relationship. He realizes that things have changed and he now sees the importance of their love. He reaches out to his partner, begging for their love and support.

In the chorus, both singers express their need for the other's love, and their fear of drowning without it. They call on their partner to be their "life saver" and save them from the struggles they are facing.

The second verse continues the theme of needing the other person's love and support to survive. Justin Bieber sings about the give and take in a relationship, and how his partner is only taking without giving anymore. Despite this, he still loves them and wants to make things work.

In the final chorus, the singers repeat their plea for their partner to be their life saver, and they express their willingness to work together to make their relationship strong.

Overall, "Overboard (Live)" is a heartfelt and emotional song about the struggles and challenges of a relationship, and the need for love and support to overcome them. The live performance showcases the vocal talents of both Justin Bieber and Miley Cyrus, and their chemistry as they sing and harmonize together.
