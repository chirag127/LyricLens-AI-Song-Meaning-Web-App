"Baby" is a song by Canadian singer Justin Bieber, featuring American rapper Ludacris. The song is about a young man who is dealing with the end of his relationship with his first love. He is heartbroken and struggling to accept that she is no longer a part of his life.

In the first verse, Justin Bieber sings about how much he loves and cares for his partner, and how he will always be there for her. He asks if they are still an item, but she seems to be playing games and denying their relationship. He is hurt when she tells him that there is someone else, and he is left wondering what went wrong.

In the chorus, Bieber sings about how he thought their love would last forever, but now he realizes that it is over. He repeats the refrain "Baby, baby, baby, oh" as a reflection of his heartbreak and confusion.

Ludacris joins in the second verse, and raps about his own experiences with his first love. He remembers how they were inseparable and how she made his heart pound and skip a beat. However, now their relationship is over and he is left feeling sad and alone.

In the final verse, Bieber repeats the chorus and reflects on the fact that he is now "all gone" without his first love.

Overall, "Baby" is a poignant and emotional song that captures the pain and confusion of a young man dealing with the end of a relationship. It is a relatable and well-written song that showcases Bieber's vocal talents and his ability to connect with his audience.
