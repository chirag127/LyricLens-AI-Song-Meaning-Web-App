"One Life" is a romantic ballad by Justin Bieber in which he expresses his love and commitment to his partner. In the song, Bieber sings about wanting to live his life with his partner and be there for her through the good and the bad. The second verse continues this theme, with Bieber expressing his desire to be with his partner and show her the love and commitment he has for her.

The chorus of the song is simple and repetitive, with Bieber singing "I only wanna live it with you" to emphasize his desire to spend his life with his partner. The song also has a catchy beat and a smooth, romantic feel to it, making it a great choice for anyone looking for a romantic ballad to dedicate to their significant other.

Overall, "One Life" is a sweet and romantic song that showcases Justin Bieber's commitment to his partner. The song's simple and repetitive chorus, along with its catchy beat, make it an enjoyable listen and a great choice for anyone looking for a romantic ballad to dedicate to their loved one.
