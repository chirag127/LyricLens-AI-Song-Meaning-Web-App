"Never Let You Go" by Justin Bieber is a love song about the singer's deep devotion to his partner. In the first verse, the singer references a fictional world where hate has been declared illegal and love is all that remains. He asks his partner for one last hug before this world becomes a reality.

In the chorus, the singer describes his love as heavenly and compares his partner to an angel. He expresses his determination to never let her go and invites her to dance with him to the music. He also dismisses any doubters or skeptics, insisting that their love is too strong to be affected by outside opinions.

In the second verse, the singer describes the joy and freedom he feels when he's with his partner. He compares the moment to finally catching a dream he's been chasing and vows to take more chances and not hesitate in their relationship.

In the final chorus, the singer repeats the refrain of "I'll never let you go" and encourages his partner to dance with him and not be afraid. He reassures her that he will always be there for her and never let her go.

Overall, "Never Let You Go" is a romantic and heartfelt song about the deep love and devotion between two people. The lyrics are sweet and expressive, showcasing the singer's commitment to his partner and their relationship.
