In "Ghost," Justin Bieber reflects on a past relationship that ended, and he misses his former partner dearly. He sings about how time cannot be borrowed and that he would leave everything behind if he could follow his former partner. Bieber expresses that even though his former partner is not physically present, the memories of them bring him joy and keep him going.

The song is emotional and showcases Bieber's vocal range and depth. It is a relatable song for anyone who has experienced the pain of lost love. Overall, it is a beautifully crafted song with a powerful message.
