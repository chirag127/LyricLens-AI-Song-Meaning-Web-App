"All Around The World" by Justin Bieber is a song about the universal desire for love and connection. In the song, Bieber encourages his love interest to embrace her beauty and not be shy about showing it. The chorus repeats the refrain "all around the world, people want to be loved" and emphasizes that, despite our differences, we all share this fundamental human desire. The song also features a guest verse from Ludacris, in which he promotes self-confidence and perseverance in the face of adversity.

The chorus repeats the theme of the universal desire for love and emphasizes that, despite our differences, we all share this desire. 

In the second verse, Bieber continues to encourage his love interest to embrace her beauty and not be shy about showing it. He also repeats the line about the DJ bringing the music back. 

Ludacris' guest verse focuses on the importance of self-confidence and perseverance. He encourages the listener to make the most of their time and not give in to negativity. He also reiterates the song's central theme of the universal desire for love. 

The song's bridge repeats the chorus and adds the refrain "all around the world, they're no different than us" to further emphasize the universal nature of the desire for love. 

In summary, "All Around The World" is a upbeat and empowering song that encourages self-confidence and promotes the idea that, despite our differences, we all share a fundamental human desire for love and connection.
