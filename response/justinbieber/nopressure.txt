"No Pressure" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Big Sean. In the song, Bieber is singing to someone who is hesitant about continuing their relationship. He tells them that they don't have to make a decision right away and encourages them to take their time and not rush into anything. Bieber also admits to making mistakes in the past and promises to do better in the future. Big Sean also chimes in, rapping about the struggles in their relationship and how they need to work through their issues together.

The song has a smooth, R&B vibe and Bieber's vocals are emotive and heartfelt. The lyrics are relatable and honest, and the song showcases the ups and downs of a relationship. Overall, "No Pressure" is a well-crafted and emotional track.
