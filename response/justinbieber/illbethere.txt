"I'll Be There" by Justin Bieber is a romantic ballad about a couple who are separated by distance but remain committed to each other. In the song, Bieber sings about how he'll always be there for his partner, even when they're apart. The lyrics express his love and devotion, and the chorus repeats the phrase "I'll be there" as a promise to always be there for his partner.

The chorus repeats the phrase "I'll be there" as a promise to always be there for his partner, even when he's gone. He sings about holding them close to his heart and being there for them no matter what.

In the second verse, Bieber sings about how he's always checking his phone to see if his partner has contacted him. He also expresses his desire to keep their love on track and promises to never let go of their heart.

Overall, "I'll Be There" is a sweet and heartfelt love song that expresses the depth of Bieber's feelings for his partner. The lyrics are simple and straightforward, but the sentiment is strong and genuine. The song is a beautiful ode to the power of love and commitment.
