"Stress" by Justin Bieber is a song about a relationship where the singer is trying to reassure his partner that she doesn't need to worry about him leaving her. He tells her that he is the only guy she needs and that there is a "viable term" in their relationship. He tells her not to stress over him and to trust in him. The song has a pop/R&B sound with a catchy beat and smooth vocals from Bieber.

In the first verse, Bieber sings about how he is the only guy that his partner sees and that she is the only girl he needs. He tells her not to stress and to trust in him. In the chorus, he repeats the line "baby don't stress" and encourages her to take a deep breath and relax.

The second verse continues with the theme of not stressing and trusting in the relationship. Bieber sings about taking things slow and enjoying their time together. He also assures her that she can trust him and that he will never leave her.

The song has a positive message about trust and communication in a relationship. It encourages partners to be open and honest with each other and to not let stress get in the way of their happiness. The catchy beat and smooth vocals make for an enjoyable listening experience. Overall, "Stress" is a fun and upbeat song about the importance of trust in a relationship.
