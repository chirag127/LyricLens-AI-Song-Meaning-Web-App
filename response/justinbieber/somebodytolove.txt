"Somebody To Love" is a song by Canadian singer Justin Bieber. The song is about the narrator's search for love and connection, and how he is willing to do anything to find someone to share his life with.

In the first verse, Bieber sings about how he would go to great lengths to show his love for someone. He would write a symphony and play it for them, or run a thousand miles just to be with them. He describes the beat of his heart as a rhythm that he wants to share with someone special.

In the chorus, Bieber repeats the refrain "I just need somebody to love" as he expresses his desire for a loving and supportive partner. He doesn't need anything else, as long as he has someone to share his life with.

In the second verse, Bieber continues to describe the ways in which he would make his partner happy. He would bring them the sun and sweep away the clouds, and spend every second and every dime to make them happy.

In the final chorus, Bieber repeats the refrain and adds the line "Is she out there?" as he continues to search for the person who will be his perfect match.

Overall, "Somebody To Love" is a catchy and upbeat song that explores the universal desire for love and connection. Bieber's vocals are strong and emotive, and the song is sure to resonate with listeners of all ages.
