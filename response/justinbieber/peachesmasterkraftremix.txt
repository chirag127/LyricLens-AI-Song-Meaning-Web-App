"Peaches (Masterkraft Remix)" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring verses from Nigerian artists Alpha P and Omah Lay. The song is a remix of Bieber's original "Peaches" track, with added verses and a new beat produced by Masterkraft.

The song opens with Bieber singing about getting his "peaches" (presumably a reference to marijuana) from Georgia, his weed from California, and taking his "chick" to the North. He sings about the positive effects of his substances, saying that they make him feel good and provide him with light.

Alpha P's verse continues the theme of drugs and partying, with references to "Kali Kuta" (a type of weed) and going to Ibiza for a summer vacation. He sings about being with a girl who gives him energy and how he doesn't care about anyone who is jealous of him.

Omah Lay's verse switches the focus to love and relationships. He sings about being with a girl who he can't let go of, even though he knows he should. He compares their love to the river Nile and sings about not being afraid of losing her.

Overall, the song is a upbeat and energetic track about the pleasures of drugs, partying, and love. The added verses from Alpha P and Omah Lay bring a unique flavor to the song, giving it a more global feel.
