"Loved By You" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Burna Boy. In the song, Bieber sings about wanting to change himself and feeling like he's not enough for the person he loves. He wishes he could change himself to be more worthy of their love, and expresses his need to be loved by them. Burna Boy's verse adds a different perspective, with him reminiscing about the early days of their relationship and how they chased after each other's love. Overall, the song is about the feeling of wanting to be loved and accepted by someone you care about.

In terms of a review, the song has a catchy melody and the collaboration with Burna Boy adds an interesting dynamic. The lyrics are relatable and capture the feeling of wanting to be loved and accepted for who you are.
