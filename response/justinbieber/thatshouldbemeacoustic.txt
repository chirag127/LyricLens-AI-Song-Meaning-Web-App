In the song "That Should Be Me (Acoustic)" by Justin Bieber, the narrator is expressing his feelings of heartache and betrayal after his significant other has moved on to someone else. He questions whether he should continue to fight for their relationship or give up and move on. The chorus repeats the phrase "That should be me" as the narrator reflects on all the moments that he should have been experiencing with his partner but is now experiencing with someone else.

In the first verse, the narrator is questioning whether his ex is doing the same things with their new partner that they used to do with him. He also wonders if their new partner loves them as much as he did. The second verse reveals that the narrator has seen his ex out with their new partner, causing him to question whether his ex was using the time they needed apart to move on to someone else.

The chorus emphasizes the narrator's feelings of sadness and regret as he realizes that he is no longer a part of his ex's life. He cannot move on until he knows for sure that their relationship is truly over.

In the final verse, the narrator decides to fight for their relationship and declares that he will never let his ex go.

Overall, "That Should Be Me (Acoustic)" is a powerful and emotional song about the pain of losing someone you love and the struggle to move on. The stripped-down acoustic version highlights Bieber's raw vocals and adds to the emotional impact of the lyrics.
