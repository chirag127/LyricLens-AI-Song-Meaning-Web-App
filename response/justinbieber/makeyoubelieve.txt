In the second verse, he talks about taking control of the relationship and taking it to the next level.

The song has a upbeat and energetic vibe, with a catchy pop beat and Bieber's smooth vocals. The lyrics are simple and straightforward, with Bieber repeating the phrase "I'll make you believe" throughout the song. Overall, the song is a feel-good anthem about finding love and being with someone who will make you believe in it again.
