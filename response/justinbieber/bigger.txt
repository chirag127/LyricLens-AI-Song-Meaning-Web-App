"Bigger" by Justin Bieber is an upbeat and empowering pop song that celebrates the growth and evolution of a relationship. In the first verse, Bieber sings about how the love between him and his partner is "bigger" and more powerful than anything they may face. He also encourages his partner to believe in him and their relationship, and promises to never let them down.

In the chorus, Bieber sings about how they have grown and matured as a couple, and how they are now "big enough" to stand in the picture and next to love. He also reminisces about his past as a "player" and "heart breaker" but emphasizes that he has grown and changed for the better.

In the second verse, Bieber sings about how he and his partner have faced challenges and obstacles in their relationship, but have persevered and overcome them. He also addresses the haters and bullies who have tried to tear them apart, but asserts that their love is too strong to be defeated.

Overall, "Bigger" is a catchy and uplifting song that celebrates the growth and evolution of a relationship. The song's upbeat melody and empowering lyrics make for an enjoyable listening experience.
