"No Sense" is a romantic pop song by Justin Bieber and Travis Scott. The song is about how nothing in life makes sense without the person they love by their side. Bieber sings about how things like the sun setting and the sky being dark don't have the same meaning without their partner. He also talks about how he's been around the world and seen a lot of things, but none of it matters without their love. Travis Scott also adds his own verse about how he breaks plans and isn't the same person without their partner. The song is a declaration of love and commitment to their significant other.

The song has a catchy beat and the vocals from both Bieber and Scott are strong. The lyrics are relatable and express the idea that love is essential for a fulfilling life. Overall, "No Sense" is a well-written and enjoyable love song.
