"Nothing Like Us" is a song by Justin Bieber in which he expresses his feelings of loss and regret after a difficult breakup. In the first verse, he reflects on the challenges they faced as a couple, and wonders if his ex-partner has been drinking to cope with the pain. He wishes he could give her what she deserves, but knows that he can't replace her or make her feel the way he does.

The chorus repeats the phrase "there's nothing like us," highlighting the unique and special connection they shared. He acknowledges that they were perfect together, but now that they are apart, he wants her to see that their love was real and special.

In the second verse, Justin admits that he gave his all to the relationship, but she pushed him away. He is lost and confused, unsure of why things didn't work out. Despite the heartache, he knows that the break up was meant to be and accepts that their relationship has come to an end.

Overall, "Nothing Like Us" is a heart-wrenching ballad about the pain of a lost love and the desire to hold onto something that has ended. Justin's emotive vocals and the catchy chorus make for a powerful and relatable song that many listeners can connect with.
