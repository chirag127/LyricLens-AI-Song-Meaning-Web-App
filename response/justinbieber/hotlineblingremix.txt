"Hotline Bling (Remix)" is a song by Justin Bieber that features a remixed version of Drake's original track "Hotline Bling." In the song, Bieber sings about a girl who has been calling another man on his cell phone when she can't reach Bieber. He expresses his frustration and disappointment with her actions and tells her to call him on his "hotline bling" if she wants to be with him. The song has a catchy beat and Bieber's vocals are smooth and emotive. Overall, it is a solid remix of the original track.
