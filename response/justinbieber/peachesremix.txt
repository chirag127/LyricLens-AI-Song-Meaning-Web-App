"Peaches (Remix)" is a song by Justin Bieber featuring Ludacris, Usher, and Snoop Dogg. In the song, Bieber sings about getting his "peaches" from Georgia, his weed from California, and taking his "chick" up north. He also sings about the texture of his love interest's skin and how he wants to be with her until the end of time. Ludacris, Usher, and Snoop Dogg also each contribute a verse to the song, with Ludacris rapping about being with his love interest, Usher singing about the girl he's with being from Atlanta, and Snoop Dogg rapping about his passion for marijuana legalization.

The song has a smooth, laid-back vibe and features some catchy hooks and impressive guest verses from Ludacris, Usher, and Snoop Dogg. Overall, "Peaches (Remix)" is a fun, upbeat track that showcases Justin Bieber's pop sensibilities and the talents of his guest collaborators.
