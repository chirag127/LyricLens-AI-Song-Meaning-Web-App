"Heartbreaker" is a pop song performed by Justin Bieber. The song is about a man who is trying to win back his former lover, who has moved on from their relationship. In the lyrics, Bieber begs his ex-girlfriend to give him a chance to make things right and promises to be a better partner. The song has a catchy chorus and a simple, repetitive melody that is typical of pop music.

In the first verse, Bieber sings about how he misses his ex-girlfriend and how he wishes she would take his call. He expresses his desire to be alone with her and tells her that he has a secret place they can go to be together. In the chorus, he begs her not to be a heartbreaker and pleads with her to give him a second chance, as his heart is breaking without her.

The second verse expands on the theme of wanting to make things right and being willing to change for the better. Bieber sings about how he has learned from his mistakes and is determined to be a better partner. He also suggests that he believes in the power of their love and wants her to believe in it too.

Overall, "Heartbreaker" is a typical pop song with relatable lyrics and a catchy melody. It is a heartfelt plea from a man who is trying to win back the love of his ex-girlfriend.
