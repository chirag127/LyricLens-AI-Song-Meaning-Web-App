"One Less Lonely Girl" is a song by Justin Bieber. The lyrics describe a man who is trying to win over a woman who has been hurt in the past by other men. He promises to be different and to treat her better, and says that if she lets him into her life, she will have "one less lonely girl" in the world.

In the first verse, the singer asks the woman how many times she has been let down and had to start over with someone else. He asks how many promises have been broken and how many tears she has cried. He also mentions that she has packed bags and taken them back, implying that she has left relationships before.

The chorus repeats the line "there's gonna be one less lonely girl" several times, emphasizing the singer's desire to be the one to make her happy and to take away her loneliness. He also sings about how he has seen many other beautiful faces before he saw her, but now all he sees is her.

In the second verse, the singer talks about how the woman has spent holidays and special occasions without him, and how other men have failed to treat her well. He promises to be different and to make her believe in love again.

The song then repeats the chorus several times, with the singer promising to put the woman first and to show her how much she is worth. He says that he can fix her broken heart and give her a new start.

Overall, "One Less Lonely Girl" is a romantic and hopeful song about a man who is trying to win over a woman who has been hurt in the past. The lyrics are heartfelt and sincere, and the chorus is catchy and memorable.
