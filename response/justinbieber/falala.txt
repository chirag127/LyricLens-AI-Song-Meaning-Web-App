The song "Fa La La" by Justin Bieber is a Christmas song that celebrates the joy and excitement of the holiday season. In the song, Justin Bieber sings about spending time with his loved ones and enjoying the festive atmosphere of the season. He also mentions wanting to give his loved ones the best gifts and being in awe of the beauty of the snow falling outside.

In the chorus, Justin Bieber sings about hearing melodies in his loved one's heartbeats, and how it makes him feel happy and festive. He also sings about decking their hearts with boughs of holly and being in the Christmas spirit.

Overall, the song is a cheerful and upbeat celebration of the holiday season, and the joy of spending time with loved ones. The collaboration with Boyz II Men adds a soulful and smooth touch to the song, making it even more festive and enjoyable to listen to.
