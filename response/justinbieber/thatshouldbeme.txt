"That Should Be Me" is a song by Justin Bieber about feeling heartbroken and jealous after a breakup. In the song, Bieber sings about how he wishes he could still be with his ex-partner, and how he sees them moving on with someone else. He expresses regret for letting them go and wonders if there is still a chance for them to be together. The song reflects on the emotions of heartache and the desire to fix a failed relationship.

In the first verse, Bieber sings about the rumors he's heard about his ex-partner being with someone else. He wonders if they are doing the same things they used to do together, and if the new person treats them as well as he did. He also mentions that they had made plans together, which he hasn't forgotten, but his ex-partner seems to have moved on.

The chorus repeats the line "that should be me" as Bieber reflects on all the ways he wishes he could still be with his ex-partner. He imagines holding their hand, making them laugh, and kissing them. He also mentions buying them gifts, which shows his desire to take care of and spoil them. However, he recognizes that it is wrong for him to continue feeling this way and wants his ex-partner to believe that they should be together.

In the second verse, Bieber talks about how his ex-partner said they needed time to think about their mistakes, but instead used that time to replace him. He sees them out at the same places they used to go together and feels hurt by the betrayal. He wonders if he should fight for their love or give up and move on.

The song ends with Bieber repeating the line "I'm never gonna let you go" as he declares his determination to win his ex-partner back. He also repeats the line "that should be me" as he reaffirms his belief that they belong together.

Overall, "That Should Be Me" is a heartfelt and emotional song about the pain of a breakup and the desire to fix a failed relationship. Bieber's emotive vocals and relatable lyrics capture the feeling of heartache and regret, making the song a powerful and relatable listening experience.
