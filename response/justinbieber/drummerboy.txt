"Drummer Boy" is a Christmas song performed by Justin Bieber and featuring rapper Busta Rhymes. The song is a re-imagining of the traditional Christmas carol "The Little Drummer Boy," and features lyrics that tell the story of the song's narrator offering to play his drum for the newborn baby Jesus. In addition to the traditional elements of the original song, Justin Bieber and Busta Rhymes add their own contemporary touches, including references to Twitter and other modern elements.

In the chorus, he repeats the lines "I played my drum for him, pa rum pa pum pum" and "I played my best for him, pa rum pa pum pum." The song then transitions into a verse by Busta Rhymes, who tells the story of how he received a message from Justin Bieber on Twitter inviting him to collaborate on the song. Busta Rhymes raps about spending time with his family and giving gifts to those in need, before heading to the studio to record the song with Justin Bieber.

In the final verse, Justin Bieber returns to singing the traditional lyrics of "The Little Drummer Boy," with the addition of some modern touches. He sings about the importance of giving to those in need and spreading holiday cheer, and encourages his listeners to "act merrily" and "act bold" in their efforts to help others. The song ends with Justin Bieber repeating the line "I'm the drummer boy so do it, do it," urging his listeners to take action and make a difference in their communities.

Overall, "Drummer Boy" is a unique and modern take on a classic Christmas carol, with catchy beats and lyrics that celebrate the spirit of the holiday season.
