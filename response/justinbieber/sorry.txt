"Sorry" by Justin Bieber is a song about a person who has made mistakes in a relationship and is seeking forgiveness from their partner. In the first verse, the narrator admits to not being good at apologizing and asks for another chance to make things right. In the chorus, the narrator expresses regret for letting their partner down and asks if it's too late to say sorry. The second verse focuses on the narrator's willingness to take responsibility for their mistakes and their desire to move past them. The chorus repeats with the narrator expressing their remorse and asking for forgiveness again.

The song is upbeat and catchy, with a dance-pop production. The lyrics are emotional and reflective, showing the narrator's remorse and desire for redemption.

Overall, "Sorry" is a well-written and performed song that effectively conveys the themes of regret, forgiveness, and redemption. It's a relatable and emotional track that is sure to resonate with listeners.
