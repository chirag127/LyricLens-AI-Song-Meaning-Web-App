"Company" by Justin Bieber is a pop song that explores the idea of finding companionship and connection with someone without the pressure of a romantic relationship. The lyrics describe a scenario where two people meet and decide to spend time together, enjoying each other's company without any expectations or obligations. The song's chorus repeats the line "can we keep each other company" and uses the word "company" as a metaphor for the presence of another person who can alleviate loneliness and provide comfort. The song has a light, upbeat melody and the lyrics are simple and catchy, making it easy to sing along to.

In terms of a verse-by-verse breakdown, the first verse describes the initial meeting between the two people, with the singer asking the other person if they would like to spend time together and offering to buy them a drink. The second verse focuses on the idea that the relationship doesn't have to be complicated and that the two people can simply enjoy each other's company without any pressure. The third verse repeats the chorus and includes the line "just be each other's company," emphasizing the simplicity and casualness of the relationship.

Overall, "Company" is a fun and upbeat song that captures the feeling of meeting someone new and enjoying their company without the need for a formal relationship. The catchy chorus and simple lyrics make it an enjoyable listening experience.
