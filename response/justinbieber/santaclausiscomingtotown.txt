"Santa Claus Is Coming To Town" is a popular Christmas song written by John Frederick Coots and Haven Gillespie. The song was first recorded in 1934 by the Harry Reser Orchestra and has since been covered by many artists, including Justin Bieber.

In the song, the narrator warns listeners to be on their best behavior because Santa Claus is coming to town. He knows when they are sleeping and when they are awake, and he knows whether they've been good or bad. The song encourages children to be good and not to cry, pout, or misbehave, because Santa will find out and they may not receive the gifts they want.

The song has a cheerful and upbeat melody, and Bieber's version adds a modern twist with his pop style and added dance beats. The lyrics are a reminder of the excitement and anticipation of the holiday season, as children eagerly wait for Santa to visit and bring them gifts.

Overall, "Santa Claus Is Coming To Town" is a classic Christmas song that captures the joy and magic of the holiday season. Bieber's version adds a modern touch and is a fun and festive addition to any holiday playlist.
