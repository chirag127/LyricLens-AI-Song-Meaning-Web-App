"Beauty And A Beat" by Justin Bieber is a party song that celebrates the singer's success and wealth, and the beauty of his romantic partner. The first verse of the song describes the singer's desire to show off his partner's beauty, and suggests that their beauty is worth more than money can buy. The second verse continues the theme of partying and enjoying the good things in life, while the chorus repeats the phrase "beauty and a beat" and describes the singer's partner as the source of his happiness. The song's third verse features a guest appearance by rapper Nicki Minaj, who boasts about her own success and takes shots at other artists. The bridge and outro of the song reinforce the theme of enjoying the present moment and celebrating with music. Overall, the song has a lively and energetic vibe, and could be considered a pop or hip-hop song.
