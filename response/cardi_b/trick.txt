The song “Trick” by Cardi B is a clear and blunt representation of the objectification of men in relationships. The lyrics are an indictment of men who take advantage of women, using them for their own financial and emotional gain. 

The song is split into two parts, each part representing a different rule in Cardi B’s relationship code. In the first verse, Cardi B sets out her rules for a relationship, which include not paying for anything, not talking about kids, not taking pictures in the club, and not expecting her to wine and dine. She also states that she needs an “unlimited supply” of anything she wants and expects the man to pay for all of it. 

The second verse is dedicated to the men who Cardi B believes are “tricks”, meaning they are taking advantage of her. She states that they are “catching feelings like a ho” and that there is “no love for a trick”. She also sets out more rules, such as not seeing her for a week after a date and not calling her “babe”. 

The chorus of the song is a blunt summary of the song’s message, with Cardi B repeating the phrase “boy, you a trick”. This is a reminder to the men that they are not getting any love from her and that they are just using her for their own gain. 

This song is an excellent example of Cardi B’s blunt and unapologetic style of lyricism. The lyrics are a reminder to men that they cannot take advantage of women and expect to get away with it. The song also serves as a reminder to women that they don’t have to put up with men who are taking advantage of them.

Linguistically, Cardi B’s lyrics show her to be assertive, confident, and unafraid to speak her mind. This is evident in her blunt rhyme scheme and her use of powerful words such as “fuck” and “thot”, which convey her attitude and strength. This is a clear representation of the power of the female voice and a reminder to men that women are not to be taken advantage of.
