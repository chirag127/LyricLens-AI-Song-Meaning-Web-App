The Good (Unreleased) by Cardi B is a powerful and emotional song about the struggles of life and the mistreatment of the good people in the world. The song starts off with the line "what's this now, how could this happen after everything I've passed," which expresses the frustration of feeling like life is constantly throwing stones at you, no matter how hard you try. The chorus "I've wasted too many tears, they just keep coming and coming, I don't even know why, but I've always wondered why" emphasizes the feeling of hopelessness and confusion that often comes with adversity. 

The verses of the song further explore Cardi B's feelings of alienation and betrayal, as she reflects on the people who have taken advantage of her and how she is constantly overlooked. She expresses how she has always seen the best in the worst, and how she is not willing to lower herself to their level. She acknowledges the pain, anger, and tears that have become a cycle in her life, and expresses her desire to break the cycle and move on. 

The song ends with the lines "Maybe it's just meant to be, betrayal, lies, fake promises," which is a powerful statement that sums up the overall message of the song - no matter how hard you try to do the right thing, sometimes life will still throw you curve balls. 

Overall, The Good (Unreleased) by Cardi B is a powerful and emotionally charged song that speaks to the struggles of life and the mistreatment of the good people in the world. The lyrics are full of emotion and social commentary, and Cardi B does an excellent job of conveying her feelings and experiences. Through her song, she encourages listeners to keep striving for the good, even when life throws them down.
