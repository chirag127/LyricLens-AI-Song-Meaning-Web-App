The song ends with the same hook that opened it, “They out here looking for revenge all day all night / See me not once but twice / All looks and no fights / See me doing me / Got 'em wondering how a bitch came a took flight.” Cardi is celebrating her success and showing her haters that she has made it despite the doubters.
