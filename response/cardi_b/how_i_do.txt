"How I Do" by Cardi B is a song about rising to the top, despite the odds and the naysayers. The song paints a picture of the artist’s journey from the Bronx slums to the top of the charts. 

The song starts off with a boastful chorus which is repeated throughout: “This is how I do, I make a fool out these bitches, they be thinking they tough/ This is how I do, I make these niggas spend their money then I show them no love.” This is followed by Cardi B’s signature flow and lyrical prowess, as she details her trials and tribulations, and how she ultimately rose above them. She speaks of how she faced adversity from the very beginning, from people who thought she was “just a stupid hoe.” Now, however, she’s able to “do a bitch greasy” and “give it to you just how you like it, girl.” She is powerful, confident, and unapologetic. 

The song also has a darker side, as Cardi B speaks of violence and the need to keep a gun on her at all times. She raps about her enemies, and how she doesn’t back down from beef. She even goes as far as to say that “beef to me is fun.” In addition to the violence, Cardi B speaks of the money and power that come with success. She brags about her newfound wealth, and how she’s able to “make a milli” off her enemies. 

The song is a testament to Cardi B's resilience and determination to succeed, no matter what obstacles she faced. The lyrics are full of bravado and attitude, and the production is hard-hitting and energetic. The song is an anthem for anyone who has faced adversity and come out on top.

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song is full of emotions, from the confident boasting of the chorus to the darker themes in the verses. Cardi B displays a range of emotions throughout the song, from anger to pride. She displays a strong sense of self-empowerment and self-belief, and this is reflected in her lyrics. Additionally, the use of slang and the references to the Bronx show her roots, and her connection to her hometown.

In conclusion, "How I Do" is an energetic and empowering track that speaks of the artist’s journey to success against all odds. The song is full of attitude and confidence, and Cardi B’s lyrical prowess is on full display. The song is an anthem for anyone who has faced adversity and come out on top.
