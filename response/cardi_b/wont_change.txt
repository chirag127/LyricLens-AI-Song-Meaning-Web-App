"Won't Change" by Cardi B is an empowering anthem about not compromising her values and staying true to herself in the face of a world that often tries to push her down. The lyrics of the song tell the story of Cardi's journey from a young wild girl with nothing to a successful artist, and the message is clear - she doesn't need anyone or anything to succeed.

The song begins with Cardi B singing, "I won't change for nobody/ Nobody won't pick no more bread for nobody/ Nobody, I ain't nobody/ Swear I can catch with the fuckin' nobody." Here, Cardi is emphasizing that she won't change her values or compromise her principles for anyone, no matter how much money or success they may offer her. She also acknowledges that she may not have the same resources or opportunities as other people, but she is still determined to succeed.

The chorus of the song reinforces this message, with Cardi singing, "Get it straight, Cardi ain't gonna change for nobody." She is making it clear that she is not going to be swayed by anyone else's opinion or expectations, and she will continue to be true to herself no matter what.

The second verse of the song dives deeper into Cardi's story, as she reflects on the struggles she has faced in her life. She sings, "Been a young wild bitch getting by nobody/ I done seen it all so I'm in a lane with nobody." Here, she is referring to her difficult past and the struggles she has gone through in order to get to where she is today. Despite these challenges, she has persevered and has become successful without compromising her values or changing who she is.

In terms of linguistic analysis of the lyrics, the song conveys a strong sense of resilience and determination. Cardi's lyrics are filled with powerful declarations of her unwavering strength and her refusal to be cowed by anyone. The use of repetition throughout the song adds emphasis and reinforces the message that she will not change who she is for anyone.

In summary, "Won't Change" by Cardi B is an empowering, inspiring anthem about staying true to oneself and not compromising one's values or beliefs in the face of adversity. Through her lyrics, Cardi conveys a strong sense of resilience and determination, and her message is clear - she won't change for anyone.
