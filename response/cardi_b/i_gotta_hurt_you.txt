Cardi B's song, "I Gotta Hurt You" is an emotionally charged story of heartache and betrayal. The lyrics explore the idea of wanting to hurt someone, as a way of expressing anger and pain. The song begins with Cardi B's admission that she could love the person she is singing to with all her heart, yet still feels the need to hurt them. The chorus reiterates this sentiment – she wants to hurt them in order to make them remember her forever. 

The verses explore what could be the reasons for her feelings: loneliness, sorrow, feeling as if she has no future. These sentiments are echoed in the bridge, which sees Cardi B asserting her strength and determination to use and abuse her partner, as a way of showing them that she is strong and will win in the end. 

A linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a range of emotions being expressed. Cardi B conveys feelings of sadness, loneliness, sorrow, and anger. Her use of repetition in the chorus and bridge adds emphasis to her words, especially the phrase "I want to hurt you". This suggests that her hurt and anger are deeply rooted, and she is determined to make her partner feel her pain.

Overall, "I Gotta Hurt You" is an emotionally charged song that conveys a range of powerful emotions. The song is a raw and honest look at the hurt and pain that can accompany a relationship, and Cardi B's lyrics reveal the depths of her feelings.
