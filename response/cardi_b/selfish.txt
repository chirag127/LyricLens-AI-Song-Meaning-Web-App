Selfish by Cardi B is a song that explores the idea of choosing to be selfish and taking care of oneself, despite the consequences it may have on others. The song begins with Cardi B rapping that she gave her partner more than she gave herself and was so loyal to them that she betrayed herself. She acknowledges that her love and trust wasn’t enough and that she needs to do for herself. 

The chorus is sung by Josh X and focuses on the difficulty of falling in love and why it’s so hard to try. He sings that the one that you truly love can hurt you the most, and Cardi B reflects on why she got so cold and why she couldn’t love and give more than she gave herself. 

In the second verse, Cardi B takes the lead and sings about finally learning to be selfish. She reflects on looking up at the sky and seeing all the pain it has caused her and wishing she had given more love. 

The lyrics of the song can be interpreted to represent the idea of learning to be selfish and taking care of oneself, no matter how difficult it may be. The song speaks to the idea of putting yourself first, even if it means sacrificing relationships and hurting the ones you love. 

From a linguistic perspective, the song uses repetition to emphasize the idea of being selfish and to drive its message home. The chorus is repeated several times throughout the song, as well as the phrase “be selfish”. This repetition helps to create an impactful and memorable song that speaks to the idea of putting yourself first. 

Overall, Selfish by Cardi B is a powerful and moving song that speaks to the importance of taking care of oneself and being selfish in order to survive. The lyrics are full of emotion and the repetition of the chorus helps to drive home the message.
