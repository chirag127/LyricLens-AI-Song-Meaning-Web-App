In Cardi B's song Ten Trick Commandments, she outlines her expectations in a relationship with a man who is a "trick" or someone who is trying to take advantage of her. She uses clever lyricism to make her point, making it clear that she's not interested in anything other than transactional relationships. 

The song starts off with the line "Boy you a trick, trick, trick, trick, trick, trick, you ain't nothing but a trick, trick, trick, trick, trick," which sets the tone for the rest of the song. She then goes on to list her ten commandments, each of which is a rule to be followed by the "trick" in order to keep the relationship beneficial for her. 

In the first few commandments, she establishes that she is not interested in any sort of emotional connection or commitment from the man and instead, is only interested in a transactional relationship. She also states that she expects the man to pay for her meals and provide her with unlimited shopping money. 

The song progresses with her further outlining her expectations and demands, eventually concluding with the line "You thought I like you for you, noooo," which signifies that the relationship she is seeking is not one of love and affection, but rather one of financial gain. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, Cardi B's lyrical style in Ten Trick Commandments is direct and to the point, with her message being clear and concise. Her language is also quite blunt and assertive, which reflects her no-nonsense attitude in relationships.

The song Ten Trick Commandments by Cardi B is a clever and direct look at transactional relationships. With her no-nonsense lyrics, she outlines her expectations in a relationship and makes it clear that she isn't interested in anything other than financial gain. The song is both informative and entertaining, and is sure to be a hit with her fans.
