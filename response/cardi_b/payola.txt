"Payola" by Cardi B is a song about financial exploitation. In it, Cardi B details the experience of being taken advantage of financially by someone she was in a relationship with. Throughout the song, Cardi B expresses her anger and resentment towards this person, as she explains how they used their money and influence to control her. 

The first verse of the song starts off with Cardi B discussing how her former partner "made a deal" with her, and how it was a "dirty deal." She goes on to say that she was taken advantage of, as she was paid "payola," a form of bribery or kickback, in exchange for her services. This verse serves to set the tone for the rest of the song, as it introduces the underlying theme of financial exploitation. 

In the second verse, Cardi B talks about how her partner had a "plan" to "rob" her. She talks about how they would take her money, and how they would "spend it on things that [she] don't need." This verse further reinforces the theme of financial exploitation, as it further emphasizes the fact that her partner was taking advantage of her. 

The chorus of the song is where Cardi B expresses her anger and resentment towards her former partner. She talks about how she will not let them "play with [her] money," and how she is going to "take it back." As she expresses her anger, she also expresses her determination to take back the money that was taken from her. 

The third verse of the song is where Cardi B talks about how she was able to take back the money that was taken from her. Here, she talks about how she was able to "get back to the top" and how she will never let anyone "play with [her] money" again. This verse serves to provide closure to the song, as it shows that Cardi B was able to recover from her exploitative relationship and reclaim her money. 

In summary, "Payola" by Cardi B is a song about financial exploitation. Throughout the song, Cardi B expresses her anger and resentment towards the person who took advantage of her, and she expresses her determination to reclaim her money. The song is lyrically powerful and provides a strong message of empowerment, as it emphasizes the importance of standing up for oneself and taking back what was taken away.

In terms of a linguistic analysis, the song can be seen as expressing a range of emotions. The lyrics express anger, resentment, and determination, as Cardi B expresses her anger towards her former partner and her determination to reclaim her money. Additionally, the song conveys a sense of empowerment, as Cardi B stands up for herself and shows that she can recover from the exploitation that she experienced.
