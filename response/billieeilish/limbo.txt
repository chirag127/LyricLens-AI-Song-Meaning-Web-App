"Limbo" by Billie Eilish is a song about the end of a relationship and the emotions that come with it. In the song, the narrator is trying to move on from the relationship, but is struggling to do so. They express their frustration and disappointment at the other person for not trying to save the relationship and for being too far away to make things right. The narrator also mentions feeling like they are stuck in limbo, unable to fully move on but unable to go back to the way things were before.

The song reflects on the feeling of helplessness and frustration that can come with the end of a relationship, and the struggle to move on. The lyrics speak to the desire to find a way to make things better, but ultimately the realization that it is not possible. The use of imagery such as "broken glass waiting to be washed away" and "arms akimbo" adds to the feeling of helplessness and frustration.

Overall, "Limbo" is a powerful and emotional song that captures the complex emotions that come with the end of a relationship. The lyrics are relatable and poignant, making it a strong addition to Billie Eilish's catalog.
