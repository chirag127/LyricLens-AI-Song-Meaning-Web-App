"Bad Guy" is a song by American singer Billie Eilish, released as the fifth and final single from her debut studio album, When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? (2019). The song was written by Eilish and her brother Finneas O'Connell, who also produced the track.

In the song, Eilish sings about being the "bad guy" in a relationship, enjoying being in control and causing trouble. She describes herself as a "tough guy" who is rough and doesn't care about anyone's feelings. The lyrics also reference her mother and how she would react to the song's lyrics if she read them.

The song's chorus includes the lines "I like it when you take control / Even if you know that you don't / Own me, I'll let you play the role / I'll be your animal," which suggest that Eilish enjoys being dominated in a relationship and is willing to let her partner have control over her.

In terms of analysis and review, "Bad Guy" is a catchy and well-written pop song that showcases Eilish's unique vocal style and the production skills of her brother Finneas. The song's dark and edgy lyrics and infectious beat make it a standout track on the album and a fan favorite. Overall, "Bad Guy" is a strong addition to Eilish's discography and a highlight of her debut album.
