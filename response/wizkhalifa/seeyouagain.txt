"See You Again" is a hip-hop/pop song by American rapper Wiz Khalifa and singer Charlie Puth. The song was written as a tribute to actor Paul Walker, who died in a car accident in 2013. The song is about friendship, family, and the memories we leave behind when we die.

In the first verse, Charlie Puth sings about how it has been a long day without his friend and how he will tell them all about it when he sees them again. He reflects on how far they have come from where they started and the good times they have shared.

In the second verse, Wiz Khalifa talks about how their friendship grew from a casual acquaintance to a strong bond. He reflects on the importance of family and how their bond will never be broken. He then talks about the memories they have made and asks his friend to remember him when he is gone.

The chorus repeats the line "I'll tell you all about it when I see you again" and talks about how the light will guide their way and every road they take will lead them home.

The song has a emotional and reflective tone, with a catchy beat and memorable chorus. Overall, "See You Again" is a touching tribute to a lost friend and a reminder of the memories we leave behind.
