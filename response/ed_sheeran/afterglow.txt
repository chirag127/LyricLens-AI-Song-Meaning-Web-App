In Ed Sheeran's song "Afterglow", the singer reflects on a past relationship and the love he shared with the person he was with. The song begins with the lyric "Stop the clocks, it's amazing/You should see the way the light dances off your hair/A million colors of hazel, golden, and red". These lyrics express the admiration and love the singer had for his partner, as well as the beauty of the moment they shared.

The second verse talks of how the relationship was in a seemingly different world with the lyric "The weather outside's changing/The leaves are buried under six inches of white/The radio is playing Iron & Wine". This suggests that the two were in a separate world, where the outside world was not as important as the love they shared.

The pre-chorus reinforces the idea of the couple being in love, with the lyrics "We were love-drunk, waiting on a miracle/Tryna find ourselves in the winter snow/So alone in love like the world had disappeared". This is followed by a chorus where the singer expresses his determination to hold onto the afterglow of the relationship, with the lyrics "Oh, I will hold on to the afterglow/Oh, I will hold on to the afterglow".

In summary, "Afterglow" is a song about a past relationship and the lingering love the singer still has for his former partner. The singer reflects on the beauty of the relationship, the feeling of being in a different world, and his determination to hold onto the afterglow of the relationship. The song is filled with emotion and longing, and it is a beautiful reminder of the power of love.
