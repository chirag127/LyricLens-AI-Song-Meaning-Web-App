"Remember the Name" is a song by Ed Sheeran, featuring rappers Eminem and 50 Cent. The song is an anthem celebrating the artists' success in the music industry, with each of the three verses detailing their journey to fame. In the first verse, Sheeran reflects on his rise to success, starting as a misfit growing up in Ipswich and being dismissed for his talent before ultimately achieving his dreams of making it big. In the second verse, Eminem raps about his struggles in the early days of his career, trying to shop a record deal and dealing with addiction, but ultimately persevered to become one of the biggest names in rap. In the third verse, 50 Cent boasts about his luxurious lifestyle and successful career, referencing his expensive clothes and cars, as well as his many hits and accolades.

Throughout the song, the chorus repeats the refrain "But it's 'bout time you remember the name / Ayy, ayy," emphasizing the artists' desire to be remembered and celebrated for their contributions to the music industry.

As a review of the song, "Remember the Name" is a strong and energetic anthem that celebrates the success of the three artists. The verses are well-written and showcase the unique styles of each artist, while the chorus provides a catchy and memorable refrain. Overall, the song is a solid addition to each of the artists' catalogues and a fitting tribute to their careers in music.
