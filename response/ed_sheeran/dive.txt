"Dive" is a song by English singer-songwriter Ed Sheeran. The song is about a person who is hesitant to fully commit to a relationship because of their partner's tendency to lead others on. In the first verse, the narrator expresses regret for coming on too strong and making mistakes in their relationship. In the pre-chorus, the narrator considers the possibilities of falling or flying in the relationship. In the chorus, the narrator asks their partner not to call them "baby" unless they truly mean it and not to tell them they need them if they don't believe it. The song is a plea for honesty and commitment from the partner.

In summary, "Dive" is a song about the struggles of committing to a relationship with someone who has a history of leading others on. The narrator wants their partner to be honest with them and to fully commit to the relationship.

In my opinion, "Dive" is a powerful and emotional song. Sheeran's vocals and lyrics are heartfelt and raw, and the song's message is relatable to many people who have struggled with commitment in a relationship. The song is a great addition to Sheeran's impressive catalog of hit songs.
