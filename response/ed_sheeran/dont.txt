"Don't" by Ed Sheeran is a song about a girl who cheats on her lover. The singer expresses his disappointment and hurt in the chorus, singing "Don't fuck with my love / That heart is so cold / All over my home / I don't wanna know that babe". The verses describe how the singer met the girl and their initial attraction, but also how he was aware that she wasn't looking for a serious relationship. Despite this, they continued to see each other and have fun together. However, when the singer finds out she has cheated on him, he is hurt and ends the relationship.

In the song, the singer portrays himself as being the one who was truly committed to the relationship, while the girl was only interested in having fun and wasn't being truthful with him. He expresses his frustration and hurt at being lied to and used.

Overall, the song is a heartbreak ballad that explores the emotions and feelings that come with being betrayed by someone you care about. The lyrics are relatable and the melody is catchy, making it a memorable and well-written song.
