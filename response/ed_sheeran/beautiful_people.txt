"Beautiful People" is a song by English singer-songwriter Ed Sheeran, featuring American singer Khalid. The song was released as the third single from Sheeran's fourth studio album, No.6 Collaborations Project.

In the first verse, Sheeran describes a Saturday night in Los Angeles, where people are out in their expensive cars and heading downtown to party. He and his partner are in the middle of it all, trying to figure out their place in this world of "beautiful people". In the pre-chorus, Sheeran sings that he and his partner don't fit in with the crowd because they are just themselves, and he wishes he could escape the superficial conversations he finds himself in.

In the chorus, Sheeran and Khalid sing about the world of "beautiful people", where material possessions and social connections are valued above all else. They are surrounded by this world, but still feel alone. They want to leave the party and be themselves.

In the second verse, Khalid takes over and sings about a similar experience in Los Angeles. He and his partner are driving around, feeling lost and out of place in the world of "beautiful people". However, he is not intimidated by the flashy lights and cameras because he has his partner with him, and that is all that matters.

In the post-chorus, Sheeran and Khalid repeat the phrase "we are, we are, we are" and affirm that they are not beautiful by the shallow standards of the party scene they are in. They would rather be themselves than conform to those standards.

Overall, the song is a commentary on the superficiality and loneliness of the "beautiful people" lifestyle, and a celebration of being true to oneself and finding genuine connection with others.

As a review, I think "Beautiful People" is a well-written and catchy song that effectively conveys a relatable message about staying true to oneself and finding genuine connection in a world that often values superficiality. Ed Sheeran and Khalid's vocals blend well together, and the song has a catchy chorus that sticks in your head.
