"Supermarket Flowers" is a ballad by Ed Sheeran about the loss of his maternal grandmother. The song begins with the narrator packing up his grandmother's belongings and reminiscing on their life together. In the chorus, he sings "Hallelujah" and refers to his grandmother as an angel who was always there to support him. Overall, the song is a tribute to the narrator's grandmother and the impact she had on his life. It's a moving and emotional track that captures the pain of loss and the difficulty of saying goodbye. The song is well-written and poignant, and showcases Ed Sheeran's talent as a singer and songwriter.
