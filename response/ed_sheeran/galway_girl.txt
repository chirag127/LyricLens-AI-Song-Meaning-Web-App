"Galway Girl" is a song by Ed Sheeran about a girl he meets in Galway, Ireland. She plays the fiddle in an Irish band and falls in love with an English man (Sheeran himself). She also plays darts and pool with him, and they drink and dance together. In the end, Sheeran sings about how he's going to write a song about her and their perfect night together.

The song has a lively, upbeat rhythm and uses Irish instruments and references, like the fiddle and trad tunes, to give it a distinct Irish flavor. The lyrics are playful and lighthearted, capturing the excitement and joy of falling in love with someone new.

Overall, "Galway Girl" is a fun and catchy love song that celebrates the joys of falling in love and the beauty of Ireland. It's a fitting tribute to Sheeran's love for the country and its people.
