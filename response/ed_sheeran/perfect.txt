"Perfect" is a love song by Ed Sheeran. The song describes the singer's feelings of falling in love with a girl and how she completes him. He talks about how they are still kids, but are deeply in love, and how he sees his future in her eyes. The pre-chorus and chorus repeat the phrase "you look perfect tonight," emphasizing the singer's admiration for his partner.

In summary, the song is about falling in love and the feeling of completeness that comes with it. The lyrics are sweet and romantic, and the melody is catchy and upbeat.

As a review, "Perfect" is a well-written and well-performed love song that captures the feeling of falling in love. The lyrics are relatable and the melody is easy to sing along to. Overall, it is a great addition to Sheeran's collection of love songs.
