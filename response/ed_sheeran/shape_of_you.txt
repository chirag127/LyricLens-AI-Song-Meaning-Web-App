"Shape of You" is a song by English singer-songwriter Ed Sheeran. The song is about falling in love with someone and feeling a strong physical attraction to them. In the first verse, Sheeran describes meeting a girl at a club and going to a bar with her and his friends. In the pre-chorus, he expresses his desire for her and invites her to dance with him. In the chorus, he sings about being in love with the shape of her body and how he can't stop thinking about her. In the second verse, he describes going on a date with her and how he's falling more in love with her every day.

In the bridge, he repeats the line "Come on, be my baby, come on" several times, expressing his desire for her to be with him. The song has a catchy, upbeat melody and Sheeran's vocals are strong and emotional.

Overall, "Shape of You" is a fun and romantic love song that captures the excitement and intensity of falling in love. It's a great addition to Sheeran's discography and is sure to be a hit with fans.
