In the second verse, Sheeran sings about how he will still be loved by the person even when his hair is gone and his memory fades. He also sings about how the person will still love him even when he is no longer able to play the guitar as well as he used to.

In the pre-chorus and chorus of the second verse, Sheeran reiterates his love for the person and how it will never grow old. He also sings about how the person's smile will forever be in his mind and memory.

Overall, "Thinking Out Loud" is a beautiful love song that celebrates the enduring nature of love and the ways in which it can endure even as people age and change. It is a sweet and romantic ballad that is sure to tug at the heartstrings of anyone who listens to it.
