South of the Border is a song by English singer-songwriter Ed Sheeran, featuring vocals from Cuban-American singer Camila Cabello and American rapper Cardi B. The song is about two people who are drawn to each other and want to spend time together. Sheeran sings about a woman with "brown eyes, caramel thighs" who catches his attention, while Cabello sings about a man with "green eyes" who she is interested in.

The pre-chorus has Sheeran declaring his love for the woman, using the Spanish phrase "te amo, mami" (I love you, mommy) to express his feelings. He also says that being with her is like a dream come true.

The chorus has Sheeran inviting the woman to join him in bed, where they can be intimate and "sweat" together. He says he will not stop until the "angels sing," indicating that he is determined to pursue her and make their relationship last. He also invites her to "come south of the border" with him, which could be interpreted as an invitation to explore their relationship and have a good time together.

In the second verse, Cabello sings about a man she sees and is attracted to. She describes his green eyes and notes that he seems interested in getting to know her. She invites him to come over and dance with her, and expresses her desire to feel his body close to hers.

The pre-chorus and chorus repeat, with Cabello adding her own vocals to the mix. She also expresses her love for the man's lips and the way he says "te amo, mami" to her. She doesn't want to wake up from this dream-like state of being with him.

In the third verse, Cardi B raps about living life to the fullest and taking risks in order to shine and be successful. She also touches on themes of materialism and luxury, mentioning diamonds, green fields, and Lamborghinis. She talks about being a strong, independent woman who can "do it all" and "never drop the ball."

Overall, South of the Border is a song about attraction and desire, with Sheeran and Cabello singing about their respective love interests and expressing their feelings for them. The chorus invites the listener to join in the fun and enjoy the moment. Cardi B's rap verse adds a touch of self-empowerment and confidence.
