"OUTRO : House of Cards" by BTS (방탄소년단) is a song that reflects a feeling of hope and resilience in the face of difficulty. The lyrics describe a house of cards, which symbolizes the fragility of life and how easily it can fall apart. The lyrics express a sense of vulnerability and insecurity despite this fragility, as the protagonist looks for a way to stay in their situation despite the risks. 

The song starts off with a prehook that speaks of knowing something yet being unable to stop it, and of time passing and things getting worse. The hook then speaks of a house of cards, where the protagonists know the end is near but still stay regardless, as if they are fools in a dream.

The lyrics of the song contain several linguistic elements that reflect the emotions of the song. There is a focus on negation and uncertainty, with words such as “bad”, “hard”, “fall”, “no way” and “can't” being used throughout. This reflects the uncertainty of the situation and the difficulty of moving forward. Additionally, the use of repetition and parallel structures create a sense of longing for something better, and the use of metaphors creates a sense of fragility and insecurity. 

Overall, "OUTRO : House of Cards" is a song about hope and resilience in the face of difficulty. The lyrics express a sense of vulnerability and insecurity despite the fragility of life, and contain several linguistic elements that reflect the emotions of the song. It is a powerful and moving song that will resonate with many listeners.
