The song "Interlude: Shadow" by BTS (방탄소년단) is a thoughtful, introspective track that explores the personal struggles of the artist. The lyrics demonstrate Suga's desire to achieve success and recognition, and his fear of the consequences of being successful. Through this song, Suga reflects on his past and present, exploring his emotions, motivations, and fears as he strives to find his own identity.

In the chorus, Suga repeats the phrase, "please don't let me shine, don't let me down, don't let me fly", expressing his fear of failure, and uncertainty of what lies ahead.

The verses of the song help to further develop the themes and ideas of the song. In the first verse, Suga reflects on his progress and accomplishments, and how his shadow seems to grow with each step he takes. He acknowledges that his ambition and his fear are intertwined and that running away from his fears will only make them stronger. In the second verse, he acknowledges that things may not have turned out as he planned, but he has still achieved a lot and should not regret his choices. The song ends with Suga reaffirming his identity, stating that no matter what happens, he and his dreams are inseparable.

The song lyrics are filled with powerful imagery and vivid descriptions, making them emotionally resonant and meaningful. Through the lyrics, the artist expresses his inner struggles and his desire to make something of himself, while also showing his fear of failure and uncertainty of the future.
