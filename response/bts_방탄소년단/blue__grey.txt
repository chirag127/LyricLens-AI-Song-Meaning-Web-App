Blue & Grey by BTS (방탄소년단) is a reflective and introspective song that speaks to the struggles of life, the desire to be happier, and the need for human connection. The lyrics are emotionally charged, with a melancholic and mournful tone throughout. 

The song begins with a plea for help, as Jung Kook laments the end of another day and asks someone to come and save him. The next verse, sung by Suga, speaks of a feeling of confusion and not knowing where things went wrong. The chorus, sung by Jung Kook, Jimin, and V, expresses a desire to be happier, and the bridge sung by J Hope and RM speaks of feeling overwhelmed and lost in a strange place. 

The lyrics of the song, particularly in the chorus, reflect the struggle to find joy in life and the desire for a better future. The lyrics allude to the loneliness and uncertainty of life, and the need for connection in order to cope with the struggles that arise. The use of blue and grey throughout the song references the sadness and gloom of life, and the need to find hope and happiness in the darkness.

The linguistic analysis of the lyrics of Blue & Grey reveals a deep sense of longing and sadness, as well as frustration and confusion. The words used throughout the song express a feeling of being stuck and of being unable to move forward. The use of metaphors such as the heavy ground and the grey city help to convey the despair and longing in the lyrics. 

Overall, Blue & Grey is a beautiful and reflective song that speaks to the struggles of life and the desire to be happier. The lyrics are emotionally charged and convey a strong sense of longing, sadness, and frustration. The use of metaphors and the allusions to blue and grey help to convey the emotions of the song and make it an incredibly moving piece of music.
