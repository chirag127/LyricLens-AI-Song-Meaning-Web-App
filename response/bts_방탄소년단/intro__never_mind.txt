"Never Mind" by BTS (방탄소년단) is an inspiring anthem that encourages listeners to stay strong and keep pushing forward, even when faced with obstacles. The song begins with Suga's rapping, which sets the tone of the track by presenting the idea of not letting anyone stop you from achieving your goals. The lyrics talk about the struggles that come with growing up and how no matter how many times you fail, you must keep pushing forward. J-Hope's rap follows, further emphasizing the message of perseverance. His verse speaks of not letting anyone or anything stand in your way and to keep going even in the face of adversity. Rap Monster's rap is the climax of the song and serves as a reminder to never give up, no matter what. Suga's chorus ties everything together, and the lyrics once again emphasize the message of never giving up, no matter how hard it gets.
