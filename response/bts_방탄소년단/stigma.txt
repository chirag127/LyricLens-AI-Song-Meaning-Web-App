"Stigma" by BTS (방탄소년단) is a song that speaks to the struggles of feeling misunderstood and isolated from those around you. In the first verse, the narrator reveals that they have been hiding something and can no longer keep it inside. They reflect on why they couldn’t have spoken up before, as it would have been too painful. The chorus follows, with the narrator expressing their regret and sorrow for not being able to protect the person they care about. The second verse goes deeper, with the narrator addressing how their words have caused deeper and deeper wounds, and how the person they care about was too fragile to really understand what was happening. The chorus once again follows, with the narrator taking responsibility for the pain they’ve caused. In the bridge, the narrator expresses remorse for their actions, and begs for understanding. 

Overall, the song speaks to the pain of being misunderstood and unable to protect the people you care about. The lyrics are filled with sorrow and regret, making for a powerful and moving song. Through its use of vivid imagery and emotionally charged language, the song speaks to the difficulty of having to take responsibility for one's actions and the pain of not being able to keep the people you care about safe. The song also speaks to the idea of stigma, or the idea of being judged and excluded for something out of one's control. It's a powerful song that speaks to the struggles of feeling misunderstood and isolated.
