The song "Stay" by BTS (방탄소년단) is an emotional ballad about longing for the presence of someone after they are gone. The chorus then kicks in with a more upbeat tone, as the singer expresses hope that they can be together no matter the distance and that they will always stay in their heart. 

The verses feature a mixture of longing and hope, as the singer expresses a desire to stay close to the person in their thoughts, even if they can't physically be together. They also express a need for the other person to stay in their heart, and in their life, no matter the distance or the circumstance. The bridge brings a more hopeful tone to the song, as the singer expresses a desire to be with the person and to keep their memory and feelings alive. 

Overall, the song is an emotional journey of longing and hope. The lyrics and instrumentation tell a story of missing someone and being unable to let go of the memories and feelings. The song also expresses a desire to stay close, no matter the distance, and to keep the memory and feelings alive.

The song also contains a lot of repetition and alliteration, which helps to emphasize the feelings of longing and hope.
