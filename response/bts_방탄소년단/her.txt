The song “Her” by BTS (방탄소년단) is an emotionally driven track that speaks to the complexity of seeking and finding love. The song opens with the lines “The world is a complex, we was lookin’ for love”, suggesting that the search for love is a complex and difficult journey. The narrator expresses disbelief in what true love is, yet still desires to find it and love like it’s a habit. From here, the song shifts to the narrator finding a new version of themselves and being confused as to what is real love and what is not.

The chorus of the song captures the essence of the song, as the narrator expresses their desire for the person they’ve found to be their answer to all of their wonders and to be their tear. The chorus also expresses uncertainty about the relationship, as the narrator wonders if they are the truth and the lie, the love and the hate, the enemy and the heaven, and the pride and the sorrow.

The second verse of the song speaks to the lengths the narrator is willing to go to in order to be the person that the person they love desires. They are willing to wear make up, dress up, and put on a mask in order to become the person that the other person loves. They also express that their own happiness is nothing compared to the other person’s happiness.

The bridge of the song speaks to the narrator’s newfound realization that they are capable of loving someone else in the way they desire to, and that they have the ability to make this love a reality. They express that they can now face the darkness and the light and be the person that the other person loves. 

The song is an emotionally charged track about the complexity of love and the narrator’s journey to understand and accept it. Through the song, the narrator expresses their willingness to go to any lengths to be the person that the other person loves, and also their newfound appreciation for the power of love.

In terms of a linguistic analysis of the song lyrics, the lyrics express a range of emotions, from uncertainty and confusion to joy and appreciation. The lyrics also touch on themes of self-discovery and transformation, as the narrator moves from a place of uncertainty and confusion to a place of clarity and understanding. The song speaks to the power of love, and how it can bring about transformation and growth in a person's life. 

Overall, “Her” by BTS is an emotionally charged track that speaks to the complexity of love and self-discovery. The song’s lyrics express a range of emotions and explore themes of transformation and growth, while speaking to the power of love.
