The bridge features Suga, RM, and Jimin, who rap about bringing friends, talking the talk, and having the medicine to keep the eyes on the ball. The message of the bridge reflects the chorus and is a call to action for the listener to live life to the fullest. 

The second chorus is similar to the first, with Jungkook, Jimin, and Jin singing about lighting it up like dynamite. The final verse, with Jungkook, J-Hope, Jimin, and V, serves as an exclamation point to the chorus, reinforcing the message of the song.
