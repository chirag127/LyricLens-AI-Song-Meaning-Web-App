The song RUN (Ballad Mix) by the South Korean band BTS (방탄소년단) tells a story of a love that is unrequited and filled with longing. The narrator expresses their feelings of being unable to let go of their love, even though they know that their beloved is no longer with them.

The song's lyrics paint a vivid picture of a love that is unrequited and filled with longing. Through its lyrics, the song expresses a sense of regret, love, and hope that the narrator feels for their beloved.

Additionally, the use of repetition, such as “run run run”, serves to emphasize the narrator’s longing and desperation to keep their love alive. 

Overall, RUN (Ballad Mix) is an emotionally powerful song that conveys a message of unrequited love and longing.
