"INTRO: Calling" by BTS (방탄소년단) is an uplifting and empowering anthem for the band’s fans, ARMYs. The song is about staying true to oneself and being strong in the face of adversity.

The first verse of "INTRO: Calling" speaks to the resilience of the ARMYs, and calls upon them to stay gold and never give up. The lyrics draw upon the idea of gold as a symbol of strength and beauty, and encourage the ARMYs to keep their spirits up no matter what. The second verse speaks to the power of the connection between BTS and the ARMYs, and calls upon them to stay together forever. 

The song's chorus is an empowering call to action, to stay strong and never give up. The lyrics, which are delivered in both English and Korean, emphasize the power of unity and the strength of the bond between the group and its fans.

Linguistically, the song's lyrics are filled with powerful and uplifting imagery. The use of the word "gold" as a metaphor for strength and resilience is particularly effective, as is the call for the ARMYs to remain united and never give up. Additionally, the use of both English and Korean in the chorus further emphasizes the power of unity and the strength of the bond between the group and its fans.

Overall, "INTRO: Calling" is an empowering and inspiring anthem, a reminder to always stay true to oneself and never give up, no matter what. The song's uplifting lyrics and message of resilience are sure to inspire and empower, and the use of both English and Korean in the chorus further emphasizes the power of togetherness.
