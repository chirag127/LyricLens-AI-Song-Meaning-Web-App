"Interlude: 뭐해 (What Are You Doing Now)" by BTS (방탄소년단) is a reflective, melancholic track that touches on themes of loneliness, longing and regret. Musically, the song is driven by a slow, mellow beat and a soft, piano-like melody. Lyrically, the track is composed of questions that explore the narrator’s feelings of regret and longing for an absent presence.

The track opens with the line “뭐해 뭐해 뭐해,” which translates to “What are you doing now?” This question sets the tone for the song, as it immediately communicates a sense of longing for someone who is no longer present. The narrator reflects on how things were in the past, wishing they could return to the way they used to be. The chorus reflects this sentiment, as the narrator questions “What are you doing now, without me?”

The second verse explores the narrator’s feelings of regret and longing, as they reflect on the mistakes they made in the past. The narrator questions whether they should have done something differently, and wonders if the absent person still remembers them. This sentiment is further explored in the bridge, as the narrator expresses their desire to return to the past and undo the mistakes they made.

Linguistically, the song is full of emotion and longing. The narrator expresses their regret and longing in the form of questions, which helps to convey their emotions in a more powerful way. The use of repetition also helps to emphasize the narrator’s longing for the absent person.

Overall, “Interlude: 뭐해 (What Are You Doing Now)” is a moving track that powerfully conveys themes of loneliness and regret. Musically, the track is mellow yet emotionally charged, and the lyrics explore the narrator’s feelings of longing and regret in a poetic and emotive way. The song is sure to tug at the heartstrings of any listener and is a powerful testament to BTS’s ability to craft emotionally resonant music.
