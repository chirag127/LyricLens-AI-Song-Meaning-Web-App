"Just One Day" by BTS is a song about longing for a single day with someone special. The song speaks to the idea of wanting to spend time with someone, but not being able to due to the complexity of life and the fast pace of modern living. The lyrics paint a vivid picture of a man wishing to spend a single day with his special someone, and the longing he feels to be able to do so. 

The song begins with Jungkook, RM, and Suga singing about wanting time for just one day with their special someone. They express how they would indulge in the sweet scent of their partner and how they would be able to sleep in the warmth of their embrace. Jungkook then sings about how he would like to explore the unknown forest of his partner and how he would like to get to know them better. 

J-Hope and Jimin then join in to sing about how they would like to spend the day together, going out to eat, watch a movie, and share a brunch together. V then continues to express his longing for a single day with his partner, wishing he could spend a day without lies or pretense. 

The song then builds to the chorus, with Jungkook, V, Jin, and Jimin all singing about how they would like to have just one day together. They emphasize the idea of wanting to “party” together, and the chorus is full of energy and joy. The song then ends with the same chorus, emphasizing the idea of wanting to spend just one day together. 

The song is an emotional and powerful ballad that speaks to the longing of wanting to spend time with someone but being unable to do so due to the constraints of life. The lyrics convey a strong longing for a single day to be together, and the emotion of the song is enhanced by the upbeat chorus. 

Linguistically, the song uses various literary devices to convey its meaning. The use of metaphors, such as comparing their relationship to a forest, helps to create a vivid and emotional image. The use of repetition in the chorus helps to emphasize the longing for just one day together and create a sense of urgency. Finally, the use of alliteration in the chorus helps to create a more rhythmic and melodic flow to the song. 

Overall, "Just One Day" is an emotional and powerful song about wanting to spend time with someone but being unable to do so due to the constraints of life.
