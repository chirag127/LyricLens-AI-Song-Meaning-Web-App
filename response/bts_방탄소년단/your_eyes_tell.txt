Jungkook takes back over in the chorus as he sings of his beloved's gaze – even in the darkness, it is so beautiful. The pre-chorus, sung by Jimin, follows with a plea to keep crying out his beloved's name, even if his hopes cannot be fulfilled. Finally, the chorus returns, with the singers affirming their mutual understanding and love.

In summary, "Your Eyes Tell" is a song about longing for connection and understanding. It speaks of the desire to be seen and believed in, even in the face of pain and darkness. The lyrics are full of emotion and imagery, and draw on the listeners’ own experiences of love, loss, and longing.
