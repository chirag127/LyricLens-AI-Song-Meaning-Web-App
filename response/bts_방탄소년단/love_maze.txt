Love Maze by BTS (방탄소년단) is a song about finding one's way in a confusing and complex world. The lyrics tell the story of two people searching for a way out of a metaphorical "love maze," and the obstacles they must face in the process. The chorus of the song is a plea to hold on to each other, not to let go, and to stay together forever.

In the refrain, Jin sings that they must rely on themselves and not be swayed by the opinions of others. RM adds that they should not use their head to calculate their love, as it is not a business, but rather something that should be embraced. 

The second verse of the song shifts focus to the two people in love, with Jimin singing that they should not listen to what others say, and that the more they say, the more confident the two become in their relationship. Suga's rap adds depth to the metaphor of the love maze, with him singing that they must remember to look for the light in the "endless maze" and "endless abyss," even though the false paths might lead them astray. 

The final chorus of the song is a plea to take each other's hands and never let go, and that as long as they stay together, they will be able to find their way out of the love maze.

Love Maze is a song that speaks to the complexity of relationships and the importance of relying on each other in difficult times. The song's lyrics, paired with the band's powerful vocals, create a captivating and heartfelt experience.
