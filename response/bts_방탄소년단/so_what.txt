BTS' song "So What" is a powerful anthem encouraging listeners to move on and break free from their worries and fears. The chorus of the song is a rallying cry for listeners to "let go" and to remember they are young and wild and free, despite the struggles they may face.

The song begins with the verse "Somebody call me right one, somebody call me wrong / I don't wanna care, so what let go". This verse establishes the tone of the song, emphasizing the idea of freedom and moving on from the struggles of life. 

The second verse sung by J-Hope delves deeper into the concept of being able to move on from struggles and fears. He sings of how there are many worries hidden inside a single breath, and that it's time to stop worrying and to realize that the listener already knows the answers. He then encourages the listener to make a stand and make a loud noise when they feel like giving up. 

Suga's verse follows, emphasizing the idea of being able to choose your own way regardless of the obstacles one may face. He sings that there is no escape, and that it's time to take a leap of faith, as it's the only way to make something happen. 

The chorus is then repeated, and the song transitions into a rap verse by RM, who encourages the listener to not give up and to keep on living despite the struggles they may face. He reminds the listener that life is long, and that it's time to take a break and enjoy life instead of worrying. 

Jin then takes the lead, repeating the chorus of the song and emphasizing the idea of being able to let go and move on from struggles. 

The song concludes with a final chorus, once again reminding the listener to "let go" and to not let their worries and fears take over. 

The overall message of the song is one of freedom and encouragement. It encourages the listener to move on from their worries and their struggles, to take a leap of faith, and to remember that they are young and wild and free. 

The lyrics of the song contain a number of linguistic elements that help to convey the emotion and feeling of the song. The use of repetition and the call-and-response structure of the chorus helps to emphasize the message of the song, while the rhyme scheme helps to create a feeling of unity and solidarity. Additionally, the use of metaphors such as "break the wall" and "cut the wind" helps to create vivid imagery and to emphasize the themes of freedom and overcoming obstacles. 

Overall, "So What" is an uplifting and powerful song that encourages listeners to not be afraid to break the mold and to take a leap of faith. The song is filled with powerful lyrics and imagery that help to convey the emotion and feeling of the song, and it is a great anthem for anyone looking to take a stand and break free from their worries and fears.
