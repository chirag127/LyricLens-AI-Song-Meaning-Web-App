The song “BEGIN” by BTS (방탄소년단) has a powerful message of hope, strength, and courage. It speaks to the struggles of growing up and striving to find one’s place in the world. The song begins with a reflection of being just fifteen years old, feeling small in the world, and unable to imagine the future. This is a common sentiment of growing up, and the song powerfully captures this feeling. The chorus is a call for support and unity, with the lyrics “love you my brother, I’m me now, I’m me, you make me begin, smile with me”. This speaks to the importance of having a supportive network of friends and family to help one through difficult times. 

The second verse further explores this theme, speaking to the power of empathy and understanding. The lyrics “You can’t endure, I want to cry instead of you” are particularly poignant, expressing the desire to take on the burden of another in order to alleviate their pain. The bridge speaks to the power of courage, with the lyrics “Even if I die, even if my hyung is sad, even if my hyung is in pain, I’m in more pain, brother, let’s cry, cry, let’s just cry”. This speaks to the idea that even in the face of despair, one can find the strength to move forward.

The song “BEGIN” speaks to the power of community and strength in the face of adversity. It speaks to the importance of understanding and empathy, and of having a supportive network of friends and family to help one through difficult times. The song also speaks to the power of courage, and of finding strength even in the darkest of times. All in all, “BEGIN” is a powerful and uplifting song that speaks to the struggles of growing up and striving to find one’s place in the world.
