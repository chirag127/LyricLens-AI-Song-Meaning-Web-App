"Spine Breaker" by BTS (방탄소년단) is a powerful and thought-provoking song that speaks to the struggles of youth in modern society. The lyrics explore the theme of trying to live up to the expectations of society, and how that can lead to dissatisfaction and unhappiness. 

Verse 1 (RM): In this verse, RM paints a picture of society where the rich and poor are divided and education is the only way to gain status, but it can still be difficult to achieve success. He also speaks to the idea of trying to fit in and be accepted by fitting in with popular trends, but this can lead to unhappiness and dissatisfaction.

Verse 2 (Suga): In this verse, Suga speaks to the idea of trying to keep up with the latest trends and wanting things that one cannot afford. He speaks to the idea of feeling pressure to have the same things as those around you and how this can lead to feelings of envy and insecurity. 

Verse 3 (Jungkook): In this verse, Jungkook speaks to the idea of trying to fit in and be accepted. He speaks to the idea of wearing “dirty clothes” and being judged by others, and how this can make one feel insecure and inadequate.

Hook (All): In the hook, BTS speaks to the idea of being a “spine breaker”, or someone who is trying to fit in and be accepted by society, but is ultimately unhappy and unsatisfied. 

Verse 4 (J-Hope): In this verse, J-Hope speaks to the idea of feeling pressure to fit in and be accepted, even if it means going against what your parents would want. He also speaks to the idea of having to make difficult decisions and take responsibility for them. 

The overall message of "Spine Breaker" is that it can be difficult to try to fit in and be accepted by society, and that it can lead to feelings of unhappiness and dissatisfaction. The song encourages the listener to think about the choices they make and how they affect their lives.

Linguistically, the song is written in a conversational tone, with RM, Suga, Jungkook, and J-Hope each sharing their own perspectives on the struggles of youth in modern society. The lyrics use a range of metaphors and symbolism, such as the idea of the “spine breaker”, to emphasize the theme of trying to fit in and be accepted. 

Overall, "Spine Breaker" is a powerful and thought-provoking song that speaks to the struggles of youth in modern society. The song encourages the listener to think about their choices and how they affect their lives, while also providing an insightful look into the struggles faced by young people today.
