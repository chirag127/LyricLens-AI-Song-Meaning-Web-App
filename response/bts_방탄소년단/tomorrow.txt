Verse 3 (RM): In the third verse, RM talks about how quickly time passes, and how life is ultimately a process of living, not just existing. He reminds the listener to make the most of their life and to not be afraid of the future. 

Verse 4 (Jimin & Jin): The fourth verse is essentially a reprise of the second verse, with Jimin and Jin singing the same words as Jungkook and Jin in the second verse, emphasizing the importance of never giving up. 

Chorus (Jungkook, Jimin, & J-Hope): The chorus is repeated again, reinforcing the message of hope and perseverance.
