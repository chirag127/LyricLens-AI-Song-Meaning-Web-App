FAKE LOVE (Rocking Vibe Mix) by BTS (방탄소년단) is a song about the emptiness of a false love. The opening verses sung by Jungkook reflect on how he was willing to put on a brave face and pretend to be happy for the person he loves, even if it meant putting on a false mask of happiness. The refrain by Jimin, Jin, and RM expresses the idea that the love that was once present is now fake and empty. In the subsequent verses, Jungkook, J-hope, and Suga describe how they had changed for the person they loved, only to be met with confusion and distance. Lastly, the outro sung by Jimin and Jungkook reflects on how the love that was once present has become false, and how an unfading flower can no longer be found in the dream they once shared.

The overall message of the song is one of sadness and emptiness, as the narrator reflects on the false love that has been experienced. The lyrics express feelings of bitterness, confusion, and heartbreak, as the narrator comes to terms with the realization that the love they experienced was not genuine. Through a linguistic analysis of the song, it is possible to detect emotions such as sadness and regret, as well as a sense of longing and despair. This is further emphasized by the use of repetition to convey the idea of the emptiness of the fake love.

In summary, FAKE LOVE (Rocking Vibe Mix) by BTS (방탄소년단) is a song about the emptiness of a false love. It is a powerful and emotional track that captures the heartbreak and regret that comes with realizing that the love you experienced was not real. The lyrics are filled with emotion and longing, and the use of repetition conveys the sense of emptiness and sadness that comes with a false love.
