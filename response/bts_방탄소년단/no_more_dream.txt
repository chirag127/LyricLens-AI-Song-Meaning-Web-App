No More Dream by BTS (방탄소년단) is a song about rejecting the rigid expectations of society and encouraging listeners to pursue their own dreams. The lyrics begin with Rap Monster asking, “What’s your dream? What’s your dream? What’s your dream? Is it just that?” This sets up the main theme of the song – that dreams should not be limited to what society expects of us. 

The pre-hook continues this theme, with Suga rapping, “I want a big house, big cars, big rings but the truth is, I don’t have any big dreams.” This line offers a contrast to the message of societal expectations and encourages listeners to pursue their own dreams, no matter how small.

In the first verse, Rap Monster, Suga, and Jung Kook reflect on their childhood dreams, which were forgotten over time. They suggest that rather than worrying about going to college far away, it is more important to make sure one is happy and content. 

The chorus follows, with J-Hope and V encouraging the listener to question what their dreams actually are and to not be afraid to pursue them. The message here is that everyone has the potential to create their own path, regardless of what society dictates. 

The second verse follows, with Suga and Jimin rapping about how society has instilled false dreams in them. They suggest that instead of having false expectations imposed on them, they should instead focus on their own dreams and aspirations. 

The pre-hook and chorus then repeat, emphasizing the idea that one should not be afraid to pursue their own dreams and not be limited by what society expects of them. 

The bridge offers a hopeful message, with Rap Monster, Suga, Jung Kook, and J-Hope rapping about how they don’t know how to live, fly, or make decisions, but they do know that they should keep dreaming. This serves as an encouraging reminder to the listener that dreams should not be forgotten. 

In summary, No More Dream by BTS is a song about rejecting the expectations of society and encouraging listeners to pursue their own dreams. The song’s lyrics offer a positive message of hope and support, reminding listeners that they can create their own paths and should never give up on their dreams.

From a linguistic analysis perspective, the song uses a range of language to convey its message. The use of rhetorical questions and imperatives throughout the song emphasizes the importance of the message being conveyed, while the use of simple language and imagery helps to make the message more relatable and accessible to listeners. Additionally, the use of repetition also helps to reinforce the core message of the song. 

Overall, No More Dream by BTS is an inspiring and uplifting song with an important message about pursuing one’s own dreams. The song’s simple yet powerful lyrics and the use of language make it an enjoyable and powerful listen.
