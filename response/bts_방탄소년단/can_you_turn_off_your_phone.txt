"Can You Turn Off Your Phone" by BTS (방탄소년단) is a song that speaks to the disconnection that can occur between people with the rise of technology and smart phones. In the song, the members of BTS ask that their friends put away their phones, suggesting that the digital world can push them away from each other. 

The song begins with Jungkook singing “Turn off your phone/ That’s what everybody’s doing, but we’re getting dumber”, establishing the disconnect between people and technology. Jimin then follows with “Turn off your phone/ Even without mentioning it, I don’t need likes”, emphasizing the point that people don’t need to constantly be looking for validation online. Rap Monster then suggests that people should talk face to face instead of using their phones, and Suga adds that even though technology has made communication easier, it’s also come at the cost of real human contact.

The chorus of the song is sung by J-Hope and V, who sing “Push and unlock, push and unlock/ I hate your table, I hate your ease/ Push and unlock, push and unlock/ Press it, your task task”. This chorus is a metaphor for the difficulty of disconnecting from technology and the need to constantly stay connected.

The song also features a rap section from Rap Monster, who talks about how people are constantly taking pictures and updating their lives, instead of spending time with each other. He emphasizes that life should not be lived through a phone, but through real experiences.

In the bridge, Jungkook and Jimin repeat the lines “Turn off your phone/ That’s what everybody’s doing, but we’re getting dumber” and “Turn off your phone/ Even without mentioning it, I don’t need likes”, emphasizing the main point of the song.

The song overall is a commentary on the current state of society and how technology can be both a blessing and a curse. People are able to connect more easily than ever before, but it can also lead to a disconnection from the people around them. BTS suggest that people should take a step back and spend time connecting with each other in real life, instead of relying on their phones for validation or communication.

From a linguistic analysis perspective, the lyrics of the song show that the artists are expressing a combination of emotions, from frustration at the disconnection of technology, to sadness at the loss of real human contact. This is expressed through the use of metaphors and simple language, which allows the listener to connect with the song on an emotional level.

Overall, "Can You Turn Off Your Phone" is an important song that speaks to the disconnection technology can bring. Through its simple lyrics and thoughtful metaphors, BTS bring to light an important issue that many people can relate to. It serves as an important reminder to take a step back and reconnect with the people around us.
