In the song “Family Song (Wednesday)” by BTS (방탄소년단), the group expresses a message of family unity and love. The lyrics of the song tell the story of a family that takes time to get together every Wednesday, despite their busy lives, to be together and enjoy each other’s company. 

The song begins with the chorus, “Wednesday family, I love you all, okay let’s go”. This sets the tone for the song, expressing the idea that the family is close and loving, and that they have a regular time to get together and be with each other. This chorus is repeated throughout the song as a reminder of the importance of family. 

The first verse of the song speaks of the family’s commitment to spend time with each other: “Let’s all make a promise to meet once in two days / It’s not hard to talk with family members / Clap your hands, clap your hands, clap to the rhythm”. This verse emphasizes the importance of communication within the family and the joy that comes from being together. 

The second verse speaks of the effort it takes to be together, despite the group’s busy lives: “Though everyone is busy, let’s take a walk with family / A little effort is enough, yeah, let’s make it happy / You can do it too, I love my family”. This verse speaks to the effort and dedication it takes to make time for family, even when life is busy. 

The song ends with the chorus, repeating the message of family unity and love. 

Overall, this song is an uplifting reminder of the importance of family and the joy that comes from taking the time to be together. The lyrics are full of heartwarming messages of love and support, and the melody is upbeat and cheerful. It is a song that can be appreciated by people of all ages and backgrounds, and it serves as a reminder of the importance of spending quality time with family. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the song contains a range of emotions and social tendencies. The lyrics express feelings of love, unity, and joy, as well as a sense of commitment and dedication.
