BTS's song "Am I Wrong" is a poignant look at the chaotic state of the world, and how individuals can get lost in the fray. The song begins with a question — "Am I wrong, falling in love with you? Tell me, am I wrong" — and the chorus furthers that sentiment, wondering if the singer is going crazy and if they are wrong.

The verses explore different aspects of the world, from people cheating and lying, to the war between hawks and sparrows, to the media and its lack of morality, to the feeling of being lost in the world. At each turn, the chorus questions whether the singer is wrong for feeling this way — "Am I wrong, am I wrong, where do I go?"

The song is musically upbeat and optimistic, with a melodic and catchy hook that serves to contrast the lyrics, which paint a picture of a world gone mad. The lyrics are clever and insightful, and the song stands as a critique of our current state of affairs, as well as a reminder that we must not forget our own convictions in the midst of the chaos.

The linguistic analysis of the song's lyrics reveals a strong sentiment of confusion and despair. The singer is questioning their own judgement and wondering if they are making the right decision, which is a sentiment that many people can relate to. The lyrics also show a sense of frustration, as the singer calls out the people and institutions that are causing so much turmoil.

In summary, "Am I Wrong" is a powerful and evocative song that speaks to the struggles that people face in a chaotic world. The lyrics are clever and insightful, and the upbeat tempo serves to contrast the more somber subject matter. The song is a reminder to stay true to our beliefs, even in the midst of chaos, and to never forget that we are not alone in our struggles.
