The song "I NEED U" by BTS (방탄소년단) is about trying to hold onto a relationship that is slipping away. The lyrics convey a sense of desperation and longing to keep the love alive, even when it seems like it's too late. In the first verse, the narrator expresses their confusion and disbelief that things have gone wrong, asking why it had to be them and pleading with their partner to not take everything away. The chorus emphasizes the narrator's need for the partner, and how they feel alone and cold without them. 

In the second verse, the narrator expresses their regret, wishing they had seen the signs and ended things before it came to this point. The pre-chorus conveys the narrator's helplessness and inability to do anything to fix the situation. The post-chorus once again reiterates their need for the partner, begging them to stay and offering the last of their love. 

The song is filled with emotions of heartbreak and longing, and the lyrics are a powerful way to express these feelings. The use of repetition and imagery serves to emphasize the narrator's need for the partner and their pain at being unable to keep them. There is a sense of hopelessness but also a desire to keep fighting for the relationship. 

The song is a heartfelt expression of love and loss, and it is an apt reflection of the pain of a broken relationship. Linguistic analysis of the lyrics reveals a range of emotions from confusion, disbelief and regret, to desperation and loneliness. The song is a powerful reminder of the fragility of relationships and the importance of treasuring them.
