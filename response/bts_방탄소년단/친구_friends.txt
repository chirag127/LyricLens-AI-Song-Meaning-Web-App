"친구 (Friends)" by BTS (방탄소년단) is an upbeat and catchy song that tells the story of a close friendship, with a focus on the precious memories and moments that make it so special. In the first verse, RM and Jimin reminisce about their first meeting in Seoul, remembering how it felt like something special to them both. The chorus then goes on to talk about how their conversations felt like homework, but how the friendship was something special, and how they should stay together forever.

The second verse finds Jimin and V recalling the memories of their school days, remembering the stories they shared on the bus and how their uniforms made them look the same. This is followed by the pre-chorus, which reminds them to remember their friendship and not to fight. The chorus then closes out the song with the same message, emphasizing the importance of staying together.

Lyrically, this song is full of emotion and longing, as the members reflect on their friendship and the moments that make it so special. The song also speaks to the power of friendship, as it emphasizes the importance of being together in the present, while also looking back fondly on the memories of the past. The lyrics also touch on the idea of eternity, as the chorus speaks of staying together forever.

The song's production is also quite unique, as it blends elements of hip-hop and EDM with a more melodic pop sound. This gives the song an upbeat and energetic feel, which fits perfectly with the message of the song.

Overall, "친구 (Friends)" is a touching and heartfelt song that captures the beauty of friendship and all the moments that make it special. It is a perfect example of how BTS can express powerful emotions and create songs that are both lyrically and musically captivating.
