Euphoria by BTS (방탄소년단) is a song that speaks of finding joy and hope amidst life’s struggles. The song begins with Jungkook singing of how a ray of light has lit up his life again, bringing with it the dreams of his childhood. He wonders if this emotion he feels is just a dream within a dream. The lyrics then take us to a metaphorical desert of dreams, where he finds a sense of a priori, a deep and profound understanding of the world. The music builds up as he finds this inner peace, and the world around him becoming clearer and more transparent. 

The pre-chorus then takes the listener to a serene landscape, with the sound of the ocean in the background, beckoning Jungkook to take the leap and step forward into this newfound euphoria. In the chorus, Jungkook calls out to the listener, asking them to take his hands and come with him to this utopia of joy and hope.

The second verse delves into Jungkook's search for a dream that was lost in a rainbow, questioning if this was meant to be. He acknowledges the sadness in the eyes of someone else, and longs for them to stay in this dream forever. The pre-chorus then repeats, further emphasizing the idea of taking a leap of faith and finding joy and happiness in the unknown. 

The song then reaches its climax in the bridge, where Jungkook tells the listener to not wake up from this dream, no matter what. He implores them to stay in this dream forever, despite the storms and sand storms of life, promising that he will never let go of the hand they are holding. 

In summary, Euphoria by BTS is a song about finding joy and hope in the midst of life's struggles, and taking the opportunity to take a leap of faith and find solace in the unknown. The lyrics are filled with metaphors of dreams, utopias, and rainbows, and the listener is taken on a journey of finding inner peace and acceptance. In terms of linguistic analysis, the song speaks of hope, resilience, and taking chances in life, while also conveying a sense of comfort and security in the face of adversity. It is a powerful anthem of hope and joy, and a reminder of the importance of taking risks in life and embracing the unknown.
