A Typical Idol's Christmas by BTS (방탄소년단) is a festive, upbeat song about the joys of the holiday season and the struggles of being an idol. The chorus then follows, with everyone singing about all the effort they have put in as an idol, and how they are grateful that they have been able to fulfill their dreams and make it this far, despite the harshness of the schedule. Jin then raps, expressing his gratitude for the yearly Christmas tradition and the support of his fans and ARMY, while Jimin and Jungkook provide an extra chorus, emphasizing the presence of someone special by their side. 

The song is a cheerful ode to the Christmas season, with a great deal of optimism and joyousness in its lyrics. The language used is simple and direct, with the repetition of certain phrases to reinforce the message. Linguistic analysis of the song reveals a sense of pride and accomplishment, as well as a feeling of appreciation and gratitude for the support of the fans.
