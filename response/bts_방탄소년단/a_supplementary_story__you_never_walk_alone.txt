"A Supplementary Story: You Never Walk Alone" by BTS (방탄소년단) is an upbeat, uplifting song that emphasizes the power of friendship and companionship. The song starts off with Suga singing about why it is so hard to be alone and how, even if we are filled with pain and sorrow, having someone to laugh with can make it easier. J-Hope provides a contrasting perspective, singing that even though he wishes he could fly, his friend's hand can be a wing for him. The song then transitions to RM, who talks about how he has chosen a path in life and how all of his hardships are part of his own destiny. He encourages his friend to come with him and fly, even if it is a difficult road. The chorus is then sung by the rest of the members, emphasizing that even if the path is long and difficult, they will never walk alone. 

The lyrics of the song are filled with powerful emotions, and the song conveys a strong message of hope and companionship. Many of the lyrics suggest that, although life may be difficult and full of hardships, having someone to share the journey with can make it easier. This message of hope and togetherness is further emphasized by the upbeat tempo and catchy chorus. 

Linguistic analysis of the song lyrics reveals a variety of emotions, including hope, joy, and confidence. The repeated use of words such as "laugh," "fly," and "walk" suggest the positive energy that comes from having a companion, while the imagery of being able to "crawl," "walk," and "run" implies the journey of life and the challenges one may face along the way. The chorus is especially uplifting, with words like "never" and "you" emphasizing the strength of the bond between the two people. 

Overall, "A Supplementary Story: You Never Walk Alone" is an uplifting song that emphasizes the power of friendship and companionship. With its catchy chorus, upbeat tempo, and powerful lyrics, the song conveys a strong message of hope and togetherness that can be a source of strength and courage in times of difficulty.
