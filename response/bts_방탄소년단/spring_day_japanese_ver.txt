"Spring Day" by BTS (방탄소년단) is a melancholic song about the feeling of longing for someone after a separation. The song opens with RM saying, "I want to see you, but it's too much to put into words - when I look at your picture, I want to see you, but there's no time." This conveys the theme of the song - an intense desire to be with someone, but the impossibility of it due to circumstances. 

The chorus, featuring Jungkook, Jimin, and J-Hope, paints a vivid picture of winter with its snowflakes, "like powder snow, dancing in the sky - if I could, I'd fly to you right now." These lyrics express the pain of not being able to be with the person the singer loves, no matter how much he wishes for it. The song also reflects on how, although things may have changed, the feelings remain the same. 

Suga and Jimin sing in the bridge, "If it's not you that has changed, then it's me - I can bear the time that flows, but it will have to change again, won't it? We all have to." This suggests that although the singer has the same feelings for the person, the circumstances of their relationship have changed. 

The chorus ends with a hopeful message, as Jimin and Jungkook sing, "Once the cherry blossoms bloom and winter ends, I want you to stay as you are, just as you are." This expresses the hope that the singer has that, despite the pain of separation, they will be reunited when winter ends and spring comes.

Overall, "Spring Day" is a beautiful song that expresses the pain of unrequited love and longing. The lyrics, combined with the melancholic melodies, create a powerful atmosphere that conveys the singer's emotions of longing and regret. The song also has a hopeful message, as it conveys the hope that the singer will be reunited with the one they love when winter ends and spring comes. The linguistic analysis of the song's lyrics reveals a sense of sadness and regret, but also a determination to keep going and a hope for reunification in the future.
