"Intro: Persona" by BTS (방탄소년단) is an introspective reflection on identity and self-definition. The lyrics explore the idea of having to answer the question of "who am I" and trying to define oneself in a world that often imposes labels and expectations on us. RM implies that the answer to this question is something we can never find, and that a few words or phrases cannot possibly capture the complexity and beauty of one's true identity. He then speaks of his own journey of coming to terms with this concept, and how he has come to accept himself. 

The chorus of the song follows with RM declaring his own name and identity, proclaiming that he is the one who will answer the questions he poses. He also expresses the desire to give others the courage to answer their own questions and find their own identities, even when it is difficult. 

The second verse of the song examines the idea of superhero-like powers, and how these can be a source of strength and identity. RM speaks of how he has become confident in himself, and is no longer letting the opinions of others define him. He also speaks of how, despite his newfound strength, he is still uncertain of his place in the world and what kind of music, truth, mission, and muse he should be pursuing. 

The song finishes with RM repeating his initial question of "who am I" as a reminder that this is an ongoing process, and that we must continually strive to define ourselves and our place in the world. 

In summary, "Intro: Persona" by BTS is a powerful and emotive exploration of identity and the importance of finding our own path in life. The lyrics are thoughtful and powerful, and the song is an inspiring reminder of the strength and courage we all have to define ourselves.
