"Lux Æterna" by Metallica is a fast-paced and energetic song that seems to be about the power of unity and connection. In the first verse, the lyrics describe a sense of anticipation and domination, as a "sea of hearts beat as one, unified." This could be interpreted as a reference to the feeling of being part of a larger whole, and the power that comes from being united with others.

The second verse talks about the feeling of exhilaration and frenzied sensation that comes from being part of a group. The lyrics mention "kindred alliance" and "feelings alike," which suggest a sense of camaraderie and shared experiences. The song's chorus repeats the phrase "Lux Æterna," which is Latin for "eternal light." This could be interpreted as a reference to the power and enlightenment that comes from being connected with others.

In the third verse, the lyrics describe a sense of liberation and the rejection of isolation. The line "never alone" suggests a feeling of belonging and support within a group. The final chorus repeats the phrase "Lux Æterna" and the lyrics "light it," which could be interpreted as a call to action to embrace the power of unity and connection.

Overall, "Lux Æterna" seems to be a powerful and uplifting song about the strength that comes from being part of a community. It encourages listeners to embrace connection and unity, and to reject isolation and loneliness.
