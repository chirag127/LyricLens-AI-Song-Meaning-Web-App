"I'm Free" by Dua Lipa is a song about the freedom to express yourself and make your own choices without worrying about judgement from others. The chorus then reiterates this sentiment with the lyrics “Love me, hold me, love me, hold me,” emphasizing that it is only through other people's love and support that she can truly be free to do what she wants. 

In the second verse, the lyrics become more explicit, with Dua Lipa singing about how she is no longer bound to what other people think and how she can now take control of her life and make her own choices. The bridge then encourages the listener to take control of their own life and be free to do what they want, as Dua Lipa sings “you can never be free until you free yourself.” 

The song ends with a reprise of the chorus, emphasizing the message of using love and support to be free to make your own choices.
