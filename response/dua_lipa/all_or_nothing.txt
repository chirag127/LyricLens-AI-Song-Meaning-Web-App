In her song "All or Nothing", Dua Lipa paints a picture of a love that is fading away and the struggle between wanting to make it work and knowing when it's time to let go. The song begins with the narrator describing how, while laying on the chest of her lover, she can feel the emptiness of the relationship. The lyrics then move to describing tattoos on the lover's skin, which could be seen as a sign of commitment, and questions about whether or not they are meant to be together. 

The chorus is a clear statement that the narrator is giving up. She sings that they used to "burn bright", but now she is turning the lights off and is no longer willing to wait any longer. She refuses to give in and accept the apologies he may make, and instead will accept the consequences of their actions. 

The second verse reveals the narrator's feelings of being stuck in a situation that should have been over a long time ago. She has been waiting for weeks, and although they are more than friends, they are in denial about the situation. 

The bridge of the song provides a powerful conclusion to the story, as the narrator makes it clear she will not accept her lover's apologies and instead will accept the consequences of their actions. She refuses to be taken advantage of and states that if her lover thinks she will keep chasing after him, he is leaving her "hanging here like a fool". She instead will accept that it is time to move on and that it's "all or nothing".

In summary, "All or Nothing" is a powerful song about knowing when it's time to let go of a relationship that is no longer working. The narrator is faced with the decision of whether to accept her lover's apologies and stay in the relationship or accept that it's time to move on and start anew. Through her lyrics, Dua Lipa conveys a strong sense of determination and resilience, allowing the listener to feel empowered in their own life decisions.

Linguistically, this song utilizes repetition to emphasize the themes of commitment, resilience, and determination. The song also contains a combination of metaphors, similes, and personifications to convey the emotions associated with a broken relationship. This helps to create a vivid atmosphere of sadness, loss, and hope, allowing the listener to identify with the narrator's journey. 

Overall, "All or Nothing" is a powerful and emotional song that speaks to the struggles of letting go of a relationship. Through her lyrics and language, Dua Lipa conveys a strong message of resilience, determination, and hope.
