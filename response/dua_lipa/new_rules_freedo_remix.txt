Dua Lipa's song "New Rules (Freedo Remix)" is a powerful anthem of self-care and empowerment. The song is about a woman who is trying to make it through a difficult relationship and is determined to stand up for herself and make sure that she is no longer taken advantage of. The lyrics of the song tell the story of a woman who is trying to break free from her partner's manipulative behavior and learn to put her own needs first. 

The song begins with a repetition of the phrase "one one one one one," which is used to emphasize the idea of the woman taking control of her life and setting boundaries. The chorus then follows with her listing the "new rules" she is going to follow in order to make sure she is no longer taken advantage of. These rules include not picking up the phone when her partner calls, not letting him in, and not being his friend. 

The chorus is followed by a verse that emphasizes the idea of practice and repetition—the woman is going to keep repeating these new rules to herself until they become second nature. The song then returns to the chorus, and this time the lyrics are even more powerful as she refuses to let him in and tells herself that if she is "under him, she ain't gettin' over him." 

The song ends with a repetition of the chorus, emphasizing the idea that the woman is committed to following these new rules and will not be swayed by her partner's attempts to manipulate her. 

The song is a powerful reminder of the importance of self-care and standing up for yourself. The lyrics are filled with emotion and power, as the woman refuses to be taken advantage of and makes sure that she is putting her own needs first. Dua Lipa's passionate delivery of the song further emphasizes the importance of taking care of yourself, and her empowering message is sure to resonate with many listeners.
