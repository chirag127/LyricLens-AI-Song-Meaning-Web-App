The song "Chasing The Dragon" by Dua Lipa is an empowering anthem about staying true to oneself and never giving up on one's dreams, despite the obstacles that life may throw in our way. In the first verse, Lipa introduces the idea of feeling burned out by life and not knowing what one is about. She then talks about why she is so determined to keep chasing the dragon, which is a metaphor for her passion and drive. 

The chorus is the main message of the song, with Lipa declaring that she will keep chasing the dragon no matter what anyone says, and that nothing about her is typical. This is a powerful statement of self-confidence and determination that encourages others to keep going in pursuit of their goals. 

The second verse talks about how Lipa's passion is her own and how it will carry her forward despite any obstacles. The song then concludes with the chorus being repeated, emphasizing the importance of not letting anyone get in the way of achieving one's dreams.

The lyrics of this song demonstrate a strong sense of empowerment and self-confidence, with Lipa reaffirming her commitment to chasing the dragon. The language style conveys a sense of determination and resilience, with Lipa emphasizing that nothing will stop her from achieving her goals. The song also touches on the idea of never settling for less than what one wants out of life, which is an important message for any listener.

Overall, "Chasing The Dragon" is an uplifting and inspiring song that encourages listeners to never give up on their dreams and to stay true to themselves. The lyrics are full of determination and resilience, while the melody and production are upbeat and empowering. It is an anthem of self-confidence and perseverance that anyone can relate to.
