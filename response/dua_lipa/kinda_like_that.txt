Kinda Like That by Dua Lipa is an upbeat and empowering song about resilience in the face of adversity. The lyrics of the song are a reflection of the singer’s inner strength and determination, and her struggle to find stability and peace in a chaotic and unpredictable world. 

The song starts with the singer expressing her feelings of being “way too damaged to go home”, and her need to keep dancing with her eyes closed, as if nothing else matters. This is a metaphor for the feeling of being overwhelmed and lost in life, and the need to search for a sense of peace and solace. The chorus of the song then follows, with the singer repeating the line “And I like that, kinda like that” in reference to her newfound strength and determination. This is a clear reminder that even in the face of adversity, the singer is still able to find something to appreciate and take pleasure in. 

The second verse of the song focuses on the singer’s relationship with someone special. She talks about how they make her “heart run back and forth”, and how they are “everything [she] want[s] and more”. This is a powerful expression of the singer’s appreciation for someone who has been able to provide her with stability and comfort. 

The bridge of the song then follows, with the singer expressing her admiration for the beauty of broken people in the street, and how everything is incomplete yet still feels a lot like her. This is a reflection of how even in the midst of chaos and uncertainty, the singer is able to find beauty and comfort in the world around her. 

The song ends with the chorus, with the singer repeating the empowering line “And I like that, kinda like that”. This is a reminder to the listener that even in the face of adversity, they can still find something to appreciate and take pleasure in. 

Overall, Kinda Like That by Dua Lipa is an inspiring and uplifting song about resilience and determination in the face of adversity. The song is a powerful reminder that in the face of chaos and uncertainty, we can still find comfort and beauty in the world around us. Through its thoughtful lyrics and empowering message, Kinda Like That is an inspiring and motivating song that is sure to resonate with listeners.
