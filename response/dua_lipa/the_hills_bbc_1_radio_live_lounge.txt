The song “The Hills” by Dua Lipa is a tale of a relationship that can be difficult to navigate. The verses of the song tell of the difficulties of being in a relationship with someone who doesn’t necessarily “fit in” with the rest of the world. The last verse then talks of the judgement of others, and the idea that the only one they can trust is themselves.

The song also speaks to the idea of being judged by others and the need to trust only yourself.
