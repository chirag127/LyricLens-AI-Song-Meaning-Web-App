The song "Save Someone" by Dua Lipa is a powerful and emotional track that speaks of the importance of looking out for and helping others in need. In the first verse, Lipa paints a vivid picture of a person standing on a corner with no money in their pockets, and how she would be there to hold out her hand to them in their time of need. She then goes on to explain that although many of the best and worst things in life come free, she can be thought of as a guarantee of support and that she would never let them go. 

In the second verse, Lipa speaks of being the person's parachute if they were on the edge of a cliff, pushing them to live and never forget her offer of help. She then encourages the listener to "return the gift" and save someone if they can. She illustrates this with a metaphor of a child beneath a light, afraid of the monsters in the night, and how she would be there to hold them until the dawn and fight for them if they needed it. 

The chorus of the song is a plea to the listener to save someone if they have the chance, as some have been chosen to wake up the broken and they should not turn away from that responsibility. The third verse then continues this theme, speaking of being there for someone in their darkest nights and offering her strength if they need it. 

In summary, this song speaks of the importance of looking out for and helping others in need. It is a powerful reminder that we should not take for granted the help that others offer us and that we should always strive to help those who are less fortunate. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the lyrics of this song are very emotionally charged and speak of the bond between two people. The words used throughout are very vivid and descriptive, creating a sense of warmth and understanding. This is further enhanced by the use of metaphors and personification, which helps to create a sense of empathy and understanding between the singer and the listener. Overall, this song serves as a powerful reminder that we should strive to help those in need and not take for granted the help that others offer us.
