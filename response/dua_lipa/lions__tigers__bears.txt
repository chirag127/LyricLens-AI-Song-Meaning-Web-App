“Lions & Tigers & Bears” by Dua Lipa is a powerful and moving song about finding comfort and security in the arms of a loved one. The lyrics reflect a deep need for protection from the troubles and worries of the world, and a longing for a safe refuge. 

The song begins with an introspective look at the singer's current state of mind, expressing a sense of emptiness and lack of direction. She looks in the mirror and out the window but can find nothing to fill the void she feels. Then the chorus sets in, with the singer realizing that when she is next to her beloved, the emptiness she feels fades away. She finds the protection she needs from her partner, and she expresses her need for this protection from the troubles of the world, from “lions and tigers and bears”.

The chorus is repeated throughout the song, emphasizing the singer's desire for safety and security in her relationship. The lyrics offer a glimpse into the feelings of vulnerability and insecurity that lurk beneath the surface, and the chorus serves as a reminder that the love of a partner can provide a safe haven. 

The lyrics of “Lions & Tigers & Bears” also invite a deeper linguistic analysis, as they are rife with metaphors and symbolism. The use of the animals in the chorus serves as a metaphor for the troubles and dangers the singer fears, while the line “all I need is you to protect me 'til tomorrow” conveys a sense of trust and reliance on her partner.

Overall, “Lions & Tigers & Bears” is a beautiful song about the power of love and the comfort that can be found in the arms of a partner. The lyrics are poignant and evocative, and the metaphors provide a meaningful insight into the singer's emotions.
