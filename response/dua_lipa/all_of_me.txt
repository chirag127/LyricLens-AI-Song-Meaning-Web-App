The song ends with the lyrics “give your all to me, I’ll give my all to you”, emphasizing the idea of giving your all to someone and trusting that they will do the same.

In summary, "All of Me" by Dua Lipa is a heartfelt and emotive song that speaks to the power of unconditional love, and the commitment and dedication involved in loving someone.
