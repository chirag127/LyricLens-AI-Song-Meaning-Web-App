The song "Want To" by Dua Lipa is a celebration of individual freedom and personal autonomy. In the first verse, Lipa expresses her desire to take control of her own life, refusing to hesitate and asserting her right to make her own decisions. The chorus expresses a similar sentiment, with Lipa declaring that it is her body and she will dance if she wants to. The second verse further emphasizes this theme of freedom, as Lipa acknowledges that different people have different ways of expressing themselves, and that it is important to accept others and their individual choices. 

Lyrically, the song is about self-expression, freedom, and autonomy. In the chorus, Lipa affirms her right to do as she pleases with her own body, without judgment from others. This expression of independence is further explored in the second verse, where Lipa encourages people to be true to themselves and embrace the freedom to make their own choices. 

The song is an empowering anthem that celebrates the beauty of individuality. The lyrics are uplifting and inspiring, and the upbeat music encourages listeners to embrace their right to be free. The song is also a reminder that it is important to respect and accept the choices of others, even if they differ from our own. 

Overall, "Want To" is an uplifting and inspiring song that celebrates individual freedom and encourages listeners to be true to themselves. The lyrics promote self-expression and acceptance of others, while the upbeat music makes it a great song to listen to when you need a reminder to embrace your autonomy and be yourself.
