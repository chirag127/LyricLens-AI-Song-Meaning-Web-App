Dua Lipa’s “Do I Wanna Know?” is a heartfelt and passionate love song about unrequited love and the hope that the object of one’s affections will someday return those feelings. The song is set to a slow and mellow melody, allowing the lyrics to take center stage.

The chorus repeats and the song ends with the singer continuing to express his hope that his affections will be reciprocated. 

The lyrics of “Do I Wanna Know?” are a heart-wrenching expression of unrequited love and the hope that the object of one’s affections will someday return those feelings.
