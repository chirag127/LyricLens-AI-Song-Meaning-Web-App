The song "Blow Your Mind (Mwah)" by Dua Lipa is a powerful and empowering anthem that encourages self-confidence and independence. The song starts out with a punchy, catchy beat and Lipa sings about knowing that she and her partner have something money can’t buy. She then goes on to sing about fighting, biting her lip, and loving until late in the night. In the pre-chorus, Lipa encourages her partner to accept her for who she is and not to try to tame her. 

The chorus follows with Lipa questioning why she is on her partner's mind if they don't like the way she talks or rocks. She then goes on to sing about how they may fight and argue but her partner will still love her blind. Lipa then states that she can "blow your mind" and encourages her partner to keep the door open if they want more. 

In the post-chorus, Lipa reiterates that she can blow her partner's mind and that there is no dollar sign that can guarantee it. 

The breakdown of the song follows with Lipa singing the pre-chorus and chorus again, but with the addition of a new line: "Hey, tell me I'm too crazy, you can't tame me, can't tame me, tell me I have changed but I'm the same me, old me, inside." This line further emphasizes Lipa's desire for her partner to accept her for who she is and not to try to change her. 

The song ends with Lipa repeating the post-chorus.

This song is a powerful anthem about self-confidence and independence. Lipa's lyrics speak to her desire to be accepted and respected for who she is and her desire to make her own decisions. The song's catchy beat and strong lyrics make it an enjoyable and empowering listen. 

Linguistically, the song is filled with powerful imagery and metaphors, such as "fighting in fits" and "biting your lip," which help to convey Lipa's desire for her partner to accept her for who she is and not to try to change her. Additionally, the repetition of the chorus highlights Lipa's determination to stand her ground and be respected. 

Overall, "Blow Your Mind (Mwah)" is a powerful and empowering anthem that encourages self-confidence and independence. With its catchy beat and strong lyrics, it is an enjoyable and empowering listen.
