The song "Secrets" by Dua Lipa is a song about a relationship that two people are keeping secret. In the song, Lipa expresses her desire to keep their relationship hidden from the rest of the world, and invites her partner to join her in savoring the love they share without anyone else’s judgement.

The song opens with the lines “Moving too fast, they say it won’t last / But I just wanna, yeah, I just wanna see for myself”. Here, she acknowledges that other people may doubt the longevity of their relationship, but she wants to ignore their opinions and instead experience their love for herself.

The next verse starts with the lines “The summer days melt, no kiss and no tell / Cause I just wanna, yeah, we just wanna”. This verse is about the couple’s need to keep their relationship a secret, to not even kiss or tell each other anything in case someone else finds out.

The pre-chorus includes the lines “Cause we can’t control this familiar feeling between us / We don’t have to say that we’re lovers, even though we’re touching each other”. The couple is aware that they can’t control the strength of their feelings for each other, but they don’t want to label their relationship as a “romantic” one.

The chorus then repeats the line “But for now let’s keep it secret”, emphasising the need for discretion.

The song ends with the repeating of the pre-chorus and chorus, with Lipa once again emphasising the need to keep their love a secret.

In summary, the song “Secrets” by Dua Lipa is a song about a relationship that two people are keeping secret. The song is about wanting to keep their love away from judgement and criticism, and savoring it while they can.

Linguistically, the song is written in a conversational style, which reflects the intimate nature of the relationship. The choice of words are poetic, with words such as “melt”, “savor”, and “familiar” conveying a sense of comfort and familiarity between the two lovers. The song also contains a sense of longing, as the couple yearn to keep their relationship a secret, yet still express their love for each other.

Overall, “Secrets” by Dua Lipa is an intimate, romantic song that captures the feeling of a relationship kept away from prying eyes. The poetic lyrics and conversational style are beautiful, and the song conveys a sense of longing and desire. It is a song that will be sure to resonate with many listeners.
