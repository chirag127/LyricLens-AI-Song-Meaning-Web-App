Dua Lipa’s song “Garden” is a reflective and introspective piece about the uncertainty of relationships and the difficulty of finding a lasting connection. The narrator is looking back fondly on the past and assessing the current state of her relationship, questioning whether it is worth continuing or if it is time to move on. 

The first verse contains imagery of a fondly remembered past, with references to swimming in the ocean and running in the open. The chorus follows a bittersweet tone, as the narrator acknowledges the impossibility of returning to the past and wonders if there is hope outside of the garden they have created. The pre-chorus builds on this sentiment, expressing self-consciousness and examining the worth of the relationship. The second verse contains an emotional plea for the other person to stay, as the narrator offers her undying loyalty.

Linguistically, the song is filled with emotion and longing. The repetition of the phrase “this garden of Eden” speaks to the desire to return to a simpler time and the fear of leaving the safety of the relationship. The use of questions and imperatives further emphasize the narrator’s uncertainty and the urgency of the situation.

Overall, “Garden” is a beautiful and heartfelt song about the struggle to maintain relationships and the difficulty of letting go. The lyrics capture the conflicting emotions that come with making difficult decisions, and the track’s evocative instrumentation serves to emphasize the intensity of the narrator’s feelings.
