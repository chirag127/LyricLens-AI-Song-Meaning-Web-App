The song "I'd Rather Go Blind (Live)" by Dua Lipa is a deeply emotional ballad about a woman's heartbreak over a man she loves leaving her for someone else. The chorus of the song further emphasizes this sentiment with the lines "And baby, and baby, I'd rather be blind now, than to see you walk away from me". 

The remainder of the song continues to express the narrator's sadness and despair, and her willingness to go blind even though she knows it's not possible. The song ends with her expressing her grief and disbelief at what has happened, and her sorrow at being left behind.

Additionally, the repetition of certain lines helps to emphasize the narrator's feelings and provide a sense of resolution to the song.
