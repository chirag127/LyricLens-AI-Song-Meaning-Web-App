Dua Lipa’s “Top Dollar” is an ode to self-empowerment and independence, a declaration of independence from material wealth and the societal expectations that come with it. The song starts off with a chorus that speaks of the artist’s preference for spending her time over collecting material possessions. She strikes a defiant note in the pre-chorus, declaring that her love is worth more than any diamonds or gold, and that she should be valued and respected as such. 

The verses of the song provide further insight into the artist’s thoughts and feelings. She acknowledges that she may not be meant to be with the person she is singing about, but she can still find joy in simple pleasures. In the bridge, the artist reiterates that her love is worth more than any material wealth, and that she should be valued as such.

On a lyrical level, “Top Dollar” is a song about self-worth and independence. The artist speaks of her preference for spending her time over collecting material possessions, a sentiment that is echoed in the chorus. The artist’s defiance towards societal expectations of wealth and material possessions is further highlighted in the pre-chorus and bridge.

On a linguistic level, the song uses a variety of language styles, including repetition and alliteration. Repetition is used to emphasize the artist’s message, while alliteration is used to create a catchy hook. Additionally, the use of rhetorical questions adds an element of urgency to the song, while the use of direct address encourages the listener to take action.

In summary, “Top Dollar” is a song about self-worth and independence. The artist speaks of her preference for spending her time over collecting material possessions and her defiance towards societal expectations of wealth and material possessions. The song’s lyrics are delivered in a variety of language styles, including repetition and alliteration, to emphasize the artist’s message. The song is an empowering anthem that encourages its listeners to value themselves and strive for independence.
