In Dua Lipa’s song “New York (Mend My Broken Heart Tonight)”, the artist takes us on a journey as she reflects on her longing for her former lover and her search for solace in her new home, New York. In the first verse, the artist expresses her desire for her former lover, but acknowledges that she must let him go due to the darkness in her soul. She then calls out to New York to mend her broken heart and save her in its arms. 

The second verse further explores the artist’s heartache, as she notes that without her former lover, she is alive but her heart is cold as ice. She then again calls out to New York, asking for it to mend her broken heart. 

In the third verse, the artist expands on her search for solace, as she reveals that she ran away from home to follow her dreams. She expresses her desire to keep her former lover with her, and again calls out to New York to mend her broken heart. 

Overall, “New York (Mend My Broken Heart Tonight)” is a heartbreaking song which explores the artist’s longing for her former lover and her search for solace in her new home. The lyrics provide an insight into the artist’s emotional state, and the use of repetition helps to emphasise her desperation for the healing power of New York. From a linguistic analysis, the song’s lyrics reveal strong emotions of heartache, sadness, and longing. 

The song is a powerful exploration of heartbreak and longing, and its thoughtful lyrics and strong emotion make it a powerful and moving listen.
