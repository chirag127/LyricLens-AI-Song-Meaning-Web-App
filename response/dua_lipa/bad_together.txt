In the song "Bad Together" by Dua Lipa, the singer reflects on a relationship that is both passionate and destructive. The lyrics suggest a strong connection between the two partners, but also a feeling of guilt or shame for being together. The song starts with the singer admitting that they know their relationship is wrong, but that they are both so drawn to each other that they can't help themselves. The chorus further reinforces this idea, with the singer declaring that they don't care what other people think of them and their relationship, and that they will continue to be bad together. 

In the first verse, the singer reflects on the pain and scars of their relationship, but also the closeness they feel when they are together. The pre-chorus further emphasizes this duality, as the singer promises to be good when they are apart, but also acknowledges that they are misunderstood. In the second verse, they emphasize the idea that they make a great team even when they are bad, and that they are stronger together than apart. 

The song is a reflection on the passionate and destructive nature of relationships, and how despite knowing that it may be wrong, two people can be so drawn to each other that they don't care what anyone else thinks. The lyrics demonstrate the use of language to express emotions and feelings, such as the use of the words "bad" and "loved up bruise" to convey the conflicting emotions that the singer feels. 

Overall, "Bad Together" is an emotionally charged song that reflects on the passionate nature of relationships and how two people can be so drawn to each other that they don't care about what anyone else thinks. The lyrics are filled with emotion and convey the conflicting feelings of love and guilt that the singer feels. It is a great song for anyone who is struggling with a difficult relationship and is looking for an anthem to relate to.
