"Swan Song" by Dua Lipa is a powerful and moving song about resilience, perseverance, and hope. The “it” that is falling could be interpreted as a metaphor for hope or a better future, which is being lost.

Despite the fear and anxiety, the singer is determined to persevere and fight for a better future. The lyrics “Tread heavily around me” could be interpreted as a call for help, for support and companionship in the fight. 

The song has a strong emotional impact, with the lyrics being reflective of the singer’s feelings of fear, anxiety, and ultimately hope. The lyrics also make use of strong imagery, with the “flicker” and “diamonds” being used to create vivid mental images of the emotions and feelings of the singer.

The song’s lyrics provide an insightful and emotive analysis of an individual’s fight against fear and anxiety, and how hope can be found in even the darkest of times.
