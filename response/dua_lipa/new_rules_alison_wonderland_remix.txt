The song “New Rules (Alison Wonderland Remix)” by Dua Lipa is a powerful anthem about reclaiming agency over one’s life, and the importance of setting boundaries and respecting oneself. The opening lyrics set the tone with “I got new rules I count ‘em”, which is a declaration of the narrator’s newfound determination to take control and create a life for herself. 

The first verse details the narrator’s struggles to break away from her partner’s influence. She describes staying up late at night, talking to herself and going “out of [her] mind”, and writing down everything she’s feeling in an attempt to save herself. This verse reflects the struggle of trying to break away from unhealthy patterns, and the power of writing things down to bring clarity and understanding. 

The chorus then describes the narrator’s newfound rules, which serve as a way to protect herself and maintain her autonomy. The lyrics “Don’t pick up the phone / You know he's only calling 'cause he's drunk and alone” and “Don't be his friend / You know you're gonna wake up in his bed in the morning” are powerful reminders of the importance of setting boundaries and respecting oneself. 

In the second verse, the narrator reflects on her struggles to break away from the pattern of her relationship, noting that no matter how hard she tries, she still finds herself “pullin’ [her] backwards”. She eventually finds her way out of the cycle, noting that she finally sees the pattern and “[she] never learn[s]”. 

The chorus then returns, reinforcing the importance of the narrator’s new rules and her newfound determination to take control of her own life. 

The song “New Rules (Alison Wonderland Remix)” is a powerful anthem about reclaiming agency over one’s life, and the importance of setting boundaries and respecting oneself. Lyrically, the song is filled with vivid imagery and powerful statements about the importance of self-respect and autonomy. Through her lyrics, Dua Lipa conveys a strong message about the importance of taking control of one’s life, and the power of setting boundaries and respecting oneself.
