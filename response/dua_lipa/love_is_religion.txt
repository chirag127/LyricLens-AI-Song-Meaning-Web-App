The song Love is Religion by Dua Lipa is a passionate ode to the power of love. In the song, Lipa expresses her belief that love is the ultimate form of religion, and that it can be a source of strength, peace, and comfort in times of hardship.

She then goes on to explain that she is willing to be the one to bring out the angel in her beloved, despite the fact that the world can be a cruel and unforgiving place.

The pre-chorus of the song further emphasizes the power of love, with the singer offering to be her beloved's "muse, ally, moon, and sacrifice". This sentiment is repeated in the chorus, with Lipa declaring that "love is religion", and asking her beloved to join her in prayer.

The second verse of the song continues to explore the power of love, with Lipa describing the fire that is rising in her heart like a choir, singing the couple's favorite song. She then expresses her desire to remain with her beloved, offering to be their "gold, delight, and soul".

The chorus and bridge of the song once again reiterate the idea that "love is a religion", and Lipa encourages her beloved to join her in prayer.

The song Love is Religion by Dua Lipa is a passionate ode to the transformative power of love.
