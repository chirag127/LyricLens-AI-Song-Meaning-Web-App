"Pretty Please" by Dua Lipa is a catchy and upbeat pop song about a person desperately wanting to be loved, and asking for that love and attention from the person they care about. The chorus of the song is a plea for the person to "put [their] mind at ease/pretty please," and for them to provide the narrator with "sweet relief/pretty please." The verses explore the narrator's feelings of longing, desperation, and need for their partner's attention. 

The first verse of the song is a reflection on how the narrator has been pretending to be okay with their partner's lack of attention, even though they are not. They reflect on how the facade they were creating has been broken now, and they are ready to open up and tell the truth, that they need the person's love.

The chorus of the song further emphasizes the narrator's plea for love and attention, with the repetition of the phrase "pretty please." This repetition highlights the desperation and longing of the narrator, and how much they need the person's love and attention.

The second verse of the song continues the narrator's plea for their partner to provide them with the love and attention that they need. They express how much they hate it when their partner leaves them unattended, and how they need their love to help them slow down their wild mind. 

The bridge of the song brings the plea to a higher intensity, with the narrator asking for their partner's help to make them feel "pretty" and "sweet relief." This is a further reflection on the desperate need for the person's love and attention, and how it can help the narrator feel better and more secure.

The song ends with the chorus, repeating the plea for their partner to provide them with love and attention.

The song's lyrics provide a raw and emotive insight into the narrator's feelings of longing and the desperation they feel for their partner's attention. The lyrics also provide a commentary on how the need for love and attention can manifest in different ways, and how it can become overwhelming if it is not given. 

In terms of linguistic analysis, the lyrics of "Pretty Please" are full of emotional language, specifically words related to longing and desperation. The repetition of the phrase "pretty please" is used to emphasize the narrator's feelings and their plea for love and attention. The song is full of imagery and metaphors that create a vivid and emotive picture of the narrator's feelings. 

Overall, "Pretty Please" is a powerful and emotive song about longing for love and attention. The lyrics are full of emotive language and imagery, and the repetition of the phrase "pretty please" emphasizes the narrator's desperation for love and attention.
