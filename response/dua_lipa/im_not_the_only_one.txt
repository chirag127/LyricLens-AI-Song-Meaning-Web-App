The song "I'm Not the Only One" by Dua Lipa is a powerful, emotionally-charged and thought-provoking track that speaks of betrayal and heartache. The lyrics paint a vivid picture of a broken relationship and the feelings of hurt, frustration, and hopelessness that come with it.

In the second verse, the singer acknowledges her partner's inaccessibility and inability to commit, implying that her partner is not capable of giving her the same level of love and devotion that she has for them.

The third verse is the singer's realization of the depth of her feelings for her partner and her fear of not being good enough for them. She also accuses her partner of lying and tearing them apart.

The bridge of the song, in which the singer states "I know, I know, I know, I know, I know, I know, I know, I know, I'm not the only one", serves to emphasize the strength of her conviction and her refusal to be broken by her partner's betrayal.

The song is a powerful expression of the confusion and despair that accompanies heartbreak and betrayal. The lyrics provide a vivid and detailed account of the emotional turmoil and pain that a broken relationship can cause.

The lyrics also provide an emotional analysis of the singer's state of mind. Through her words, she conveys her feelings of hurt, disbelief, and desperation, as well as her determination to not be broken by her partner's actions.
