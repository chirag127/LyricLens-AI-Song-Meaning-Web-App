The song "Want To (Demo)" by Dua Lipa is a lively and uplifting anthem that celebrates self-love and freedom. The song opens with the lines, "Gimme release, keep the drugs/Gimme relief, keep the love/Just gimme a piece of that one piece of that/Give it to me, don't judge, just give it to me," emphasizing the importance of both physical and emotional liberation. The chorus further reinforces this message of independence, repeating the phrase "I won't stop, not now" in a way that encourages listeners to keep pushing for the freedom that they desire. 

The song also encourages listeners to not be afraid to take risks, as indicated by the lyrics, "Some people do, but some they just like to talk okay/Some shoot the breeze, but some like to leave a mark okay/Some need the light, and some people like the dark okay." This encourages listeners to take chances and not be afraid of failure. 

The song culminates with the lines, "Don't cry for us, no time for us/When we're at some party, when you grab somebody," which further emphasizes the importance of being true to oneself and not letting others dictate what one does or does not do. 

In summary, "Want To (Demo)" is an empowering song that celebrates self-love and encourages listeners to take chances and be true to themselves. Through its catchy and uplifting beat, the song motivates and inspires listeners to take risks and be unapologetic in their pursuit of freedom. 

The lyrics of the song also provide insight into the artist's emotional state. By repeating the phrase "I won't stop, not now," Dua Lipa conveys the importance of perseverance and determination in the face of adversity. The lyrics also emphasize the importance of taking risks and not being afraid of failure, which is reflective of the artist's attitude towards life. 

Overall, "Want To (Demo)" is an emotionally charged and inspiring song that celebrates self-love and encourages listeners to take risks and be unapologetic in their pursuit of freedom. Through its uplifting beat and empowering lyrics, the song is able to motivate and inspire listeners to take chances and be unafraid of failure.
