The song "25/8" by Dua Lipa is a catchy and upbeat song about spending an entire night together without worrying about the time. It speaks of the thrill of being with someone and the joy of being in the moment instead of focusing on the future. 

The first verse starts off with the singer's friends calling her in the morning, suggesting that she has been out late the night before. She responds that she has been busy, but the only thing missing is her clothes. This is a metaphor for letting go of the restrictions of everyday life, and instead focusing on having fun with her partner. 

The pre-chorus is a call to action for the listener to join her in the moment and not worry about the time or the outside world. She sings about being in between the sheets with her partner and how it's like heaven. 

The chorus is an invitation for the listener to join her in her "no sleep" world and ignore the clocks. She invites the listener to join her in her "unlimited" and never-ending night, and to "get it now" before it's too late. 

The second verse speaks of the singer's feelings of hunger and a rush of adrenaline when she is with her partner. She invites her partner to keep the blinds closed and to keep the night going. 

The pre-chorus and chorus repeat, but this time with more urgency as the singer speaks of needing all the extra hours and power to do the things she wants to do. 

Overall, the song is a celebration of embracing the moment and not worrying about the time or the future. It speaks of the joys of being with someone and the thrill of being in the moment. The song is catchy, upbeat, and sure to make the listener want to join in and celebrate the night. 

In terms of a linguistic analysis of the song, the lyrics suggest a sense of excitement and joy, with an undercurrent of urgency and a desire to make the most of the moment. The lyrics are filled with metaphors and symbols, such as the missing clothes, which suggest a desire to let go of the everyday and embrace the present. The chorus is particularly powerful, as it speaks of a never-ending night and an invitation to join in the celebration. 

Overall, "25/8" by Dua Lipa is a catchy and upbeat song that speaks of the joys of being with someone and the thrill of being in the moment. Its lyrics are filled with metaphors and symbols, and its chorus is particularly powerful, making it a song sure to get the listener up and dancing.
