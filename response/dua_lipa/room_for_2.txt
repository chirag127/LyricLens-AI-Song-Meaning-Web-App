The song "Room for 2" by Dua Lipa is an uptempo pop song with an empowering message about the power of karma and the importance of not allowing oneself to be taken advantage of. 

In the first verse, Lipa expresses her frustration with a person who is blaming her for their insecurities and is treating her poorly due to them. She sings that she has never done anything wrong, but the person has shut her out of their life. The second verse sees Lipa reflecting on how the person has been treating her, and how their behavior has been unjust. 

The chorus of the song finds Lipa singing about the power of karma, and how the person who has done her wrong will eventually feel the consequences of their actions. She is hopeful that one day the person will change, and that they can both move on and leave the past behind. 

The third verse sees Lipa reflecting on the person and how they have treated her, and how they have barely remembered her name. She is hopeful that the person will eventually feel complete. 

The lyrics of "Room for 2" express the power of karma, and how one should never take advantage of another person. The song also conveys a message of resilience and the importance of standing up for oneself. 

From a linguistic perspective, the song contains a mixture of positive and negative emotions. The positive emotions are expressed when Lipa is singing about the power of karma and being hopeful that the person will eventually change. The negative emotions are expressed when she is singing about the person's unjust behavior and how they have taken advantage of her. 

Overall, "Room for 2" is an empowering and uplifting pop song that conveys a strong message about resilience and the importance of standing up for oneself. It is a song that is sure to resonate with many listeners.
