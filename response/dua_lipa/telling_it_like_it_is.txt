The song “Telling It Like It Is” by Dua Lipa is a powerful declaration of self-confidence and independence. In the song, Lipa sings about not wanting to be tied down by a relationship she doesn’t feel ready for, and how she won’t be forced into something she doesn’t want. The chorus is a bold statement of her commitment to standing her ground: “I’m just telling it like it is / I don’t need you to say those words over / I’m just telling it like it is.” 

The first verse focuses on Lipa’s awareness of the other person’s feelings and her refusal to be manipulated by them. She tells them to “leave this girl alone” and “delete my number from your phone”, illustrating her unwillingness to be taken advantage of. In the second verse she expands on this theme, saying “Rather die a thousand times tonight than take you home”, emphasizing the strength of her convictions.

The third verse is an expression of her self-confidence and independence. She declares that she’s “not messed up baby, not heartless”, and that she’s “into shopping but no baby departments”, emphasizing that she is not interested in settling down. Finally, the chorus is repeated in the fourth verse, further reinforcing her bold stance. 

Linguistic analysis of the song reveals the strong emotions behind Lipa’s words and the powerful determination behind her message. The use of repetition with the chorus and the repeated words (“won’t”, “don’t”, “go”) emphasizes the strength of her convictions and her desire to be heard. The song also contains elements of defiance, as seen in her refusal to be taken advantage of and her emphasis on remaining independent. 

Overall, “Telling It Like It Is” by Dua Lipa is a powerful anthem of independence and self-confidence. The song is full of emotion and determination, and contains a strong message of standing up for oneself and declaring one’s own truths.
