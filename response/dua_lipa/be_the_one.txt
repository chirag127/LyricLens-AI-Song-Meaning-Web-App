The song “Be The One” by Dua Lipa is an upbeat pop song about finding love and loyalty in a relationship. In the first verse, the singer expresses her admiration for the person she loves, saying she sees the moon when she looks at them. She is aware of the fact that she is not fooling anyone, and that when the person she loves is gone, all the lights go out and she realizes she was wrong. In the chorus, the singer expresses her devotion to this person, saying that she will do anything for them and that she got inside their head. 

The second verse further emphasizes the singer’s commitment, as she again mentions that she will do anything for them and that she won’t let them down. In the bridge and the last chorus, the singer asks if the other person will be hers, expressing her desire to get to know them better and prove her loyalty. 

The lyrics of the song express emotions of admiration, devotion, and desire, and the upbeat melody conveys a feeling of hope and optimism. The song is an uplifting anthem of love and loyalty, and it serves as a reminder that even in difficult times, true love will always find a way. 

The song is a well-crafted pop tune with inspiring lyrics and a catchy melody. It is a perfect example of Dua Lipa’s impressive songwriting ability, and it is sure to remain a fan favorite for years to come.
