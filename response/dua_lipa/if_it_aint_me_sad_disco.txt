Dua Lipa's song "If It Ain't Me (Sad Disco)" is a powerful and emotive track that speaks to the pain of unrequited love. The song opens with the lyrics ‘I bet we’re higher than the people on cloud nine, this connection got me feeling like I’ve known you my whole life’, evoking a sense of a connection so strong that only the two people involved can truly understand it. The imagery of the shadows knowing each other in the light further reinforces this idea, as shadows represent the hidden parts of ourselves that we keep hidden from the world. 

In the pre-chorus, Lipa sings of the confusion and fear she feels over the possibility of the relationship ending. She admits that she’s not ‘really thinking clear’, and that the situation feels ‘too good to be true’. This fear is further explored in the chorus, in which she sings of how the thought of losing him fills her with dread, and how she will be left ‘dancing with my heart broke’ if it isn’t her. 

The second verse sees Lipa reflecting on the ‘devil dancing in my mind’, a metaphor for the doubts and fears that have crept into her thoughts. She acknowledges that these doubts can ruin her night, but that looking into his eyes helps her to shake them off. This is followed by the pre-chorus and chorus again, further reinforcing the idea that she is scared of losing him and the devastation it would bring. 

In summary, "If It Ain’t Me (Sad Disco)" is a powerful and emotive song about the fear of losing a partner and the sense of dread that comes with it. The lyrics explore the idea of an intense connection between two people and the fear of not being able to keep that connection. The song is full of imagery and metaphors that help to convey the emotions of the song, and the chorus is incredibly powerful and evocative.

From a linguistic standpoint, the song uses a range of techniques to evoke emotions and express the sense of dread that Lipa is feeling. The use of metaphors such as the ‘devil dancing in my mind’ and the ‘shadows knowing each other in the light’ help to bring her emotions to life, and the repetition of the chorus reinforces the idea of her fear and dread. Additionally, the use of words such as ‘babe’, ‘baby’, and ‘love’ help to add an intimate and personal feel to the song. 

Overall, "If It Ain’t Me (Sad Disco)" is a powerful and emotive song that speaks to the pain of unrequited love. The lyrics are full of imagery and metaphors that help to bring the emotions of the song to life. The use of repetition and intimate language helps to reinforce the message of the song, and the chorus is incredibly powerful and evocative. It is a beautiful and moving song that speaks to anyone who has ever felt the fear of losing someone they love.
