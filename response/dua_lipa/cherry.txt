Dua Lipa's song "Cherry" is a heart-wrenching ballad about betrayal and the pain of unrequited love. The lyrics tell the story of a woman whose partner has left her for someone else, and she expresses her anger and hurt at the situation. The chorus is particularly powerful, with the singer asking "Cherry, how could you do me this way?" and emphasizing the betrayal and hurt she feels. 

The verse lyrics tell a story of a relationship that has soured, with the singer blaming her partner for not being honest with her and for manipulating her. The pre-chorus reinforces this point, as she questions why her partner had nothing to say to her, and how he could do this to her after all the ups and downs they had been through. 

The chorus reiterates the singer's feelings of betrayal, hurt and anger as she questions how her partner could look into her face and still do this to her. The bridge of the song further emphasizes the hurt and betrayal of the situation, as she tells her partner that she will never take him back and that he is no longer welcome in her life. 

The song's lyrics are full of emotion and heartache, and the use of imagery and figurative language throughout the song helps to paint a vivid picture of the heartache and betrayal felt by the singer. The song's language is also full of anger, hurt and frustration, which helps to portray the singer's emotions in a powerful and effective way. 

Overall, "Cherry" is an emotionally powerful ballad that effectively captures the hurt and betrayal felt by the singer. The lyrics are full of emotion and imagery and the use of figurative language helps to bring the song to life.
