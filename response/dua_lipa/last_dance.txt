The song “Last Dance” by Dua Lipa is a powerful and emotionally charged ballad that speaks to the feelings of insecurity and longing that can be found in a relationship. In the opening verse, Lipa speaks of the attraction between her and her lover and the fear of the potential crash that could come if they pursue their relationship. The chorus goes on to emphasize the power of their connection, as she sings of feeling “at home” and being “built to last” when she is with her lover. The bridge of the song speaks to the pain of being apart from her lover, and the difficulty of reconciling the emotions that come with it. 

The lyrics of this song are highly emotive and full of imagery, allowing the listener to feel the full range of emotions that Lipa tries to convey. The lyrics are written in a conversational style, with Lipa using the words “we” and “our” to emphasize the relationship between her and her lover. The chorus has a particularly powerful message, as Lipa sings of the strength of their bond and their “last dance”, speaking to the idea that this could be the last time they are together. 

Linguistically, the song is full of powerful imagery, with Lipa using words such as “fatal”, “crashin'”, and “burnin'” to evoke a sense of danger and intensity. Additionally, the repetition of the words “last dance” throughout the song emphasizes the urgency of the situation and the power of the relationship between Lipa and her lover. 

Overall, “Last Dance” is a powerful and emotional ballad that speaks to the feelings of insecurity and longing that come with being in a relationship. Through her lyrics, Lipa is able to evoke a sense of urgency and intensity, while also conveying the strength of the bond between her and her lover. The song is full of powerful imagery and emotionally charged language, making it an emotionally resonant and powerful listen.
