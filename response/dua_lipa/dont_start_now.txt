The song Don’t Start Now by Dua Lipa is an upbeat pop anthem that celebrates self-confidence and moving on from a past relationship. The song is about not letting an ex-lover have any power over the singer’s life or emotions, and instead choosing to be strong and empowered. The lyrics in the song depict the singer’s journey of self-discovery and independence after the end of a relationship. 

In the first verse, the singer addresses her ex-lover, telling them that they should not expect to come back into her life and find her in the same place that they left her in. She has changed since the relationship ended and has moved on in a way that is “scary” to the ex-lover. The chorus further emphasizes the singer’s message of not wanting her ex-lover to come back into her life, telling them to “walk away” and not start caring about her now. 

The second verse of the song talks about the journey of self-discovery the singer has gone through since the end of the relationship. She acknowledges the pain that the breakup caused her, but also talks about how she has survived and is now “better on the other side”. This verse further emphasizes the message of self-empowerment that the song is conveying. 

The chorus of the song is repeated throughout the song, emphasizing the singer’s message of not wanting her ex-lover to come back into her life and start caring about her again. The song ends with the singer telling her ex-lover to “walk away” and not come back, as she is now in a much better place than when he left her. 

The lyrics of the song contain a message of self-confidence and resilience, as the singer is able to use her experience to become a stronger and more independent version of herself. The language used in the song is straightforward and direct, making it clear that the singer is done with the relationship and not interested in having her ex-lover in her life anymore. Additionally, the upbeat tempo of the song helps to emphasize the message of empowerment and resilience. 

In terms of its linguistic analysis, the song Don’t Start Now by Dua Lipa expresses a sense of resilience and self-confidence that are often experienced by those who have gone through a difficult breakup. The direct and straightforward language used in the lyrics help to emphasize the singer’s message of not wanting her ex-lover to come back into her life, and the upbeat tempo of the song helps to convey a sense of empowerment and strength. Overall, the song is an anthem of self-confidence and resilience that encourages listeners to move on from past relationships and to become stronger and more independent versions of themselves.
