The song "Break My Heart" by Dua Lipa is a heartfelt and emotional ballad that speaks to the power of love and the fear of being hurt. She is scared of the possibility of being hurt, yet she is unable to resist the pull of love.

She ruminates on the idea that she could have stayed home and avoided being hurt, but she was unable to resist that feeling of love. The song ends with Lipa questioning if she is really in love with someone who could break her heart.
