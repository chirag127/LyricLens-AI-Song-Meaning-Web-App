"New Rules (Demo)" by Dua Lipa is a song about the need to make and enforce boundaries in a romantic relationship. The lyrics focus on the difficulty of setting and sticking to these boundaries, as well as the need to accept that some relationships are not meant to be. 

The first verse of the song is about the narrator's attempt to make sense of her relationship and to make sense of her own emotions. She talks about her sleepless nights, her attempts to write down her thoughts and emotions, and her realization that she is stuck in the same situation with the same person. 

The pre-chorus reflects the narrator's need to set boundaries for herself, in order to save herself from being hurt and taken advantage of. She tells herself not to pick up the phone when he calls, not to let him in, and not to be friends with him. 

The chorus reflects the narrator's need for self-discipline and her determination to enforce the rules she has set for herself. She repeats that she has new rules and must tell them to herself, in order to make sure she sticks to them.

The second verse is about the narrator's difficulty in following her own rules and the power of her emotions in the face of her attempts to remain strong. She talks about her struggle to move on and her realization that she has to learn to break the pattern of being drawn back into the same situation.

The pre-chorus and chorus repeat and reinforce the need to stick to the new rules. The narrator acknowledges that her rules are difficult to follow, and she has to practice and repeat them to herself in order to make sure she remembers them. 

In summary, "New Rules (Demo)" is a song about the need to set boundaries and to accept that some relationships are not meant to be. The narrator goes through a difficult journey of realizing that she needs to set rules for herself in order to save herself from being hurt and taken advantage of. The lyrics show the narrator's struggle to stay strong and follow her own rules, and her determination to make sure she sticks to them. 

Linguistically, the song is filled with repetition and personal pronouns, which serves to emphasize the narrator's emotional journey and her need to enforce her own rules. The language is direct and clear, reflecting the narrator's need to be firm in her convictions and her determination to stay strong. 

Overall, "New Rules (Demo)" is an emotionally charged song that emphasizes the need to make and enforce boundaries in a relationship. The lyrics are direct and clear, and the repetition serves to reinforce the narrator's struggle and her determination to stay strong.
