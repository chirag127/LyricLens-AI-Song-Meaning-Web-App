The song “IDGAF” by Dua Lipa is an anthem of empowerment and resilience. The song begins with Lipa recounting a moment when the person she is singing to calls her “all friendly”, telling her how much they miss her. She responds by saying that it’s funny and, given her success and her songs, that they should find someone else to listen to them. This sets the tone for the rest of the song, with Lipa reaffirming her decision to cut off the person and her need to be done with the relationship. 

In the chorus, Lipa sings “so I cut you off, I don’t need your love ‘cause I already cried enough, I’ve been done, I’ve been movin’ on since we said goodbye.” This chorus captures the sentiment of the song; Lipa is done with the relationship and does not need their love or apology anymore, as she has already been through the heartache of the relationship. 

The second verse continues to recount the end of the relationship, with Lipa remembering the weekend when her friend caught her partner “creepin” and quickly moves on to the decision she made to end it all. The chorus follows and then leads into the post-chorus, where Lipa states that she does not care about her partner and that she is done with them. 

The breakdown section serves to drive home the message of the song, with Lipa repeating the chorus and then adding that she does not care about them and does not want them begging for her attention. The song ends with Lipa singing “boy, I don’t give a fuck”. 

In summary, “IDGAF” is a song about empowerment and resilience, with Lipa standing her ground in the face of heartache and moving on from a difficult relationship. The lyrics are filled with energy and strength, making it a powerful anthem of self-love and acceptance. 

Linguistically, the song is filled with strong language, such as the repeated refrain of “I don’t give a fuck”, which serves to emphasize Lipa’s decision to move on and her refusal to be taken advantage of. The use of strong language also helps to convey the strong emotions that Lipa is feeling, such as anger and frustration. Additionally, the song has a clear narrative structure, with Lipa recounting the end of the relationship in the verses and then delivering the message of empowerment in the chorus and post-chorus. 

Overall, “IDGAF” is a powerful anthem of resilience and empowerment. Lipa’s strong lyrics and clear narrative structure make it an unforgettable song that is sure to be an anthem for many people.
