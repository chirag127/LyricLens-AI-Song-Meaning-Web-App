The song "Begging" by Dua Lipa is a heartfelt plea to her lover to stay with her forever. The song's lyrics express a deep desire to have a lasting connection with the person she loves, conveying an emotional intensity that is both powerful and vulnerable. In the first verse, Lipa sings of the loneliness she feels when her partner is not with her, and her need for him to be her remedy. She then goes on to plead with him to stay with her, promising to always hold his head up and break his fall. 

The chorus emphasizes her desperation to keep the relationship alive, with her bones and lungs begging for her lover's love. In the second verse, Lipa muses on the impossibility of finding a love like hers, and the thought of losing her beloved is too much for her to bear. The bridge and breakdown reiterate her promise to always be there for her partner, and the chorus is repeated one last time for emphasis. 

Lyrically, the song conveys a powerful emotion of longing and desperation for the person Lipa loves, with her begging for them to stay with her forever. By repeating the chorus, she emphasizes her need for the relationship to remain strong, and her fear of losing them. Additionally, the imagery of her bones and lungs begging for love conveys the intensity of her emotion and her desire to keep her partner in her life. 

Musically, the song is a mid-tempo pop ballad, with a steady beat and a catchy melody. The production is relatively sparse, with a mix of acoustic and electronic instruments and a catchy chorus. 

Overall, "Begging" is an emotive and passionate plea for a lasting relationship, expressing an intensity of emotion that is both powerful and vulnerable. Through her lyrics, Lipa conveys a deep longing for her lover, begging them to stay with her forever. Musically, the song is a catchy pop ballad, with a steady beat and a memorable chorus.
