Dua Lipa's song "Jealousy" is a powerful expression of the pain and anguish caused by a partner's jealousy. The track begins with a haunting combination of drums and synthesizers that sets the tone for the song's dark themes. In the first verse, Dua Lipa describes her partner's jealousy, saying that the more love she gives, the more her partner rips her apart. This line implies that her partner's jealousy has caused them to become distant, and that their relationship may be crumbling.

The following chorus reinforces this idea, with Lipa singing "you're so full of that jealousy," and expressing how her partner's actions are making her feel. This chorus is backed by a more upbeat instrumentation, with a strong beat and heavy bass that contrasts with the more subdued verse.

The second verse describes the partner's attitude more explicitly, with Lipa singing "you told me I'm a liar, liar, liar," and "you're acting like a fool." Here, Lipa is expressing her frustration with her partner's refusal to trust her and their attempts to control her behavior.

The song's bridge features a more melodic instrumentation as Lipa sings about her partner's "ego fighting for the crown," a line that implies her partner's insecurity and their need to control the relationship. The bridge also serves as a transition to the final chorus, which is more intense and energetic than the previous chorus and highlights the feelings of hurt and betrayal that Lipa has experienced.

In summary, "Jealousy" is a powerful song that captures the pain and anguish caused by a jealous partner. Through her lyrics, Dua Lipa expresses her frustration and hurt, and her refusal to allow their jealousy to control her. Linguistically, the song features a number of literary devices, such as alliteration and repetition, that help to emphasize the emotions of the lyrics. Overall, the song is a powerful and emotive expression of the pain of jealousy.
