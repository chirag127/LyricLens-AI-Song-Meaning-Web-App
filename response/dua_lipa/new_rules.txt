Dua Lipa's song "New Rules" is an empowering anthem about regaining control over a situation and setting boundaries with someone who doesn't reciprocate your love. The lyrics of this song tell the story of a person trying to overcome a toxic relationship, and Lipa encourages the listener to find strength in their newfound independence. 

The song starts off with the repetition of "one" to emphasize the importance of the rules she's about to lay out. In the first verse, Lipa talks about how the person in the relationship is "talkin' in [her] sleep at night" and is making her "crazy out of [her] mind". She recognizes the situation and decides to acknowledge it, writing it down and reading it out in an attempt to save herself. The refrain highlights that her love isn't reciprocated, but despite this, she continues to tell herself that she can get through it. 

The pre-chorus further emphasizes Lipa's advice to let go of this person, saying "Don't pick up the phone, you know he's only callin' 'cause he's drunk and alone" and "Don't be his friend, you know you're gonna wake up in his bed in the morning". This exemplifies the idea that she needs to remove herself from this situation, as it will not have a positive outcome. 

The chorus provides the listener with the "new rules" she has set for herself. She knows she needs to practice these rules and learn them by heart so that she can get through the tough situation. The song ends with a repetition of the pre-chorus to remind the listener that the only way to get over someone is to not let them back in. 

The song "New Rules" is a powerful anthem about taking control and setting boundaries. Through her lyrics, Dua Lipa encourages the listener to take charge of their situation and remove themselves from a toxic relationship. The song conveys a message of strength, resilience, and self-empowerment, and this is reflected in the language used throughout the song and the emotions it evokes. The song has a strong message of self-love and self-worth, and this is an important message that has resonated with many listeners. 

Overall, "New Rules" is an inspiring song that speaks to many people. Its message of self-love and empowerment is an important one, and its catchy tune and strong lyrics have made it a popular anthem.
