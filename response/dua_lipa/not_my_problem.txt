The song “Not My Problem” by Dua Lipa is a confident, empowering anthem conveying the message of taking responsibility for one's own actions, and not letting the actions of others define them. In the first verse, Lipa expresses her frustration with a significant other who is trying to take her down like an anchor, and is making her feel like she is to blame for their anger. In the chorus, Lipa makes it clear that the person’s issues are their own, and that it is not her problem.

The second verse takes a more introspective approach, as Lipa reflects on her own mistakes and misguided behavior, and how it is ultimately her responsibility to take control of her life. In the bridge, Lipa realizes that she has to stop letting the words of others define her, and that she needs to take ownership of her own mistakes and move on.

The song’s lyrics contain several linguistic devices, such as alliteration and repetition, to emphasize the message and create a more powerful impact. The lyrics also contain a lot of imagery, such as the metaphor of the anchor in the first verse, which help to convey the feelings of being weighed down by another person’s actions.

Overall, “Not My Problem” is a powerful song that conveys the message of taking control of one’s own life and not letting the actions of others define them. The use of imagery, repetition, and alliteration make the song’s message even more powerful, and the chorus is an empowering reminder to take responsibility for one’s own decisions and actions.
