"Let Me Love You" is a song by DJ Snake featuring Justin Bieber. In the first verse, Bieber sings about believing in a beautiful future with someone, but realizes it was all an illusion. In the pre-chorus, he sings about not giving up and wanting to love the person through difficult times. The chorus repeats the lines "let me love you" and "I won't give up".

In the second verse, Bieber discusses the idea of not falling asleep at the wheel and continuing to work towards their goals. He also expresses confidence in their abilities and worth. The pre-chorus and chorus repeat before the bridge, in which Bieber pleads with the person to not let go of their relationship. The chorus repeats at the end of the song.

Overall, the song is about not giving up on a relationship and continuing to fight for love. Bieber's vocals are emotive and the production by DJ Snake is captivating. In my opinion, "Let Me Love You" is a beautiful and emotional love song.
